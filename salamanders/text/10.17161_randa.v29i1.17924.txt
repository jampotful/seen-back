Opistoglyphous snakes in the genus Leptodeira will
chew prey in order to enhance venom delivery (Savage 2002;
Solórzano 2004) and are known to feed on treefrogs and their
eggs (Arroyo-Trejos and Mora 2016; Platt et al. 2016). Our
observation adds a novel predator to the list of those known
to take Wood-colored Salamanders.
