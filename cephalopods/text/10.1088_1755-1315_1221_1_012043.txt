Abstract. Octopus is a commodity that has important economic value and high export value.
Gugop Distric is one of the producers of octopus in the form of fresh products and processed
products developed by Kube Mangat. It is said that the octopus species in Indonesia are
dominated by Octopus cyanea, but there is no scientific certainty for the type of octopus in this
Gugop village. This study aimed to identify morphometric analysis is for octopus. This
research conducted in May-July 2022. Information on octopus resources in Aceh is still
limited. Information about octopus biology needs to be known as basic information in octopus
resource management. A total of 35 octopus samples were obtained, there are 5 key
characteristics in Octopus cyanea including ocellus, bluish-white spots, brown markings all
over the body, diamond-shaped papillae on the neck and head. The presence of false eye spots
indicating that this species is Octopus cyanea. The size trend based on the total length of the
octopus showed 6 classes with the highest total in the 68-72 cm category, namely 9 individuals.
These results indicate that the octopus caught is still in a suitable size for capture feasibility
and has entered the reproductive period.

1. Introduction
Octopus is a biota belonging to the phylum Mollusca, class Cephalopoda. In general, octopuses have
eight arms attached to the head, have a beak-shaped mouth, have two rows of suckers under each arm,
and have hectocotylus arms in males [1]. Octopuses can be found in marine waters around the world
[2], with habitats on the bottom of rocky and sandy waters. Octopuses have a distribution ranging from
coastal waters to a depth of 1000 m (continental shelf) [3]. One of the habitat that currently produces a
lot of octopus resources in Aceh is Pulo breuh, on the Pulo Aceh, precisely.
Pulo Breuh is a small island in the north of Aceh which has high potential for marine biodiversity
[4]. Octopus is one of the leading commodities from Pulo Breuh, Pulo Aceh district, which is exported
to several local and national areas. Octopus is a fishery product that has a high economic value [5].
The high demand and selling price of octopus makes octopus a favorite catch that is caught regardless
of size and number. Overfishing will have an impact on the availability of octopuses in nature and the
balance of the ecosystem. The management of octopus resources is needed to balance the efforts to
utilize the octopus. Effective resource management requires species species certainty by recognizing
the key identifying characteristics of the octopus.
Identification by measuring length and weight can provide information on stock composition to
estimate the availability of octopus sizes in the aquatic environment in which they live, mortality,
growth, reproduction, and life cycles so as to provide information about the size range of octopus
organisms that are suitable for catching [6] and increase the selectivity of fishing catches. [7].

collection in the waters of Pulo Breuh, Pulo Aceh District, Aceh Besar (Fig. 1). Morphological

comparison species refers to [11] and [12]. After the identification characteristics are obtained, then
proceed with the measurement of morphometric characters, namely head length (HdL), weight (W)

3. Results and Discussion
3.1. Octopus cyanea description
Morphological observations was obtained the number of 35 octopus specimens that were still
intact and fresh (Figure 2). The initial observation seen was the color of this octopus which
has light brown to blackish brown streaks on the dorsal and along the arms (Figure 6). The
head is protruding and moderately sized, there is a slightly hard structure on the dorsal side of
the head, the eyes are small and protruding. This octopus had 2 row of suckers on its eight
arms. The left arm of the octopus has a pair of ocellus in the form of a black oval-shaped
circle surrounded by a brownish-white ring called the false eye (Figure 3). The ocellus is a
pattern shaped like a dark spot surrounded by a pale or cream colored ring on the inside and
dark brown-black on the outside. The ocellus is present on the crown side of the arm above
the third arm [10], [14]. Along the arms of this octopus has a pattern like a pattern of bluishwhite spots. This bluish-white spot pattern is a key characteristic that distinguishes the O.
cyanea octopus from other octopus (Figure 7). This color pattern is related to the camouflage
ability of O. cyanea to various habitats [15]. According to [16] the color of O. cyanea
becomes blackish brown when approached, touched or disturbed.

Octopus cyanea has as many as 4 papillae with diamond formations on the neck and head
that is shaped like a blackish gray pseudo protrusion (Figures 4 and 5). In its lifecycle, O.
cyanea can produce various color patterns, ranging from dark brown to black, streaked with
indistinct patterns, to gray and white marked with black ocelli. Active octopuses generally
show an indistinct coloration. When in the nest it shows a pale to white color with two red
bands running from the body to the eyes and down to the dorsal crown of the arm [10].

The frequency distribution of Octopus cyanea's mantle length ranges from 10-14 cm (Fig. 10). This
size shows the octopus caught in accordance with the size suitable for catching. [17], said the first
adult octopus with a coat length of 7 cm. The sample octopus was caught in July-August when the
west season. Catching octopuses during the west season causes many obstacles because it is difficult
for fishermen to go to sea due to strong winds and big waves. So that this period becomes a pause for
octopus resources in reproducing and can develop without any intervention from outside the
ecosystem. According to [17], octopuses are abundant in the period from december to march, namely
during the east season as evidenced by the abundance of fishermen's catches.
4. Conclusion
The octopus found in the waters of Pulo Breuh, precisely in the Gampong Gugop, is of the type
Octopus cyanea. The key characteristic that forms the basis of identification is that on the arms of
these three octopuses there is an ocellus that is oval in shape and surrounded by a white line. The
whole body of this octopus has a blackish brown pattern. Along the eight arms have bluish-white
spots. The morphometric characteristics measured included total length, weight and mantle length.
The total length produced is 6 classes, where the class that has the most individuals is in the medium
category in the range of 68-72 cm. The weight class of octopus was obtained as many as 7 with the
highest frequency in the range of 856-968 grams with 13 individuals. While the mantle length class
obtained 5 classes with the highest frequency at 12 cm as many as 12 individuals. These results
indicate that the octopus caught is still of a suitable size and has entered the reproductive period.
