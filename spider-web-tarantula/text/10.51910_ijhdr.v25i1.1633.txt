Introduction: Ultra-high diluted compounds (UHDs), traditionally considered to lack any active
substances due to dilutions beyond the Avogadro limit, exhibit distinct properties that challenge
this assumption [1,2]. This study qualitatively examines the nature and structure of UHDs
through scanning electron microscopy (SEM). UHDs, derived from various sources, including
metals, non-metals, chemicals, plants, animals, and nosodes, were analyzed for the presence of
visible residues termed Essential Original Matter (EOM), which formed under standard
conditions [3] and are observable to the naked eye, presenting unique, stable structures distinct
from placebos. Objectives: To identify and document the specific structural patterns in UHD
solutions and analyze the morphological and qualitative characteristics of EOMs. Methodologies:
Five UHDs of the dilution strength of 1,000,000X (1000cH or 1M) were prepared using ultra-pure
water. SEM was employed to examine isolated EOMs deposited on lamellae and photograph them.
Control samples consisted of lamellae with pure water only.
Results: SEM analysis revealed filamentous or elongated structures with variations in curvature,
surface texture, and structural integrity. Anacardium officinalis exhibited a highly curved, twisted,
rough, and irregular surface. Tarantula H. displayed a long, thin, smoother structure with
negligible undulations. Sulphur appeared stretched, fragile, and partially broken with
pronounced irregularities. Medorrhinum had a rough, irregular surface with pronounced
curvature, while X-ray UHDs were stiff, straight, and layered. Structural differences were
observed in curvature, surface texture, and thickness, with Anacardium officinalis and
Medorrhinum showing greater curvature, while X-ray UHDs remained straighter. Conclusions:
These findings demonstrate that UHDs exhibit diverse physical properties linked to their
preparation and composition. Contrary to the assumption that UHDs are vacuous, observable
EOM structures were identified. Further quantitative investigation is necessary to understand the
development and characteristics of EOM.
