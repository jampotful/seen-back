## Other problems

1. Section 1 said sharpness "does not divide" the sunfish from the reef fish, using 1.3 to 4.6 c/° for "two reef damselfish". That range mixes behavioural and anatomical estimates, and one of the two fish is a dottyback. Compared like with like, the reef fish resolve 1.29 to 1.71 c/° by behaviour against 3.6 to 5 for the sunfish, which is two to four times coarser. By anatomy they come to 3.2 to 4.6, against 3.4 to 6.7 predicted from the Bluegill's cones. The checked sheet states both comparisons. This bears on the recommendation, which folds the reef fish into `fish` at a `cpd` of 4, and on open question 1.
2. The `rate` value of 1 is the app's own choice, since the sources fall on both sides of 60. The checked sheet marks it `invented`.
3. Open question 5 gives lampreys and hagfish 0.07% of fish records, while section 1 gives 0.08% to lampreys, hagfish, lungfish and the coelacanth together. Both may be right, but the sheet gives no separate count for lampreys and hagfish, and the audit did not check one.
4. Section 4, item 1 said that outside the circle the surface reflects like a mirror. No opened source states it, and the checked sheet now says so.
5. Wording: no em dashes, no "matters", and no "not X but Y" constructions were found. American spellings appear only in published titles, which stay as published.
