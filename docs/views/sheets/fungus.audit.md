# Audit of the fungus sensing sheet

Sheet audited: `fungus.md` (views `fungus` and `slime`). Corrected sheet: `fungus.checked.md`. The audit ran from 09:35 to 10:06 UTC on 12 October 2026, against `BRIEF.md` and `AUDIT.md`.

How the pages were read. The shell could reach no outside host (every download, including davidmoore.org.uk and iab.kit.edu, got proxy 403), so every page was read through WebFetch, which returns a summary with short verbatim quotes. Each figure below was matched against a verbatim fragment of its source unless the line says otherwise. The fetch tool asked for a permission prompt before opening an address it had not seen in an earlier result, and those prompts went unanswered, so guessed publisher addresses were refused and other copies were found through WebSearch.

Pages refused during the audit: the Europe PMC REST search for doi:10.1101/gad.1369605 (HTTP 429, rate limited; not retried); https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1315395/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC304150 (browser check); https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.84.1.104 and https://www.pnas.org/doi/pdf/10.1073/pnas.84.1.104 (fetch error); https://genesdev.cshlp.org/content/19/23/2888.full, a guessed rupress.org address for Galland and Russo, and https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC304150/ (permission prompt unanswered). Other copies were found for everything except Galland and Lipson (1987).

## Anything else wrong

- Section 1 lists ten orders as the largest, but Hymenochaetales (91,408 records in the repository file and in today's GBIF facet) ranks above Peltigerales (89,412) and is missing. A sentence is added.
- Section 1 repeats the unsupported link between parietin and the algae ("screens blue light from them", "filters blue out before it reaches the algae"). Reworded.
- Section 3: in `M`, the 550 nm clause from Galland and Lipson is dropped; in `gain`, "caps made under" becomes "fruit bodies formed under".
- Section 4, nearness: "in light or dark" becomes "whatever the surrounding light", since Cohen and colleagues say "independent of ... ambient light".
- Section 5: Kuroda's issue is corrected to 23, Gauslaa's issue 4 is added, the lines for Moore 2016, Galland and Lipson, Moore 1994 and Masui are amended, and Terashima and colleagues 2005 is added as source 28.
- The 3 to 4 hours in the Down row stays because Moore and colleagues 1996 state it verbatim, but the same group's 1992 measurements (mean tip angle about 47 degrees at about 3 hours) suggest many stems take longer.
- The recommendation names Myxomycetes and Ceratiomyxales for `slime`, while the repository's slime mould group also holds one record of Dictyosteliales, a social amoeba. Left as is.
- The engine claims check out. `src/cam.b.js` loads the matrix with `gl.uniformMatrix3fv(u.u_M, true, ...)` and applies `u_M * c`, so each row of `M` makes one output channel and equal rows give grey, as section 3 says. Line 199 sets the averaging weight with `Math.max(a, .2)` when `EXT_color_buffer_float` is missing, as section 4 says.
- Wording: I found no em dashes, no "not X but Y" or "Y, not X", nothing said to matter and no praise. US spellings appear only inside quotations, titles and the common name "Dog Vomit Slime Mold", which stay as printed.
