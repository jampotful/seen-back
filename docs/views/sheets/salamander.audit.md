- **Section 3, `fovea`.** The quotation "which is magnified" was not verbatim (see Wiggers and colleagues, 1995). The words "in the tongue-shooters only" implied a comparison the study did not make. Both are reworded.
- **Unsourced statements left in place and now flagged.** These are that the red-backed salamander sits outside the tongue-shooters' tribe (section 3 `fovea` and open question 5), and that the water-dwelling families "keep their eyes" (section 1).
- **Section 1, caecilians.** "Light, colour and direction" claimed direction, which the source does not discuss; it now reads "light and colour with no image". The quoted sentence is now marked as the preprint's interpretation.
- **Section 3, why `gate` stays unset.** It called the maze marks "still visual marks". The abstract does not say whether they moved; reworded.
