# Audit of the sensing sheet: crab, woodlouse and other crustacean

Audited on 12 October 2026, from 18:51 to 19:40 UTC. Every source below was opened in this session with WebFetch unless the line says otherwise. Shell downloads were refused by the proxy (HTTP 403), so all reading went through WebFetch. The corrected sheet is `crab.checked.md`.

## Sources

- **Zeil and Al-Mutairi, 1996.** Corrected. The full text (https://cob.silverchair.com/jeb/article-pdf/199/7/1569/3391178/jexbio_199_7_1569.pdf) gives "νs=0.39±0.096 cycles degree−1; ∆φh=1.33±0.32 °" (N=12), says horizontal resolution "varies little throughout the visual field", fits the vertical angle as ∆φv=0.0025(n−41)²+0.25 (no unit in the equation; the figure axis reads degrees), and measured vertical resolution "at 30 ° azimuth intervals". Two corrections: the paper says some of its graphs stop at 45 degrees above and below the horizon and that the real field extends further (the sheet said the field reached past 45 degrees "in some preparations"), and the full title ends "(Ocypodidae, Brachyura, Decapoda)". - **Zeil, Nalbach and Nalbach, 1986.** Confirmed (abstract only). - **Bagheri and colleagues, 2020.** Confirmed. ## Anything else

- The grades are uneven. "All around" and "Sharpness" are graded `g` because one fiddler crab's figure is drawn for all crabs, while "Pace" and "Night" rest on one fiddler crab species and are graded `m` although "Pace" also drives `rate` for every crab. Both pass the rule that `m` rests on a measurement in the creature named, so I left them. If the owner wants a grade to describe the drawn view, "Pace" should be `g`.
- Zeil, Nalbach and Nalbach worked with the 1986 family boundaries. It is my own inference, from no source opened, that their Grapsidae may include crabs now placed in Varunidae or Sesarmidae; the abstract lists no species, so the "absent" marks for modern Grapsidae and Portunidae in section 1 are approximate.
- The statements that a phone camera does not record polarization and that a web page cannot read humidity still have no opened source; the sheet already says so.
- DOIs were not resolved for any source; addresses were opened directly. The DOIs of Zeil and Al-Mutairi (1996), Zeil (1990), Garfias and colleagues (1995) and the two sources the audit added are unchecked.
- Wording: no em dashes, no "not X but Y" constructions and no use of "matter" were found. "Color" appears only inside a paper's title. The Battelle source line's "a UV opsin in eccentric cells, not yet confirmed by recording" sat close to the "Y, not X" form and now reads "a UV opsin in eccentric cells that recordings have yet to confirm".
