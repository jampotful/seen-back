# Jumping spider view: parameter table for review

One page of settings behind the `jumper` view of Regards, a phone app that shows a person a photographed animal's view of the world. Grades: m measured in this animal or in typical members of the group, g measured in a relative or a wider group, i a guess from nearby evidence, x left out of the picture. "The app's" marks a value the app chose where the evidence runs out. Please fill the last two columns where you disagree or know better, and answer the six questions below the table.

## B. The gaze strip

| Setting | Value and units | Grade | Source | Your value | Your comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strip width and height | 2 by 20 deg. One straight strip is the app's choice. | m | Land 1969 | | |
| Jump (saccade) speed | At least 150 deg/s (a 15 deg jump within 0.1 s). The engine uses 300 deg/s; the excess over 150 is the app's. Body turns run 120 to 1,200 deg/s. | m | Land 1969; Land 1972 (Metaphidippus harfordi) | | |
| Latency from movement to first turn | Shortest 80 to 120 ms; most turns begin within 100 ms of the target stopping; about 0.5 s in a cueing task. The engine draws 0.1 to 0.5 s (the app's). | m | Land 1971; Ferrante et al. 2024 | | |
| What starts a jump | A small movement of a target already in view. Still targets, flashes in place and whole-field movement (gratings) start nothing. The app removes the phone's own movement by gyroscope or image motion. | m | Land 1969; Land 1971; Zurek et al. 2010 | | |

## Questions

1. In the species most photographed in Florida (*Anasaitis*, *Lyssomanes*, *Platycryptus*, *Colonus*, *Hentzia*, *Menemerus bivittatus*), do the principal eyes have only ultraviolet and green classes, or has any shown a red filter or a third class?
2. Do the possible blue-sensitive receptors found by opsin labelling in some side eyes (*Menemerus bivittatus*) give those eyes colour, or only brightness, and does the colour seen by the principal eyes carry over to the wide scene the side eyes watch?
3. What are the flicker fusion frequency and photoreceptor response time of the principal eyes and of the front side eyes in any jumping spider?
4. In a free-living spider on a wall, how often does the gaze strip jump to a new target, how long does it stay on one, and does the 5 second suppression of a looked-at place apply to the principal eyes as well as the front side eyes?
5. At one instant, how much of the field do the two retinal strips resolve together, and is one straight strip a fair way to draw the pair for a person?
6. Has the red-light depth result of Nagata et al. 2012 been repeated in other species, and how large is the second, defocused image in natural scenes?
