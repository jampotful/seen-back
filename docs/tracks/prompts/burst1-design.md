# Burst 1: the design (Fable if its allowance is separate, else Opus)

You write the two documents every later agent builds from. Read first: `docs/COVERAGE.md`, `src/cam.a.js` (`EYES`, `paramsOf`), `src/cam.b.js` (the engine's three passes), `docs/tracks/engine.md`, `docs/views/sheets/*.md`, `docs/views/drafts/*.md`, and the drafts on the branches `research/birds`, `research/cat-dog` and `research/ground-invertebrates` (`git fetch origin 'refs/heads/research/*:refs/remotes/origin/research/*'`). Grep for the sections on new effects and the senses the camera lacks; read only those. Stop after 90 minutes. Push to `agent/design` within 10 minutes of starting and every 15 minutes.

1. **`docs/views/ATLAS.md`.** One entry per sensing mechanism that any creature the app names uses (expect fifteen to twenty-five: sight that registers only motion, a gaze that jumps between targets, colour classes beyond three and ultraviolet, polarization, a sharp band or spot in a wide blurred field, slit and bar pupils, dim-light sight and photon noise, flicker and pace, substrate and web vibration, airflow, hearing and sound localization, echolocation, heat, electric fields, chemistry, light sensed with no image, head stabilization and bobbing, and whatever else the sheets show). For each: the mechanism in plain words; the evidence (cite the sheet or log line); the groups and views that have it, with their record shares from `docs/COVERAGE.md` and the sheets; what the phone can sense in its place (camera, microphone, speaker and microphone together for echoes, motion sensors, compass, clock, ambient light from the camera's exposure) and what it can output (the screen, the vibration motor where the platform allows, the speaker); and **two or three structurally different ways to draw it**, each a few sentences a person could picture at once, with a cost on a phone (low, medium, high). The ways must differ in kind, as a continuous frame differs from a sequence of held snapshots and from an effect felt through the hand. The options the owner values most are unexpected, immediately graspable as a visual or spatial proposition, and produce desire; strategic soundness alone is not enough. At least one option per mechanism should reframe what the view is, as a view that is mostly felt through the hand reframes a view that is seen. The owner picks one per mechanism. Mark what is invented. Do not write rows.
2. **`docs/ENGINE-DESIGN.md`.** The module list the engine needs to draw every entry, in build order by how many record-weighted views each unlocks; for each module the uniforms and state it needs and how it composes with the others in the existing three passes; the **spec schema** for a view (a JSON file naming modules, parameters with grade and `invented` flags, and rows) that `cam.a.js` would load, so that a view is data; the tuning panel and a compare slider (human view against the creature's); and a **visibility floor** method: for each parameter, the smallest change that is visible on a phone-sized render (measure by rendering a fixed clip at two values and comparing frames), so that research stops at that precision.

Writing: plain sentences, Canadian spelling, no em dashes, no "not X but Y", no fragments. Final report at most 300 words: the mechanisms, the module order, the three mechanisms whose drawing options you find most striking, and your last commit.
