Start with `burst2-common.md`. Your track is `everyday`; work on `agent/everyday` in your own clone. Stop after 210 minutes. The research desk owns `docs/views/**` and `research/*` branches; the hardening agent (`agent/hardening`) is changing `src/` at the same time, so fetch and rebase `main` before every merge and keep your edits to the functions you need; if both of you bump `BUILD` in `src/cam.a.js`, take the larger number plus one. You write `tools/species/`, `tools/world/`, `models/`, `data/`, `src/` (recognition only), `test/`, workflows you need under `.github/workflows/` and `ci/`, and dated entries in `docs/LOG.md`.

The gap, from `docs/HANDOFF.md` ("Known and open"): cattle, dogs, sheep, most houseplants and common garden flowers are missing from the species list, and the name a small model gave stands when the larger model is sure of the kind and unsure of the species. A first-time user photographs a dog, a cat, a pet bird, a horse, a cow, a chicken, a pigeon on a street, a pothos, a monstera, a snake plant, a rose, a sunflower, a tulip, a lawn. Each should get the right kind and a view, in Alachua County and anywhere else.

1. Read how the species list, its place-and-season prior, the pocket model's head and the larger model's labels are built (`tools/species/README.md`, `tools/world/README.md`, `RESULTS.md`, `docs/tracks/head.md`, `docs/tracks/worlddb.md`, `docs/ENGINES.md`, `models/*/manifest.json`, the workflows `data`, `world*`, `lab`, `traindata*`). Write down in `tools/species/EVERYDAY.md` where a species must be added for each path to know it.
2. Build a curated list of about 150 domestic and cultivated species (pets, livestock, poultry, feral pigeons, the commonest houseplants, garden and cut flowers, lawn grasses, common vegetables and fruit trees), each with its GBIF key and scientific name taken from data you retrieve (build machines reach GBIF; never from memory), its kind of eye or view as the app maps it, and a prior that holds wherever people live (indoors, gardens), outside the wild-observation counts.
3. Add them on every path from step 1. Any model text embedding is computed on build machines with the existing workflows. Measure on photos: build machines can fetch iNaturalist photos marked captive or cultivated for these species (use the existing photo tools; keep photos off `main`). Pass marks: on the new species, the kind right at least 85% and the species right at least 60% with the larger model; no pass mark in `docs/tracks/head.md` and no existing test-photo score drops by more than 1 point.
4. Fix the small-model name: when the larger model is sure of the kind and unsure of the species, show the kind and its view without the small model's species name (or the larger model's top species when its own confidence clears the page's existing threshold). Test it.
5. Ship only what passes: run `python3 build.py`, `python3 build.py --check`, `node test/check4.js try` and the recognizer checks in `test/rec/`; bump `BUILD`; merge to `main` yourself; add to `docs/LOG.md` what shipped and the measurements. If a mark fails, ship nothing for that path and write the measurement down.

Push `agent/everyday` at least every 15 minutes. Final report, at most 200 words: species added and where, the measurements against the marks, what shipped, the bug fix, last commit.
