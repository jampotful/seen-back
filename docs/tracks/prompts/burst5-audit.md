# Burst 5: the batch audit of every research log (Sonnet)

Start with `burst2-common.md`. Your track is `audit`. The key works and the owner's Console workspace is capped at $150 (the extraction runs have used about $31 of it); this audit may spend up to $12, and each push must set its own ceiling. Stop after 120 minutes. Do not launch research units and do not touch the owner's Drive; the round 2 research thread runs those, so leave any `research/*-met` branch's files unchanged (read them only).

Queue entry 6. The question for every record in every unit's evidence log is whether its quote supports its stated value, trait, taxon and grade, so that a spec's numbers can be trusted.

1. **The logs.** Every branch `research/<unit>` holds `docs/views/evidence/<unit>.jsonl` (about 4,700 records across the first fourteen units, and the round 2 units `mammals-met`, `birds-met`, `herps-met` and `insects-met` that the research thread has finished; list the branches with `git ls-remote --heads origin 'research/*'` and fetch each with a bounded fetch). Read `docs/views/research/BRIEF.md` for the log format and grades.
2. **The auditor** as `tools/lit/audit.py`, in the pattern of `tools/lit/extract.py` (Message Batches API, Haiku 5.5, a cached system prompt, a script check on the reply). For each record it sends the record and its quote and asks: does the quote state this value for this taxon (yes, partly, no), is the units and conversion right, is the grade (`m` measured on the taxon, `g` borrowed from a relative, `i` invented) honest, and what is wrong in one sentence. Only the quote and the record are sent, never a private paper. Records whose quote is empty or whose source is a book the audit cannot see go to a separate list.
3. **The run.** A job `.github/workflows/lit-audit.yml` with `ci/lit/audit.sh` and `ci/lit/audit.env`, in the pattern of `lit-table` (push to `run/lit-audit/<label>`; results to `ci`, and the flagged records to the branch `pub/lit-audit`). Run it over every log. Spend a small run on one unit first to check the verdicts by eye.
4. **The flagged records.** For records the auditor marks `no` or `partly`, read the source passage through the literature table (`pub/lit-table`, searched by doi and quote; or `pub/lit-t1`'s text) and decide: confirm, correct the value or grade, or retract. Write the decisions to `docs/views/audit/<unit>.jsonl` on `agent/audit` (the unit, the record's id, the verdict, the corrected record or the reason). Do not edit the research branches or the logs; a later step applies decisions. Report the share flagged per unit.

Final report, at most 250 words: the share each unit has flagged, what the flags show about which traits and grades go wrong most, the spend, where the files are, and your last commit.
