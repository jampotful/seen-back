# Audit of a desk screening unit (desk-screen.md rules, 18 October 2026)

When the unit includes evidence records, audit them under the scientific-context and counting section of `oa-audit.md` (29 October 2026), in addition to the evidence rules the unit was written under. Report evidence and search-record counts separately; the search and wanted-line counting rules below do not exempt scientifically consequential omissions in evidence.

You audit search records and wanted lines you did not write. The launch prompt names the unit (`harvest-N`) and its branch. Work in a worktree made from `origin/main` and read the unit with `git show <branch>:<path>`. Read `docs/tracks/progress/audit-39.md` for the counting rule (audit 42's report was never committed; the faults of audits 40 to 42 are the opening paragraph of `desk-screen.md`), and `docs/tracks/prompts/desk-screen.md` for the rules the unit was written under.

When an assignment also includes evidence or skipped leads, use BRIEF's 40-word quote limit and the quotation-integrity rules in `oa-read.md`. There is no 125-character evidence limit. Historical audit decisions based solely on that length do not establish that a lead lacks usable evidence. Reopen the source and check scientific support, taxon, conditions, provenance and faithful quotation; never reinstate a record solely because its length passes. A tool output cap calls for legal source retrieval or an explicit retrieval gap, not a negative search finding.

1. Mechanical gate first: `python3 -I tools/views/screen_candidates.py --check <searched file> --wanted <wanted file>` on the unit's files (saved to /tmp). Any problem it prints fails the unit without further checking; report the count and stop.
2. Draw the sample before reading anything: every search record line, `random.seed(<N>)`, `random.sample(lines, 20)` (all if fewer). Write the 20 line numbers into your report first.
3. For each sampled record: (a) `queries` repeat `python3 -I tools/views/harvest_get.py <Genus>` word for word, then list the "web: " searches; (b) `chased` is a DOI printed in the citation step or a reason the output supports; (c) take three of its `screened` entries (all if fewer) and judge from the abstract in the harvest output, or a page a WebSearch of the title returned, whether the work plainly holds no measurement of the trait; (d) run your own two WebSearches for the trait ("<Genus> <trait words>", "<common name> <trait words>"; traits that share words share them, as desk-screen.md step 2 lists) and one more of your choosing, and look for a measurement of that trait in a species of the genus the record missed. WebFetch only a URL your own WebSearch returned; if a fetch asks for permission or is refused, do not retry it.
4. An error is: queries that differ, an unsupported `chased`, a screened work that does report the measurement, or a measurement the record missed. Wanted lines are checked for DOI, authors and year against a page you opened; faults there are listed but not counted.
5. Write `docs/tracks/progress/audit-<M>.md`: the sample, a verdict per record with a one-line reason, the error count and rate, and the rate pooled with the desk's other audits of desk-screen units. Under 5% passes. A sample with exactly 5% errors (1 in 20) is not decided: draw 15 more records and judge the pooled rate (audits 59 and 60 did so), which THOROUGH.md's "above 5% is checked again" leaves open.

Never open ResearchGate, academia.edu, Sci-Hub or LibGen results, even to check; no library login or proxy; never commit paper text. Commit to the branch your launch prompt names with the attribution trailers your platform reminder gives. Reply in at most 100 words: sample, errors, rate, pooled rate, verdict, last commit.

Proxy mirrors count as library proxies: never open a result whose host is a library's proxy or mirror (a host holding "ezproxy", "libproxy", "elogim", "idm.oclc.org", "proxy." or a university library's name before a publisher's domain), even when it opens without a login (audit 43 reached springerlink.bibliotecabuap.elogim.com once).
