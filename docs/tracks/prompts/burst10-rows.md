Start with `burst2-common.md`. Your track is `rows`; work on `agent/rows` in your own clone. Stop after 150 minutes. API spend: up to $6, Sonnet 5.5 through a build-machine workflow in the pattern of `views-compile.yml`, plan-only by default, and plan-only again when you finish.

1. **The four live rows known to be false** (round 2 found them on 13 October): the deer's 310 degrees has no source; the wader and bird ultraviolet rows are false for waders, ducks and vultures; the squirrel flicker rate is wrong for tree squirrels; big cats have round pupils. For each, read the evidence on `main`, then correct the row in `views/<key>.json` (or, with no evidence for the claim, lower its grade to `i` and say so plainly, or remove it), in the voice of VOICE.md. One commit per view. Run `node test/check4.js try` and `python3 -I tools/views/check_spec.py` after.
2. **A mechanical voice check**, `tools/views/voice_check.py` with its test: it reads every row of every view and reports each broken rule of VOICE.md that a script can see (the word "people" or "person" for humans; mentions of the app, phone, screen or view in a row, or a last sentence about the view; the banned-word list and the "not X, but Y" forms in the owner's standing rules; sentence fragments by a simple heuristic; Canadian spelling; more than three sentences; unrounded numbers with no job). It prints counts per rule and per view and writes `docs/views/voice/REPORT.md`.
3. **A rows pipeline**, `tools/views/rows.py`, that drafts rows per view from the evidence: it reads the evidence logs with the audit's decisions applied (`tools/views/audit_rule.py`, `docs/views/audit/decisions.jsonl`), the carry rules and the view's spec, computes each row's grade per species, and asks Sonnet (batch, cached system prompt holding VOICE.md and its fifteen example rows) for one to three sentences per row. Every draft passes `voice_check.py` and a check that each number in it appears in a cited log record for that species or its stated relative; failures are retried once, then listed. Output goes to a `rows_draft` block in the view's JSON, never over the live rows. Run it on the build machine for the eight views with the most records first (bird, insect, spider, jumper, dog, cat, bee, plant) and report in `docs/views/voice/REPORT.md`: pass rates, the rows that failed and why, and ten drafts beside the live rows for the owner to read.

Merge to `main` yourself after `node test/check4.js try` and the tests of the tools you touched pass; fetch `main` first and rebase, because other agents push there. Final report, at most 200 words: what is corrected, voice-check counts before and after, pass rates of the drafts, spend, last commit.
