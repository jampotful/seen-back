Trait question (reported, not ruled on; the owner's ruling that UV counts under sense_light_nonvisual stands and no line is counted on this ground). As a general documented mechanism, UV-B acts on plants both as a signal (a photoreceptor-mediated acclimation programme whose classic outputs are flavonoid sunscreen accumulation and antioxidant gene induction) and as a damaging dose (oxidative stress, membrane lipid peroxidation, photosystem II inhibition); I give this without a source and from general knowledge. Neither paper tests a photoreceptor or a wavelength series, so no line separates signal from damage. On the readouts themselves: line 4 (quercetin accumulation) and line 2 (peroxidase induction) are regulated responses of the kind a signal reading fits best; line 1 (ROS and membrane peroxidation) and line 5 (Fv/Fm decline) are readouts of damage at a dose, and line 3 (chlorophyll a/b) is a pigment change the paper itself calls temporary and the abstract calls not dramatic. If the owner later narrows "UV counts" to responses that read UV as a signal, lines 1, 3 and 5 are the first to fall.
