## Checks
- Trait: the Material and methods section ("Material and methods") prints "Eye sizes are measured as the maximum diameter from either the dorsal or the frontal view." It is the only eye sentence in the Methods besides the abbreviation list. eye_diameter_mm stands; units mm from "All measurements are in millimetres (mm) ...".
- Abbreviation expansions: in the publisher XML each eye label is `<abbrev xlink:title="anterior median eye">AME</abbrev>` and so on; the expansion is an attribute (tooltip), never element text. The HTML-to-text fetch of the preview page shows the expansions inline, which is a rendering artefact of the tool. The desk's quotes (without expansions) are exact. The desk's "inference, UNCHECKED" is now checked.
- Quotes: both sentences reassembled from the XML match the desk character for character (en dashes in AME–AME etc., semicolon after PLE, final stop). One whole sentence each, 22 words by check.py count, nothing cut, no qualifier dropped. Sentences before/after as the desk states (male: preceded by "AER and PER procurved slightly."; female: preceded by "Carapace 0.97 long, 0.76 wide; opisthosoma 1.44 long, 1.09 wide."; followed by the MOA sentences).
- Values: male AME 0.05, ALE 0.05, PME 0.05, PLE 0.05; female AME 0.04, ALE 0.07, PME 0.06, PLE 0.06. Each line's label, value and sex match digit for digit; no zero values.
- Specimens: male sub-heading prints "Male (holotype; Figs 4A, B, 6A)." (no code; Type material ties the holotype to IZCAS-Ar44648). Female sub-heading prints "Female (paratype IZCAS-Ar44658; Figs 5A, B, 6B)." Both sentences sit under these run-in headings in "Description.", so each sentence is tied to one specimen. The "Variation." paragraph gives body-size ranges (4 males, 4 females) and no eye data, so no contrary or range values are omitted.
- Headings as printed: "Taxonomic account" > "Family Hahniidae Bertkau, 1878" > "Subfamily Hahniinae Bertkau, 1878" > "Genus Goblinia Lin & Li, gen. nov." > "Goblinia tiane Lin & Li, sp. nov." > "Description." The desk's `where` omits "Taxonomic account" and drops the comma before "sp. nov.".

Publisher's own free full text: accepted.
