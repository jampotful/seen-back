# Audit 1001: desk-d505, lines 4 to 7 (Otomops harrisoni, sense_echolocation)

Scope: lines 4-7 of docs/views/evidence/desk-d505.jsonl only (Patterson et al. 2018, PeerJ 6: e4864, DOI 10.7717/peerj.4864). 3 WebSearches; WebFetch only on URLs from those results: peerj.com/articles/4864 (twice), peerj.com/articles/4864.pdf, mammaldiversity.org/taxon/1005239. PMC, zendy and library-catalogue results were not opened.

## Rulings on the desk flags

- Sample means: each line is the mean of one species at one site (Chyulu Hills n = 4 bats, Mt Suswa n = 6 bats, ten calls per bat). rules.md lists "pooled records" with synonyms, hybrids and cultivars as errors; it does not define a single-species site mean as pooled, and main holds about 438 sense_echolocation lines filing means or ± values (e.g. Kogia breviceps, oa-7; Coelops frithii, desk-d325). The Musanze "Otomops sp." column is not filed, and the two Kenyan sites are filed separately, so nothing crosses taxa or sites. Kept. If the owner's reading is that any sample mean is a pooled record, all four lines drop together.
- Identification: calls were assigned "by association" (no genetically sampled bat was recorded; vouchers kept). The PDF says vouchers from the Chyulu and Suswa roosts included only O. harrisoni and calls Chyulu Hills the site "where only O. harrisoni were genotyped"; the authors call for non-invasive genetic samples. This supports filing under O. harrisoni with the qualifier in conditions; grade m kept.
- Authority: MDD taxon 1005239 (opened from own search) prints Otomops harrisoni, "Validity status: species", Ralph, Richards, Taylor, Napier & Lamb 2015, Molossidae, Chiroptera, Kenya in range. Correct.
- Source: PDF byline Bruce D. Patterson, Paul W. Webala, Michael Bartonjo, Julius Nziza, Carl W. Dick, Terrence C. Demos matches "Patterson BD, Webala PW, Bartonjo M, Nziza J, Dick CW, Demos TC"; "PeerJ 6:e4864", 2018, DOI correct; title verbatim. Licence: HTML prints "This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License" (rest returned paraphrased); PDF prints "Distributed under Creative Commons CC-BY 4.0". Accepted.
- Table cells: Table 1 headers "Otomops harrisoni Chyulu Hills, Kenya (n = 4)", "Otomops harrisoni Mt Suswa, Kenya (n = 6)", "Otomops sp. Musanze, Rwanda (n = 6)"; rows "Peak frequency (kHz) 17.18 ± 0.42 (16.40–17.95) | 11.86 ± 0.72 (9.52–13.80) | 11.48 ± 0.74 (9.44–13.49)" and "Call duration (ms) 10.72 ± 1.10 (7.57–12.67) | 9.74 ± 1.21 (7.04–15.16) | 8.41 ± 0.99 (5.03–11.34)". Every value maps to the right column digit for digit. Quotes are whole rows within 40 words.
- Methods read (Materials and Methods > Echolocation call recordings and analysis): D1000X (printed "Petterson D1000X"), 384 kHz, 16 bit, 512-point FFT Hanning, Kaleidoscope v.3.1.4b, ten best-SNR calls for each of 16 bats, search-phase selection. Method field accurate. No eye-size lines in range, so no eye check applies.
- Repeats: "Otomops" and "peerj.4864" grepped over the whole docs/ tree on origin/main: absent outside desk-d505. Correct.

## Verdict per line

Line 4 (Chyulu Hills, peak frequency 17.18 kHz): ERROR, fixed.
Line 5 (Mt Suswa, peak frequency 11.86 kHz): ERROR, fixed.
Line 6 (Chyulu Hills, call duration 10.72 ms): ERROR, fixed.
Line 7 (Mt Suswa, call duration 9.74 ms): ERROR, fixed.

Shared faults on all four lines:
1. conditions says "the table has no caption or footnote, the S.E. is stated in the text". False: the PDF prints the caption "Search-phase call parameters for Otomops from Kenya and Rwanda." with "Means (±S.E.) are presented, with observed ranges in parentheses."; the HTML reader dropped it. The search-phase qualifier was missing. (counted: misstated source)
2. conditions says "the Chyulu Hills roost is the one where only O. harrisoni were genotyped", implying Mt Suswa is weaker; the paper says vouchers from both Kenyan roosts were O. harrisoni only. Misleading for the identification of lines 5 and 7. (counted with 1 as the line's error)
3. conditions carries stray fragments "Chyulu Hills (first)" / "Mt Suswa (second)" and a garbled "Mt Suswa / Chyulu Hills counterpart". (form)
4. Significant site difference stated without its statistic; added F2,13 = 12.583, P = 0.001 for peak frequency, maximum P = 0.005, minimum P = 0.024, duration P > 0.05, and the authors' "unknown whether differences documented here reflect specific or regional differentiation". (form)
5. where did not print the row label or column header as printed; now names caption, Discussion placement, row and column. (form)
