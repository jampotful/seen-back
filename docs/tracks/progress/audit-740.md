- 20, 21, 22, 23 (S. yejiei male AME, ALE, PME, PLE): ERROR, 4 faults each (faults 2 to 5 above). The preceding and following sentences are correct.
- 24 (H. sherwoodae AME): ERROR, 2 faults.

  - Trait eye_diameter_mm, value, unit, authors, DOI, licence and WSC state are correct.
