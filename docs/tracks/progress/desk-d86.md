## Advice
Thai and Iranian regional journals, Springer's 1970s Oecologia pages, cris.bgu.ac.il (Gutterman) and Revista de Biología Tropical print whole short abstract sentences and are the cheapest places to find quotable arid-zone results; Elsevier and MDPI pages usually give long sentences or no abstract.
