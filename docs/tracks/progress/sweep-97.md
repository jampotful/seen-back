# sweep-97 progress (Round 3 genus sweep, batch sweep-2, 40 genera)

Done 15 October 2026. Every genus has a search record in `docs/views/searched/sweep-97.jsonl` (59 lines; the plant genera have one line for light, day and eyes and one for chemical sensing). Each genus had at least two searches (scientific name, then common name or family, with trait words).

Evidence logged (6 records, all in `docs/views/evidence/sweep-97.jsonl`):
- Carex pensylvanica obligate short-day flowering (McGinnis et al. 2014, abstract page, m, partial) and Carex brevior dark germination (Kettenring et al. 2006, abstract page, m, partial). Both are congeners of Carex lurida.
- Panicum virgatum photoperiod and dormancy break (Guo et al. 2017, abstract page, m, partial).
- Thelypteris dentata spore germination needs continuous white light, reversed by far-red (Colli and Takak, as spelled on the page, 1992, no DOI on the page, m, partial).
- Cephalanthera, two unnamed green species, light sets the degree of myco-heterotrophy (Preiss et al. 2010, abstract page, g, partial).
- Ciconia ciconia narrow, vertically long binocular field (Martin and Shaw 2010, abstract page, g, partial).

Wanted lines (3): Martin and Shaw 2010, Flores et al. 2011 (cacti photoblastism), Hornych et al. 2021 (fern antheridiogen).

Nothing found for the other genera. Notable negatives: Tiliqua fovea is already measured in T. rugosa (no fovea), so only pupil shape and horizontal field remain open; Arge similis (Wen et al. 2022, open full text) gives eye type and facet counts only, with no interommatidial angle; Pogona, Lampropholis and Leiocephalus have no named-genus paper in the lizard reviews opened.

Limits: PMC pages returned a browser check most of the time, doi.org links were refused by the fetch tool, and several publisher pages (Cambridge Core) were blocked. Quotes from abstract pages come from the fetch tool's verbatim excerpts. An Animal Diversity Web page on the steppe lemming (crepuscular) was read and not logged because it is an encyclopedia page.
