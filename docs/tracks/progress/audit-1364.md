## Line 1, Posidonia oceanica, sense_thermal, shoot mortality

Quote check against the PDF Results "Shoot Mortality" text: "Temperature was the main factor that affected shoot mortality along the experiment (P < 0.05)." **Quote MATCH**, whole sentence, 14 words, starts at the sentence start. The preceding sentence gives "(P < 0.01, 3-way ANOVA; Figure 4 and Table 1)", and Statistical Analysis says the model had plants (EU, OL), temperature and nutrients as fixed factors, with EU and OL analysed individually by 2-way ANOVA when the plant factor was significant. So the quoted P < 0.05 is the pooled three-way result (Supplementary Table 2).

Faults (one record error, two faults):
(a) **Pooled-site record.** The value rests on a sentence that pools the Bacoli (EU) and Ischia (OL) plants. Table 1, Shoot mortality block, Temperature (T) row, read cell by cell on the HTML and the PDF: "Temperature (T) | 1 | 151.06 | 1.20 | 0.31 | 527.964 | 10.15 | 0.01" (OL MS, F, P then EU MS, F, P). The effect holds for EU (P = 0.01) and not OL (P = 0.31). The note disclosed the split, but under rules.md a pooled record is an error and disclosure does not cure it. Fixable: record the EU per-site result from this whole table row (9 words by check.py count), n = 3 aquaria per treatment for EU, with the OL null carried in the conditions. I chose this over restating the main-effect sentence because that restatement would still be a two-site value.
(b) **Omitted significant result.** Conditions say "relative growth rate showed no significant differences between plant groups or treatments". That repeats the text ("Statistical analysis revealed no significant differences between plants and treatments"), but Table 1 prints for RGR "N X T | 1 | 0.00 | 5.05 | 0.04 | 1.6E-06 | 1.76 | 0.20", which is a significant N x T interaction in OL plants. The record states a null that the paper's own table contradicts and omits the significant cell. Fixed in conditions.

Not counted: the conditions' claim that a supplementary caption describes T1 to T4 inconsistently could not be checked (supplement not opened) and is replaced in the Replacement by "the full T0 to T4 schedule is in Figure 1C, not read". The Methods/HTML give shoot mortality as change in shoot number between T1 and T4, and the Results put most mortality between T2 and T4 (Supplementary Figure 2). Mortality percentages (EU -41.6% T, -27.9% NT, -23% N; OL -15.7%, -15.6%, -7%) match the PDF. The OL sentence on the page reads "As for EU plants, T induced the highest mortality (−15.7%) ..." as printed.
