## Findings

Lines 1-4 (Icius alboterminus, JoTT 12(4): 15475-15480, DOI 10.11609/jott.5371.12.4.15475-15480).
- MOVED TO sensory_anatomy. The Materials and Methods, read whole, are seven sentences: 'Samples were hand collected and preserved in 70% ethyl alcohol.' 'The specimens were studied under a Dewinter Zoomstar-II stereomicroscope.' 'All measurements are in millimeters (mm).' then palp/leg segment order, drawings, SEM and depository sentences. None defines an eye diameter. The brief requires a printed Methods definition for eye_diameter_mm, so all four lines move to sensory_anatomy; the 'Eye diameter' label is kept in structure/measurement as printed wording.
- Eye sentence verified exactly on two copies: 'Eye diameter: ALE 0.17, AME 0.24, PLE 0.11, PME 0.06.' (10 words). Lines are in the printed eye order ALE, AME, PLE, PME; each value matches its label. Unit mm printed in Methods.
- Authors: the inline publisher copy, the article page and WSC print 'Prajapati'. The downloadable PDF's extracted text drops every 'ti' ligature ('Atributon', 'Creatve', 'tbia', 'Saltcidae', 'Prajapat'), so 'Prajapat' is not a printed form. Corrected to 'Prajapati DA, Kamboj RD'; collector 'D.A. Prajapati' likewise.
- Licence sentence on the inline copy: '© Prajapati & Kamboj 2020. Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.' plus the JoTT unrestricted-use sentence; article page: 'JoTT is registered under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY), ...'. Open licence holds.
- Specimens examined verified verbatim (four males: GJSP90243A 1, B 2, C 1; no type material). Description opens 'Male (Images 1A–H, 2A–H):' with no specimen code; the measured male is not stated. Heading path: Taxonomy, Icius Simon, 1876, Icius alboterminus (Caleb, 2014); no Class, Order or Family heading printed. The desk's longer heading list (Short Communication, Abstract, Keywords, Abbreviations...) was not verified and is dropped from conditions.
- Journal header printed '26 March 2020 | 12(4): 15475–15480'; article page Vol. 12 No. 4 (2020). Title verified verbatim. WSC current page: ACCEPTED, Salticidae, cites Prajapati & Kamboj 2020a: 15476 (m).

Lines 5-8 (Pellenes iva, JoTT 17(8): 27391-27395, DOI 10.11609/jott.9757.17.8.27391-27395).
- Eye sentence verified: 'Eye measurements: AME 0.27, ALE 0.13, AER 0.87, PME 0.03, PLE 0.13, PER 1.04.' (14 words by check.py). The sentence prints PER, not PE. Lines follow printed order AME, ALE, PME, PLE and log the eye values, never AER or PER.
- Methods read whole: 'All the measurements are in millimeters.' and an abbreviation list (ALE, AME, PLE, PME, RTA, CTA); no eye-size definition, so sensory_anatomy is right.
- Material examined verified verbatim: WON104450A two males, B one male, C one male; no type material. Description 'Male (Images 1–7)' names no code; Variation section says 'The studied specimen has the following variations when compared to the holotype male from Tamil Nadu' without saying which male. Measured individual not stated; sex male is printed, so nothing is dropped.
- Authors byline 'Subhash I. Parmar 1, Heena Prajapati 2 , Pranav J. Pandya 3 & Dhruv A. Prajapati 4' matches 'Parmar SI, Prajapati H, Pandya PJ, Prajapati DA'. Title, header '26 August 2025 | 17(8): 27391–27395', DOI and CC BY 4.0 licence (PDF and article page) verified.
