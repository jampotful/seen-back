## Limits of this audit

WebSearch returned result titles and links with no snippets, so I judged results by title and by the pages I opened. A result whose title does not name the genus, and whose page I did not read, could hold a value; the leads above are those cases that I think could change a verdict. The desk's screening rests on titles and abstract starts, and the species lists that sit in full texts and supplements (leads 1 to 7) are out of reach of both the desk's screening and mine. The fetch tool answers a prompt over a page with a small model, so a statement such as "no Chrysoteuchia in Table 1" is that model's search of the text I received, and a table the tool cut off would escape it. My "confirm" verdicts rest on the same kind of reading plus the pages I opened, with the limits stated in each line above.
