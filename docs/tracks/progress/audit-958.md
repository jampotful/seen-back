Totals: 8 lines judged. All 8 are fixable, 0 are dropped and 0 are held out. There are 0 counted errors and 32 note faults (4 per line, all in shared text).

These faults are shared by all eight lines:
1. **`method` field.** The Methods quote starts mid-sentence ('other relatively short measurements …') with no cut mark. The printed sentence opens 'Measurements (adults only, snout–vent length and tail length to the nearest 1 mm using a steel ruler; …'. The replacement gives it whole, with cuts marked.
2. **Heading path in `where` and `note`.** The printed path is Results > Taxonomy > 'Trimeresurus lii Cai, Liu, Liang, Wang & Ding, sp. nov.' > Diagnosis. The unit gave 'Taxonomy > Trimeresurus lii sp. nov.', dropping 'Results' and the printed authority. It also omitted that Table 2 is first cited under Materials and methods > Morphological comparison. No Class, Order or Family heading is printed, and the unit is correct on that point.
3. **`conditions` field.** The unit says 'Type series as printed', but its paratype list is regrouped by sex and so does not follow the printed order. It also omits the printed 'Other types (n = 4 …)' block of juveniles and a subadult. The replacement gives the printed order.
4. **Removed claims in `note`.** 'Crossref not checked' is removed under the brief. The unit also said the byline was unread and the authors were taken from the Reptile Database. I read the byline on the page (Bo Cai, Yubo Gou, Gang Wang, Fengjing Liu, Dong Liang, Xiaodong Gu, Haijun Gu, Haolong Fang, Yuzhou Liu, Qiujie Li, Li Ding), so the provenance now names the article. The author string itself was already correct.
