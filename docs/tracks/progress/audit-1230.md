Printed: "Measurements followed Fei et al. (2009) and Rowley et al. (2013), and were taken with a digital caliper to the nearest 0.1 mm." ## Reader-expanded label (ERROR, all four lines)

The publisher HTML inserts an expansion in parentheses after every abbreviation: "SYS (Museum of Biology, Sun Yat-sen University) a007743", the Table 4 header cells "SYS (Museum of Biology, Sun Yat-sen University) a 007743", and the ratio label "HDL (head length)/SVL (snout-vent length)". The page reader identified "(eye diameter)" after "EYE" in both the table row label and the Methods list as the same kind of inserted expansion. The printed row label is "EYE". The desk's quote "EYE (eye diameter) 2.9 3.2 3.2 3.1" and its method quote "EYE (eye diameter) eye diameter (...)" both carry the expansion as printed text. ERROR on lines 1 to 4, fixed to "EYE 2.9 3.2 3.2 3.1" (5 words).
