## Limits of this audit

WebSearch returned result titles and links with no snippets, so I judged results by title and by the pages I opened. A page whose title does not name the genus, and whose text I did not read, could hold a value. The leads above are those cases. The desk's screening of every record rests on titles and abstract starts (`harvest_get.py` prints the first 350 characters of each abstract), and the species lists that sit in full texts and supplements (leads 1 to 4) are out of reach of both the desk's screening and mine. The fetch tool answers a prompt over a page with a small model, so a statement such as "no moose in the text" is that model's search of the text I received, and a table the tool cut off would escape it. My closures from main's logs carry the limits of main's readers: for example, the Chan et al. rows leave out the supplement. My "confirm" verdicts rest on the same kind of reading plus the pages I opened, with the limits stated in each line above. A sample of 35 with 1 error still allows a true rate up to 15%. The 16 wanted lines without a page of mine are unverified and could carry a wrong year or DOI that main's registry fill has already corrected for 20 of the 26.
