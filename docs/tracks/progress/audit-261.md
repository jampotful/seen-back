## Check of the records

### Line 1, Calanus / behaviour_vision (negative polarotaxis, ratio 1.9)

Both sentences MATCH on nature.com. **ERROR, counted once, for two faults.**
(a) Scientific meaning, light-level confound (causal interpretation): the Methods give the trap intensities as "0.7 ± 0.4" and "1.2 ± 0.5 × 10–11 μE cm−2 nm−1 s−1 for the unpolarized vs. polarized traps respectively", while the authors also write that the design ensured "the light sources in the paired traps projected the same light intensity". On their own figures the polarized trap was the brighter one on average, and the authors do not discuss the difference (tool summary, second pass). The record gives a single intensity (10^-11) and presents the result as avoidance of polarized light. Fewer animals in the brighter trap could also be a response to intensity, so polarization and brightness are not separated. Whether the difference is significant is my own inference from the printed means and s.d.; the paper gives no test. The record also gives "partial polarization 68%" without saying the unpolarized trap was 15.4 ± 7.9% polarized, and "420 to 700 nm light" where 420 to 700 nm is the band the intensity was integrated over (spectra measured 400 to 700 nm).
(b) Quote form: the quote joins the Results ratio sentence and "Calanus spp. displayed negative polarotaxis.", which is the first sentence of the Discussion (followed there by "This finding is consistent with the laboratory-based observations of Manor et al."), as one string with nothing to mark the break. Each sentence is whole, and `where` names both places, but the string as quoted is not in the text. Two consecutive whole sentences are accepted (audits 244, 256); two sentences from different sections are not consecutive.
Also: the record's "(C. finmarchicus and C. helgolandicus, about 9:1)" reads as the sample's make-up. The paper says the two species occur in the area "typically at a ratio of 9:1" and the catch was not identified to species. Statistic: one-sample t-test on arcsine-transformed ratios, p = 0.010 (not in the record). Genus rank and grade g are right. The value says no more than the two sentences ("entered" for "attracted to" is harmless). Replacement below.
