# desk-d128 (30 October 2026): plant sense_touch in woody and crop species

Count on main before filing (grep of docs/views/evidence and docs/views/audit/corrected): plant sense_touch lines exist for Arabidopsis thaliana (Benikhlef 2013, Darwish 2022, Pantazopoulou 2023), Bryonia dioica, Cucumis sativus, Cucurbita pepo, Hordeum vulgare, Mimosa pudica, Phaseolus vulgaris, Pisum sativum, Ricinus communis, Triticum aestivum (all Jaffe 1973), Passiflora gracilis and Echinocystis lobata (Darwin 1906), plus Dionaea, Drosera, Aldrovanda. No sense_touch line for Brachypodium (only B. pinnatum, light), Oryza, Populus, Pinus, Eucalyptus, Acer or Cuscuta (their lines are other traits).

Filed: 3 evidence records in docs/views/evidence/desk-d128.jsonl, all Brachypodium distachyon, trait sense_touch, grade m, partial false, from Coomey et al. 2024, Quantitative Plant Biology 5: e5 (DOI 10.1017/qpb.2024.5, Crossref checked: 17 authors in printed order, CC BY 4.0). check.py: 0 problems. No search records, no wanted lines.

## Held out
- Oryza sativa, Zhao et al. 2018, Archives of Biological Sciences 70(1): 129-139 (rubbing 30, 60, 90 times/day for 35 days lowers height; CC BY 4.0 on the journal page): abstract only; the full-text PDF host (abs.serbiosoc.org.rs download and pdf_128) returned unanswered permission requests, so the cultivar or subspecies (pooling check) could not be read. Refile if the PDF can be read.
- Triticum aestivum, Hindhaugh et al. 2021, Annals of Botany 128(5): 589-603 (CC BY; one brushstroke a day suffices): cultivars JB Diego and Mulika, so cultivar-bound; species already on main from Jaffe.
- Passiflora caerulea, Klimm et al. 2023, Advanced Science 10(28): 2301496 (CC BY 4.0, read via Europe PMC XML): contact-coiling force of 6 to 140 mN in n = 4 tendrils, but it is a coiling force and water-status result rather than a touch-sensing measurement; held as borderline for the owner.
- Populus nigra bent stems, De Zio et al. 2020 Front Plant Sci 11: 590985: static 90 degree bending for 5 months, reaction wood and hormones; the page reader says the paper does not test touch; not a contact response. Hybrid aspen flexing (SLU/UPSC 2023) is Populus tremula x tremuloides, a hybrid.
- Pinus pinaster wind acclimation (Defossez et al. 2021, Forestry): guyed against swaying stand, wind loading not contact; no DOI or licence seen. Pinus jeffreyi wind bending (Forest Systems) not opened.
- Coutand et al. 2010 Annals of Botany 105(2): 341-347 (stem bending in Eperua falcata, E. grandiflora, Tachigali melinonii, Symphonia globulifera, Bauhinia guianensis): Oxford "All rights reserved", no open licence.
- Cuscuta: Tada et al. 1996 Plant Cell Physiol 37(8): 1049-1053 (C. japonica needs far-red plus tactile pressure) and Haidar et al. 1997 Weed Research 37(4): 219-228 (C. campestris, C. indecora): no open licence reached; Bernal-Galeano et al. 2022 (Plant Physiol, rights reserved) and Bawin et al. 2022 (CC BY, far-red induction, touch not measured); Shimizu and Aoki 2019 is a review.
- Hydrangea macrophylla 'Wudu' bending (Frontiers Plant Sci 2024): cultivar. Tomato Garner and Bjorkman 1996: cultivar, no licence. Pisum tendril and PMC-hosted older papers: PMC returned reCAPTCHA, not read.
