## Limits of this audit

WebSearch returned titles and links with no snippets, so I judged results by title and by the pages I opened. A page whose title does not name the genus, and whose text I could not read, could hold a value; the leads above are those cases. The fetch tool reads a page through a summarizer, so a statement that a paper "names no Eurystomus" rests on the text the summarizer received, which can be an excerpt (the TARA thesis is one). Pages behind bot checks and redirects to hosts that no search of mine returned stayed unread, and I looked for no other route to them. The statements about what main holds come from the gap queue, the canon file and both row stores on origin/main, searched by DOI, taxon, family and author string; a source cited under another spelling would escape that search.
