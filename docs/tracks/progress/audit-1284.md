# Audit 1284: desk-d625 lines 30 to 31 (Ansonia karen, VTD)

Auditor: Opus auditor 1284. Scope: lines 30 and 31 of docs/views/evidence/desk-d625.jsonl (file order) only; other auditors take lines 1 to 29.
Paper: Suwannapoom et al. 2021, Vertebrate Zoology 71: 763-779, 10.3897/vz.71.e73529. Reached through an own WebSearch (exact title) that returned the arphahub article_preview page and the XML download URL; ASW page opened from an own WebSearch result. WebSearches used: 3.

## What I read myself
- Table 1 on the publisher article_preview page: header in printed order SVL, HL, HW, SW, SL, DNE, IND, IOD, ED, UEW, VTD, TD, T-ED, FAL, HAL, 1FL, THL, TIL, FL, 1TL, IMTL, OMTL, 3FDW, HLL, FLL (VTD is the 11th value after the specimen cell). Rows ZMMU A-7615 and AUP-00665 sit in the females block; every cell of both rows matches the unit's quotes digit for digit (VTD 1.6 and 1.5).
- Materials and methods > Morphological data and analyses: the precision sentence ('... to the nearest 0.01 mm.') and the list lead-in match. The VTD and HTD list entries are both present, so vertical and horizontal are unambiguous; ED is defined as 'horizontal diameter of eye'. 'TD' alone appears nowhere in the text, only as an unexpanded column header in Tables 1 and 3, which confirms the unit's choice not to file the TD column.
- Table 1 caption: 'Raw mensural data from the type series of Ansonia karen sp. nov.' followed by 'Abbreviations are listed in the Materials and methods; an asterisk (*) marks the holotype specimen.'
- No Class, Order or Family heading is printed in the Taxonomy section (the unit says so correctly).
- ASW (Ansonia-karen page under Amphibia/Anura/Bufonidae): authority string, 'Vert. Zool., Senckenberg, 71: 767', 'Holotype: ZMMU A-7605,by original designation.'; the Comment records no split, synonymy or redelimitation. The name is valid and the type series stands.
- Repeats: git grep of 'Ansonia karen' and the DOI over docs/ on origin/main finds only the desk-d601 eye_diameter_mm row (ED, holotype) plus queue/progress mentions (desk-d603 also noted the undefined TD column). No tympanum row; not a repeat.

## Not read by me
- The licence sentence, the Holotype and Paratypes paragraphs, and the byline: after the table and Methods reads, every further fetch of the article_preview page and the XML URL returned 'Site under maintenance'. The unit's licence sentence (CC BY 4.0) and type-material text are therefore the unit's reading, not confirmed by this audit. I did not work around the maintenance page.

## Verdicts
- Line 30 (ZMMU A-7615, VTD 1.6, adult female): value, row, column, block and unit confirmed; 1 fault (wording, fixed below). Licence and paratype text unconfirmed by me.
- Line 31 (AUP-00665, VTD 1.5, adult female): value, row, column, block and unit confirmed; 1 fault (wording, fixed below). Licence and paratype text unconfirmed by me.

## Faults
Count: 2 (one per line, same fault). Scientific errors: 0.
- Lines 30 and 31: the method field puts the page reader's rendering 'vertical tympanum diameter (VTD (vertical tympanum diameter); vertical diameter of tympanum)' inside quote marks as printed text, and the where field calls the header 'printed' as 'VTD (vertical tympanum diameter)'. The inner expansion is the reader's (the TD header, which has no Methods definition, is shown bare; the ED and HTD entries show the same doubled pattern), so it is not printed. The d624 precedent marks such expansions as not quoted. Fixed: the method names it as the reader's rendering and states the printed definition; the where field says the header is printed 'VTD'. Also added to the note that TD appears only as an unexpanded header in Tables 1 and 3.
- Not faulted: the unit gives the Methods passage in the method field, as the d623/d624 shape does.
