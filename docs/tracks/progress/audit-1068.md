# Audit 1068: desk-d525, lines 3 to 6

Auditor: Opus auditor 1068, 3 Nov 2026. Range judged: lines 3 to 6 of docs/views/evidence/desk-d525.jsonl (file order). Nothing else judged.

## Brief mismatch (stated first)
The launch brief's desk flags describe a different unit (Odendaal, Jacobs & Bishop 2014, BMC Evol Biol 14: 60, Rhinolophus capensis RF, 9 records). The desk-d525 file in this worktree holds 10 records: Salsamendi et al. 2012 (lines 1-2) and Vu et al. 2025, Biodiversity Data Journal 13: e165516 (lines 3-10). Lines 3 to 6 are Vu 2025: Hipposideros griffini (peak frequency, duration) and Rhinolophus thomasi (peak frequency, duration). I audited the lines actually in the file; the Odendaal flags, the eye-diameter clauses and the Salsamendi clause do not apply to these lines. The desk should check which unit file was meant to be copied in.
