## Faults

1. Headings not printed by the page added to `where` and to conditions ("Class Arachnida, Order Araneae, Family Philodromidae") (lines 1-8). Fixed: where = "Taxon treatment, Philodromus uljin sp. nov., Description, Male (holotype)" / "... Female (paratype)".
2. Methods misreported: conditions say the only eye abbreviations are AER and PER; the page defines ALE, AME, PLE, PME and the interdistances too (lines 1-8). Fixed with the printed abbreviations and the second measurement sentence.
3. False discrepancy: page catalogue codes said to differ from WSC repositories; they agree (lines 1-8). Fixed; the four type records with no printed type field also stated.
4. Crossref claim in the note (lines 1-8). Removed.
