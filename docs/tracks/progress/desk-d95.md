# desk-d95 progress (geophytes and bulbs, new vein)

Started and finished 29 October 2026. Branch research/desk-d95. About 45 WebSearch and 45 WebFetch calls. 6 evidence records in docs/views/evidence/desk-d95.jsonl, 1 wanted line, no search records. Accepted names were confirmed on POWO or WFO pages opened through WebFetch (surfaced by WebSearch); no record rests on GBIF alone.

## Filed
- Tulipa iliensis (paper prints Tulipa thianschanica), sense_chemical. GA3 83.7% at every dose, 200 mg/L called negative in the Discussion, KT 82.7%, GA3 100 + KT 10 best, KNO3 not significant. Zhang W et al., Agronomy 10(11): 1765, 2020, 10.3390/agronomy10111765. WFO wfo-0000743013 page lists the synonym; accepted wfo-0000743576.
- Muscari comosum, sense_chemical. 3% KNO3 priming against unprimed (T50 37.31 against 44.85 d) but equal to hydro-priming and PEG; nothing germinated at 20 C. Castronuovo D, Cardone L, Candido V, Agronomy 14(1): 225, 2024, 10.3390/agronomy14010225. POWO 538476-1 accepted.
- Amorphophallus muelleri, sense_chemical. GA3 on corms: 2-year corms 11.1 to 63.9% against 8.3%, bulbils and 1-year corms little changed, abnormal inflorescences throughout. Santosa E et al., The Horticulture Journal 88(1): 92-99, 2019, 10.2503/hortj.UTD-016. POWO 84409-1 accepted.
- Spiranthes ochroleuca, sense_light_nonvisual. Seedling photoperiod on P723 (fresh weight 0.68 and 0.66 g lit against 0.35 g dark; etiolation). Zale PJ et al., Applications in Plant Sciences 10(5): e11494, 2022, 10.1002/aps3.11494. POWO 242761-2 accepted.
- Eranthis hyemalis, sense_chemical. GA3 brought germination about a month forward at 4 C in darkness; final 81 to 87% against 76%; nothing at 23 C. Tipirdamaz R, Gömürgen AN, Turkish Journal of Botany 24: 143-145, 2000. WFO wfo-0000670674 and POWO 711189-1 accepted.
- Lapiedra martinezii, sense_light_nonvisual (partial, abstract only). Light changed speed, not final percentage. Juan-Vicedo J et al., Seed Science and Technology 44(1): 199-206, 2016, 10.15258/sst.2016.44.1.02. POWO 65720-1 accepted.
