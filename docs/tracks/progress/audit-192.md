Faults noted, not counted:
- Line 1: `where` says the VUB page holds the abstract, but the same page offers the full PDF under CC BY-NC-ND; `access` "abstract" and partial true were avoidable. The abstract's "GA3−0, 50, 500, and 1000" conflicts with the Methods' 10, 50, 500 and 1000 plus a 0 control; a refile should follow the Methods and note the abstract's printing. The full text adds: no germination in darkness without GA3; white light lowest overall (3.70% against 8.33% red and 10.67% darkness, p = 0.1); photoperiod dropped in model selection; no germination under red light at 500 mg/L or white light at 10 and 500 mg/L; darkness with 1000 mg/L gave the shortest cotyledons (2.50 mm); Light x GA3 effects on cotyledon (p = 0.43) and leaf (p = 0.51) not significant. The full text calls 50 mg/L significant at p = 0.063; a refile should give the p value.
- Line 2: the value's "and sped it up" holds only for the Cox model at 3, 10 and 15 mg/L (2.7, 3.1 and 3.8 times); the paper also says controls had the fastest MGT (10.76 ± 3.53 days) and SL the slowest (15.23 ± 1.22), which the record does not carry beside its p = 0.40 null. The method gives 60 days; the Results say "After a 42-day observation period", and the record does not flag the conflict. S 2021 under smoke-water reached 4.7% (not significant), and seed generation did not affect the SL response (p = 0.61); neither is carried. The three p values come from a model across lots with generation as a predictor; the conditions' "in the S 2022 lot" fits the percentages, not the tests. The quote carries the GR24 result only; the smoke-water result has its own short sentence ("treatments with smoke-water had a significant inhibitory effect on seed germination", with statistics) and could be a separate record. The abstract's "below 5% across all tested dilutions" is not in the value.
- Lines 3 and 4: `journal` "Revista de Biologia Tropical" omits the accent of the journal's name (Revista de Biología Tropical); the page prints only the abbreviation, so not counted. No DOI exists on the PDF, and a Crossref title query found none; the URL stands in. The journal is peer-reviewed with a printed volume and issue, as the header shows.
