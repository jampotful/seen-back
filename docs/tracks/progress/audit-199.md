Faults noted, not counted:
- Line 1: the value's "germination in darkness was lower at every temperature" overstates 15/5 C, where 0.7 against 0.3% is below the LSD of 6.36. Left uncounted because the only exception is the temperature at which both treatments gave under 1% (the paper: "Both species showed very low germination (< 1%) at 15/5°C"), the value prints 0.7 against 0.3 beside the LSD in conditions, and every temperature with real germination (20/10 C up) shows a dark reduction above the LSD, so direction, magnitude and range are not misread. A merge should say "lower at 20/10 to 35/25 C; under 1% either way at 15/5 C". The conditions could add the paper's "No differences were observed between populations in either species", the reason the populations were pooled.
- Lines 1 and 2: the light/dark trial is the paper's only light treatment. Burial depth is not a light trial (A. viridis emerged best on the soil surface, 72%; A. retroflexus at 1 cm, 67% in Results against 87% in the Abstract); rightly not filed under light.
- Line 3: the same study's TIBA (98.1% to 29.8%) and TIBA + IAA (96.1%) results are hormone responses of the same plants and could be filed as their own records; the value also omits the 0.43 mM embryo (torpedo stage, 828.21 against 1070.35 um) and seed-shape results, which are development, not germination. The quote is a weak pick ("almost the same" leans on the previous sentence); the abstract's 98.0% to 16.5% sentence or the Results' 0.07 mM sentence would carry the headline. The paper gives no year-by-year germination results for 2016 and 2017.
- Line 4: the Discussion says "The low germination percentage in the absence of treatment observed in 2018 was lower than previously reported", so the 95 to 99% falls are relative to an unusually low control; the text gives no control percentage (which the record says). Left uncounted because no absolute value can be stated from the text and the relative reductions logged are the paper's own; a merge should add the sentence to conditions. The 2019 viability estimate ("20% in controls but 66% in GA3 treated ones") is not carried beside the 2018 series. The Abstract's "from 58% to 99%" pools the two years; the Methods name a t-test for 2019 while the Fig. 3 caption gives a binomial GLM chi-square test; per-dose significance for 2018 is not reported. The `where` says the page footer names no licence; the page fetched here links CC BY 4.0, and Crossref gives CC BY 4.0.
- Lines 3 and 4: WFO acceptance rests on the December 2022 snapshot (see Accepted names).
