## Check of the record (line 1, Scaphoideus titanus, sense_vibration)

Quote: both sentences MATCH as consecutive whole sentences of the abstract ("Partners established a vibrational duet up to 6 cm gap width between leaves." / "Ablation of the antennae showed that antennal mechanoreceptors are not essential in detection of mating signals."). 29 words, not mid-sentence.

Value against the quote. "no female response at 7 cm" is not in the quote; it is the Results sentence "At 7-cm distance between leaves, none of the females responded to MCS." It qualifies the same finding (it bounds the 6 cm range) and the note identifies and quotes the supporting passage, which is what the oa-read.md contextual-correction exception requires, so it is not counted. "maintained" is stronger than the abstract's "established"; uncounted wording fix below.

Design (three passes, tool summary unless quoted). Test 1: a live male and female on leaves of two separate grapevine cuttings, "The gap width between the upper and lower leaf surface ranged from 0.5 cm to 7 cm"; signals "recorded with a laser vibrometer"; "females were observed to reply to male calls up to a 6-cm gap distance"; "Within the 5-cm gap distance, most females replied to male calls"; mate location "were observed only at shorter distance" (the Discussion places duets ending in location at the two shortest gaps); call-fly behaviour "prevailed at longer distances"; no proportion is printed for 6 cm and n per gap is in Fig. 1 only. Test 2: no insects; a minishaker played a pre-recorded male call into the lower leaf at gaps of 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 11 cm and the mean substrate velocity of the upper leaf "progressively decreased with the distance" (the printed percentages came back garbled and are not used). Test 3: ten pairs, intact males, antennae-ablated females; on the same leaf "all females responded"; across a 5 cm gap "seven out of ten females responded". Dominant frequencies of the male call "in the range between 80–300 Hz".

Rearing. Eggs in two-year-old grapevine canes "collected from organic farms in Northern Italy (Povo, Trento, Italy)"; hatching in a climate chamber; nymphs on grapevine leaves over agar; tests on "virgin, sexually mature males and females at least 8 days old". No field-collected adults. The record's `conditions` ("laboratory-reared virgin adults ... eggs from grapevine canes collected on organic farms in northern Italy") is right. Laboratory cuttings only; the authors give no field gap distances, and they describe partly overlapping separated leaves as "probably" a more natural case. Uncounted: conditions could say "grapevine (Vitis vinifera) cuttings in the laboratory".

**Airborne sound versus leaf vibration (the owner question).** The paper's Discussion: "From our results it was not possible to determine explicitly whether the vibrational signals were detected as air-borne sound" or as substrate vibration, "However, some observations, indicate the latter as the more probable hypothesis" (fragments verbatim). The supporting observations (tool summary): some insects sat on the outer sides of the leaf blades, where the two leaves would block low-intensity airborne sound; earlier work shows vibrating leaves produce air movements with the substrate signal's temporal pattern; and the large, partly overlapping leaves of this set-up. Test 2 shows the call does induce measurable vibration of the receiving leaf. Ruling: this is a fair sense_vibration record, for three reasons. (1) The signal is produced and, on the authors' favoured reading, received as substrate (leaf) vibration; only the crossing of the gap is through air, which the value states ("across an air gap"). (2) The open owner question (audits 213 and 242) concerns a stimulus delivered as airborne sound to an organism that does not otherwise use substrate signalling; here the animal's own substrate-borne mating signal is the stimulus. (3) The detection modality is left open by the authors and the record says so in plain terms in `note` ("it was not possible to determine whether signals were detected as air-borne sound or as substrate vibration induced in the leaf; the authors favour substrate vibration"). Under oa-audit.md that limitation is "already unambiguously conveyed elsewhere in the record", so it is not counted. Because it bears on the trait key itself, I recommend moving it into `value` (text below), uncounted. If the owner later rules that a signal crossing air in any leg is sense_hearing, this record would move with that ruling; flagged, not counted.

**Counted (scientific meaning; `method` misstates the behavioural design).** `method` reads "behavioural playback and natural-signal tests". The only playback in the paper is Test 2, a minishaker driving the lower leaf with a pre-recorded call and "no real insects", which measures transmission between leaves, not a behavioural response. Every behavioural result the record logs (duet to 6 cm, none at 7 cm, 7 of 10 ablated females at 5 cm) comes from live males calling to live females, whose call amplitude was neither set nor reported per trial. "Behavioural playback" tells a reader the 6 cm range was obtained with a controlled stimulus level, which would make it read as a calibrated detection threshold; it is a natural-call range under laboratory conditions. That changes the applicability of the logged range (oa-audit.md). The method should also name the transmission test. Counted once. Fix below.

Omitted contrary or limiting results. (a) 7 cm null: carried in value and note. (b) 3 of 10 ablated females did not respond at 5 cm: carried ("seven of ten"). (c) Mate location occurred only at the shortest gaps and call-fly behaviour prevailed at longer gaps: not in the record. The record logs detection range, which these do not reduce, and it does not claim mate location across the gap, so this is harmless (uncounted); adding it to `note` is recommended. (d) No intact-female comparison at 5 cm is printed in the text (Fig. 1 not read), so the ablation claim rests on the authors' abstract wording, which the quote reproduces; harmless. Grade `m` (one named species measured) right; not partial (full text read).
