## Thermal ruling (lines 3 and 4)

Question: is a preferred body temperature (Tpref) chosen in a thermal gradient a measurement of a sense (sensing or discriminating temperature) or of thermoregulatory preference?

Strictly, it is the second. A gradient choice presupposes that the animal senses temperature, but the number logged is the regulated target of a thermoregulatory controller. It moves with internal state (here, feeding status shifts it by about 5 C; in main's Callosobruchus rows, sex and water balance), and it says nothing about the threshold, resolution or range of the thermosensors. A sensory measurement would be a detection or activation threshold, a discrimination step, a tuning curve or a receptor's firing (main's Rhodnius TRPA5B, Camponotus ST-neuron 1.6 C resolution, Agriotes neurons, Drosophila cooling-rate bound).

Main's convention nevertheless includes whole-animal gradient choice under `sense_thermal`. Rows read (git grep origin/main docs/views/evidence for "sense_thermal", 87 rows): Tropilaelaps mercedesae (desk-d196:1, gathered at the 32 to 40 C end of a 15 to 40 C gradient), Callosobruchus maculatus (desk-d198:2 and 3, sex difference 29.8 against 28.4 C and a mating-status null, thermal-gradient choice), Folsomia candida, F. similis and Proisotoma minuta (cooler-side choice, 10.1673/031.007.2201), Aedes aegypti and Culex heat-seeking (desk-d144, desk-d219), Anopheles coluzzii heated-target landing, Xenopus laevis cold-induced melanosome response, alongside the receptor and neuron rows above. Audit 280 ruled "a thermal gradient choice by whole animals is a behavioural preference, and sense_thermal ('other senses, string with figures') takes it; it is not reported as a detection threshold, and the record does not call it one", and audit 282 applied it to Callosobruchus. BRIEF.md line 100 defines `sense_thermal` only as "other senses", "string with figures". No vertebrate Tpref row is on main under `sense_thermal`; the one vertebrate Tpref row (herps:76, Sceloporus magister, parietal-eye removal) is filed under `sense_light_nonvisual` because the manipulation is a light organ.

RULING: main's convention includes preference trials, so lines 3 and 4 are audited in full, not held out. Condition of filing (as audit 280): the record must say it is a behavioural thermoregulatory preference and not a sensory threshold. Both records do ("preferred temperature is a behavioural choice, not a receptor threshold"). Owner question carried forward: lizard Tpref is a very large literature (thousands of species-level values); if the owner wants `sense_thermal` to hold thermosensation proper, a separate preference key or a rule restricting gradient trials would be needed before the desk mines it.

Amalosia value ("depended on feeding status (significant effect)"): as filed it is a qualitative direction, not a measurement, and BRIEF asks for a "string with figures". Main has accepted figure-free values only for nulls or where the magnitude sits in `conditions` (audit 282, Callosobruchus line 2). Here the paper prints the measurement in one 34-word Results sentence, so the desk's reason (every numeric sentence exceeds 125 characters) is the retired character cap, not a lack of a quotable value.
