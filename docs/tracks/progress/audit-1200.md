Scope: lines 7 to 12 only (file order). Pages opened, all from my own WebSearch results: the publisher article_preview page and the publisher XML (article/123251/download/xml/), the two ASW species pages (Amnihyla kikori, Amnihyla grinpela), and the author's institutional-repository PDF (Griffith), used only to confirm typeset characters where the page reader had altered them, never as the source copy. ## Faults
1. Quote integrity (all 6 lines). The page reader rendered the `abbrev` xlink:title attributes as text, so the quotes contain words the paper does not print: 'EYE (horizontal diameter of eye) 4.4 ...' and 'TYM (horizontal diameter of tympanum including tympanic annulus) ...'. The XML label cell holds only 'EYE'/'TYM', and the typeset PDF prints 'EYE 3.3', 'SVL 27.5;'.
2. Quote start and qualifier (lines 11, 12). The quote starts mid-sentence at 'SVL'. It drops the sentence opening 'These data were determined for the holotype (all measurement in mm):', which carries the holotype and unit qualifiers.
3. Method field (all 6). The Methods list entries are misquoted with the inserted expansion; printed is 'EYE – horizontal diameter of eye;'. The claim that 'the paper does not say which of the two instruments took which measure' is false: EYE and TYM are both in the dissecting-microscope (ocular micrometer) list, and the callipers list holds SVL, TL, HL and HW only.
4. Conditions (lines 7 to 10). Table 1 is described as a 'two-line header' with a 'line 2' starting PNGNM. It is a single 15-cell header row in which PNGNM is the ninth cell, a specimen column. The mapping is right but the description is wrong. Lines 11 and 12 conditions repeat the inserted expansions and omit the paratype R72340.

Counts: 6 lines judged and 6 with errors, all fixable. Faults: quote 6, quote start/qualifier 2, method 6, conditions 6, so 20 faults. None dropped, none held out.

## Verdicts
- Line 7 (kikori EYE 4.4, holotype): error, fixed. The value and mapping are correct; the quote and method are not.
- Line 8 (kikori EYE 5.6, female paratype R72337): error, fixed. The identity is printed in the header and is the only F, so the line is kept.
- Line 9 (kikori TYM 1.4, holotype): error, fixed.
- Line 10 (kikori TYM 2.0, R72337): error, fixed.
- Line 11 (grinpela EYE 3.3, holotype): error, fixed. The quote is now the whole sentence.
- Line 12 (grinpela TYM 1.3, holotype): error, fixed.

Note on the grinpela quote: the publisher page reader returned the entries with expansions and cut after 1.3. The bare entries and the colon after '(all measurement in mm)' are confirmed from the typeset PDF text; the values are identical on the publisher copy.
