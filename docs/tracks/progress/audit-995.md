## Repeat question (desk flag): lines 3, 4 and 5 are NOT repeats of main

Main's only row for this species and DOI anywhere under docs/ (grep by species and DOI) is desk-d343 line 5, the holotype VNUF R.2021.01, 1.8 mm. Its conditions text prints, exactly: "Value filed is the holotype, 1.8 mm; seven type specimens, EyeD 1.8-2.5 mm." That is a range over seven specimens; it attributes no value to any paratype. Line 3 (VNUF R.2023.01, 1.8), line 4 (VNUF R.2023.02, 1.8) and line 5 (IEBR R.5152, 2.5) add specimen identity and sex that the conditions text does not carry; the 1.8 low end is already attained by the filed holotype. Unlike desk-d463 (conditions printing specimen-attributed values such as Zoica dulong male AME 0.05), nothing here is a per-specimen finding. Not counted as repeats; kept. Owner question (not counted): main's quote reproduces the whole EyeD row and its conditions list the column order, so all six values are mechanically recoverable from that main row; if the owner reads quote-plus-column-list as "carried", all six lines (not only 3 to 5) would be repeats.
