## Line 1, Myotis kalkoae, holotype ear length 11.9 mm

The sentence was returned in 11 adjoining pieces under the Diagnosis heading; it opens "The measurements of the holotype (in mm) were as follows: body length – 35, tail length – 28, length of the free tail tip – 0.5, ear length – 11.9, tragus length – 5.5, tibia length – 12.4, ..." and runs through skull values to "MCM3 (crown length of the maxillary tooth row.) – 4.9." The filed quote is verbatim from the sentence start to "tragus length – 5.5," with the cut marked; no heading joined; what is dropped are other measurements, not a qualifier. 35 words. Value 11.9 says what the quote says. Methods: "External measurements were taken to the nearest 0.1 mm using a dial caliper"; E and Tr are named without landmarks, as the record says. Tables 1-3 hold samples, PCA loadings and distances; no table gives ear or tragus for the species, so "no external-measurement table" is right. No paratype or female ear or tragus values are printed for the species; the only ear range nearby is M. sowerbyi males (11.0-12.66, n = 7) in the Discussion, another species. Verdict confirm. Uncounted: licence check, author initial, fixation sentence ("Three specimens of the Myotis kalkoae sp. nov. ... fixed in 75% ethanol") added.

## Line 2, Myotis kalkoae, holotype tragus length 5.5 mm

Same sentence and quote as line 1. Ear (pinna) and tragus are separate structures with separate printed values, so two records from one sentence are two measurements, not a double count (same practice as audit 460's two Bagarius records from one row). Verdict confirm. Uncounted changes as line 1.

## Line 3, Murina milinensis, holotype EL 12.74 mm

Page: heading "Measurements (in mm) and body weight (in g) of the holotype." then the line "HB: 34.68, EL: 12.74, EW: 7.89, TRL: 7.81, TRW: 5.35, HFL: 5.31, FL: 30.02, TIB: 12.75, ..." ending "CPH: 3.20; BW: 3.36." The quote starts at the line start, does not include the heading, ends at "FL: 30.02, […]" with the cut marked; dropped are other measurements. 15 words. EL definition (desk flag): this paper's Methods, re-read in the publisher JATS, define "ear length (from lower edge of external auditory meatus to tip of pinna)"; it is measured from neither the notch nor the crown, and the record's `measurement` field states the paper's definition correctly. Instrument "recorded to the nearest 0.1 mm using digital calipers"; "All specimens used for morphological studies were fixed in 95% ethanol". Type-series values are not in the quoted line: the Description gives "EL 12.48–14.58 mm" and Table 4 gives males 12.48–14.58 (13.46 ± 0.82, n = 6), females 13.12–13.95 (13.55 ± 0.34, n = 4). The holotype value lies inside the male range at its low end; it is a single-specimen record labelled as holotype, so the omission does not change direction or scale, and it is not in the same sentence. Uncounted; the replacement adds the ranges and removes "not re-read". Verdict confirm.

## Line 4, Soriculus dexingensis, holotype EL 7 mm

Page: "Measurements of holotype (mm)." then "W = 12.1 g, HB = 80, TL = 59, HL = 15, EL = 7, CIL = 23.54, PIL = 11.01, ..." ending "ML = 14.96, LTR = 9.39." The quote starts at the paragraph's first measurement (the heading is a separate line and is not joined), cut marked, 20 words. Precision (desk flag): the page prints "EL = 7", a whole millimetre, as are HB, TL and HL; Table 1 (new species under "Soriculus sp.", N = 11) prints EL "7 ± 0" with range "7", so the whole series reads 7 mm at that precision. The value 7 says no more than the quote. Methods: "The external measurements of these specimens were taken in the field, including weight (W), head-body length (HBL), tail length (TL), hind foot length (HL), and ear length (EL)."; the 0.01 mm digital caliper sentence concerns craniodental metrics. The record's `method` says the Methods give no procedure; they do say fresh field measurement. That incompleteness does not change the value's scale or interpretation (fresh-specimen pinna measurement to the millimetre is the shrew convention and the value is right), so uncounted; the replacement states it and adds the Table 1 series. Verdict confirm.
