# sweep-89 (Round 3 genus sweep, batch sweep-2)

Date: 15 October 2026. 40 genera searched at genus level (two queries each, first titles read, a few candidate pages opened).

Result: no measurement could be opened for any open cell, so no evidence records were added and no wanted lines were made. Every genus has a search record in `docs/views/searched/sweep-89.jsonl`.

Pages opened and found to name none of the batch genera: the Gliridae retina study (Peichl and colleagues, Lynx 2022), the lab-rat retina review (10.3389/fnana.2022.994890), the nocturnal-fovea paper (10.3389/fnint.2014.00061), a mole-rat light-perception thesis, a Virginia opossum cone paper, the amphibian pupil-shape preprint (10.1101/2021.08.15.456426) and a bush dog skull thesis.

Caveats: the sources of the nearest records for Poelagus, Speothos and Podomys (and the frog motion record) were not opened, so those records carry a `chased_note`; a later sweep with library access could check their citing literature. Several general search results were blocked or returned only encyclopedia pages, which are not valid sources and were not logged.
