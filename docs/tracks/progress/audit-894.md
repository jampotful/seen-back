## TRAIT: MOVED TO sensory_anatomy (read this)

**The Material and methods print NO eye-diameter definition.** The only eye sentences in the Methods are, as printed: "All measurements are given in millimetres." and "They were taken with an accuracy of one tenths of a millimetre, with the exception of eye and labium measurements taken with an accuracy of one hundredth of a millimetre." The Morphology abbreviations list only names the eyes (ALE, AME, PLE, PME). There is no sentence of the kind "Eye diameters as AME, ALE, PME, PLE ...". The quoted Description sentences print the label **'Eyes diameter'** (plural 'Eyes', singular 'diameter'), not 'Eye diameters'. Under the launch brief (no Methods definition -> sensory_anatomy; precedent keeps eye_diameter_mm only for a printed 'Eye diameters' label), all eight lines move to **sensory_anatomy**. One trait reading per paper: lines 1-8 and 17-32 of the same paper should follow (their auditors to confirm their labels). The desk's standing label ruling (desk-d446/d447: a label naming diameters keeps eye_diameter_mm) would keep them; that remains the carried OWNER QUESTION, and this audit follows its launch brief.

Unit: mm, printed ("All measurements are given in millimetres.").
