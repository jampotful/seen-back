Line 3: `acuity_cpd` with `method` "anatomical: ray tracing..." is the form BRIEF's trait table asks for ("method says anatomical (which measure) or behavioural (which test)"). Main grades author-computed anatomical acuities of a named species `m` (893 acuity_cpd lines on evidence and corrected with anatomical, optical or calculated methods are `m`, for example Bembix palmata "anatomical: Nyquist limit from interommatidial angle" and Lyrurus tetrix "anatomical: estimated from eye size"); `i` is used where the compiler or a review did the arithmetic or assumed a cell class (Ictidomys "sheet's arithmetic", Vulpes "beta ganglion cells assumed").
