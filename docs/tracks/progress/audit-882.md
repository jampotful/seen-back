# Audit 882: desk-d447 lines 36-42 (Cuiambuca borborema, C. vacabrava; EJT 856: 152-169)

Auditor split: 882 judges lines 36-42 of docs/views/evidence/desk-d447.jsonl (file order): 36-37 C. borborema female holotype PME, PLE; 38-41 C. vacabrava male holotype AME, ALE, PME, PLE; 42 C. vacabrava female paratype IBSP 43277 AME. These match the launch line map. I opened pages with WebFetch only at URLs from my own WebSearch results: europeanjournaloftaxonomy.eu/index.php/ejt/article/view/2035, europeanjournaloftaxonomy.eu/index.php/ejt/article/view/2035/8405, sciencepress.mnhn.fr/en/periodiques/european-journal-taxonomy/856/152, wsc.nmbe.ch/species/61137, wsc.nmbe.ch/species/61135, zenodo.org/records/7625575, 7625571 and 7625573, and Plazi treatment pages for vacabrava and borborema. I used 11 WebSearches. Crossref was not read.

## HELD OUT: all 7 lines (publisher full text not reached; printed eye order not read)
None of my search results gave a URL for the publisher PDF (download/2035/8407 or view/2035/8407). The article page lists that PDF, but its URL never appeared in my results, so under the access rule I did not open it. The galley view/2035/8405 did appear in my results, but two fetches returned only the ~100-word abstract. Sciencepress.mnhn.fr also shows only the abstract. The Plazi treatment pages return only a redirect placeholder. Zenodo 7625571 (the article's source.pdf) shows metadata and gives no file URL, and Zenodo 7625575 is Fig. 1. The Frankfurt repository copy (publikationen.ub.uni-frankfurt.de) returned an Anubis bot-protection denial, and I did not try to get around it.

As a result I read neither the Methods sentence that fixes the order of the listed eye values nor either eye sentence. I cannot quote the Methods sentence. The unit itself says it saw that sentence only as a fragment ('Leg measurements are listed as: total length (femur, patella, tibia, metatarsus, tarsus); eye diameters') plus the fetch tool's paraphrase of the order. The brief says a value assigned to an eye by position, with no printed order quoted, is an error. On that basis all 7 lines are named for dropping. The trait reading (eye_diameter_mm, from the label 'Eye diameters') is kept. It was not moved, because I could not read the whole Methods. The unit's note that the measurement unit is printed ('All measurements are in millimeters.') could not be re-read either.

There is one piece of supporting evidence, and it is not confirmation. The Cuiambuca genus treatment deposited on Zenodo (7625573, from my search results, no licence shown) prints 'AME larger than ALE' and 'PME smaller than PLE'. The vacabrava male values (0.24, 0.19, 0.15, 0.18) and the borborema female values (0.22, 0.20, 0.15, 0.20) are consistent with this. It does not print the order in which the values are listed. The confirmed Uaica order (desk-d378, EJT 989) comes from a different paper and settles nothing here.
