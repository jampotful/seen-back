## Faults
- a (counted, all 8): provenance misstated. The method gives 'free-flying recordings in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama' plus released bats. The paper prints 'Recordings of molossid echolocation calls were obtained in Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Bolivia, and Brazil' over ten years, releases in Costa Rica and Panama, and Venezuelan high-flying reference individuals 'collected with a shotgun'. The method drops two countries and one recording mode and does not say that per-species sites are not printed.
- b (counted, all 8): sample composition omitted. Conditions say 'The paper states neither the number of individuals ...'; the Figure 3 caption prints 'Symbols represent sequences (individuals) per species.', equating the 17 sequences with individuals. Also omitted: the analyses used 'mean values per call type within recordings' to correct for pseudo-replication.
- c (counted, all 8): false scope claim. Conditions say 'other call types of the species are printed in neighbouring rows and not all are filed here'. All three of Table 1's M. molossus call types are filed.
- d (format, all 8): licence sentence cut after 'in any medium' with no cut mark and without 'Copyright: 2014 Jung et al.'; now given whole.
- e (format, all 8): quote keeps the text-layer '6' for ±; decoded in the replacement and checked digit for digit (34 words by check.py count).
- f (format, all 8): source.url names the PMC article page, which the unit did not read (its note and progress file say the rcastoragev2 PDF); the url now names the copy read. The note's 'Low/Middle/High ... are defined in that footnote' overstates it: the footnote defines call type, not the labels.
- g (format, all 8): MDD taxonomy note 'previously included M. tropidorhynchus (formerly under the name milleri) and M. verrilli' omitted; added, with the remark that the paper does not say which concept it used. Also added: the Discussion places M. molossus among '12 of the 18 molossids' calling 'within a narrow frequency range of 20–35 kHz' (Figure S2: peak frequencies) although the Middle and High rows print EF 36.8 and 39.8 kHz.
