## Checks
- Citation: XML front matter prints authors Wei Xiao, Xinbin Chen, Xiangjian Wu, Yuanqiang Pan, Ju Chen, Guohua Yu, Huayuan Huang, so "Xiao W, Chen X, Wu X, Pan Y, Chen J, Yu G, Huang H" is correct. Title is verbatim. Volume 102, issue 2, pages 671-686, year 2026, DOI printed as https://doi.org/10.3897/zse.102.184792. The journal string is correct.
- Methods, whole "Morphometric measurements" subsection read. It has seven sentences: caliper, terminology, character list, PCA, scatter plots, t-tests and Levene's test. The diameter is named: "Seventeen morphological characters were measured: ... snout length (SL); eye diameter (ED); tail length (TAL); ...". The precision sentence is "Morphometric data were obtained using a digital caliper to the nearest 0.1 mm." So eye_diameter_mm stays and the unit is mm. Table 2 also prints the unit: "Measurements (mm) of ...".
- Table 2: the header row reads "ID | Voucher no. | Sex | TOL | SVL | HDL | HDW | SL | ED | TAL | ...", so ED is column 9. Rows 1 to 5 come back cell by cell exactly as the five quotes give them. ED by row: GXNUYU000981 M 2.9, GXNUYU000982 M 2.9, GXNUYU000983 M 3.1, GXNUYU001042 M 3.0, GXNUYU001043 M 2.7. The table has no footnote. The caption has two sentences, and the unit quoted only the first.
- Holotype cross-check: "Description of holotype." prints "Total length (TOL) 131.0 mm, snout-vent length (SVL) 67.8 mm, tail length (TAL) 72.2 mm" and "eyes relatively large (3.0 mm)". Both agree with row 4.
- Type material as printed: the Holotype block reads "GXNU YU001042, adult male ..." and the Paratypes block reads "Three adult males (GXNU YU000981, GXNU YU000982, and GXNU YU000983) and one adult female (GXNU YU000984) collected ... on 10 May 2025 ...; and one adult male (GXNU YU001043) collected on 16 July 2025 ...". The codes, sexes and counts in the lines are correct. Each measured specimen is named by the voucher code printed in its own row, so nothing is inferred. The paper contradicts itself: Sampling prints "On 11 May and 16 July 2025", while Paratypes prints 10 May. This is now noted in the lines.
- Tables 3 and 4 give male mean ± SD of size-adjusted data (ED 0.025 ± 0.0020). They are correctly not filed.
- Heading path: Materials and methods > Morphometric measurements; Results > Phylogenetic relationship, Morphological study, Taxonomic account > "Tylototriton guilinensis sp. nov." > "Holotype." / "Paratypes.". Taxonomic account is a subsection of Results, and Table 2 is cited in Morphological study. No Class, Order or Family heading is printed. The unit's `where` left out that Taxonomic account sits under Results.
- Licence: the printed sentence is "This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0), which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the original author and source are credited." The publisher's own free full text is CC BY 4.0, so access "open" stands. The unit closed its quote after "(CC BY 4.0)," with no cut mark.
- Validity (ASW): the breadcrumb reads "Class: Amphibia > Order: Caudata > Family: Salamandridae" and then "Subfamily: Pleurodelinae > Genus: Tylototriton > Species: Tylototriton guilinensis". The heading is "Tylototriton guilinensis Xiao, Chen, Wu, Pan, Chen, Yu, and Huang, 2026", and the original citation is "Zoosyst. Evol., 102: 678. Holotype: GXNU YU001042, by original designation." The page lists no synonymy, gives the species its own account and prints no explicit "valid". The name is valid on the brief's authority, and family Salamandridae is correct.
- Repeats of main: git grep of docs/ on origin/main (a4330991d) for "Tylototriton guilinensis", "zse.102.184792" and "184792" finds nothing, and no Tylototriton evidence is on main. These lines repeat nothing.
- The unit field reads desk-d459 on all five lines. Opened is true, grade is m, and each quote has 20 words.
