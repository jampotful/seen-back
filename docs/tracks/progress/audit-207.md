Pooled-species question (lines 2 and 3). I found no rule on main that makes a pooled two-species record an error in itself. The rule on main is that a value the paper gives for species pooled is a group value and is held to grade g (audit 8, as applied in sweep-79:1, retracted because it was graded m; audits 12 and 13 "Pooled or unnamed-species records are `g`"); pooled two-species sentences filed at g have been accepted (audit 16, sweep-101:2; audit 139 line 7; audit 144 line 2, the desk-d61 Cyathea delgadii record). Line 2's sentence ("The anthesis in the two species lasts an average 12 h ...") pools H. undatus and H. polyrhizus; the record is graded g and its value says so in words ("stated for H. undatus and H. polyrhizus together, not for H. undatus alone"), so it complies. Line 3's sentence names both species but does not pool them: it separates them (H. polyrhizus begins around 20:00 h, one hour before H. undatus), so the H. undatus value is that species' own observation and m is right. The desk holding out pooled sentences elsewhere (its Oenothera five-species sentence) is a stricter practice than the rule, not a rule.
