For every line I could not read the following myself: the whole licence sentence, the printed byline, the Methods eye and tympanum items verbatim, the type-material paragraphs, the heading paths, the Table 1 cells, and the volume, issue and page strings. Earlier audits read the table cells and their mapping before the outage: audit 1280 or 1281 for line 9, audit 1283 for lines 10 to 16, all 25 cells of each row. Those audits found no value errors. I could not quote an eye-size passage verbatim from the paper. As read by audits 1283 and 1284 in the page reader's rendering, the Methods print an eye diameter, 'eye diameter (ED (eye diameter), horizontal diameter of eye)', for A. karen. As read by audit 1280, the Methods print 'ED (horizontal eye diameter)' for I. chrysolophus. In both papers, therefore, an eye diameter is defined, the tympanum lines are not eye measurements, and sensory_anatomy is the right trait. The unit is mm in both papers: the precision sentence '... to the nearest 0.01 mm.' and the caption '(in mm)'.
