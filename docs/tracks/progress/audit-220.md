Trait question (the owner's sense_chemical reading, which I apply and do not override). The experiment is in vitro: spores sown on Murashige and Skoog agar into which GA4-Me, ABA or IAA had been pipetted (dissolved in MeOH, solvent air-dried), grown in darkness after a 24 h red-light pulse. What is measured is a living gametophyte's developmental response (protonema elongation, cell-division pattern, antheridium formation) to a plant hormone. The culture-medium exclusion, as I read the brief's list (extracts, oils, processed tissue, cut material, pollutant injury, nutrient-ion uptake) and its one use in the progress files (desk-d109, Senecio cineraria "cultivar, culture medium"), targets medium composition as the variable (salts, sugars, nutrient uptake) or tissue that is no longer a whole living organism; here the medium is a constant basal carrier and the variable is the hormone, as in the GR24 agar assays and GA-germination lines already on main (desk-d63, quote-limit-recovery lines 1 and 2) and in the Hornych cultivation assay for this very species. GA4-Me is the paper's antheridiogen stand-in ("GA4-Me induces single or multiple antheridia on it"), which is squarely the antheridiogen response the reading admits. ABA and IAA are hormone responses of a living plant, admitted by the reading, but they are applied at pharmacological doses (10 or 100 µM, below) on top of GA4-Me, not as signals the fern meets from neighbours; the records say this in conditions, which is what applicability requires. I judge all three in scope; if the owner reads "culture-medium clauses" to cover any agar assay, all three fall, and so would the Hornych and GR24 lines on main.
