## Advice
Springer pages (Plant Growth Regulation, Discover Plants, Oecologia) and repository copies print whole short abstract sentences; Crossref search by ISSN lists a journal's germination papers fast.

## Desk disposition after audit 178
Audit 178: 3 of 6 errors, all dropped (whole unit covered): lines 1 and 2 Laggera alata and Laggera crispata light (the paper reports a 1.53-day delay in germination initiation under red light, and the records stated the reverse or omitted it), line 3 Laggera alata smoke and KNO3 (omitted that KNO3 alone had no significant effect, the smoke x KNO3 interaction at p = 0.013, smoke alone affecting only days to germination, and the smoke x red-light interaction). Refile the Laggera records only with those results carried. Impatiens walleriana, Mitragyna rubrostipulata and Nauclea latifolia merged. Open item for the owner: the Uganda repository item for the Mitragyna and Nauclea paper lists a full-text PDF that states no licence and was not opened; if the owner rules the deposit authorised, refile those two from the full text.
