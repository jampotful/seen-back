## THE TRAIT RULING (loud)

**BOTH LINES MUST MOVE FROM eye_diameter_mm TO sensory_anatomy.** I read the whole Materials and methods of each paper on the publisher page.
- Bolitoglossa muisca (ZooKeys 1158): the Methods print the caliper sentence and then list eye diameter (EYD) among the "additional measurements only for the description of the holotype:". There is no definition of the dimension: no axis, no landmarks, nothing beyond the label.
- Pristimantis donnelsoni (ZooKeys 1180): the Methods print "Morphometric measures were taken with an electronic caliper (precision ± 0.01 mm and approximated to 0.1 mm), following the descriptions by Duellman and Lehr (2009)" and then a list that includes ED. The ED entry gives a label and a citation and no printed definition. Only EN is given extra wording.
eye_diameter_mm is filed only when the Methods print the ED definition (desk-d602 angle, desk-d601 Phalotris shawnella precedent, desk-d598 Ichthyophis). A label alone or a citation does not meet that test. The label "eye diameter" does name the dimension, so these values belong under sensory_anatomy with a note (the d601 treatment). They should not be dropped. Unit mm is printed in both cases: in the quoted clause for line 1, and as the run-in "(in mm)." for line 2.
