## Advice
The WebFetch summariser prints a sentence whole only when it is 125 characters or fewer, which makes it a usable length test; ask for a numbered list of abstract sentences. Shade and photoperiod papers in Revista Ceres, Revista Arvore and Pesquisa Agropecuaria Brasileira gave whole short sentences more often than germination abstracts did.
