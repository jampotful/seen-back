## Advice
PeerJ, Frontiers, Plants and Plant Diversity print whole short results sentences and their full texts open on PubMed Central or the publisher; the Hengduan Mountains seed-dormancy series (Peng, Yang, Li, Sun and co-authors) is the richest source in this vein, but several of its species are not accepted at species rank in POWO. Search hits for Journal of Arid Land, Grassland Research, Nordic J Bot and Basic and Applied Dryland Research returned no usable single-species trial.
