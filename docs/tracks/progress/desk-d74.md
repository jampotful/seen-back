## Filed (5 records, docs/views/evidence/desk-d74.jsonl)
- Thlaspi arvense, sense_chemical and sense_light_nonvisual (two records). Saini HS, Bassi PK, Goudey JS, Spencer MS, "Breakage of Seed Dormancy of Field Pennycress (Thlaspi arvense) by Growth Regulators, Nitrate, and Environmental Factors", Weed Science 35(6): 802-806, 1987, DOI 10.1017/S0043174500079376. Authority POWO 290477-1 opened, accepted. Chemical quote: ethephon plus GA3 gave 100% germination (GA4+7 result carried in value). Light quote: KNO3 had no effect in the dark (KNO3 with light and a 12 to 22 C shift gave 100%).
- Avena fatua, sense_chemical. Saini HS, Bassi PK, Spencer MS, "Interactions Among Ethephon, Nitrate, and After-Ripening in the Release of Dormancy of Wild Oat (Avena fatua) Seed", Weed Science 34(1): 43-47, 1986, DOI 10.1017/S0043174500026424. POWO 391443-1 opened, accepted. Quote: ethephon promoted germination but stunted seedling growth; the additive ethephon plus KNO3 result is in value (the sentence naming it begins "Both chemicals", so it is not the quote).
- Centaurium erythraea, sense_light_nonvisual and sense_chemical (two records). Mijajlovic N, Grubisic D, Giba Z, Konjevic R, "The effect of plant growth regulators on centaury (Centaurium erythraea Rafn) seeds germination", Archives of Biological Sciences 57(1): 25-28, 2005, DOI 10.2298/ABS0501025M. POWO 326619-2 opened, accepted. Red light gave more than 80% germination and darkness none; GA3 and GA7 replaced light, ABA and growth retardants inhibited (carried in value). Older search records on main for Centaurium (diel, eye_type, and one listing sense_chemical and sense_light_nonvisual as found none) predate this paper, which is not in the repository; the desk may want to reopen that last null.
