- Substantive errors (value, quote, specimen, sex, trait, licence, taxon, bibliography, repeat): 0. Nothing is dropped and nothing is held out.
- Tidied, not counted:
  - `conditions` printed two Methods sentences and the licence sentence as fetch-tool pieces. The piece breaks fell at different points from the page's, but the joined text is identical. The corrected lines give them as whole verified sentences.
  - The note's account of where the eye sentence was split is a fetch artefact. It is replaced by this audit's verification statement.
