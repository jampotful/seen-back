- **Pooled sexes (desk flag; ruling).** Methods: "Specimens were not sexed because external sexual differences in Nectophrynoides are subtle, ... and reliable determination of sex would require dissection; therefore, females and males were not analysed separately." Acceptable, provided conditions state that sexes were not determined and n; the filed measurement fields state n and "sex not determined". Replacement conditions quote the reason and add the right-side and juvenile-exclusion rules. Not counted.
