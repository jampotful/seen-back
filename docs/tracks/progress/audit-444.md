## Unit-wide checks

- **Trait key.** sense_echolocation for call emission is main practice: desk-d325 (Barbastella peak frequency and source level, Coelops duty cycle) and desk-d326 (Pipistrellus terminal frequency) file emission parameters under sense_echolocation. Accepted for lines 3 to 6. Each emission record says in its conditions that it is not a hearing threshold. Lines 3 to 5 describe what the animal does with its calls (beam aim, frequency drift, site means). That is emission, and no sensory capacity is claimed, so no overreach.
- **evidence_kind.** Main files emission parameters as `physiological_response`. Audit 438 accepted the d326 Pipistrellus terminal frequency because "sense_echolocation with physiological_response matches 4 existing desk records on main". Lines 3, 4 and 5 use `behavioural_response`. SENSORY-ANATOMY.md defines that kind as "The tested organism's response, discrimination or null under stated controls". None of the three tests a stimulus. They report spontaneous call properties. Audit 441 counted an inconsistent evidence_kind as a filing error, so I count lines 3, 4 and 5 here; each is fixable. Line 6 is a playback discrimination test, and `behavioural_response` is right for it. Owner question: beam-scanning angle (line 3) is a motor act and sits nearest to behaviour. If the owner wants emission behaviour separated from emission acoustics, that needs a ruling.
- **Repeats.** Searched all of docs/ by species and by DOI. The six DOIs appear only in the progress notes of desk-d217, d325, d326 and d328, as unread leads, with no evidence row. On main, Pipistrellus pipistrellus has one row (desk-d326, Goetze et al. 2016, terminal frequency; a different paper and value), Crotalus durissus has vision rows only (Tashiro et al., Gower et al.), and Philaenus spumarius has vision rows only (oa-13b). Rhinolophus ferrumequinum appears only in try-species.json. Rhinolophus clivosus and Rhinolophus nippon have no evidence rows (d326 held out an R. nippon paper over its licence). No repeats.
- **Names.** Mammal Diversity Database pages opened: Rhinolophus clivosus (taxon 1004669) is a valid species, Cretzschmar 1828, Rhinolophidae, Chiroptera. Rhinolophus ferrumequinum (taxon 1004684): the taxonomy note says "previously included R. nippon", and its country list does not include China. Rhinolophus nippon (taxon 1004722) is a valid species, Temminck 1835, with countries Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Japan, Nepal, North and South Korea and Myanmar(?). Its synonyms include Rhinolophus tragatus B. H. Hodgson 1835 and Rhinolophus ferrum-equinum tragatus G. M. Allen 1938. Pipistrellus pipistrellus was not reopened; audit 438 opened MDD taxon 1005632 (valid, von Schreber 1774). Crotalus durissus is in docs/views/gaps/gbif-names.tsv as EXACT, ACCEPTED (2444433). Philaenus spumarius is not in gbif-names.tsv. It is on main with family and order (oa-13b), and a search showed it as the current binomial (Wikipedia, NZOR, Plazi). GBIF is refused by the fetch tool. None of the three bat names is in gbif-names.tsv.
- **Licences.** For each PLOS page I opened, the page prints "distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License". From Crossref, read here: pone.0090622, 0060752 and 0062710 are CC BY 4.0 with content-version "unspecified", which is accepted under the rules (unspecified plus page-printed CC Attribution). pone.0187769 and 0199703 are CC BY 4.0 vor. Licences are OK for lines 2 to 6. Line 1's page could not be read (see below).

Audit 438 left the same fault uncounted, so the owner may want to settle which rule applies. The desk records have no `family`/`order` keys at all, so the replacements add keys in raw JSON.
