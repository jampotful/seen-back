# desk-d176 (non-insect, non-cephalopod invertebrate photoreception)

Main audit (evidence, audit/corrected, audit/ALL, by order and trait): crustaceans, scorpions, horseshoe crab, harvestmen, chitons, cubozoans and gastropods already hold records. Open cells used: behaviour_vision for Calanus (copepod), Meganyctiphanes norvegica, Talitrus saltator, Tripneustes gratilla and Strongylocentrotus intermedius (no Echinoidea on main); eye_type for Parribacus japonicus, Scyllarides squammosus and Ibacus novemdentatus (only a Scyllarides latus acuity record on main).
