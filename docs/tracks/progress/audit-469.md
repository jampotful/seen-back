## Line 1, Philonthus decorus, oleic acid repels both sexes, hexane repels males

Abstract sentence verbatim: "A strong repellent activity against both sexes of Ph. decorus was caused by oleic acid, while hexane repelled the males." Results: "Hexane was a strong repellent against the males and was neutral to the females."; "High repellent activity against both sexes of Ph. decorus was exhibited by oleic acid." The value says no more than the quote.

Fault (note fact and design): the note says "Effects are the authors' qualitative scale with no printed statistic". The categories are bands of a numeric attraction index (Table 3: "0.125–0.249—strong repellent activity", "0.500–1.999—absence of activity"); Methods print the index (beetles on the four squares nearest the odour source over those on the four remotest) and "were compared using ANOVA"; Table 4 ("n = 22; compounds are arranged in the order of increase in significance of differences in their effects on the males and females") is an image on the page and was not read. n = 22 is freeze frames from one video ("Each video lasted for over 6 min (22 freeze frames)"; each set of 50 "once a day"), not independent runs, and no blank or solvent control arena is described. The record states the absence of statistics wrongly and omits the pseudo-replicated design. Verdict **correct** (conditions, note).

## Line 2, Matsumurasca onukii, 65 of 120 playback tests with a female response

Section 2.1.2 first sentence verbatim: "A total of 65 females responded to the MCaS playback out of a total of 120 tests performed." The next sentence carries the null the note gives ("... none of the MCaS parameters affected female responses (p > 0.05; Table S2), which indicated that whether the female responds to the MCaS was only related to the female's physiological state ..."). Playback: "Repeated units of a pre-recorded MCaS (duration = 1.491 s; Df = 340.8 Hz) were used to synthesize a 5 min playback signal at 2.5 s intervals ...". No explicit definition of a female response; no silence (no-playback) control reported.

Fault 1 (sample composition): conditions say the adults were "collected at the Tea Research Institute ... in 2023 and 2024"; the tested insects were "from the second and subsequent generations" reared in an insectary, "Sexually mature seven-day-old virgin E. onukii adults". The record misstates the animals' origin and omits age and mating status.

Fault 2 (omitted results and control): the same section prints significant results among the 65 responding tests, "The multiple linear regression model of NFS1 was significant (F4,60 = 2.765, p = 0.035)", "the effect of IMCaS-S0 was significant (t = 2.012, p = 0.049)", and DelayID "(F1,63 = 4.976, p = 0.029)", DMCaS-S1 "(t = 2.231, p = 0.029)"; and with no silence control spontaneous female signalling is not separated from response. Neither is in the record. Verdict **correct** (method, conditions, note).

## Line 3, Eublaberus posticus, light-startle response to lights on and lights off

Abstract sentence verbatim in two pieces: "In our first experiment, we found that LSR, and habituation of LSR, occurs to both lights being turned on" / "and lights being turned off." Results as in the note: "(estimate = −1.918, p = 0.005)" for light-on trials and "(estimate = −2.839, p < 0.000)" for light-on acclimation (printed "p < 0.000"). Lights "(32 watts, 2196 lumens each)" and "two 3-watt red light bulbs" verbatim.

Fault (omitted qualifiers and sample): Experiment 1 Results also print "When considering the probability of startle when the acclimation light was on (top row), the trends were less clear." and "Additionally, no clear differences were observed between light-off and light-on trials.", which qualify "occurs to both" for the light-on-acclimated half; and "startle probability somewhat increased with weight (estimate = 0.411, p < 0.003)". The subjects were "adult cockroaches that had intact antennae and legs, and at least half of their wings" (n = 96, sex not stated); the record gives neither stage nor the sex silence, and its "(not wild-caught)" is an inference where the page prints founders "obtained from Roach Crossing ..., Josh's Frogs ..., and Cape Cod Roaches". Startle defined as "any change in location inside the apparatus during the trial" (added, uncounted). Verdict **correct** (conditions, note).
