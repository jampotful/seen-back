No displacement, velocity, acceleration, dB or intensity value appears anywhere in the paper (the only m/s figure is the speed of sound in water) (tool summary for the absence, checked on two passes). Ruling: a delivered frequency with no amplitude is not enough. Frequency fixes the stimulus's spectral content only; detection and response of a lateral-line or vibration sense depend on amplitude at a given frequency, the same way a hearing response depends on dB at a given frequency. So this is the vibration equivalent of the audits 1097, 1098, 1099 case (no level at the animal), not of 1096 and 1100 to 1103 (level stated at a point at the subject). HOLD OUT lines 1 to 3.
- Control: device-off condition printed, "When the device was off, all populations showed no right side or left side preponderance (all populations P > 0.35)." Met.
- Trait fit (uncounted, for the owner): what the lines measure is turning side during the vibration-attraction response, a laterality finding; the vibration only attracts the fish. Noted in each Replacement note.
