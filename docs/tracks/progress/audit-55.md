## Limits of this audit

WebSearch returned result titles and links with no snippets, so I judged results by title and by the pages I opened. A page whose title does not name the genus, and whose text I could not read, could hold a value; the leads above are those cases. The fetch tool reads a page through a summarizer, so a statement that a long paper "names no grouse" rests on the text the summarizer received, which can be an excerpt; the Haarlem thesis is a case where the text stopped before the table I wanted. Pages behind bot checks (PubMed Central, CSIC, bioone), JavaScript (eLife) or access limits (Michigan State), and files that came back as binary data, stayed unread, and I looked for no other route to them. The screening of every record rests on titles and stored abstracts, as the records say in `where`. The statements about what main holds come from the gap queue, the canon file, the taxonomy files, the evidence logs and the audited logs on origin/main, which I searched by DOI, title, author and taxon string; a source cited under a different spelling would escape that search.
