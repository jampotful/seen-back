## Line 2, Afrogarypus foordi, eyes_count 2 (eye pairs)

Description, Carapace: "Carapace: Strongly sub-triangular, narrow furrow posterior to the eyes (Figs 1A, C, 3H)." then "Two pairs of corneate eyes situated on ocular tubercles, located about one-third away from the anterior edge." Quote verbatim, whole sentence, 17 words; value 2 pairs says no more. Introduction: "Geogarypids are four-eyed and rather flat pseudoscorpions"; no other eye sentence (Diagnosis, Remarks, Methods); consistent.

Fault (scientific meaning, applicability): the page's Description has no holotype or sex heading and is of the species: its first Carapace sentence cites Fig. 1A (female holotype) and Fig. 1C (male paratype), other entries read "in both sexes", and Methods give chaetotaxy "as ♀(♂)". Material: holotype 1♀ (NMBAP 00279), paratype 1♂ (NMBAP 00466) and 1♂, 2♀, 1♀ 3♂ other material. The record's `method` ("preserved specimen", singular) and `conditions` (opening "Holotype female (NMBAP 00279)") present the eye count as resting on the female holotype alone. This is the mirror of audit 463's lines 1-2 (there a holotype-only description was widened; here a species description is narrowed to one specimen), and it misstates the basis of the observation; nothing else in the record corrects it. Borderline in effect (narrowing understates rather than overstates the support), counted for consistency with audit 463's sample-basis ruling. Verdict **correct** (method, conditions).
