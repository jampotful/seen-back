### Evans et al. 2023 (lines 1, 2), Drosophila larvae, sense_thermal

Quotes. Line 1 MATCH (Section 3.5; preceded by a period, so it is a whole sentence). **Line 2 starts mid-sentence:** the printed sentence is "In the bar graph example shown, L2 larvae demonstrate significantly different turning rates for the histogram bin centered at 0°C/s compared to the bin centered at −0.002°C/s." (fragment questions on two passes: "In the bar graph example shown, L2 larvae demonstrate" present; "L2" preceded by a comma). The record drops the opening clause. Quote-form fault. The full sentence is 27 words.

Genus-rank rule. **Counted, lines 1 and 2 (taxon, rank, grade).** The paper names one species: the Introduction speaks of "simpler model systems like the fruit fly Drosophila melanogaster", and the rest of the text says "Drosophila larva(e)". Methods 2.1 lists the strains (wild-type Canton-S, R11F02>GCaMP6m, Ir25a2 mutants, Ir25a>TeTxLC and rescue lines) with no species and no stock centre (tool summary). The audit brief's grade rule gives m for one named species measured in the source and g for groups. Nothing in the paper is a group of species. The unit's genus/g filing turns a measurement of one species into a statement about the genus, which changes applicability (Drosophila species differ widely in thermal range), and it keeps these records from joining main's D. melanogaster sense_thermal line (oa-6:745). The closest standing rule is audit 204's (applied in audit 254): a record goes at species rank when the paper presents the finding for the species and the record states where the name is printed. The correction is species rank, grade m, with the naming stated in `conditions`. If the owner prefers the unit's stricter reading (the species must be named in Methods or Results), lines 1 and 2 lose this count; line 2 stays counted for its quote.

Values and context, confirmed: sine amplitude 0.05 to 1.0 °C, period 60 s, offset 17 °C (Section 2.6); "The lowest modulation (A = 0.05°C, or peak cooling rate −0.0045°C/s)" (Section 3.4) "was the smallest sine wave we could generate given the precision of our equipment" (Section 3.4); the three stages "are indistinguishable, and all of them are significantly above the Δ(ΔF/F) = 0 level" (3.5); "~10 animals for each of the 15 conditions" (Figure 6 legend); larvae "held in place using gentle compression from a cover slip" (2.6); gradient 13 to 21 °C on 22 × 22 cm agar, around 20 larvae per run, 15 min (2.2); "+0.006°C/s is also shows a turning rate with a significant difference from adjacent bins"; "We would thus conclude that L2 larvae are sensitive to cooling rates of at least that amount." Upper bound, not threshold, is stated correctly on line 1. Rate-of-change sensitivity fits sense_thermal ("temperature sensing; string with figures", tools/lit/extract.py).

Uncounted: (a) A × 2π/T for A = 0.05 °C and T = 60 s gives about 0.0052 °C/s, not 0.0045 °C/s (my calculation; the 1.0 °C amplitude does give the paper's −0.1 °C/s). The record correctly reproduces the paper's printed figure, but the discrepancy belongs in `conditions`. (b) The Figure 7 legend calls the −0.002 °C/s comparison the behavioural upper bound on sensitivity and says significance was tested by bootstrapping; 3.5 reports that mid-L3 and especially late-L3 larvae show reduced sensitivity to temperature change. Line 2 could carry both.

### Zhang and Pan 2021 (lines 3, 4), Nilaparvata lugens, sense_magnetic

Quotes. Line 3 MATCH and line 4 MATCH (PeerJ PDF, two passes; the χ2 = 10.261 and p = 0.088 are as printed). Both are whole sentences.

Design. The cross-tube assay has two phases (PDF, tool summary). In phase 1, ten rice seedlings sit at the north arm end, insects are released at the centre and collected near the food after about ten hours. In phase 2 the collected insects go into a new cross tube with no rice seedlings, and arm choice is recorded for 0.5 h. Food was always north and never moved, and the arms were aligned to the cardinal points. Four fields were tested: local GMF, NZMF (about 500 nT in a 150 mm-radius central sphere), horizontal component reversal and vertical component reversal. Chi-square on the four-arm distribution with Bonferroni-corrected pairwise tests, degrees of freedom never stated. Adults "within 48 h of emergence regardless of gender or mating status"; 15 W lamp 320 to 680 nm; shade cloth over the coils; 12 replicates per field. GMF 52,487 ± 841 nT, inclination 56.29 ± 1.02°, declination 5.30 ± 0.59°. GMF N = 241, 87, 157, 132; NZMF 110, 96, 119, 107; vertical reversal 139, 120, 163, 130, with 25.18, 21.74, 29.53, 23.55% (χ2 = 9.371, p = 0.102); IscA1 knockdown cut north from 39.06% to 28.82% in GMF.

**Counted, line 3 (scientific meaning; method, conditions, note).** (a) `conditions` says "food placed in the north arm in this test ('original food')". The test tube held no food; north was the food direction of the training phase, so the result is a choice of the formerly rewarded direction. (b) The record omits the horizontal component reversal, one of the four fields in the title's "component reversal". Under it, most insects still went north, "similar to that in GMF" (χ2 = 87.872, p < 0.001; Fig. 4 caption N = 191, 93, 122, 91). So the north choice did not follow the reversed horizontal component, and food direction and magnetic north were never separated (Discussion: insects were to "associate magnetic field information under the normal GMF with the presence of food in a particular direction"). The record's note says only that the preference "disappears in the near-zero field and vertical-reversal field", which reads as a clean field-dependence. This omission changes the causal interpretation of the north preference.

**Counted, line 4 (scientific meaning; note).** The NZMF null and its percentages are right. But the note's reading, "showing that a normal field is needed for the orientation preference in the GMF record", is contradicted by the omitted horizontal-reversal result: a field with its horizontal component reversed also kept the preference. The paper shows that removing the field or reversing its vertical component abolished it, not that an unaltered field is needed. The method also omits the two-phase design (same fix as line 3). Counted once.

Uncounted: the Fig. 3 caption counts in GMF sum to 617, against the stated "511 ± 69" total (line 3 carries both figures side by side without comment); a four-arm χ2 of 10.261 sits poorly with p = 0.088 (and 9.371 with p = 0.102), and the paper states no degrees of freedom. These are the paper's own inconsistencies; the records copy them faithfully and should flag them.
