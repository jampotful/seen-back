## Checks (Biodiversity Data Journal 13: e137852, Hao, Yu & Zhang 2025)

- Materials and methods read whole (XML). Measurement sentences: "Specimens were measured by the measuring tool of Leica LAS V. 4.3 software." "All measurements are given in millimetres." "Total length of body excludes chelicerae." Then palp/leg formats, vulva clearing, deposition (MHBU) and the abbreviation list (ALE, AME, PLE, PME, MOA as eye names only). No sentence defines an eye dimension or says "diameter". The unit printed is millimetres.
- Trait fork, stated loudly. The eye sentence prints the label "Eye diameters and interdistance". The unit brief accepts "a diameter definition in the Methods or the label", and main keeps label-only records as eye_diameter_mm (desk-d362, audit 479), so eye_diameter_mm is kept. Audit 737 ruled the other way for desk-d414 (label 'Eye diameters', Methods silent, moved to sensory_anatomy). If the lead applies 737's reading, lines 56 to 59 (and the other Latouchia lines 40 to 55) move to sensory_anatomy together. The replacement notes say this.
- Eye sentence (preview page, two pieces joined with one space, exact): "Eye diameters and interdistance: AME 0.11, ALE 0.25, PME 0.15, PLE 0.19, AME–AME 0.14, AME–ALE 0.12, ALE–PLE 0.13, PME–PME 0.30, PME–PLE 0.07." That is 22 words by check.py count. The filed values match label, eye and sex digit for digit (57 ALE 0.25, 58 PME 0.15, 59 PLE 0.19).
- Measured specimen as printed: "Male (Holotype, MHBU-ARA-10000055)." Type block: 'Material', with one 'Holotype:' entry (sex male; institutionCode 'MHBU-ARA-10000055; KYUARA#1983'). There is no 'Paratypes:' entry. No female of L. wenchuan is described or listed, so leaving the female unfiled is correct.
- Headings as printed: Taxon treatments; 'Latouchia  Pocock, 1901' (no word 'Genus'); 'Latouchia wenchuan Hao, Yu & Zhang, sp. nov.'; Material; Description. No Class, Order or Family heading is printed anywhere. FAULT: the desk's `where` adds an unprinted 'Genus'. Fixed.
- Licence (XML, whole sentence joined from three pieces): "This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0), which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the original author and source are credited." Accepted. FAULT: the desk gave only a summary. Fixed.
- Citation: the page prints "Hao L, Yu K, Zhang F (2025) Description of five new species from southern China, with note on the type species of Latouchia Pocock, 1901 (Araneae, Halonoproctidae). Biodiversity Data Journal 13: e137852." The XML gives authors Long Hao, Kun Yu, Feng Zhang; volume 13, no issue element; epub 02 01 2025. The authors, title, year and journal string ('Biodiversity Data Journal 13: e137852') are right.
- DOI. The publisher page prints https://doi.org/10.3897/BDJ.12.e137852, and the WSC reference prints the same. The publisher XML article-id, however, prints 10.3897/BDJ.13.e137852 (raw element read twice). The page-printed DOI is kept as the brief directs. FAULT: the desk did not report the conflicting XML DOI. It is now recorded in the note as unresolved (Crossref not read). The lead may want a Crossref read from a searched URL before merge.
- WSC current: spec-data/71023 gives 'Latouchia wenchuan Hao, Yu & Zhang, 2025', ACCEPTED, in Latouchia Pocock, 1901, Halonoproctidae Pocock, 1901. There are no synonyms or transfers. Taxon, family and authority stand.
- Repeats: git grep of origin/main docs/ and data/ for 'wenchuan' and '137852' finds only Pholcus wenchuan (desk-d362) and a Wenchuan locality in desk-d296. Neither is the same species or DOI, so there is no repeat.
- The unit field reads desk-d415 on all three lines. Other note faults: the desk's 'PME– PME in the female sentences' remark does not apply to wenchuan (no female), and its L. yaoi mention was not verified (no yaoi heading through the wenchuan treatment). Both were removed. The Methods were only partly quoted; they are now quoted.
