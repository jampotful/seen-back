## Albino ruling
LFR2024015 is a pigment mutant whose eye is itself abnormal ("The iris was blood-red, with a reddish pupil."), and the paper sets it apart from the "Three typical specimens". An eye-diameter record from it does not show the species' typical eye. Ruling: line 22 is held out (named for dropping); its corrected line is still given below with the flag in the note.
