Faults noted, not counted:
- Line 1: Abstract sentence 1 ("seeds require incubation at fluctuating temperatures to terminate dormancy") is not carried, so the record never says the seed was dormant at constant 20 C, the state in which the red-light and GA3 reductions of psi_b(50) were measured. The `method` lists GA3 and red light but omits the ABA, fluridone and paclobutrazol response tests and the ABA-content assay (sentence 4); fluridone has no result in the abstract, so nothing is misstated. The red-light comparison's baseline (20 C without GA3, by sentence 5) is not named.
- Line 1, GA3 as a separate sense_chemical line: the held-out reason calls the GA3 result "a null", but sentence 5 is a positive GA3 effect (psi_b(50) lowered at 20 C). That sentence runs to about 200 characters, so it cannot be the quote of an abstract-only record; the only GA3 sentence under 125 characters is sentence 7, the null. Whether an abstract-only GA3 null (with the sentence-5 reduction in its value) may stand as its own sense_chemical record is the owner's call; filing it under the light record's conditions, as here, misstates nothing.
- Line 2: the significant moisture x light x time interaction (F13,1525 = 4.01, P < 0.001) is not stated beside "soil moisture was marginal (P = 0.053)". The treatment-specific closing times in the value are that interaction, so the reading is not changed, but the statistic should sit next to the marginal main effect. The seed localities are in Supplementary Table S1 ("localities from where the seed came"), which the record's "the paper does not give the number of populations" overlooks; Table S1 was not opened here.
- Line 3: F2,18 = 48.62 is the three-group test (night, morning, petal limbs removed), not a night-against-day test; the value attaches it to "higher at night than in the day". Benzaldehyde's own test was significant (F1,8 = 9.57, P = 0.015); the record leaves the benzaldehyde sentence out because of the E-caryophyllene null, which loses a same-direction result but adds no error. Flower age (1 to 4 d; flowers protandrous) and the rise of p-benzoquinone after petal excision are not carried.
