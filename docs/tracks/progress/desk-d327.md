2. Camponotus blandus, behaviour_vision: innate UV over blue and green preference (Yilmaz et al. 2017, 10.1242/jeb.158501); blue-versus-green null in note; lab colony.

6. Scarabaeus satyrus (printed Escarabaeus), polarization: orientation threshold DoLP 0.04 to 0.32 (Foster et al. 2019, 10.1242/jeb.188532); DoLP 0.11 not significant.
7-8. Rhinecanthus aculeatus, colour_vision and behaviour_vision (Cheney et al. 2013, 10.1242/jeb.087932).
9. Lyssomanes viridis, behaviour_vision: no green preference, prefers second darkest gray (Tedore and Johnsen 2016, 10.1242/jeb.129122).
10. Neohelice granulata (printed Chasmagnathus granulatus), behaviour_vision: looming escape timing (Oliva et al. 2007, 10.1242/jeb.02707).
