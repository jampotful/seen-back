## Advice on yield
Acta Horticulturae (ishs.org) species-level photoperiod abstracts gave six of the sixteen records (Kalanchoe, Hydrangea, Helianthus, Lilium, Salvia x2); the pages print authors, pages and DOI. Sentence length is the main loss: ask WebFetch for each abstract sentence with its character count rather than a summary. Cultivar papers dominate floriculture, so search species-level trials (Salvia, Impatiens, Anemone) and check docs/views/evidence on main first (Anemone coronaria, Cosmos and Mirabilis were already there). Horticulture Journal flower-opening abstracts are nearly exhausted (Eustoma, Portulaca, rose, Ipomoea nil are the whole pool). The NBRP reference pages (rrc.nbrp.jp) print a full abstract and the DOI for Ipomoea nil papers. Synonym traps met: Perilla ocymoides to P. frutescens, Pharbitis to Ipomoea nil.
