## Trait keys and value form

- sense_hearing is in check.py's TRAITS. Main uses it for behavioural as well as physiological responses to sound (e.g. Phidippus audax freezing to low airborne tones; Emblemasoma auditrix minimum acoustic energy to elicit a response), so a behavioural response threshold fits the key. Ruling on the value: the paper itself defines the term ("A behavioural response threshold was determined by the lowest sound level" " for which TTM was significantly shorter than the TTM for the Silent treatment.") and names 90 dB a threshold in Results, Discussion and Abstract; it also states, verbatim, "It is possible that the behavioural response threshold in this species could be lower, and therefore the acoustic" "sensitivity higher than that measured as there was no sound level treatment intermediate to the Lowest sound" "treatment (90 dB) and the Silent treatment." So 90 dB is a threshold by the paper's operational definition and an upper bound on the true one. The value's "(the lowest level tested)" is a limiting qualifier taken from the Methods, not a claim beyond the quote; the conditions already carry "the true threshold may lie lower" and that it is a metamorphosis-timing response, not an audiogram. Value wording accepted. The response measured is time to metamorphosis to the first juvenile instar, checked at 6 h intervals; the record says TTM and settlement cue, correctly.
