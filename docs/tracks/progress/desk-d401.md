# desk-d401 progress

Angle: spider eye-size sentences in two EJT papers on families not yet filed. 60 records (log docs/views/evidence/desk-d401.jsonl), check.py 0 problems for this log.

## Filed
- EJT 917: 74-93 (10.5852/ejt.2024.917.2389; Wang, Irfan, Lu, Zhang, Zhang, 2024; Corinnidae, Spinirta): hongyui, liuae, simianshan (male holotype, female paratype each), yintiaoling (male holotype; female unknown), aviformis and quadrata (non-type vouchers, both sexes; female first described here per WSC). 11 sex lines x AME/ALE/PME/PLE = 44 records, eye_diameter_mm (Methods: 'Eye sizes were measured as the maximum dorsal diameter.'; millimeters).
- EJT 947: 109-129 (10.5852/ejt.2024.947.2623; Korai, Jäger, 2024; Sparassidae, Heteropoda): dulongensis (male holotype, female paratype), longa (female holotype), vaginalis (female holotype). 4 sex lines = 16 records, sensory_anatomy (the 'Eyes:' line prints no diameter label; Methods define no eye dimension).

## Weak spots
- All text came as fetch-tool excerpts of the publisher PDFs (shown in my search results); eye sentences are joined from consecutive pieces, Heteropoda lines are cut with [...] after the four values because the tool would not return whole lines.
- Heteropoda dulongensis male and female print identical four values (0.30, 0.55, 0.50, 0.65); possible repeat in the paper, recorded as printed.
- H. dulongensis female is 'Female (paratype)' with no code (3 paratype females); Heteropoda catalogue numbers are not printed (CBEE only).
- Spinirta aviformis: paper prints 'aviforma', WSC current spelling aviformis used in taxon. Measured aviformis and quadrata specimens are non-type vouchers.
- Spinirta: Crossref licence 'unspecified' plus page-printed CC BY; Heteropoda Crossref not reached (publisher PDF and article page give CC BY 4.0).
- Neighbouring sentences for aviformis and quadrata were not returned.

## Not filed and why
- Heteropoda hainanensis and H. bawanglingensis (same paper): hainanensis is a replaced homonym in WSC (valid H. acies); bawanglingensis dropped to hold the unit at 60 records (valid, female holotype, AME 0.27, ALE 0.49, PME 0.41, PLE 0.48 as printed; lead).
- Zodariidae EJT 1016 (Shafaie, Pekar, Seyyar, 2025; 10.5852/ejt.2025.1016.3063): the download URL in my search results would not fetch; lead.
- Plazi, Zenodo and aggregator copies of Theridiidae, Oonopidae and Heteropoda papers (not publisher pages); Arachnologische Mitteilungen (bioone PDF returned no text).
