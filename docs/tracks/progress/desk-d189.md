## Records
- Wu et al. 2024, Insects 15(10): 780, DOI 10.3390/insects15100780 (Crossref CC BY 4.0 vor; page footer CC BY): Protaetia brevitarsis (ACCEPTED in gbif-names.tsv, 1080863). Three records from Results 3.1: male choice of 650 nm over 380, 440, 520, 580 nm and white (520, 580 and white did not differ); female choice of 650 nm; and, among six red stimuli, females preferring 730 nm and males 730 and 635 nm. Confound kept in conditions: stimulus illuminance was unequal (Table 1: 600 lx at 650 nm, 4500 lx white; Table 2: 460 lx at 730 nm), lux is human-weighted, and the paper never says intensities were matched. The most-chosen stimuli were the dimmest by lux. Percentages are in figures only. Main holds only review lambda_max rows for this species; sweep-49 notes the paper in a searched line but files no row.
- Yang et al. 2024, Insects 15(2): 129, DOI 10.3390/insects15020129 (Crossref CC BY 4.0 vor): Spodoptera frugiperda (ACCEPTED, 5109855). Two records: males, blue stronger than UV (F2,12 = 5.665, p = 0.023), blue versus green not different; females, no differences among UV, blue, green (F2,20 = 2.723, p = 0.09), a null that limits the headline claim that blue attracts more moths. Each LED tested against darkness; 100 lux by lux meter, so photon flux not matched across UV, blue and green.
- Ekejiuba and Meyhoefer 2026, Insects 17(3): 246, DOI 10.3390/insects17030246 (Crossref CC BY 4.0 vor; page metadata CC BY 3.0): Encarsia formosa Gahan, 1924 (Accepted in Catalogue of Life via gbif.org/species/1365418; not in gbif-names.tsv; ITIS fetch returned a different taxon). Two records: green LED trap 67% recapture, p = 0.0001; no difference among blue, red, cyan, yellow. All traps but UV were set to equal photon flux (8.1 umol m-2 s-1); UV was measured and converted, not adjusted (17%, p = 0.0026). Not on main under any trait.

All quotes were seen on two or more fetches of the mdpi.com article pages (the tool returns fragments under about 100 characters; sentences were rebuilt from them and punctuation checked on a last pass). Two quotes carry a marked cut (Wu male, Wu female) because the whole sentences run over 40 words; the cut clauses are stated in conditions.
