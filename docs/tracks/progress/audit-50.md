## Observations on the unit (outside the count)

1. **Nine record genera and Testudo have no family or order in the queue.** docs/views/gaps/queue.jsonl and cells.jsonl give blank `family` and `order`, and `closest` none, for Pachycephala, Lasiocampa, Lampides, Glaucopsyche, Histrionicus, Aricia, Aporia, Hamadryas, Rhaphigaster and Testudo (Orgyia, Paonias, Melissodes, Camponotus and Aeshna have both). None of the ten is in taxonomy-extra.tsv, although `harvest_get.py` prints the GBIF family in its header. The citation step therefore never ran for them, and the records' sentence "the queue holds no measured relative of the genus" is accurate for the queue and misleading about the animals: main holds measured Anatidae rows (Anas platyrhynchos lambda_max 420 nm and Branta sandvicensis 413 nm, for example), so with taxonomy in place I expect Histrionicus lambda_max_nm (line 32) would have had a chased DOI; that expectation is my inference, not a result. The records for Aporia and Hamadryas name the blank entry as the cause. Adding the ten genera to taxonomy-extra.tsv and rerunning the citation step is the repair.
2. **A DOI chased for another trait.** The Orgyia records (lines 1 and 2, outside the sample) chase 10.1016/j.asd.2020.101002, the Feller et al. acuity compilation, for the flicker traits, because the harvest prints one citation step per genus; the queue's closest flicker record for Orgyia is insect#392, whose citers were not chased.
3. **Genus homonyms.** The harvest took Aricia (a lycaenid butterfly) to be a saturniid, and the unit read the Lycaenidae review as well, which its note explains. Hamadryas is also a baboon, and the harvest's Hamadryas listings include primate papers.
4. **Camponotus, Aeshna and Testudo have wanted lines and no search records.** The unit holds back every open trait of those genera behind a wanted line, which desk-screen.md allows, and the harvest outputs of the three genera hold vision papers that no record screens (a Camponotus compressus compound-eye paper, a Camponotus consobrinus thesis, dragonfly colour-vision reviews, a Hermann's tortoise colour-discrimination experiment). The wanted lines name only some of them. Because those genera have no records, nothing about them counts toward the rate.
5. **Wanted-line fields.** Authors are blank on 14 of the 15 lines (all but line 14); titles read "title not shown in the search result" on lines 3, 4 and 7 and a bare page title on line 9; year and DOI are blank on lines 3, 4, 7 and 9. Desk-screen.md step 6 forbids empty author fields.
