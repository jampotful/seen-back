## Limits of this audit

WebSearch returned result titles and links with no snippets, so I judged results by title and by the pages I opened. A page whose title does not name the genus, and whose text I could not read, could hold a value; the leads above are those cases. The fetch tool reads a page through a summariser, so a statement that a long paper "names no Histrionicus" or "names no genus" rests on the text the summariser received, which can be an excerpt. The screening of every record rests on titles and abstract starts, as the records say in `where`, and the species lists that sit in full texts and supplements are out of reach of both the desk's screening and mine.
