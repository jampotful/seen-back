Every page fetched came from my own search results: europeanjournaloftaxonomy.eu article/download/2977/13413 and article/view/2977; article/download/2205/9489; the ASW pages Pristimantis-fouqueti (Craugastoridae > Pristimantinae) and Adenomera-guarani (Leptodactylidae > Leptodactylinae). Checked on the publisher PDFs: author lines (all six EJT 1004 authors including Michel BLANC; all five EJT 888 authors), titles, the volume and page strings ('European Journal of Taxonomy 1004: 259–293 (2025)', 'European Journal of Taxonomy 888: 1–45'), DOIs, the whole licence sentences ('This work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0)' with no full stop on 1004; with a full stop on 888), heading paths (1004 prints Class, Order and Family with authorities; 888 prints no Class, then Order 'Fischer von Waldhein, 1813', Family, Subfamily and Genus), Table 2 captions, both header rows and the TD rows cell by cell. On main, git grep for species and DOI over docs/ finds only the desk-d613 ED rows, which are a different measurement, so no line here is a repeat.
