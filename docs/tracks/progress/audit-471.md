## Unit-wide rulings

- **Trait key (desk flag; ruling).** Main has two forms for these sentences. Audit 433 kept Jingneta qishan "Eye sizes and interdistances: ... PME 0.04 ..." (desk-d322, on main) under sensory_anatomy/anatomical_measurement because "Eye sizes" is undefined and not stated as a diameter, and moved Pholcus chaoyang lines printing "diameters" to eye_diameter_mm. Audit 464 filed Speleoticus hei "Eye sizes and interdistances" under eye_diameter_mm because its Methods say "Eye diameters were measured at the widest part." The test is therefore whether the paper defines eye size as a diameter. Rules.md (31 Oct) has no sentence about spider eye diameters; the precedent comes from audits 433 and 464 only.
  - ZooKeys 1249 (Zoica): Methods "All measurements are in millimeters." and no eye-measuring sentence. sensory_anatomy stands (lines 1, 2).
  - ZooKeys 1251 (Moneta): Methods "All measurements are given in millimetres." and no eye-measuring sentence. sensory_anatomy stands (lines 3, 4).
  - ZooKeys 1174 (Lin & Li): Methods print "Eye sizes are measured as the maximum diameter from either the dorsal or the frontal view." (read twice). These figures are diameters, so eye_diameter_mm is the key and a reader of eye_diameter_mm misses lines 5 and 6 as filed. Counted fault on lines 5, 6. This also bears on 1174 lines 7-11 (audit 472).
  - Value form: each value is one labelled number in mm, not a ratio; units mm from Methods. Not a list, so no range misreading.
- **PME only (desk flag).** Main holds a PME-only record from an "Eye sizes" list (Jingneta qishan, desk-d322, after audit 433). Audit 464 split Speleoticus because its value was an unlabelled four-number list, which reads as a range; it did not require every eye to be filed. Filing PME alone is an acceptable choice when conditions give the other eyes, which all six lines do. Not a fault.
