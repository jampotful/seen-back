# desk-d177 (sound responses of freshwater and marine invertebrates)

Main audit (evidence, audit/corrected, audit/ALL): no sense_hearing records for Neocaridina, Neohelice, Crassostrea, Acartia, Mytilus or Eurytemora. Euprymna berryi and Doryteuthis pealeii hearing already on main (Putland 2023 not refiled).

Papers read (each CC BY 4.0 in Crossref, vor, and on the Frontiers page): Azarm-Karnagh et al. 2023 (Front Ecol Evol 11: 1091314), Sal Moyano et al. 2023 (Front Mar Sci 10: 1050148), Ledoux et al. 2023 (10: 1104526), Kuhn et al. 2023 (10: 1134792), Aspirault et al. 2023 (10: 1111466).

Records: 8 in docs/views/evidence/desk-d177.jsonl (sense_hearing, behavioural responses, categorical; two are null results for Mytilus edulis and Eurytemora herdmani). All carry "partial": true (abstract sentences; stimulus details from Methods).

Dropped or held:
- Lillis 2015 (PeerJ 3: e999, oyster larval recruitment to reef sound): Crossref lists no licence.
- Lillis 2016 (PeerJ 4: e2557, Orbicella faveolata settlement): CC BY in Crossref, but every sentence carrying the value is over 40 words.
- Pysanczyn 2023 (Front Mar Sci 10: 1111599) is a mini-review; Cones 2022 (Front Mar Sci 9: 1070290, squid pile driving) is CC BY but Doryteuthis pealeii already has hearing records on main and the measure is an in situ alarm response; Gigot 2023 (Venus verrucosa larvae): CC BY, results are mortality and settlement with mostly null sound effects, abstract not read verbatim.
- Not tried: Pulver 2022, Feller and Cronin 2016, Schneider 2017, Li 2018, Lattenkamp 2021 leads; magnetic, thermal and touch cells for marine invertebrates (searches returned only paywalled or repository copies).

Name check: WoRMS and Wikipedia fetches were unanswered (blocked). Names are the standard accepted names as printed (Neohelice granulata, older name Chasmagnathus granulatus); no authority was opened, flag for the audit. Sal Moyano is given as a compound surname.
