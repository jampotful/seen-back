Angle: spider taxonomy outside the Southwest University / Chinese-Korean Pensoft group. One paper reached in full-enough text: Iranian Dysdera survey. 60 records (log docs/views/evidence/desk-d388.jsonl), check.py 0 problems.

## Filed
- ZooKeys 1146: 43-86 (10.3897/zookeys.1146.97517; Zamani A, Marusik YM, Szűts T, 2023; Dysderidae): 14 new Dysdera species, 20 sex-specific Description sentences 'Eye diameters: AME x, PME y, PLE z.', three records each (AME, PME, PLE), trait eye_diameter_mm because the sentence prints 'Eye diameters'. Species: achaemenes (F), bakhtiari (M), hormuzensis (F), iranica (M, F), isfahanica (M, F), mazeruni (F), persica (M, F), sagartia (M, F), verkana (M), xerxesi (M), damavandica (M), medes (M), tapuria (M, F), genoensis (M, F). Sentences print no unit; Methods say 'All measurements are given in millimetres.' (declared as UNITS NOT PRINTED in every conditions text).

## Weak spots
- All text came through the fetch tool as excerpts of zookeys.pensoft.net/article_preview.php?id=97517 (shown in my own search results). Eye sentences, sub-headings and neighbouring sentences are tool-returned; only D. iranica had its full Description read in consecutive pieces. For six species (achaemenes, bakhtiari, hormuzensis, isfahanica, mazeruni, persica) Diagnosis/Comments were not read for eye wording; conditions say so.
- The page prints only collection acronyms (ZMUT, MMUE, MHNG, SMF), no catalogue codes. Sub-headings are 'Male (Holotype).' / 'Female (Holotype).' only for iranica, persica, sagartia, tapuria, genoensis (males) and mazeruni (female); other males/females print just 'Male.' or 'Female.' and no specimen is tied to the measurement. Females are never labelled paratypes.
- D. tapuria female prints total length 18.1 and carapace 7.77 long (about twice the male's 9.55 and 3.76) with eyes 0.30/0.30/0.34; recorded as printed, possible typo in the paper (UNCHECKED).
- 'where' lists the headings actually returned (Taxonomy, species heading, Description, sub-heading); the species-group and Family/Genus headings between were not all read for later species.
- Licence: fetch tool reported the CC BY 4.0 Creative Commons Attribution License; first part of the sentence verbatim. Crossref vor not reached (UNCHECKED).
- WSC: no WSC page for any of the 14 species reached in search results (UNCHECKED). Owner question: confirm all 14 are accepted names with this paper as original description.
