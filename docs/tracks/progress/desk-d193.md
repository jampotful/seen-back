Method note: WebFetch returns quotes of at most 125 characters, so only whole sentences seen intact in that form were filed; each was re-checked against the page with a yes/no fetch.

Held out:
- Zeugodacus tau (same Naiem sentence): ITIS lists Bactrocera tau as the valid name, so the paper's name could not be matched on a reachable authority.
- Austruca lactea (Joo et al. 2025, Biology 14(8): 962, vibration, 120 and 250 Hz): no reachable authority page for the name (only GBIF, which is barred).
- Aedes aegypti (Eilerts et al. 2018, Insects 9(4): 147): the only short sentence says hexanoic acid sensitivity was maximal at ZT 10-12, but the Results report no significant time-of-day effect for that odorant, so the quote would overstate.
- Carcinus maenas (Barr and Elwood 2024, Animals 14(5): 770): the candidate quote is a clause of a longer sentence.
- Pham et al. 2021 Vietnam bats, Niklaus et al. 2020 rat ERG, Faraone et al. 2020 Ixodes: values sit in sentences or table rows the tool returned only as paraphrase.
- ITIS jsonservice URL was refused (permission not answered); ITIS servlet pages found through search were used instead.
