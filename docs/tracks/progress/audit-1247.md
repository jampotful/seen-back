## Faults found

1. TAXON (all eight lines; not fixable in place). The ASW B. hengshanensis page reads "Redelimited by the naming of Boulenophrys gutu by Kuang, Wei, Luo, Pei, Cao, Zhang, Huang, Pu, and Shi, 2025", and the ASW B. gutu page reads "Formerly confused with Boulenophrys hengshanensis". Kuang et al. 2025, Animals 15(18): 2745, DOI 10.3390/ani15182745 (opened), state: "Thus, we suggest identifying these specimens as B. hengshanensis, while the fifteen specimens (e.g., CIB NYK20221125003) collected in this study and those collected in 2021 by Qian et al. [13] should represent the new species." and "The specimens collected from Yanshou village in Mt. Hengshan in 2021 should be allocated to the new species, B. gutu sp. nov." All eight measured paratypes (CSUFT HS2106xx, collected 4 June 2021) belong to that 2021 series. Under the unit's authority the measured specimens are no longer B. hengshanensis; the records file values of B. gutu specimens under B. hengshanensis. Refiling as B. gutu would rest on a second paper's allocation of a series it does not list voucher by voucher, so I name the lines for dropping rather than refiling. The same fault very probably reaches lines 9 to 22 (every CSUFT paratype is a 2021 specimen); the HNNU holotype and HNNU 1997:6-17 stay B. hengshanensis (Kuang et al. also say the holotype is male, not female as Qian et al. print).
2. HEADING PATH (all eight lines; fixed). `where` omits the printed path. The Results print "Family: Megrophyidae" (so spelt) and "Subfamily: Megophryinae" with no authorities above "Boulenophrys hengshanensis sp. nov."; no Class or Order heading is printed.
3. METHODS WORDING (all eight lines; fixed). `method` gives the definition as "eye diameter (ED (eye diameter))". That is the page reader's abbreviation expansion (it renders every abbreviation, and every voucher code, with its expansion); the printed definition is "eye diameter (ED)".
