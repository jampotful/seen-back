- Capsicum annuum (Riahi et al. 2022, Front Ecol Evol 9: 776827, CC BY): all seven volatiles raised the JA and SA pathway markers PIN2 and AMP1; ASR1 (ABA) unchanged (P = 0.1749); Y-tube nulls recorded in conditions.
- Zea mays (Sakurai et al. 2023, Front Plant Sci 14: 1141338, CC BY): SA up, JA unchanged after 7 days of weed volatiles; field null/partial results (corn borer only under goldenrod) in conditions.
- Arabidopsis thaliana (Kinoshita et al. 2026, Front Plant Sci 17: 1829872, CC BY): receivers respond to insect-damaged neighbours without GLVs (Col-0 has no HPL); no emitter damage gave no signal; P values for the receiver comparison not readable through the fetch tool.
