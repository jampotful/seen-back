## The fork in sense_chemical for plants

Two senses of the trait lead to different counts, so I put the fork on the table.

Sense A is the sense the 14 records state. Their `where` lines accept a candidate only if it reports a plant "sensing or sending airborne signals" (or a chemical signal), the harvest's one added query is "volatile signalling", and the screened reasons set aside "extract chemistry", "a composition analysis for quality" and "food-aroma chemistry of collected leaves". It is also the sense of the canon: canon-3#631 to #676 hold 46 plant rows, and each is a plant that responds to airborne volatiles from neighbours or was tested as a receiver of them.

Sense B adds chemistry of tissue taken from the plant (extracts, distilled oils, headspace analyses of picked material) and chemical treatments of a plant, counted as measurements of the trait. Part of the sweeps' log follows sense B. Of the 28 sense_chemical rows that carry the marker `eye: none (plant)`, I read 7 as a plant responding to a chemical or other cue (harvest-15#5, harvest-19#2, recheck-24-fixed#2, sweep-14#7, sweep-14#8, sweep-33#15, sweep-42#9), 18 as volatiles given off by living flowers or leaves, or a described scent (sweep-13#5, sweep-22#4, sweep-34#4, sweep-34#15, sweep-39#2, sweep-39#3, sweep-39#8, sweep-40#10, sweep-40#11, sweep-40#13, sweep-40#17, sweep-41#5, sweep-41#6, sweep-42#4, sweep-42#5, sweep-42#13, sweep-44#2, sweep-46#10) and 3 as chemistry of tissue: a diethyl ether extract of lily-of-the-valley flowers (sweep-13#11), the hydrodistilled oil of sea fennel (sweep-40#5) and the root-bark extract of Oplopanax horridus (sweep-40#14). The classification is mine, made by reading each value and method field, and it is not a field in the files. The notes on the three tissue rows call them an extract profile (sweep-13#11), a chemical output of the tissue (sweep-40#5) and a statement of what the plant contains (sweep-40#14); none claims that the plant was tested for sensing, and the notes on the last two say that no test of sensing is involved. Five more sense_chemical rows carry a variant of the marker. Two ("none (plant chemistry)": sweep-33#4 and sweep-43#3) log floral volatiles, and their notes say they record what the flower emits and do not show the plant sensing a chemical.
