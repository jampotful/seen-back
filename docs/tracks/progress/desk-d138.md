## Desk disposition after audit 224
Audit 224 found the single record an error, so nothing was merged. Two grounds: the value said bud set "ranged from" Julian day 217 to 247 while the quoted sentence ("For example ...") gives two of five population means and the paper does not tabulate the other three, so it never says these two are the extremes; and all trees grew under the same natural day length at Indian Head in 2007, so the two values show a difference between origins, not a response to light (the day-length reading is the Discussion's, from earlier work). Each value is one population's mean over 15 genotypes and three replicates; a refile would have to say so and would need an owner ruling that origin contrasts under one natural day length count as sense_light_nonvisual. The held Populus tremula lead faces the same objection. Photoperiod is already heavily covered on main; the vein is near exhaustion for open-licence wild-species papers. Betula papyrifera (Tedla et al. 2020, bud-set null under four manipulated photoperiod regimes, CC BY) remains the best lead once a WFO species page surfaces.
