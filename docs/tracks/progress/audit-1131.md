## Per line

Line 4, Pristimantis sinschi (tympanic membrane and annulus absent). Heading path, holotype code, sex ('an adult male'), SVL 25.7, preservation sentence, paratypes with field numbers, Diagnosis (2), Table 3 EW and altitude disclosures all verified. Fault: (1) the conditions quote the Comparisons as saying the species 'lacks a tympanum'; the page prints 'Pristimantis ventrimarmoratus and P. sinschi both lack a tympanum' under the heading 'Comparison' (misquote). Added without counting: holotype field number IWU 367; the Methods list 'horizontal tympanum diameter (TD)' as a measured variable, yet no TD is printed for any type (consistent with absence); the Variation paragraph does not mention the tympanum; the Abstract wording 'mainly characterised by absence of tympanum'.

Line 5, Pristimantis pulchridormientes (TD 31% of ED, 0.31). Heading path, holotype code and sex, paratopotypes and paratype, preservation and Diagnosis (2) verified. Faults: (1) text/measurement contradiction left undisclosed: the holotype measurement list prints 'ED (eye diameter) = 2.7' and 'TD = 0.5', a ratio of about 0.19, and Table 1 gives ED 2.3-2.7 mm for all nine males, so the paper's own measurements contradict 31% (the record cited TD 0.5 but omitted ED 2.7 and called 31% consistent with the Diagnosis' 3x only); (2) false claim about main (desk-d343 eye_diameter_mm row on the same species and DOI). Fixed without counting: 'two cuts marked' (one); 'differentiated from the surrounding skin' paraphrased (printed 'differentiated of surrounding skin'); 'the Methods list no definition of TD' (no tympanum variable is listed at all); 'Duellman and Lehr (2009)' added.

Judgement asked by the desk: the Diagnosis' '3x' (about 0.33) and the 31% are mutually consistent as rounded statements, but both are contradicted by TD 0.5 / ED 2.7 in the same holotype account. The desk skipped Diasporus citrinobapheus and Leptobrachella yachangensis for comparable conflicts; for consistency the owner may prefer to drop line 5. The replacement keeps the printed 0.31 with the conflict stated loudly in conditions (the audit-1127 Noblella precedent), and I flag it for the owner's ruling.
