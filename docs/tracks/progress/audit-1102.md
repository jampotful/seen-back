## Per-record findings

### Line 5, startle in four of six
Quote: "The first response to the AHD exposure was an acoustic startle response in four of six harbour porpoises" | "(HP1-HP3 and HP6, Fig. 1b)." Two abutting pieces, one sentence, MATCH, 23 words. Value says no more.
Verified: pound-net capture, 24 h, handling max 19 min mean 9, DTAG types (GPS HP1, HP3, HP5; ECG HP2, HP4), source level 189 dB, initial RL 98 to 132 dB re 1 µPa rms-fast 125 ms, 0.9–7 km, band-pass 10–46 kHz, 15-min pre-exposure control.
Not carried: the stimulus frequency and pulse pattern (14 kHz, 500 ms, random 0.6–90 s; harmonics 28 and 42 kHz); the per-animal initial RL (Table 1: HP1 130, HP2 131, HP3 132, HP4 98, HP5 99, HP6 106) and the Fig. 1 caption's "HP4 and HP5 received the lowest sound pressure levels ... and did not startle to these pings."; HP5's Discussion level ("96 dB re 1 μPa, rms-fast, HP5") against Table 1's 99 with footnote a (pings among the first 34, low SNR); ranges printed only for the GPS animals; all six juveniles. The record's "capture, handling and tagging may have influenced responses" overstates the authors, who say "it is possible that the recent tagging affected cardiac responses in the two animals" (HP1, HP2; tool summary for the animal IDs) and read the untagged drone trial as against tagging-induced sensitisation of the flight response (tool summary). The "separate untagged animal" control is a drone-observed untagged experiment with 80-dB-reduced control pings (tool summary).
**Error (stimulus frequency, per-animal level-dependent result and sample composition omitted; tagging caveat overstated).** Fixable. `where` "Results, Behavioural responses (startle)": subsection heading not seen; Replacement writes "Results" (uncounted).
