## Limits of this audit

WebSearch returned titles and links with no snippets, so I judged results by title and by the pages I opened. A page whose title does not name the genus, and whose text I could not read, could hold a value; the leads and unread pages above are those cases. The fetch tool reads a page through a summarizer, so a statement that a paper "names no little gull" rests on the text the summarizer received, which can be an excerpt; for the Cantlay PDFs the summarizer read species lists from a figure and from supporting tables. Pages behind bot checks (PubMed Central, PubMed, BioOne) and redirects to hosts that no search of mine returned stayed unread, and I looked for no other route to them. The statements about what main holds come from the gap queue, the canon file, the taxonomy files and both row stores on origin/main, searched by genus, family, order, DOI and author; a source filed under another spelling would escape that search.
