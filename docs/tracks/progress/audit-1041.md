## Verdicts (lines 9 to 16)
All eight lines share the same faults, which are fixable:
- (a) Value: '(mean ± 0.10 as printed …)' does not name the dispersion statistic, although the caption prints SE.
- (b) Journal string: hyphen in place of the printed en dash (23931–23951).
- (c) Missing Methods caveat: sexes and sites were pooled after a Kruskal-Wallis test of no difference ('(p <0.05)' printed so).
- (d) Missing Discussion caveats for the species:
  - M. paululus: other Philippine FmaxE records of 62.0–73.0 kHz, 73 kHz and 65.9 kHz, plus 65.5 ± 4.8 kHz in Borneo for 'M. australis'; DFA correct classification only 80.7%, with call overlap with T. pachypus.
  - H. antricola: often misidentified in the field with H. bicolor; the only Hipposideros not identified unambiguously by DFA; frequencies higher than the Table 2 records (134.6 to 142 kHz).
- (e) Conditions say 'four caves' for each species and name 'Cavinti Cave' as a site. Table 1 marks M. paululus at CC, SC and PC, and H. antricola at PC and KC only. The Methods name the CC site Cathedral Cave in Cavinti, Laguna. 'A modest sample' is unsupported editorial wording and was removed.
- (f) Note: the licence was given as a reader paraphrase without the full printed sentence and context, and the heading path was not stated.
