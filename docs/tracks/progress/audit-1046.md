Faults:
- a (counted, all 8): provenance misstated. The method says 'free-flying recordings in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama' with released bats in clearings. The paper records in 'Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Bolivia, and Brazil over a period of 10 years', includes Venezuelan reference recordings of high-flying individuals 'collected with a shotgun', does not say the Brazil recordings were of free-flying bats, and never says where or how any species was recorded. The method thus drops two countries and a recording mode and implies a per-species origin the paper does not print (for N. mattogrossensis it implies Panama, outside its MDD range of Colombia, Venezuela, Guyana, Brazil, Bolivia). Also omitted: the analyses used 'mean values per call type within recordings' to correct for pseudo-replication.
- b (counted, lines 7-8): conditions state that N. macrotis 'is also printed with a second call type that is not filed here'. False: Table 2 prints only the 'Low/(fm) qcf down' row for this species. The progress file says so correctly; the records contradict it. The unexplained 'Low' label on a single-type species and the Discussion's 'low calling frequencies of Nyctinomops macrotis' remark are now given.
- c (not a fault, noted): the url names the PMC article page while the note says the PMC PDF was read; the replacement points to the PDF copy this audit read.
- d (format, all 8): licence sentence cut after 'in any medium' with no cut mark and without its opening 'Copyright: 2014 Jung et al.'; now given whole. MDD status given as 'valid/living' (page prints 'Living', 'Found in the wild'). Footnote described as 'read as applying to Tables 1-3' although Tables 1 and 2 print 'For abbreviations and statistical results, see footnote in Table 3.'
- e (format, all 8): quotes kept the text-layer '6' for the printed ± (e.g. '32.661.7'); the replacement decodes to ± as main's Tadarida row does, checked digit by digit; heading path and citation line added to the note.

Counts: 8 lines judged, 8 errors (all fixable), 0 dropped, 0 held out. Counted faults 10 (a 8, b 2); format faults 16 (d 8, e 8).
