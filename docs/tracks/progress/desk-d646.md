## Desk disposition after audits (4 November 2026)

Audits 1351, 1352 and 1353 judged all 16 lines (new trait eye_diameter_mm for species that already had a pupil_shape row: Gracixalus liusanjieae 2, Nidirana chongqingensis 3, Protobothrops flavirostris 1, Leptobrachella wumingensis 9, L. yongshunensis 1). One line was dropped and 15 records merged (check.py 0 problems both variants): audit 1351 dropped line 4 (Nidirana chongqingensis, ED 5.3 mm, specimen SWU0001435) because that specimen is not in the printed type material (this matches desk-d603 on main). Every other line was an error by the auditors' count but fixable: page-reader tooltip expansions presented as printed text (for example the row label "ED (eye diameter)" and ED items in tooltip order), caliper-sentence and Methods cuts without marks, a paraphrased Methods sentence, missing headings (Results, Morphology, Variation; Systematics wrongly placed under Results; "Material and methods" not "Materials"), paratype lists paraphrased but labelled as printed, a quote that dropped the printed "(SVL 33.2 mm)", and the Nidirana Table 2 quote that keeps the page's spaces after decimal points ("ED 5. 5"). Audit 1353 reported 4 of 4 in error; 1352 7 of 7; 1351 5 of 5 (16 faults); so every merged line was counted as an error by the auditors, all fixable wording or citation faults, none a value, specimen or repeat error except the one dropped line. Merged under the desk's practice for wording-only faults and disclosed as far above the 5% line.

Disagreement between auditors, decided by the desk: audit 1353 (not its assigned line) said to drop the Leptobrachella wumingensis holotype (line 7, ED 3.2 mm) because the text prints ED/SNT 0.96 while Table 2 gives 3.2/3.4 = 0.94 and the Methods measure to the nearest 0.1 mm; audit 1352 (the assigned auditor) ruled it within rounding and kept it. The desk checked the arithmetic: with 0.1 mm rounding, ED 3.15-3.25 over SNT 3.35-3.45 spans 0.91-0.97, so 0.96 is not a conflict; the holotype is kept. Owner question: the holotype-ratio-conflict precedent (Rhacophorus qiongica, Boulenophrys dalaolingensis) concerned direct value conflicts; whether a ratio inside rounding counts is the owner's to settle. Bibliography note from audit 1353: Leptobrachella yongshunensis is ZSE 101(1): 103-117 as the unit gave it (the desk's audit brief wrongly said 101(4)).

Skipped by the unit (no ED definition, ratio-only, conflicting or garbled tables, or no mm unit): Microhyla ninhthuanensis, M. daklakensis and Micryletta hekouensis define eye length only; Cyrtodactylus zhenkangensis, C. menglianensis and C. arnei define ED as ear diameter or length; C. jayadityai has OD only; Lycodon walli, Bungarus suzhenae and L. irwini have no eye definition; Vietnamophryne abscondita defines EL; Boulenophrys lichun text 0.34 of HDL against Table 2 0.36; Theloderma khoii holotype 4.7 mm in text against 4.6 mm in Table 3 with garbled columns; Phyllodytes gravata ratios only; Amnihyla hastula table without unit; Scutiger kanjiroba ratio only. No unit slip and no classifier denial.
