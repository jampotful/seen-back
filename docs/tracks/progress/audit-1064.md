# audit-1064: desk-d524 lines 1 to 2

Range judged: docs/views/evidence/desk-d524.jsonl lines 1 and 2 (file order). Opus auditor, 3 Nov 2026. 6 WebSearches, 6 WebFetches.

## Brief mismatch (read first)
The launch brief described desk-d524 as 9 Rhinolophus capensis resting-frequency rows from Odendaal, Jacobs & Bishop 2014 (BMC Evol Biol 14: 60). The unit file holds no such rows: it has 10 records, and lines 1 and 2 are Rhinolophus andamanensis from Srinivasulu et al. 2019, PLOS ONE (10.1371/journal.pone.0213562). The Odendaal flags match desk-d522, which is already merged on main (commit 5074e721e, audits 1062 and 1063). I judged the lines actually in the file. Grep of 10.1186/1471-2148-14-60 over docs/ finds only desk-d522 files. The brief's eye-diameter and Vu et al. instructions do not apply to lines 1 and 2.

## Checks (both lines share one source)
- Page reached: journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0213562, returned by my own WebSearch (allowed_domains journals.plos.org). The unfiltered exact-title search returned only bioRxiv, PMC and aggregators, as the unit said.
- Authors as printed: Chelmala Srinivasulu, Aditya Srinivasulu, Bhargavi Srinivasulu, Gareth Jones, so "Srinivasulu C, Srinivasulu A, Srinivasulu B, Jones G" is right. Year 2019, PLoS ONE 14(10): e0213562, DOI as filed. Title matches the printed title.
- Licence: page prints "© 2019 Srinivasulu et al. This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons" + "Attribution License, which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the…" (two reader pieces; the end matches the unit's sentence). Open, accepted.
- Methods > Echolocation call analysis: the D500X sentence matches the unit's method quote piece by piece ("Echolocation calls of R. andamanensis were collected from different locations in Andaman" / "using a Pettersson D500X bat detector (Pettersson Elektronik, Uppsala: frequency range 5–190 kHz" / ", 500 kHz sampling rate)."). "For the analysis, frequency at maximum energy (FMAXE) and duration were taken into consideration." matches. "Calls were recorded from bats at rest and in flight" is printed, so the unit's no-resting-frequency caveat is correct. The same sentence (about 130 characters) goes on to say "some calls would have been subjected to Doppler shift compensation"; the unit did not carry that clause. The rest/flight confound itself was disclosed, so I count this as a fault, not an error, and add the clause in the Replacement.
- Quote (Results > Echolocation calls): "The calls (n = 156) had a mean frequency of maximum energy of 57.65 ± 0.56 (SD) kHz (56.4–58.5 kHz)" + "and a mean duration of 46.63 ± 8.59 ms (30–69 ms)." Whole sentence, en dashes as printed, at most 40 words (check.py 0 problems). Values in both lines match digit for digit and say no more than the quote. The reader confirms that the paper gives no number of bats, so "number of bats not stated" is right.
- Species: MDD taxon/1004657 (opened from my own search) prints Rhinolophus andamanensis, Dobson, 1872, species, Living, Rhinolophidae, Chiroptera. Family, order and class fields are correct.
- Repeats of main: grep over the whole docs/ tree for "Rhinolophus andamanensis" and "pone.0213562" outside desk-d524 finds no hit. "andamanensis" hits are Phelsuma andamanensis (intake-6), Cuapetes andamanensis (canon-4) and an audit-336 MDD remark on R. affinis. No overlap with desk-d330 or d521/d522.
- Unit field reads desk-d524. Grade m, access open, partial false: correct. The unit's note makes no Crossref claim.
