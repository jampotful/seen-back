## Desk disposition after audit 235
Audit 235 found 9 of 13 records in error (69.2%), all fixable, so all 13 were merged with the auditor's corrections. The 8 Streptocephalus mackini notes (and, uncounted, the 4 Triops notes) misattributed an opsin doubt: the paper says the five rhabdomeric opsins reported for notostracans disagree with its absorptance models, offers co-expression of two opsins as an explanation, and says its models agree with the Henze and Oakley (2015) phylogeny; anostracans such as S. mackini express four opsins, matching the four fitted classes. The notes now state that and give the evidence ratios of the alternative models (Triops 1.15 for the GFKRD four-class model and 25.7 for the three-class SSH model; S. mackini males 7.40 and 2581; females 645). All 12 notes also say no confidence limits are given for the fitted peaks and that lmax1 lies within 12 nm of the 350 nm lower edge of the stimulus range, and carry the corrected volume and issue provenance (volume 221 in the PDF header, issue 10 from the publisher URL and the Lund repository record). The Platynereis dumerilii record had left the eye field empty, so a reader would take the 500 nm peak as what the eyes see; the eye field now says the authors place the receptor (Go-opsin1) likely in single cells of the cirri and that the r-opsin1 eye photoreceptors are not integral to the reflex, and the note carries that mutants and cirri-removed worms reduced but did not abolish the reflex. The progress note's line that the authors flag a conflict with the opsin phylogeny should read: a conflict between the five notostracan opsins and their four-class models, with anostracans expressing four. Lesson: when a paper discusses a conflict, attribute it to the taxon the paper attributes it to, and fill the eye field whenever the receptor is not the eye. Held out: Gühmann 2015 (Platynereis larval phototaxis, closed), Ephoron virgo (Egri 2022), box jellyfish (only Tripedalia, already on main), a receptor_classes record from the Lessios abstract.
