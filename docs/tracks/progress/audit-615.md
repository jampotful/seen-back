## Trait decision
Whole Methods of both papers read (fetch summaries plus exact sentences). Neither defines how eye size was measured: Wang prints 'All measurements are given in millimeters (mm).' and an abbreviation sentence (ALE, PME, illustration terms); Zhang prints the same mm sentence and 'ALE = anterior lateral eye, AME = anterior median eye, PME = posterior median eye, L/d = length/diameter' (L/d is the tibia ratio). So the decision rests on the label. Decision: KEEP eye_diameter_mm. The run-in label 'Eye interdistances and diameters' (Zhang: 'Eye inter-distances and diameters') covers two pair-named figures (PME–PME, PME–ALE), which are interdistances, and one bare 'PME' figure, which can only be the 'diameters' the label names; that is a fair reading of 'Eye diameters' for the PME, and it matches audit 433 and the Pholcus lines already on main (desk-d322, d362, d381, d382).
OWNER QUESTION: does a combined 'Eye interdistances and diameters' label satisfy the ruling's 'Eye diameter(s)' clause? desk-d390 kept Micropholcus 'diameter PME x µm' as sensory_anatomy (audit 603), which reads the same kind of label more strictly. If the owner says no, all 33 desk-d393 lines move to sensory_anatomy.
