Abandonment (2.5.3): "If the focal crab evacuated the shelter after meeting the invader inside the shelter, it was counted as an abandonment of the shelter."; probability = "(the counts of abandonment)/(the counts of encounters with the invasive crab in the shelter)."

Experiment Two results (3.2.1, 3.2.2; tool summary with verbatim fragments): Day 1 daytime, "shelter color did not affect the counts of occupation" (F3,20 = 0.769, p = 0.525) and duration likewise (F3,20 = 1.064, p = 0.386); Day 2 daytime counts F3,20 = 8.372, p = 0.001 and duration F3,20 = 5.814, p = 0.005 (blue longer than yellow and black, not clearly different from white); night Day 1 null (F3,20 = 0.641, p = 0.598), night Day 2 counts significant (F3,20 = 3.906, p = 0.024; blue and black over yellow). Experiment One (four-colour choice) daytime blue was favoured on both days (Kruskal–Wallis, e.g. Day 1 counts H = 8.549, p = 0.036, blue over yellow only); nights null.
