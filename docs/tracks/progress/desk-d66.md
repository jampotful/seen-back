## Advice on yield
Shade and irradiance trials were the richest vein late in the run: five of the last eight leads filed (search '<species list> shade levels light intensity growth'). Neem under shade (Floresta e Ambiente 2015) has only one sentence under 125 characters and it is a method line.
Flower-opening time searches ("anthesis" plus a genus list) gave the cleanest short sentence (Passiflora edulis, SciELO). Acta Horticulturae pages give authors, pages and DOI, and Aloe vera came from an ORNL record that prints the full citation. The commonest losses were cultivar-only designs (peppers, flax, cannabis), abstracts whose result sentences all run past 125 characters (Jatropha, Eucalyptus, Citrus), and pages that print no DOI or author list. WebSearch returns titles only, so each lead costs a fetch; ask the page for each sentence with its character count. Crop genera are mostly on main already for light and chemical; the open cells sit in tropical plantation crops (Elaeis, Hevea, Piper, Cocos, Mangifera, Musa), where I found no species-level abstract with a short result sentence.
