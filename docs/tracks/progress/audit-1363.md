## Per line

### Line 1, Hordeum vulgare, sense_magnetic (Shabrangy et al. 2021)

Quote reassembled: "The optimized treatment was found to be 7 mT, which caused a significant increase in seed germination rate," / " accelerating the root emergence, with growth stimulation in the seedling including root and shoot length" / " and fresh biomass of barley (Figures 1A–D)." MATCH, whole sentence, 40 words. Value says no more than the quote. Contrary results confirmed: "In condition A, seeds were exposed to MF on MS medium." then "Prolonged exposure to MF caused a decrease of seed germination rate and growth characteristics, ..." and "Short exposure of seeds in MS medium with the same flux density of MF (7 mT) for 6 h exposure caused growth inhibition of seedlings, which was less prominent than in long exposure (Supplementary Figures 4A–D)." Statistics: "determined by onesample t-test for three biological replicates" and "Statistical analysis was done using the student t-test." (disclosed). Control: "The same conditions were used for control without the power supply." Replicates: "Three replicates were used in the experiment with a minimum of 30 seeds in each treatment." The value is fileable: the germination-rate increase is named significant for condition B and the contrary MS-medium results are carried.

Fault (one record error, fixable): **dose-optimisation provenance misstated.** The sentence right before the quote is "Several MF flux densities and exposure times were examined (Shabrangi et al., 2010, 2011)." The conditions say 7 mT was optimized "out of several flux densities and exposure times that were examined, the others are not listed in the text", which reads as untabulated treatments of this study; the paper cites the choice to the authors' earlier papers and tests no other flux density here. Also corrected without counting: the Figure 1 legend does tie Figures 1A–D to condition B ("which were grown on agar without MS medium", 6 h per day, 5 consecutive days), so "not stated" is replaced; the short-exposure qualifier "less prominent than in long exposure" is added; the note's "logs only the germination endpoint" contradicted the value (which also logs growth) and is fixed.

**Line 1 FIXABLE.**

### Line 2, Triticum aestivum, sense_thermal (Fan et al. 2021)

Quote reassembled: "Compared with control, FT had a similar spike number but significantly lower grain number, 1,000-grain weight," / "and, therefore, yield (minus 17%)." MATCH, 21 words. Design read: "The randomized block design was adopted for field experiments with three replications."; plot "8 m2(2 m × 4 m)"; passive night warming with "a removable plastic membrane", "from around 19:00 p.m. to 07:00 a.m."; "FT increased the mean nighttime temperature of the winter wheat canopy by 1.96◦C."; treatments CK, FT (anthesis to maturity), WT (tillering to jointing), ST (jointing to booting). Table 2 grain yield as the record gives; interaction row "F-C×T 0.036 1.158 0.152 1.697" not significant; Table 1 prints "Yangmai 18". Pooling: the 17% pools two cultivars of one species (my arithmetic on Table 2: 16.7% and 17.4%; summed yields 17.0%); a single-species record across cultivars is not a cross-taxon pooled record, the per-cultivar yields are carried, and the interaction is null. Fileable as one species record. Membrane humidity/wind confound carried.

Fault (one record error, fixable): **replication overstated.** Conditions say "Two seasons (2013 to 2014)" and "whether the same plots were warmed in both seasons is not stated". The paper prints "from 2013 to 2014" and "The seeds were sown on October 26, 2013." — one winter-wheat season; neither "two growing seasons" nor "two years" appears. Corrected to one season; the warming-facility question (one 3 m by 5 m facility described, per-block facilities not stated) replaces the false premise.

**Line 2 FIXABLE.**

### Line 3, Solanum lycopersicum, sense_thermal (Ohtaka et al. 2020)

Quote (Abstract) reassembled: "Under positive DIF (when DT – NT > 0), in contrast to the control temperature (25°C/20°C, DT/NT), high temperature" / " (30°C/25°C) increased stem length and thickness, as well as the number of xylem vessels." MATCH, 33 words; every figure in the value is in the quote. DIF null confirmed: "under negative DIF treatment (25°C/30°C) stem elongation was inhibited, but stem thickness and the number of xylem vessels were not affected."; "the expression of SlPIF4 ... was not affected by DIF treatment (Figure 4H)." Cultivar "Managua RZ"; treatments at "300 μmol photons m–2 s–1 of light for 7 d" after 5–6 d at 23°C/23°C; Figure 1 legend "(n = 7)" for length and diameter, "(n = 5)" for xylems, "*P < 0.01; **P < 0.001 (Student’s t test)". No growth chamber is named and the only replicate statement is for the microarray, so the chamber-confound disclosure is right.

Fault (one record error, fixable): **xylem endpoint scope and measured organs omitted or misstated.** Figure 1E counts "Number of xylems (diameter > 50 µm) in the cross section" of hypocotyls only; the conditions give n = 5 without that scope. The method says "stem, hypocotyl and epicotyl length and diameter measured", but Figure 1 plots only hypocotyl and epicotyl length (B) and diameter (C), and the Methods do not say how they were measured.

**Line 3 FIXABLE.**
