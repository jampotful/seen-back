## Faults (line 1)
1. ERROR, method: 'each snake received each stimulus three times in random order at least one day apart' is wrong. The paper prints 'We performed no more than four tests per day per animal, with at least an hour between the tests' and 'there was a minimum of one day between the days each animal was tested'; tests on the same day were an hour apart.
2. ERROR, omitted significant result and caveat: the multivariate GLMM of both sexes 'showed that males and females differed in terms of INT and RUB' (males more active), and that model 'showed convergence issues, with divergent transitions and R-hat values > 1.1'. Neither was in the record. Also omitted: male GTF highest toward the C25 and C27 ketones 'although these were not significantly different from the other compounds'.
3. Fault, units: 'zero-inflated GLMM ... on counts of chin-rubbing bouts and positive interest scores' blurs the models. Chin-rubbing used zero-inflated Poisson; interest used a binomial model in the Results while the Methods name zero-inflated binomial (the paper conflicts with itself).
4. Fault, licence: the sentence was quoted only to '(CC BY 4.0)' with no cut mark; now whole.
5. Fault, date: 'published 25 July 2025' could not be confirmed. The article page as read prints no published, received or accepted date (only (c) 2025), so the claim is removed. Year 2025 stands.
6. Minor: latency note 'only those tests included in that statistic' is an inference; replaced with the printed n = 128, range 1-593, SD 162.2. Night temperature (about 17 C) added. Reptile Database URL replaced with the one opened from this audit's own search.
