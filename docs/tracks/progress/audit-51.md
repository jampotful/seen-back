## Limits of this audit

WebSearch returned result titles and links with no snippets, so I judged results by title and by the pages I opened. A page whose title does not name the genus, and whose text I could not read, could hold a value; the leads above are those cases. The fetch tool reads a page through a summarizer, so a statement that a long paper "names no Alauda" or "gives no receptor spacing" rests on the text the summarizer received, which can be an excerpt. Pages behind bot checks (PubMed Central, HAL, open.fau.de) or needing JavaScript (eLife), and PDFs that came back as binary data, stayed unread, and I looked for no other route to them. The screening of every record rests on titles and abstract starts, as the records say in `where`, and the species lists that sit in full texts and supplements are out of reach of both the desk's screening and mine. The statements about what main holds come from the gap queue, the canon file, the evidence logs and the audited logs (docs/views/audit/corrected) on origin/main, which I searched by DOI, title and author string; a source cited under a different spelling would escape that search.
