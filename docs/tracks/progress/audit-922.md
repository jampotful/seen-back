# Audit 922: desk-d460, lines 1 to 2

Unit desk-d460 (2 records, spider, trait sensory_anatomy). This audit judges lines 1 and 2 in file order. The line map checks out against the jsonl: line 1 is Kambiwa neotropica PME diameter 50 µm and line 2 is AME diameter 30 µm. Both come from the male ZFMK Ar 24707 in European Journal of Taxonomy 1054: 1–126 (DOI 10.5852/ejt.2026.1054.3276).

Sources were opened only from URLs in this audit's own WebSearch results: the EJT PDF download (article/download/3276/14463/), the EJT article view page (article/view/3276) and WSC spec-data/32640. 2 WebSearches were used. No Crossref call was made.

## TRAIT VERDICT (one reading for this paper): sensory_anatomy stands
The whole Methods was read ('Material examined', 'Taxonomy and morphology', 'Abbreviations' and the molecular, niche-modelling and chromosome subsections). The only eye-size passage is one sentence, joined from two pieces: 'Measurements were done on a dissecting microscope with an ocular grid and are in mm unless otherwise noted; eye measurements are ± 5 µm.' This gives a precision. It defines no eye dimension. The Abbreviations list only names the eyes ('AME = anterior median eye(s),', 'PME = posterior median eye(s).'). The printed label is the per-eye 'diameter PME' / 'diameter AME'. The genus description uses 'PME diameter' / 'AME diameter' the same way. Neither is the heading-style 'Eye diameters' label that the desk's eye_diameter_mm precedent needs. **Decision: sensory_anatomy for both lines.** This matches desk-d429 Caipira mineira, which uses the same Methods wording from the same lab. The unit is µm as printed in the eye sentence, and the Methods sentence's 'unless otherwise noted' allows it. The values are not converted.

## Checks common to both lines
- Quote: 'Distance PME–PME 50 µm; diameter PME 50 µm; distance PME–ALE 20 µm; distance AME–AME 10 µm; diameter AME 30 µm.' is a whole sentence of 18 words. The sentence before it ends 'carapace width 0.50.' Both labels and values match digit for digit.
- Provenance: the measured specimen is tied to the sentence on the page by the heading 'Male (ZFMK Ar 24707)' directly above 'Measurements.'. The same code is printed in the legends of Figs 15 to 17. Fig. 15 reads 'Fig. 15. Kambiwa neotropica (Kraus, 1957); ♂ from Brazil, Rio Grande do Norte, Lajedo do Arapuá, ZFMK Ar 24707.' The code also appears in New records as 'CHNUFPI 9113 [deposited in ZFMK Ar 24707]'. Sex, code and locality are therefore printed, and the lines are kept. The account never calls this male a type. The holotype is SMF RII/6776a (♂) and the paratypes are SMF RII/6776b (1 ♂, 4 ♀♀).
- Licence: the PDF prints 'This work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0)' with no final punctuation. The view page prints 'This work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.' The wording differs but both say CC BY 4.0, so there is no conflicting tag and the licence passes.
- Source: authors 'Huber BA, Meng G, Král J, Ávila Herrera IM, Carvalho LS' match the printed byline order. The title is verbatim. The journal line is 'European Journal of Taxonomy 1054: 1–126' with no separate issue number, 'Published: 14 May 2026'. The DOI is as printed. All confirmed.
- Registry: WSC 32640 lists Kambiwa neotropica (Kraus, 1957) as ACCEPTED, current genus Kambiwa Huber, 2000 (Pholcidae), TYPE SPECIES, original combination Ninetis neotropica Kraus, 1957.
- Repeats: a grep of docs/ outside the unit for 'Kambiwa' and 'ejt.2026.1054' finds only progress notes (desk-d422 and desk-d424, where the PDF was not reached). No evidence is on main.

## Lines 1 and 2, Kambiwa neotropica PME 50 µm / AME 30 µm: ERROR in each (fixable)
- ERROR (heading path): `where` omits the printed 'Results' and 'Taxonomy' headings above 'Class Arachnida Lamarck, 1801'. The Class, Order, Family and Genus headings with authorities are printed and correct. No subfamily or tribe heading and no trailing colons are printed.
- ERROR (registry misstated): the note says WSC 'cites Huber et al. 2026 (EJT 1054) for the holotype'. WSC actually cites 'Huber et al., 2026: 25, figs. 3A, 4A, 7A, 10C, 15A-C, 16A-F, 17A-C, 18A-F (male and female)' and says nothing about the holotype.
- ERROR (conditions misstate the page): (a) it says the New records list is 'the only printed place where I read the code ZFMK Ar 24707', but the code is printed five times, including the Fig. 15 legend that gives the ♂'s locality; (b) it quotes the preceding New records entry without its printed '– Rio Grande do Norte • 1 ♂, 9 ♀♀, 2 juvs;'; (c) it says 'Females' carries only the tibia line, but that paragraph also prints 'In general similar to males but sternum without humps and chelicerae without stridulatory files.' and genitalic characters. None of these changes the filed value.
- Fault (not counted): the Methods sentence was given in pieces. The replacement joins it.
