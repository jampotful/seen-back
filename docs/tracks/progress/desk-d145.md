## Desk disposition after audit 230
Audit 230 found 2 of 4 records in error (50%), both fixable, so all 4 were merged with the auditor's corrections. The two Eigenmannia records carried a unit error of a factor of 1000: the PDF text layer prints the micro sign as "m", so thresholds read mV/cm and conductivity mS/cm, when the figure axes ("µVp–p cm−1"), the Regensburg repository abstract and the paper's own figure of a 3 mV/cm discharge field at a threshold of 1/3000 show µV/cm and 100 µS/cm. The DF record was also misdescribed as food-rewarded, clamped conditioning when these are jamming-avoidance thresholds with unclamped stimuli; method, eye, conditions, value, quote and where were rewritten. The 0.6 µV/cm minimum comes from the 3 conditioned fish of the study's 9. The progress note above this section repeats the mV figures and is wrong; the corrected values are in the evidence file. Uncounted faults applied: curly quotation marks in the Galleria title and the 2-of-20 silent moths at 280 kHz in line 4's conditions. Lesson for the next unit: when a PDF text layer garbles unit symbols, check the figure axes and an independent abstract before logging any number, because a plausible-looking value can be off by 1000. Held out: Putman 2013 (salmon, fisheries correlation), Lankheet 2017 (Gryllus tympanum, page blocked), Ficedula hypoleuca (no DOI), Aedes aegypti ear (tuning shifts only), sturgeon bycatch (PDF returned no text).
