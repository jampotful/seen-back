# desk-d145 progress (animal vein: thin sense_* cells in taxa not yet covered)

Scope: electroreception, insect hearing, magnetoreception; main checked by taxon (evidence, audit/corrected, ALL, searched, wanted) before filing. Eigenmannia and Galleria hearing had no sense_* line on main. No search records, no wanted lines (the vein is sense traits, not harvest genera).

Records: 4 in docs/views/evidence/desk-d145.jsonl, from open full-text PDFs read through Results and Discussion.
- Eigenmannia sense_electric x2 (Kaunzinger and Kramer 1995, J Exp Biol 198(11):2365, 10.1242/jeb.198.11.2365; Crossref via WebFetch): lowest threshold 0.6 mV/cm from one fish; means 1.27 (DF +1 Hz) against 25.07 mV/cm (DF 0), N=6 in that experiment. The paper names the fish only "Eigenmannia sp.", so rank genus, grade g. GBIF lists the genus as accepted (Sternopygidae, Gymnotiformes); the Catalog of Fishes was not opened (search returned only snippets).
- Galleria mellonella sense_hearing x2 (Moir, Jackson and Windmill 2013, Biol Lett 9:20130241, 10.1098/rsbl.2013.0241; Crossref via WebFetch; strathprints repository PDF): best neural 80 kHz at 54.9 dB SPL (n=20); 280 kHz 82.9 (n=18) and 300 kHz 86.8 dB SPL (n=15), 5 of 20 moths silent at 300 kHz and 90 dB SPL; behavioural confirmation lacking per the authors. GBIF accepted, Pyralidae.

Quote method: PDF text layers garble units (cm-1 as "cm21", micro sign, plus-minus); signs were normalised and the note says so; quotes confirmed on two fetches. Two quotes carry one marked ellipsis to meet 40 words.
