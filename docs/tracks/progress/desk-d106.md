## Desk disposition after audit 196

Audit 196 found 3 errors in 3 records; nothing merged and no evidence file is kept. Populus nigra herbivore-induced volatiles (omitted that all four compounds differing between caterpillar species were higher after L. dispar, the mixed-species null, and the feeding-intensity effects), Populus nigra diel (omitted the undamaged-tree result that green leaf volatiles and cyclic monoterpenes were almost exclusively emitted in light periods; the volatiles are leaf volatiles in a chamber, so whether the diel rule covers them is for the owner), Trifolium pratense (the quote states only the control level; "grown alone" is wrong as every pot held six plants; G. pratense did reduce DMNT and interacted with herbivory). Refile all three from the full texts (both BMC pages carry CC BY); Salix fragilis and Solidago altissima are wanted-line candidates.
