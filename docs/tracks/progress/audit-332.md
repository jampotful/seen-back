## Trait-key ruling (line 1)

Main's animal sense_light_nonvisual rows are all extraocular or non-image light sensing: parietal eyes (Anolis, Iguana, Lacertilia, Podarcis, Sceloporus), brain or ganglion photoreceptors (Argiope, Limax, crayfish, Spisula), dermal or skin light sense (Lumbricus, Oxidus, Biston, Octopus arm, Uropygi skin), extraocular newt compass (Taricha), eyeless planarian photophobia (Schmidtea), opsins outside the retina. tools/lit/extract.py defines the key as "light sensing without an image (skin, brain, plant photoreceptors)". Phototaxis by animals with eyes is on main under behaviour_vision (insects-met 20-22 Harmonia axyridis "phototactic response peak at 365 nm"; ground-invertebrates 18 Scorpiones, 55 Opiliones, 102 Chilopoda). The plant light rulings in rules.md do not apply to animals. The paper says, verbatim, that at this stage embryos "have large, well developed eyes", frames the experiment as "hatching phototaxis" testing "a contribution of light or visual cues to hatching orientation", and says the study "does not address to what extent these embryos are capable of perceiving images" (tool summary for the last). No extraocular mechanism is proposed. Ruling: behaviour_vision (a behaviour showing light is seen and used as a directional cue). **Counted (trait key), fixable**, together with the design omissions below. The timing experiment (photoperiod, continuous light and dark) would be a diel or behaviour_vision question if filed later; it is not filed here.
