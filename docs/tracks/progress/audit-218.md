Trait question: the paper never uses "thermonasty" or "thermonastic"; it frames petal angle change as behavioural thermoregulation and petal movement in response to temperature. The growth-chamber experiment (line 2) varies temperature with light schedule held at 14 h, so it supports temperature as the cause; light intensity is not reported. The field result (line 1) is an association: irradiance was not measured, and day of year is the only seasonal covariate ("DOY accounts for changes in solar angle over the season"). Both fit sense_thermal as the desk files it, with the field caveat below.
