## Advice
The fetch helper hides every sentence over 125 characters, so only short-sentence abstracts are usable. Best returns: MDPI Applied Sciences/Plants pages, Texas J Sci, Springer Planta pages. Untried: Najas, Ottelia, Nuphar, Ruppia with a DOI source, Rumex palustris GA/ABA companions, other Sarracenia and Drosera hormone germination papers in small regional journals.

## Desk disposition after audit 168

Audit 168 judged all 5 records correct (0 of 5 errors) and the desk merged all five (Enhalus acoroides, Stratiotes aloides, Rumex palustris, Salicornia fruticosa, Sarracenia alata) and the wanted line (Hydrocharis morsus-ranae, authors empty by design). Salicornia is filed under the accepted genus (POWO and WFO treat Sarcocornia as its synonym). Faults noted and not counted:
- Stratiotes aloides: PMC confirms the authors but not issue 10(4); the source was a core.ac.uk repository copy because the PLOS site was rate-limited.
- Rumex palustris: the Springer page prints DOI 10.1007/s00050160 (the record follows it), while one index prints 10.1007/s004250050160. The seven authors are confirmed on an index record, not the publisher page; the issue comes from a library index. A later pass should check which DOI resolves.
