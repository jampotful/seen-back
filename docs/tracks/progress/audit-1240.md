## Fault found on all eight lines (location)

The reader places Table 1 on pp. 10-11 inside the G. albomaculatus account, right after its 'Differential diagnosis' heading, and the G. woosteri holotype description (p. 14) says 'For morphometric measurements see Table 1.'; ASW cites the woosteri name at p. 13, after the table. Fixable; corrected in every replacement line.
