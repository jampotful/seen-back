2. Where: omits the subsection that holds Table 3 ('Variation.': 'Morphological variation of C. viruela is presented in Table 3.') and the column header label ('Sp. #'); now added.
3. Conditions, type material: labelled 'as printed' but condensed; the holotype's printed sex line was replaced by the Description's opening ('Subadult female, relatively large (51.5 mm SVL)'), the locality was cut to 'Highway 175, 1.9 km ENE of La Esperanza', and paratype fields ('1 adult female', '1 adult male', localities, collectors, dates) were paraphrased or dropped. Now the full Holotype and Paratypes paragraphs as printed, tooltip expansions left out.
4. Conditions, table header: the unit reported 'group labels FEMALE and MALE' over the specimen columns and a Sex row 'Female, Male, …'. Table 3 prints no sex group labels; sex is a row printed in capitals ('Sex | FEMALE | MALE | …'). Corrected, and the SVL row label given without its tooltip.
5. Method: Methods passage paraphrased ('list the entry') and the Table 3 caption abbreviation list marked as not read, so the unit's mm for the paratype columns rested on inference. Now the Methods list sentence quoted with cut marks, the caption's 'ED (diameter of eye);' and 'All measurements in mm.' quoted.
