# desk-d288: eye counts of small and immature stages (springtails, centipedes), 6 records

Branch research/desk-d288 from origin/main. About 40 WebSearch and WebFetch calls. `python3 -I tools/senses/check.py | grep 'd288\.'`: 6 records, 0 problems. Quote lengths 5, 9, 22, 25, 29, 39 words. Crossref was read for all six DOIs except the Insects one (read) and ZooKeys 459 (read); every page URL was returned by a WebSearch first. Whole-docs grep and all desk-d2xx branch evidence files checked for each species and DOI before filing; no repeats.

Filed (docs/views/evidence/desk-d288.jsonl):

2. Trogolaphysa reneaui (Paronellidae), "Eyes 1 + 1 (Figure 5C), sometimes seemingly eyeless.", holotype female, Taxonomy 4(1): 199-236; order left empty.
3. Lithobius (Sinuispineus) sinuispineus, nine ocelli per side, ZooKeys 980.
4. Lithobius (Sinuispineus) minuticornis, 8 to 10 ocelli per side, ZooKeys 980.
5. Lithobius (Ezembius) tetraspinus, 8 to 10 ocelli per side, ZooKeys 741.
6. Lithobius (Monotarsobius) zhangi, 5 to 6 ocelli per side, ZooKeys 459 (39-word sentence; boundaries checked on both sides).

Dropped:
- Caponina alejandroi, bochalema, huila (EJT 813, six eyes): the eye sentence is longer than the tool's 125 characters and was only reachable by asking the tool for pieces, which the brief forbids; left as a retrieval gap (the OA text is at europeanjournaloftaxonomy.eu, article 1735).
- Iandumoema smeagol (ZooKeys 537) and Ochyrocera dorinha (Subterranean Biology 41): already filed by d281 and d282.
- Trogolaphysa synopsis accounts of older species (Eyes absent, Eyes 2 + 2, Eyes 4 + 4 and so on): compiled from earlier descriptions, review-sourced, not filed. T. welchi: description not readable on the page returned.
- Lithobius hualongensis and L. sui (ZooKeys 892): counts given as formulas (1 + 3, 3, 1) that need summing; variation is in a separate section. L. varioporus, L. ternidentatus: on main (d283).
- Olpium caputi (ZooKeys 1192): "4 eyes ..." is a clause ending in a semicolon. Paratemnoides assimilis: clause. Allochthonius (ZooKeys 1139): remarks clause, already held out by d287.
- Leptonetidae (ZooKeys 1218): only "Eye sizes and interdistances" lists with no unit in the line; not filed.
- Larval stemmata: Photinus extensus (ZooKeys 1104), Platycnemis phasmovolans (ZooKeys 1221), Limacodidae (ZooKeys 1261, figure legends only), Dichodontocis (ZooKeys 1218): the eye text is a clause in a long sentence or a figure legend; Oecetis and Lepidostoma larvae (ZooKeys 766): eye statements are position clauses without counts. No larval stemmata count in a short whole sentence was reachable with this search tool.
- Haslundiella iranica (ZooKeys 806): "Compound eyes wider than long ..." is a shape ratio, not a count.

Names: no checklist opened (ChiloBase pages not returned by search this time); each species is described as new in the cited paper. Order for the springtails follows the paper's title (Symphypleona) or is left empty (Paronellidae).
