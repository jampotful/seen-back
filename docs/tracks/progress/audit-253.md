## Checks per paper

Malik et al. 2023 (lines 1 to 3).
- Quotes: line 1 MATCH (Results, whole sentence); line 2 MATCH (Abstract, whole sentence); line 3 MATCH (Abstract, whole sentence). Values contained.
- Confirmed (tool summary): stage 42/43 for pigmentation, petri dishes in two identical chambers in 24 °C or 6 °C incubators; antagonist PF05105679 on pigmentation only; AITC (TRPA1 agonist) "had no effect on skin pigmentation at any of the tested concentrations (Fig. 4a)" and CBA (TRPM4 blocker) "did not affect the lightening induced by cooling at any of the tested concentrations (Fig. 4c)" (both verbatim excerpts), as line 2's note says; pinealectomized tadpoles still lightened.
- **Counted, lines 1 and 2 (scientific meaning, `conditions`).** Both say "the only cold temperature tested for the main response is 6 °C, so no threshold or tuning curve is given here". The paper has a temperature-response relation. In a gradual-cooling run (tadpoles in 100 or 500 ml at 24 °C moved to 6 °C, solution temperature monitored), "the plots of temperature versus pigmentation index show a similar sigmoidal function for the two fixed volume containers" (Results, Fig. 2e insert), and the Discussion gives the midpoint: "the sigmoid curve for the variation in skin pigmentation with temperature (EC50 ~14 °C; Fig. 2e, insert)" (PDF, MATCH as an excerpt). The Discussion compares it with the 13.89 °C half-activation of X. laevis Trpm8 currents, a cited value (ref. 54), not measured here. For a sense_thermal record the response midpoint is the threshold-like figure, so stating that none exists misstates applicability. Counted once per record.
- **Counted, line 3 (scientific meaning, causal interpretation).** The value records only that tadpoles swim less at 6 °C than at 24 °C. In an ectotherm, cold slows movement through its direct effect on muscle and metabolism, so that result alone is not evidence of sensing. The paper's evidence that the slowing is sensed is that the TRPM8 agonist acts at warm temperature: "The locomotor response of tadpoles placed at 24 °C was reduced significantly by WS12 in a dose-dependent manner (Fig. 5f)." (Results, MATCH). The antagonist was not tested on locomotion (tool summary). The record carries neither, so a reader cannot tell a sensory result from a passive thermal one. Also its "locomotor apparatus not described in the text reached" is wrong: single tadpoles in 35 mm dishes of MMR at 24 °C or 6 °C, filmed, total distance swum tracked with Kinovea 0.8.15 (Methods, tool summary); stage 45/46, n = 8, N = 2 as recorded.
- Line 2's value "needs a TRPM8 channel" says more than pharmacology shows; uncounted, reworded below with the line 2 fix.
- Grade m right on all three.
