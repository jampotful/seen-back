# desk-d132 (30 October 2026): plant sense_chemical in parasitic plants and their hosts

Count on main first (grep of docs/views/evidence and docs/views/audit/corrected, any trait): sense_chemical lines exist for Striga hermonthica (Bebawi 1986), Striga asiatica (Hsiao 1981), Orobanche aegyptiaca, ramosa, purpurea, foetida, Cuscuta pentagona (Runyon 2006), Cuscuta campestris (Nagao 2025), Pedicularis olympica and kansuensis, Castilleja affinis (Bashyal 2025), Santalum album, Viscum coloratum. None for Orobanche cumana, Triphysaria, Phtheirospermum, Rhinanthus, Alectra; none for the three papers filed below (no Krupp, Aoki or Irafasha line, none of the DOIs).

Filed: 3 evidence records in docs/views/evidence/desk-d132.jsonl, grade m, partial false, check.py 0 problems.
- Striga hermonthica, Aoki et al. 2022, Front Plant Sci 13: 1077996 (Crossref checked, CC BY): prehaustorium induction by syringic acid 100% at 5 µM, acetosyringone about 80% at 20 µM. Carried: the Figure 1 legend lists SyA at 20 µM, so the 5 µM figure rests on the sentence; no statistical test named; no water-control rate in the text; seedlings germinated with (+)-strigol first.
- Orobanche cumana, Krupp et al. 2021, Front Plant Sci 12: 699068 (Crossref checked, CC BY): germ tubes grew toward purchased costunolide, 76 ± 17% within 2 mm, 48 ± 11% in the outer zone. Carried: GR24 gave no orientation (equal bending); no solvent control reported; whether 48% differs from chance is not stated; exudate/oil-extract sentence is 43 words and pools an extract, not logged.

Carried: negative control percentage not reported, no test statistic in the text, GR24 a comparator, issued year 2023 online versus 2025 print.

## Desk disposition after audit 219
Audit 219 found 1 error in 3. Line 2 (Orobanche cumana, Krupp et al. 2021) was dropped: it omits the Figure 5 null in the same test (10^-7 and 10^-6 M gave nearly even bending, 55 +/- 13% and 56 +/- 12%, against 68 +/- 8% at 10^-5 M), the percentages are shares of oriented germ tubes only (germ tubes with no clear direction are excluded), and GR24 was added to the disc in the tests at 10^-6 M and below. Refile with those corrections (the purchased pure compound is acceptable for the trait). Lines 1 (Striga hermonthica, Aoki 2022) and 3 (Striga hermonthica, Irafasha 2025, printed 7(2) 2025, Crossref online 2023) merged with the auditor's notes. Held-out lead: Triphysaria versicolor (Wang 2020), quote ready, POWO page unreadable.
