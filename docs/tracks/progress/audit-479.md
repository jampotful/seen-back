## Unit-wide rulings

- **Trait key (desk flag; ruling).** Methods print "All measurements are given in millimeters (mm)." and "Leg measurements are shown as: total length (femur, patella, tibia, metatarsus, tarsus)."; nothing defines how eyes were measured. The difference from ZooKeys 1245 (audit 477) is the wording of the data sentence itself: there the sentence reads "Eye sizes and interdistances:", which leaves "size" undefined; here every sentence reads "Eye interdistances and diameters:", and the only single-eye numbers in it (PME, AME) can only be the diameters it names. This is the exact sentence form of Pholcus chaoyang (ZooKeys 1232), which audit 433 moved from sensory_anatomy to eye_diameter_mm and which sits on main under that key. Audits 476/477 (no "diameter" anywhere: sensory_anatomy) and 474/475 (Methods "diameter": eye_diameter_mm) are kept consistent by one rule: eye_diameter_mm where the page itself calls the number a diameter, in Methods or in the data sentence. eye_diameter_mm stands, units mm.
