## Faults (same five in each line, all fixable)

1. conditions misquotes the holotype as 'SAMA R69448 (field no. FN SJR 8623), adult male'; printed 'SAMA R69448 (Field number: FN SJR 8623), adult male'.
2. method quotes the TyD entry as 'TyD (horizontal diameter of tympanum) horizontal diameter of tympanum;', a page-reader expansion with wrong final punctuation; printed 'TyD horizontal diameter of tympanum.'.
3. method gives the Methods measurement passage second-hand ('as returned by the page reader') instead of quoting it; the note does not quote the Methods tympanum or eye passage as the brief requires.
4. Table 1 caption quoted as ending at 'all measurements in mm.' with no cut mark though the caption continues; header cell given as 'Reg.-No' (printed 'Reg.-No.').
5. ASW citation string drops 'Berlin,' ('Zoosyst. Evol., 93: 266'), and the note does not state that no Class, Order or Family heading is printed; paratype lines file the table under 'Description of the holotype' where the type-series citation stands under 'Morphological variation.'.
