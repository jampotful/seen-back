### Line 1, Mecopoda elongata, sense_hearing, travelling-wave velocity 4 to 12 m/s

Quote whole and verbatim on both passes: "In the bushcricket, traveling wave velocity increases from 4 to 12 ms−1 for an increase in frequency from 6 to 48 kHz." MATCH, 22 words; value says no more. Preparation verbatim: "For the preparation, the animals were anesthetized with CO2." "Their wings, mid-legs and hind-legs were clipped off." Coherence criterion, calibrator and n = 6 phase result as recorded.

**Error (calculated vs measured stimulus/quantity; stimulus range misstated; level omitted).** (a) The velocity is calculated, not measured: "Velocity and wavelength of propagation are the most important parameters for traveling wave characterization" "and they can be calculated from the phase response (2)."; the record's method reads as a direct vibrometry value. (b) The method's "tone stimuli 6 to 60 kHz" does not match the Methods list, verbatim "pure sine wave tones in the following steps:" " 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 36, 48 & 60 kHz." (6 kHz absent; the quoted sentence and abstract give 6 kHz). (c) The record says sound level is not stated; the Figure 3 caption gives "(9–48 kHz) at 80 dB SPL.", the Results "within a level range of 30–110 dB SPL", and "we observed a non-linear compression of the velocity response to varying sound pressure levels."; the level and n behind the Figure 4 "Mean traveling wave velocity" are not stated. Sibling result not carried: "The wavelength decreased for the same frequency range from 0.67 mm to 0.27 mm." and "However, the slope is much smaller than that of mammals." Fixable.

### Line 2, Mormyrus rume, sense_electric, echo latency 19 to 25 ms

Quote whole: page "The preferred latency, or 'echo response', ranged from 19 to 25 ms and occurred in response to all electrical playbacks." The tool reported straight single quotes around 'echo response' on the second pass; the record has double quotes (uncounted character fix; tool may normalise). 20 words; value says no more. Fish (n = 8, 98 to 170 mm SL, 5 male, 3 presumed female), seven playbacks, ten successful presentations in random order, at least 10 min inter-trial interval, the 72 ms trend and the locomotor nulls with chi2 values all verified verbatim. Additional design verbatim: "approximately six years of age"; "Water temperature and conductivity were kept constant at 25.2 ± 1.2°C and 100 ± 3 μS cm-1 during all experiments."

**Error (controls and analysis basis omitted).** (a) Besides the seven playbacks ("Playbacks consisted of IDI-sequences that had previously been recorded from freely behaving M. rume"), the design had two electrically silent controls, C1 (moving dummy) and C2 ("No movement was induced during control condition C2."); the record describes only the playbacks. (b) The echo response is a ratio against a modelled expectation: "These latencies were compared to the distribution that would be expected if the IDI-sequences of playback" "and fish were two independent time series." "...by calculating the ratio of observed to expected latencies at the mode of the observed latency distribution." No statistical test of the echo response is described, and the page does not say whether the 19 to 25 ms range spans fish or playbacks (tool summary); the null "No consistent differences in the ratio between observed and expected latencies were found" carries no statistic. Fixable.
