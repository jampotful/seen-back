## Domestic status (Apis mellifera, line 4): ruling

Acceptable; not held out. The no-domestic-breeds rule bars breed-, cultivar- and strain-level records (a named breed, line or cultivar standing in for a wild taxon). Apis mellifera is a wild species whose colonies are managed; the bees here are a species-level record of unknown subspecies taken from "colonies housed on the roof of the University of Konstanz, Germany." (verbatim; no subspecies, line or breeding stock named). Main already holds about 80 species-level Apis mellifera rows from managed colonies (audit/corrected/bee.jsonl 43, methods.jsonl 20, canon-3 7, canon-4 3, desk-d155 4, desk-d183 3 and others), so refusing managed honey bees would contradict main's standing practice. What the record must do, and does not quite do, is state the origin without calling it irrelevant: the note's "Managed colonies are not a domestic breed record here" is right, and the conditions should add "subspecies not stated" (replacement text). The same reasoning covers line 2's wild-derived outbred vole colony (the desk's owner question): a laboratory colony of a wild species founded from wild animals is not a domestic breed, and the record carries its origin.
