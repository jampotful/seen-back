## Desk disposition after audit 193

Audit 193 found 4 errors in 6 records, all dropped: Trema tomentosum (the light test shows germination in both understorey and gap light; only the temperature requirement was gap-only), Dyera costulata (R:FR was significant for stomatal conductance, palisade cell length, stomatal density and internode length, so the "exclusively PFD" claim fails), Cunninghamia lanceolata (omitted that red and 1:1 light increased root collar diameter), Cedrela odorata (the open full text was unread: light test was white, red and darkness at 25 C, and germination percentage did not differ). Merged: both Guazuma ulmifolia records and the Ferraz, Arruda and Van Staden 2013 wanted line. Refile candidates: the four dropped records from full text; Bertholletia result from the CC BY version; Miconia chamissois; wanted lines for Calycophyllum candidissimum, Tecoma stans, Macaranga and the Ochroma GA3 paper.
