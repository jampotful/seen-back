### Lines 3 to 5, Boana marginata (Antunes et al. 2024)

Quotes verified verbatim on the Springer page: line 3 "During the stimuli, there was a decrease in the number of notes per minute (p = 0.048)." (Results); line 4 in two abutting pieces "The number of notes A (Fig. 2a) was less frequent than that of the B notes, and there was no difference" | " between the periods in any of the analyzed parameters (p > 0.05)." (whole sentence, joined correctly); line 5 Abstract in two abutting pieces "However, a 55 dB stimulus—equal to 200 m from the road edge—did not change the call in this species," | " suggesting a minimal distance to implement noise-inflicting infrastructures." (whole sentence, em dashes as printed). Values say no more than the quotes. Line 5: the Results print no 55 dB test (tool summary: "The Results don't mention 55 dB at all"); the record already says so.

Stimulus-level ruling (the desk's weak spot). The records say "the paper does not clearly say the dB was measured at the male". The Methods do say what the level was set to: Section 2.3 "The final calibration of the right level of noise to be received by each frog was performed in the field" | " using a sound-level meter to ensure the level."; Section 2.4 "Reproduction levels were field adjusted using the SLM to reproduce the intensity observed and measured" | " on the original road." The meter position during calibration is not printed. Ruling: **KEEP**. The paper states the calibrated level as the level received by each frog, which is a level at the animal under the precedent ("frequency and level at the animal, or a stated measurement point"), as audit 1096 kept 75 dB at about 1 m where the male sat. The giraffe (audit 1098) gave a level with no reference point at all, which is different. The meter position remains unprinted and the stimulus band at the frog is not given; both are now in conditions so the owner can overrule. The boring alternative reading: "to be received by each frog" is the authors' intent, and with the meter position unprinted the level actually reaching the frog is unknown. If the owner reads it that way, these three lines fall with the giraffe lines.

Errors (counted once per line, lines 3, 4, 5): the conditions misstate the stimulus definition (omit the calibration sentence) and omit the randomised order ("Treatments were randomized, and one of the sequences was presented to each recorded specimen"; six possible orders; the example sequence printed lists B2 and B3 but not B1). Both are fixed in the Replacement.

Uncounted, fixed: site name FLONA-SFP and dates (March to October 2016, about one hour after sunset); analysis (PERMANOVA with individuals as blocks, post hoc t-tests); meter model; the "(per reader summary)" points now read (silences, holding up to four days); line 3 and line 5 now carry the B-call effect sizes (pulses p = 0.019, inter-pulse interval p = 0.003, silence 0.021 s vs 65 dB 0.030 s, t-test p = 0.029); no non-traffic control sound stated. 18 males recorded, nine called in all periods: confirmed.

- Value contradicts the paper's own test (counted). Table 3a "Treatment: Flyovers yes/no | 1 | 0.351 | 0.351 | 2.148 | 0.144", and Results "The flyover treatment did not have a significant effect on the time spent in either group of behaviors (Table 3)." The record files "less time moving" as a result while noting only the assessing conflict.
