1. Quote misprints the voucher cell: "ZSM 293/2010 HT ..." where the cell prints "ZSM 293/2010 (HT)" (likewise "(PT)" in lines 2 and 3); parentheses dropped.
2. Method says the Table 1 header "defines 'ED (horizontal eye diameter)'"; that is the reader's abbreviation tooltip. The printed definition is the Methods "ED, horizontal eye diameter;", which the line never quotes.

5. Note: DOI sourced from bionames rather than the publisher (publisher XML confirms it); "Not on main" omits that the species is on main under other traits (no repeat). Lines 2 and 3 notes cite the 293/2010 voucher as their reader example (copy-over).

Values, specimens, sexes, units, trait, DOI, licence and bibliography are right in all three.
