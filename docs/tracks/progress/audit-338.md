Discussion verbatim: "As our tissue fixation was not optimized for electron microscopy, the substructure of the aardvark tapetal fibrils may be" "an artefact."; "Unfortunately, our material did not allow us to determine whether the domains with their changing fibril orientations" "are contained intracellularly (suggesting a tapetum cellulosum) or whether they are extracellular" "(suggesting a tapetum fibrosum)."; "Franz [5], Matas et al. **Error (authors' limitation on the value's basis omitted).** The value's "features of a choroidal tapetum fibrosum" rests on TEM of collagen-like fibrils, and the authors state that their fixation was not optimised for EM, that the fibril substructure may be an artefact, and that their material could not decide between an intracellular (cellulosum) and extracellular (fibrosum) arrangement. The record omits all of this and its method names only "gross eye inspection and histology", not TEM. The record also gives the prior literature only as "earlier reports had denied a tapetum", though two earlier studies (Walls; Paszta et al.) report one. Fixable. Uncounted: the conditions field repeats line 2's axial-length sentences ("n = 1 eye for the axial length ..."), which do not apply to a tapetum record; zoo names; live-animal eyeshine in Fig 2.

Morphometrics, verbatim: "Density estimates in the central part of the retina from wholemounts and sections give similar results (within 4–10%)" "with the exception of *Lampanyctus parvicauda* for which estimation from sections underestimated (by∼30%) the rod density" "compared to the rod density estimated in wholemounts." **Error (size dependence of the range omitted; specimen source over-stated).** (a) The value is a range of peak densities across three individuals, and the authors attribute intraspecific differences in peak density mainly to the size of the individuals, larger fish having higher densities; the record does not say so, so a reader takes the spread as individual variation at a common size. (c) The paper says nothing about shrinkage; the record should say so, since wholemount density depends on it. Fixable. Uncounted: the section-versus-wholemount comparison is given only as the 30% exception, not the 4-10% agreement it is an exception to; value form [650000, 760000]; OBIS name check.
