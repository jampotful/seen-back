# sweep-94 (Round 3 genus sweep, 15 October 2026)

Batch: 40 genera (37 rodent genera of Muroidea, Sciuridae, Dipodidae, Gliridae, Anomaluridae and Capromyidae; the bird genera Megatriorchis and Aramidopsis; the bovid Pseudoryx). Every `closest` was empty.

Result: no measurement found for any genus or congener, so no evidence records and no wanted lines. Each genus has one search record (two searches, scientific name and common name or family, with the traits' words) in `docs/views/searched/sweep-94.jsonl`, with `chased` set to the DOI of an opened paper whose reference list was checked for the genus (Allen et al. 2020 for Murinae and Octodontidae, Gunter et al. 2024 for hamster, vole and sigmodontine genera, Peichl et al. 2022 for the dormouse Chaetocauda) or to the standard "none" string.

Limits met: PubMed and PMC pages returned a browser check, so Hawk Eyes I (Accipitridae visual fields) and the hamster cone paper (Visual Neuroscience, 1999) were seen by title only and are not logged. Peichl 2022 (Glis, Graphiurus) is already in the evidence log. Octodontidae relatives with measurements (Octodon, Spalacopus) were read as abstracts only.
