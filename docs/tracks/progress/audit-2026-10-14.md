# Precision audit, 14 October 2026 (sweeps 5 to 27)

The precision test of `docs/views/research/THOROUGH.md`, run by Opus on the evidence written by sweeps 5 to 27 (`docs/views/evidence/sweep-*.jsonl`, sweep 23 skipped because it has no evidence; 312 records, 218 with a `partial` field and 207 marked partial).

## Sample

Seed 20261014 (`random.Random`). Records were assigned to a view by order: insects (Lepidoptera, Hymenoptera, Hemiptera, Coleoptera, Orthoptera, Odonata, Mantodea, Diptera), birds (the avian orders), mammals (Rodentia, Perissodactyla), herps (Testudines, Crocodilia/Crocodylia, Anura, Squamata), plants (everything else). Four records were drawn per view without replacement, each record weighted 3 if `partial` was true and 1 otherwise. An id is `<file>:<line number>`. 18 of the 20 drawn records are partial.

Each record's source URL was opened (WebFetch) and checked for the value, unit, taxon, grade and quote. One record page (Marmota) lacked the abstract and its PDF link was blocked; the same abstract was read from the repository's Dublin Core API record, so every record was verifiable.

## Error rate

2 of 20 verifiable records are wrong or have a quote not found: **10%**, above the 5% threshold. By the THOROUGH rule, the batches they came from are checked again:

- **sweep-21** (Platanthera bifolia, quote not found)
- **sweep-25** (Meliphagidae, wrong grade)

## Patterns

1. **Quotes rebuilt by a summariser.** Nearly every partial record was read "through a summariser", and quotes come back paraphrased or stitched together. Four more records (sweep-7:15, sweep-8:7, sweep-13:3, sweep-17:13) carry quotes that are not verbatim, although their values are correct. In sweep-21:2 the rewording changed the fact: "collected during the night" became "night-time scent", and that became a `diel` record. `tools/views/quotecheck.py` cannot catch this for web sources, because there is no PDF to check against.
2. **Grades on inferred or group-level evidence.** sweep-25:5 gives `m` to a family-rank claim inferred from opsin sequences in other species, while sweep-13:10 grades the same kind of evidence `g`. Sweeps need one rule for opsin-sequence predictions and family-rank records.
3. Minor: a pooled value is attached to one species (sweep-11:17, 436.7 nm), and one value omits the breed (sweep-19:8, 3.566).

Suggested recheck order: sweep-21 and sweep-25 in full, then every `diel` record and every record whose method says "inferred", "predicted" or "opsin", across all sweeps.
