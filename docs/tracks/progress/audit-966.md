## What the paper prints (checked)
- Methods (unlabelled opening block, p. 267, list pp. 267-268): 'OD = orbit diameter taken horizontally between the external eyeball borders;' 'All measurements were recorded in mm with a Mitutoyo digital caliper to the nearest of 0.1 mm.' 'Measurements were usually made on the right side of the specimens unless otherwise stated.' No ED or other eye-diameter entry is printed. The only other subheadings are Molecular data and Statistical analysis.
- Table 3 (p. 286, continued p. 287): the header, Status, SEX, HL and OD rows match the unit digit for digit. OD reads '4.0 | 4.0 | 4.0 | 4.0 | 4.0'. The holotype ratio OD/HL 0.26 (4.0/15.4) agrees, and so does OE/OD 1.1 (OE 4.4). The column-to-specimen mapping, the sexes (M M F F M) and the type status are correct.
- Type material: the holotype is ♂ adult MZMU 3795; the paratypes are 2 ♂♂ MZMU 3791 and 3792, and 2 ♀♀ MZMU 3793 and 3794.
- Authors (PDF and article page, same order): Bohra SC, Deb A, Thongni G, Bhattacharjee R, Biakzuala L, Lalremsanga HT, Swargiary P, Roy R. The unit is correct. The title is verbatim and the DOI is correct. The running heads and the citation print 1048: 265–303. The first-page header misprints 256–303. No issue number is printed.
- Licence: the PDF prints 'This work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0)' with no full stop. The article page prints 'This work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.' The licence is accepted.
- Reptile Database: the page prints 'Cyrtodactylus jayadityai BOHRA, DEB, THONGNI, BHATTACHARJEE, LALREMSANGA, SWARGIARY & ROY, 2026' and Higher Taxa 'Gekkonidae, Gekkota, Sauria, Squamata (lizards: geckos)'. The holotype is MZMU 3795. The citation reads 'European Journal of Taxonomy, 1048(1), 265–303'. No status word is printed and the synonym field holds only the original name. The species is valid, in Gekkonidae.
- Main: a grep of docs/ and data/ for jayadityai, ejt.2026.1048 and article 3255 finds nothing outside this unit. These lines are not a repeat.
- Quote 'OD 4.0 4.0 4.0 4.0 4.0' is the whole row, 6 words by check.py count. It is accepted.

## Faults (each applies to all five lines)
1. TRAIT: the lines are filed as eye_diameter_mm, but the Methods define only 'orbit diameter' and print no eye-diameter definition. They move to sensory_anatomy under desk precedent (C. nebulicola desk-d476, E. jhuma desk-d353). This is a counted error. One point stays open for the desk: the holotype Head sentence once calls OD 'the eye diameter' ('OE/OD 1.1'). If the desk accepts that as a definition, eye_diameter_mm 4.0 stands.
2. EYE SIDE: the unit says 'side not stated', but the Methods print 'Measurements were usually made on the right side of the specimens unless otherwise stated.'
3. HEADING PATH: the unit omits 'Results', 'Phylum Chordata Haeckel, 1874' and the printed authorities (Class Reptilia Laurenti, 1768; Order Squamata Oppel, 1811; Family Gekkonidae Gray, 1825; Genus Cyrtodactylus Gray, 1827). It also omits 'Referred material (based on genetic data only)'.
4. UNMARKED CUTS in the Type material quotes: the holotype block drops '(Figs 4–5, 8A).' and the paratype block drops 'INDIA • ', the '•' separator and '(Figs 7, 8B–D).'
5. NOTE: the note says 'new trait: eye_diameter_mm (same key as main)', which is self-contradictory and is dropped with the trait move.
Not counted: the article-page licence was paraphrased, the Table 3 placement was imprecise (the table splits across pp. 286/287 around the Variation and Sexual dimorphism sections), and the 256–303 first-page misprint was not recorded.
