## Advice
link.springer.com (Journal of Ecology and Environment), koreascience.kr / koreascience.or.kr PDFs (Korean J. Plant Res.), and Crossref work records open and print whole sentences. Ask for "sentence by sentence with character counts" and confirm the quote with a yes/no request. Flower Research Journal full text is on ijfs.org, which did not open; KoreaScholar copies have extraction typos.
