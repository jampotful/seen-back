# desk-d131 (30 October 2026): plant sense_thermal in wild species

Count on main before filing (grep of docs/views/evidence and docs/views/audit/corrected): sense_thermal plant lines exist for Arabidopsis, Asparagus, Hypericum, Lupinus, Medicago, Mirabilis, Oryza, Pisum, Raphanus, Solanum, Sorghum, Zea, Helleborus, Phalaris, Glycine, Oxalis corniculata (Park 2026), Oxalis triangularis (You 2023), Symplocarpus foetidus (Ito 2004), Pyrus and Malus (Heide and Prestrud 2005), Dipsacus fullonum, Heliotropium amplexicaule. None for Tulipa, Crocus, Adonis, Papaver, Ranunculus, Gentiana, Philodendron, Arum or Nelumbo. Argentina anserina is on main only under sense_light_nonvisual (a shade paper), not thermal.

Potentilla anserina), sense_thermal, grade m, from Koski, Heiling and Apland 2024, Ecology Letters 27(9): e14524, DOI 10.1111/ele.14524 (Crossref read: three authors in this order, volume 27, issue 9, licence CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; the NSF Public Access Repository copy states the same licence).
- Field: high-elevation flowers reduced petal angle by 10 degrees per 5 C rise in air temperature (Figure S3 sentence, whole, 17 words). The low-elevation figure (< 4 degrees), the null for opening/closing timing (Table S4), n = 562 flowers and the supplementary-table gap are in conditions.

## Held out
- Gentianopsis paludosa, Hou et al. 2022, Ecology and Evolution 12(1): e8490, DOI 10.1002/ece3.8490, CC BY 4.0 (Europe PMC XML). Abstract sentence "The results showed that high/low temperature contributed more to the flower closure than low RH, while illumination intensity had no significant effect on it." read twice; Results: 14 C or 25 C applied at 10:00 closed flowers 2 h early (full closure 12:00), 22.5 C had no effect, N = 10 per treatment, no P values for temperature, authors conclude an endogenous rhythm. Held only for the name: POWO page (369390-1) returned a client error three times, and three WFO searches surfaced only genus and unrelated species pages. Strong refile lead once both pages can be read. (Gentianella-style synonymy in WFO not checked.)
- Nelumbo nucifera, Li et al. 2022, Int J Mol Sci 23: 11950 (CC BY 4.0): receptacle 33.8 C at ambient 22.3 C, one ambient value, text states nothing on heat output versus ambient; fails the Nelumbo rule. Sun et al. 2021 was dropped in audit 212.
- Magnolia denudata, Wang et al. 2022, Front Plant Sci 13: 955665 (CC BY): thermogenic = gynoecium 4 to 6 C above air, but only a sample-classification line in Methods; Liu et al. 2017 Front Plant Sci 8: 430 measures no temperature.
