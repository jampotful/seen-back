# Audit 1280: desk-d625 lines 1 to 8 (file order)

Assigned lines 1-8: Ingerophrynus chrysolophus, TD (Herpetozoa 38: 271-297, 10.3897/herpetozoa.38.e165173). These are holotype ZMMU A-8030 and paratypes A-8031 to A-8037. Line 9 (A-8039) belongs to another auditor.

Pages opened, all from my own WebSearch results: herpetozoa.pensoft.net/article_preview.php?id=165173 (two full reads, then 'Site under maintenance' on six retries spread over about 20 minutes; not worked around) and the ASW page Amphibia/Anura/Bufonidae/Ingerophrynus/Ingerophrynus-chrysolophus. I used no Crossref, no proxy and no other copy.

## What I confirmed myself
- Values and mapping: correct on all 8 lines. On the publisher page, Table 1 has THREE column blocks: (1) ZMMU ID, Locality, Status, Sex, SVL, HL, HW, HD, S-N, IND, N-E, ED, IOD, ELW, ETD; (2) ZMMU ID, Sex, ESD, TD, CRL1, PGW, PGL, FLL, FHL, FAW, HAL, F1, F2, F3, F4, IMC; (3) ZMMU ID, Sex, OMC, FEL, TIL, TFL, FOL, T1-T5, IMT, OMT, CRL2. I read every cell of rows A-8030 to A-8037 in block 2 and all of them match the quotes. Sex cells match: F, except A-8034 which is M. The block-2 row order on the page is A-8035, A-8030, A-8036, A-8037, A-8031, A-8033, A-8032, A-8034, A-8039. The page reader expands the ID cell to 'ZMMU (Zoological Museum of Moscow University) A-8030', which is an abbreviation tooltip, so the desk's 'ZMMU A-8030' is right.
- Methods: the reader paraphrase shows a Mitutoyo digital caliper, to 0.01 mm and rounded to 0.1 mm, right side, characters after Grismer (2007), with entries ED horizontal eye diameter, ETD and TD horizontal diameter of tympanum. An eye diameter is therefore defined, so these tympanum lines stay under sensory_anatomy. The unit is mm, from the caption '(in mm)'. Table 1 is cited under Materials and methods > Morphological examination. No Class, Order or Family heading is printed.
- Byline (13 authors, in order), title, Herpetozoa 38: 271-297 and DOI all agree with the page.
- ASW: the name is valid in Bufonidae; it prints 'Holotype: ZMMU A-8030, by original designation.' Its Comment ('Formerly confused with Ingerophrynus parvus (its sister species) ...') records no later split or redelimitation, so the type series stands.
- Repeats: a git grep of the species and the DOI over docs/ on origin/main finds only the nine desk-d484 eye_diameter_mm rows, a desk-d610 progress mention, and a desk-d486 note that names this DOI only as a page not used. There is no tympanum row, so these lines are not repeats.

## Faults (same on each of lines 1-8)
- (a) Wrong `where` path: 'Results > Morphological differentiation'. Table 1 sits under Materials and methods > Morphological examination (my read agrees with audited main row d484).
- (b) The note's heading path 'Systematics > Ingerophrynus chrysolophus sp. nov.' leaves out 'Results' and the printed authority in the species heading.
- (c) `conditions` says 'Table 1 is printed in two column blocks'. There are three.
- (d) The caption is quoted with a Latin 'c'. The page prints a Cyrillic 'с' ('Ingerophrynus сhrysolophus'), and the reader and main row d484 agree on this.
- (e) `method` leaves out the printed instrument, precision and side ('Mitutoyo digital caliper ... nearest 0.01 mm ... rounded to 0.1 mm', 'right side'), which the d623/d624 shape carries.
- (f) 'Type material as printed' is a paraphrase. It drops the coordinates and elevation and adds 'same locality and date', which is not printed. The Methods entries are given in reader form ('TD (horizontal diameter of tympanum)'), while main d484 shows the printed form puts the abbreviation after the phrase ('horizontal eye diameter (ED);').

Counts: 8 lines judged; 0 value, mapping, species or repeat errors; 48 text faults (6 per line), all corrected below.

But the Pensoft page went into maintenance before I could read verbatim the whole licence sentence, the exact printed Methods TD item, and the species-account heading. My reader gave the licence (CC BY 4.0) only in paraphrase. By the brief, I may not take these from second-hand text. The Replacement lines below fix faults (a) to (f).
