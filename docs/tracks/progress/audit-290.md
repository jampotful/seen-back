**Trait key (counted, fixable).** The paper's 15 to 40 deg is a spatial resolution stated as the period of one cycle of a sine grating, which is exactly what acuity_cpd keys, in reciprocal form: 1/40 = 0.025 and 1/15 = 0.067 cycles/degree. behaviour_vision is the key for behavioural responses that are not one of the measured optical quantities; filing an acuity under it hides the value from every acuity_cpd reader and leaves main's 0.11 (a modelled acute-zone value) as the species' only acuity. The desk's reason ("the paper gives an angle in degrees, not cycles/degree") does not hold, because the paper itself defines the degree figure as a grating period, so the conversion is exact arithmetic, not an assumption. If the owner prefers a behaviour_vision row, its value should be the behavioural threshold (a 10 deg target detected, 6.7 deg not) with a Results sentence as its quote, not the modelled resolution.
