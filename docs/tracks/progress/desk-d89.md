## Advice
Page summaries refuse sentences over 125 characters, so ask for "sentence by sentence with character counts"; revistas.udec.cl, revistabosque.org, revistas.unal.edu.co and lillo.org.ar open and print abstracts. Most abstracts in this vein carry their result in sentences over 125 characters; Crossref journal queries (api.crossref.org/journals/<ISSN>/works?query=...) list candidates fast.
