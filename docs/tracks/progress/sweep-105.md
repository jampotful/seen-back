# sweep-105 progress
Batch: sweep-105-batch.json (40 genera, all run in order). Two searches per genus (scientific name, then common name or family words) were read at title level, with the evidence logs grepped by script for each genus and its species.
Tool limits that shaped the result: WebFetch returns a model summary rather than page text, quotes were capped near 125 characters, and PubMed, PMC, the Royal Society, Wiley and several Frontiers pages were refused. No measurement could be copied verbatim from an opened page, so no evidence record was written.
Chase papers: Bostrom et al. 2016 for the ten bird genera, Newman and D'Angelo 2024 for the three artiodactyl genera, Briscoe and Chittka 2001 for the insect genera (Hemiptera, Lepidoptera, Coleoptera), Ogawa et al. 2023 for the two Hymenoptera, Liebau et al. 2015 for the four frog genera. For the three snake genera (Simoes et al. 2016) and the two lacertid genera (Perez i de Lanuza and Font 2014) the fetched copy had no reference list, so only the text was checked; those two groups deserve a second citation chase.
Wanted lines added: Hart et al. 2016 (emu pigments) and Martin and Coetzee 2004 (hornbill visual fields). Authors, year and DOI came from the OpenAlex records of those papers.
Leads not turned into wanted lines because no DOI could be confirmed from an opened page: Hogg et al. 2011 and Stokkan et al. 2013 (reindeer), Schiviz et al. 2008 (artiodactyl cone topography, includes Muntiacus reevesi), Tyler et al. 2025 (reindeer photoreception, Functional Ecology).
