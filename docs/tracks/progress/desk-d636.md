## Desk disposition after audits (4 November 2026)

Six fresh Opus audits (1324 to 1329) covered all 12 reptile pupil_shape lines. Every pupil value was confirmed and no line repeats main. Three licence sentences the unit had read only in part (Achalinus hunanensis, Calamaria berezowskii, Gloydius lateralis) were read whole by the auditors. Audit 1328 moved the light-dependence of the Trimeresurus loong pupil out of the value (now "vertically elliptical") into conditions as a qualitative observation with no defined light level. Audit 1325 recorded that the Calamaria berezowskii paper prints no lectotype designation sentence (Table 4 uses a "Syntype" column) and thanks others for photographs of type specimens, so the pupil may have been read from photographs; the record stays taxon-clean. Audit 1324 recorded that Achalinus hunanensis QHU 2024030 is non-type material. The remaining faults were wording: tooltip text inside quotes, unmarked cuts, type material copied from the Reptile Database or missing paratypes, and heading paths. All 12 merged from the auditors' replacement text.
