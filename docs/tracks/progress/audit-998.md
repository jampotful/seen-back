## Findings

**LINES 1-6 MOVE TO sensory_anatomy.** The ZooKeys XML (opened from my own search) prints under Materials and methods only 'eye length (distance between anterior and posterior corners of eyelid);' after 'The following morphological characteristics were recorded', and 'Measurements were taken with a digital caliper (Electronic Digital Caliper) to the nearest 0.1 mm.' The words 'eye diameter' and ED do not appear in the paper. Under the desk rule this is sensory_anatomy. The unit's claim that eye-length records on main are filed as eye_diameter_mm is false: main files EL-only skink rows as sensory_anatomy (Sphenomorphus tamchucensis, desk-d476). The Occidozyga nishikawai precedent (desk-d480) stayed eye_diameter_mm only because that paper prints 'EL: Eye diameter'; it does not apply here.

- Values: the EL row, re-read cell by cell, is 'EL | 3.11 | 2.9 | 2.72 | 3.2 | 3.24 | 2.7 | 2.7–3.2' under columns 'IEBR R.6329 | IEBR R.6330 | HUS. 2024.01 | HUS. 2024.02 | HUS. 2024.04 | HUS. 2024.03 | Min-Max', with sex row Female, Male, Female x4 and the sub-row 'Measurements (in mm)'. All six values and sexes match. The page really does print Min-Max 2.7–3.2 against the 3.24 cell. This is an inconsistency inside the paper and the unit handled it correctly.
- Type material: the paratype paragraph prints 'HUS.2024.03 (PAT 134) (Fig. 7); and adult female, HUS.2024.04 (PAT. 164), adult female, bear the same data as the holotype.' The unit quoted it without '; and adult female,'. That is not as printed, and no sex follows HUS.2024.03 in the text (Table 2 gives Female).
- Licence: the unit gave a fragment 'per the page reader'. The whole sentence has now been read from the XML.
- Heading path: Results > Taxonomic account > Scincella truongi sp. nov. No Class, Order or Family heading and no Taxon classification block is printed. The unit did not state this.
- Authority: I opened the Reptile Database page from a search result. It lists 'Scincella truongi PHAM, ZIEGLER, PHAM, HOANG, NGO & LE, 2025' in Scincidae, with no status word. Authors, title, ZooKeys 1226: 319-337 and the DOI match the XML.
- Main: the species, DOI, IEBR R.6329 and HUS.2024 return no evidence line. Only desk-d311 and desk-d489 progress list it as not filed ('ED undefined / absent'), and nothing on main repeats it.

**Line 7.** The XML gives title, DOI, ZooKeys 1011: 85–100, authors Jia-Guang Xiao, Zheng-Sen Yu, Na Song, Tian-Xiang Gao and the CC BY 4.0 licence. The Methods print only 'All measurements were made with dial calipers and dividers to the nearest 0.1 mm.' and no eye definition. Table 3 is headed 'Morphometric measurements (mm) and counts'. The row 'Eye diameter (ED) | 8.1 | 6.8–11.6' sits in the mm block above the 'As % of SL' sub-header. The second ED row (17.9) sits under 'As % of HL'. So the mm value is confirmed and eye_diameter_mm stands under the quoted-row rule.

The unit said 'no taxonomy headings printed'. That is wrong: 'Family Sillaginidae Richardson, 1846' and 'Sillago Cuvier, 1817' are printed under Results > Taxonomic account. The unit's paratype locality list is unverified and has been replaced with the printed paratype opening.

**HELD OUT:** no Catalog of Fishes species page was reached. The unit had only getref 38073. My two catalogue searches returned no species page for the name, and the one species page returned (spid=26794) is Ostorhinchus nigrofasciatus. Drop line 7 until a species page is opened.

No Crossref claim appears in the notes.
