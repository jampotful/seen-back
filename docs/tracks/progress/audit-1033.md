## Decision on the contradiction: the unit's resolution holds
- Eye vs eye-mouth: the lectotype and DLR195 descriptions print 'EyeD: 0.7 mm, larger than eye-mouth distance 1.0 mm; EyeD 8.8% of HL.' and 'EyeD: 0.7 mm, larger than eye-mouth distance 1.1 mm; EyeD 8.4% of HL.' The numbers, the Diagnosis ('eye diameter less than eye-mouth distance') and six comparison sentences make EyeD the smaller. The printed percentages settle EyeD itself: with Table 4 HL 8.0 and 8.3 mm, 0.7 mm gives 8.75% and 8.43% (printed 8.8% and 8.4%); a swapped EyeD of 1.0 or 1.1 would give 12.5% or 13.3%. 'larger than' is a wording slip; the Table 4 EyeD cells stand.
- Sexes: Variations ('Adult female GXNU DLR194 …', '… adult male GXNU DLR195 …') is outvoted by Table 4 (♂/♀), the Sampling sentence and the DLR195 description ('Adult female, collected from Mt. Gongga, Moxi Town, Luding County'). The lines' sexes are right.

- Table 4 (page reader, exact characters): Voucher No. 'ZISP 8823 | – | GXNU DLR194 | GXNU DLR195 | GXNU 20221215002'; Type of specimen 'Lectotype | Syntype | – | – | –'; Sex '♂ | – | ♂ | ♀ | ♀'; EyeD (mm) '0.7 | – | 0.6 | 0.7 | –'. Each value matches its column.

- Licence CC BY 4.0 sentence as the lines give it; DOI, 9 authors in printed order, title, 100(3): 897-911, 2024 correct.
- Reptile Database: own species page, 'Calamaria berezowskii GÜNTHER, 1896', Higher Taxa starting 'Colubridae, Calamariinae', 'Types Lectotype: ZISP 8823, adult male', Liang et al. 2024 listed with this DOI; no explicit 'valid'; its pavimentata comment is historical. Family Colubridae correct.
