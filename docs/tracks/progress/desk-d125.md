# desk-d125 (30 October 2026): plant sense_vibration

Count on main before filing: no plant sense_vibration line (all existing lines are spiders, a turtle review and Araneae).

Filed: 6 evidence records in docs/views/evidence/desk-d125.jsonl, no search records, no wanted lines. All grade m, partial true (abstract quotes; full text not opened for Brito, Results of Veits read through a page reader).
- Arabidopsis thaliana, Body et al. 2019, Front Plant Sci 10: 810 (CC BY 4.0): 24 h caterpillar-chewing playback lowered most phytohormones (record 1) and changed beta-ionone and methyl salicylate emission (record 2).
- Arabidopsis thaliana, Ghosh et al. 2017, Front Plant Sci 8: 100 (CC BY on page; Crossref lists no licence): 7 of 13 mechanosensitive ion-channel genes differentially expressed after 500 Hz, 80 dB sound.
- Oenothera drummondii, Veits et al. 2019, Ecol Lett 22(9): 1483-1492 (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, Konstanz repository PDF): sweeter nectar within 3 min of bee-sound playback (record 4); flowers vibrate to pollinator sound but not to higher frequency (record 5).
- Solanum rostratum, Brito et al. 2020, R Soc Open Sci 7(9): 201010 (CC BY 4.0): flower transmits imposed vibration with dominant 300 Hz conserved; mechanical transmission, not a sensory threshold.
WFO and POWO accepted for all three names (POWO read for Oenothera and Solanum rostratum: "This species is accepted"; for Arabidopsis the page reader did not return the status words, same basis as desk-d124).
