## Errors (same on every line)
1. Conditions say 'seven paratypes'. Printed: 'Six specimens (two adult females, four adult males): GIB1972746, GIB1972748–50, adult males, ...'. Seven is the whole type series (holotype plus six).
2. Conditions quote the holotype as 'GIB1972747, adult male, from Lengshui River, Mt. Foding Nature Reserve, Shiqian County, Guizhou'. Printed: 'Holotype • GIB1972747, adult male, from Lengshui River of Mt. Foding Nature Reserve, Shiqian County, Guizhou Province, China'. The quote changes 'of' to a comma and cuts 'Province, China' with no cut mark.

## Faults (same on every line)
3. Licence given as a paraphrase ('article open access under the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0)') instead of the printed sentence. Licence itself is CC BY 4.0 on the publisher's own page, so access 'open' stands.
