## Errors found
1. All eight lines (sample composition omitted). The Statistical analysis says Kruskal-Wallis tests 'compared echolocation call parameters between sexes and across the four study areas' and prints 'No significant difference in the call parameters was observed for all species analyzed (p <0.05), thus, data were pooled in subsequent analyses.' The filed means therefore pool the sexes (per-species sex counts not given) and, for H. lekaguli, both caves where it was recorded (CC and SC). The desk conditions name the sites but say nothing about pooling across sexes and sites, or the odd printed p wording. Error under the design-confound rule (sample composition); fixed in conditions.
2. Lines 9-12 (H. lekaguli; contrary result and taxon caveat omitted). Discussion: 'Previous records of this species from southeastern Asian countries such as Thailand and Peninsular Malaysia showed FmaxE ranging from 45–50 kHz which is relatively higher than those recorded in this study (37 kHz).' and 'Further study is needed to assess the taxonomy of the Philippine population of H. lekaguli.' The authors leave the identity of the measured population open; the desk records omit both. Fixed in conditions.
3. Lines 13-16 (H. diadema; taxon caveat omitted). Discussion: 'For H. diadema, there appears to be not much variation in its FmaxE when Philippine populations are considered (Table 2).', followed by the Esselstyn et al. (2012) point that three species may lie within H. diadema across its global range and that FmaxE outside the Philippines varies. Omitted by the desk; fixed in conditions (counted together with error 1, one error per line).

Faults fixed, not counted: en dash in journal pages (8); `where` heading row (8); MDD authority wording (8); `method` said 'FmaxE from the power spectrum' on D, Fini and Fter records, whose parameters the Methods measure from spectrograms (6); `method` omitted FFT size and window (8); value said 'mean ± x as printed' without the SE label the caption and Statistical analysis give (8); note carried a T. melanopogon spacing remark that concerns another species' lines (8, removed).
