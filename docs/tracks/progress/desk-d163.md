# desk-d163 (plant vein: sense_light_nonvisual, set-light and light quality; 30 October 2026)

Branch research/desk-d163. Eight records in docs/views/evidence/desk-d163.jsonl from three papers, all Frontiers in Plant Science (CC BY copyright line read on the PDF; Crossref lists CC BY 4.0).

## Records
- Solanum lycopersicum (cv. Zhuanhong No. 2), Liu X et al. 2023, 10.3389/fpls.2023.1164768: far-red up / blue down on seedling height and hypocotyl length; UV-A (6 umol) null for height, hypocotyl, stem diameter.
- Pinus yunnanensis, Liu Y et al. 2025, 10.3389/fpls.2025.1619386: root NSC falls with shade; two null results (3-year-old root biomass; 1-year-old stem and root biomass at the deepest shade levels).
- Glycine max (cv. Nandou 12), Yang F et al. 2018, 10.3389/fpls.2018.00056: height and stem mass fraction (lower PAR, lower R/Fr), biomass (added far-red), AQY null.

Name authority: each of the three species opened on POWO (accepted) and WFO (accepted) with its own page; ids are in each record's note.

## Held out
- Cucumis sativus (Liu X et al. 2023, same paper as tomato): POWO accepted, but no WFO species page could be surfaced (genus page only).
- Euonymus japonicus (Wei Y et al. 2023, 10.3389/fpls.2023.1263795, night light; Rosa hybrida in the paper is a hybrid): POWO accepted, WFO page not surfaced by five searches.
- Nicotiana tabacum cv. Y85 (Liu Q et al. 2023, 10.3389/fpls.2022.1042981, light 12 h against constant dark, about 90% against 65%): POWO accepted, WFO species page not surfaced.
- Lonicera insularis (Lee J et al. 2024, 10.3389/fpls.2024.1484114, germination only in light): neither authority surfaced for the species.
- Schiedea kaalae and S. hookeri (Powers et al. 2020, 10.3389/fpls.2020.01116, evening scent peaks): POWO accepts S. kaalae, WFO lists it as Unplaced.
- Glycine max, Hitz T et al. 2019, Agronomy 9(8): 428 (light intensity and R:FR, three cultivars): authorities fine, but the publisher page shows no CC statement in its text (only a metadata rights link to CC BY 3.0 while Crossref says 4.0).
- Medicago sativa diel (Chen M, Zuo X-A 2018, BMC Ecology 18: 28, Table 1: anthesis begins 07:30-08:00): authorities fine (WFO wfo-0000213468), but the Methods could not be opened (redirects were refused) and no licence text was seen.
- Glycine max, Lei Y et al. 2025, 10.3389/fpls.2025.1648079 (midday high light durations): treatment days and plant age not stated.
- Glycine max shade (10.3389/fpls.2022.1015414): no licence line on the page read.
- Cunninghamia lanceolata (10.3389/fpls.2026.1765282): already on main (desk-d110).
- Carpinus betulus (10.3389/fpls.2022.1055984) and Lonicera pages already on main/wanted; PMC pages returned a reCAPTCHA page and were left alone.

## Limits
WFO species pages could be surfaced for only a minority of names by WebSearch; that, not the supply of papers, set the yield. Crossref was reachable through WebFetch only (curl is refused by the proxy). Leaf-volatile day/night timing, UV-damage endpoints and sense_touch/sense_thermal were not touched.
