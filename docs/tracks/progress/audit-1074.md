## Verdicts

- Line 1 (Water, 55.2 ± 2.4): ERROR (fixable). The Table 1 footnote attributes the Water rows to an earlier paper: 'nine bats trawling mealworms floating on the water surface in the flight cage (Weinbeer and Kalko, 2007)', and the Methods say the air trials 'were arbitrarily interspersed by experiments with mealworms floating on the water surface (Weinbeer and Kalko, 2007)'. The record omits this provenance, so the value reads as new 2013 data and a later record from the 2007 paper would duplicate it undetected. The method field also applies the per-individual statistics sentence to these data after dropping its qualifier 'In the first set of experiments with prey suspended in air,' (and '(Hurlbert, 1984)' without a cut mark); the sentence names the air experiments only.
