# desk-d198 progress

Animal sensory-trait unit (plant screening in the shared brief is superseded). 4 records filed in docs/views/evidence/desk-d198.jsonl, from 3 open-access papers.

Records
- Gallus gallus, sense_hearing: ABR range of best sensitivity 750 to 2000 Hz in 1 to 2 day old chicks (Ordiway et al. 2024, Neuroscience Insights, CC BY-NC 4.0).
- Callosobruchus maculatus, sense_thermal, two records from one paper (Małek and Czarnoleski 2021, Insects 12(4): 310): females preferred warmer habitats than males; no effect of mating status or gift size (null).
- Tripterygion delaisi, behaviour_vision: weak fluorescence effect, retroreflective versus nonretroreflective models (van der Schoot and Michiels 2025, Front Ecol Evol 13: 1728992).

Checked and not filed
- Delphinus capensis (Houser et al. 2022): the IUCN CSG page on the SMM list says D. capensis is dropped as a recognised species; not filed. Other Houser species are already in desk-d171; Kogia breviceps sentences came back cut.
- Lucilia sericata (Galindo-Cuervo and Molina 2023): already in sweep-34.
- Athetis dissimilis EAG (Front Physiol 2025, 1619418): whole sentences intact but the copy read had no licence line, stimulus concentration or n; held out.
- Oophaga pumilio / Walkowski 2024: only pooled-group threshold sentences, one over 40 words; held out.
- Culex quinquefasciatus and tarsalis thermopreference (Reinhold 2022): value sentences came back only in pieces; held out.
- Slipper lobsters (Hayasaka 2022): spectral-peak sentence about 75 words, copy shows no licence; held out.
- Epinephelus awoara ABR, Cx. territans, Asiophrida: already on main or earlier units. Pitviper (JEB 244478): no open licence statement. Grasshopper mouse vocalisation (Behaviour 2020): no licence statement.
