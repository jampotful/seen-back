## Eye-size definition (both lines may stay under eye_diameter_mm)
- Line 3: the Methods list opens with 'Following variables were measured according to Batista et al. (2016a) and Duellman and Lehr (2009):' and holds the entry 'ED (Eye Diameter) Eye Diameter as length of exposed eye'. That is a printed definition. The other Methods sentence is 'Measurements were taken to the nearest 0.1 mm, using a stereomicroscope and precision digital calipers.' Table 2 prints 'all values are in mm and separated by sex'. The unit is mm.
- Line 4: the Methods sentence 'We took 14 measurements that reflect size and proportional shape of the salamanders: […] diameter of eye opening (ED).' defines ED. The next sentence is 'Measurements were taken to the nearest 0.1 mm using vernier calipers.' The holotype paragraph prints '(in mm)'. The unit is mm.

Faults (fixed in the Replacement, not counted):
- Line 3: the `where` drops 'Results' from the printed path. The path as printed is Results > Taxonomic account > Pristimantis gretathunbergae sp. nov. > Measurements of the holotype, with no Class, Order or Family headings. The paratypes 'Seven males, three females.' were not stated. The `method` cited the section heading for units, and the heading does not print mm, so I replaced it with the Table 2 caption phrase. Family: ASW serves the species page under both a Craugastoridae and a Strabomantidae URL path. I kept Craugastoridae to match the recent Pristimantis rows on main (desk-d594, desk-d602). Main is mixed on this point (audit 468 and audit 1129 filed Strabomantidae), and the desk owner should rule on it once for the whole genus.
- Line 4: the `where` 'Species account > … > Measurements (holotype)' prints headings that are not on the page. The treatment sits directly under Results, and 'Measurements' is its own heading (a tp:treatment-sec title in the XML). The paragraph begins '(in mm), limb interval and tooth counts of the holotype.' The quote therefore joined that heading onto a sentence and ran across two sentences. I reduced it to the opening of the single list sentence, cut marked. The paratypes 'Thirteen specimens, all from Oaxaca, Mexico.' ('7 females', '6 males') were not stated.

Evidence errors: 1 of 2 (line 3). Search records: none in my lines. Drops: none, since both lines are fixable.
