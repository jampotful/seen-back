## What I read myself
- Byline (Jörn Köhler, Pablo J. Venegas, Ernesto Castillo-Urbina, Frank Glaw, César Aguilar-Puntriano, Miguel Vences), journal string 7(2): 195-209, year 2023 and DOI all match the unit.

The TD entry is tympanum diameter (measured horizontally) and the ED entry is horizontal eye diameter (the reader shows tooltip expansions, one misspelt 'orizontal'). An eye diameter is defined, so these tympanum lines correctly stay under sensory_anatomy. The unit is mm and no side is stated.
- Headings: Results > Molecular relationships, Morphology, Bioacoustics; 'Chimerella mira sp. nov.' sits at the same heading level as Bioacoustics. It has no authority, and the reader showed no Taxon classification labels and no Class, Order or Family heading. This agrees with the unit's 'Results > Chimerella mira sp. nov.' path. Main row d601 instead gives 'Results > Bioacoustics > ...' and a Taxon classification box. I did not see that box, and main is not edited here.
- Measurement sentences: the holotype paragraph opens 'Measurements (in mm). SVL ... TD ...' and the Variation paragraph opens 'Measurements (in mm) of the paratype are as follows: ...'. TD 0.7 (holotype) and TD 0.6 (paratype) match the quotes.
- Type material: the holotype is 'MUSM ... 40278 (FGZC 6233), adult male (Fig. 3a–c), ...' collected 6 November 2019. The paratype is '... 40264 (FGZC 6215), adult male (Fig. 3), same data as holotype, but collected on 5 November 2019.' The museum-acronym expansion is a reader tooltip.
- ASW: 'Chimerella mira Köhler, Venegas, Castillo-Urbina, Glaw, Aguilar-Puntriano, and Vences, 2023', Evol. Syst., 7: 199, 'Holotype: MUSM 40278, by original designation.' The Comment points to the original publication and to the 2025 Salamandra map and tree, and records no split, synonymy or redelimitation. The name is valid in Centrolenidae (Centroleninae), so the type series stands.
- Repeats: git grep of the species and DOI over docs/ on origin/main finds only the two desk-d601 eye_diameter_mm rows (ED 2.7, ED 2.4), plus mentions in desk-d601/desk-d610 progress and desk-queue. There is no tympanum row, so neither line repeats main. No Crossref claim appears in the notes.
