## Faults (same three in each line; all fixable)

1. **Heading path.** `where` gives 'Results > Taxonomic account > Gekko tesselatus sp. nov. > Variation'. The page prints, under Results (h1) > Taxonomic account (h2), the lines 'Family Gekkonidae Oppel, 1811', 'Genus Gekko Laurenti, 1768', 'Subgenus Japonigekko Wood, Guo, Travers, Su, Olson, Bauer, Grismer, Siler, Moyle, Andersen & Brown, 2020', then the species heading 'Gekko tesselatus Xu, Ma, Cai, Qi, Matsukoji, Poyarkov, Sun, Jiang & Peng, sp. nov.' and the h3 'Variation.'. The record omits the Family, Genus and Subgenus lines and shortens the species heading (the same omission audits 961-962 corrected on d479). The page reader calls the three taxon lines bold text rather than headings; the replacement includes them and says so. No Class or Order line is printed.
2. **Tooltip text quoted as print.** The note correctly says the label's appended definition is tooltip text, but `method` then quotes "'EL (maximum ear opening diameter)'" and says Table 4 "defines" it, and `conditions` quotes the holotype description as "'EL (maximum ear opening diameter) 1.12 mm'". The printed Methods item is 'EL = maximum ear opening diameter;'; the parenthesis is the article_preview tooltip, which the reader shows on every label (Methods item renders as 'EL (maximum ear opening diameter) = maximum ear opening diameter;'). The replacement quotes the printed item and gives the holotype value as EL 1.12 mm without the tooltip.
3. **Method misstatement.** `method` says the Deli sentence is printed "without the reader naming them"; the page names them (the list after the colon begins 'SVL (snout–vent length, ...'). The replacement says so and gives the whole Mitutoyo sentence.

Also tightened, not counted: conditions now quote each specimen's own type-list entry; the holotype type-list entry is given as a paraphrase because I did not re-read its exact characters.
