## Faults (lines 14, 15, 16, 18, 19: five each; line 17: the same five plus a repeat)
1. Note misstates main: it says the desk-d366 rows hold no C. multisyllaba row. Main holds two (ED/SUL 0.126-0.143; holotype TyD/ED 0.42, a tympanum ratio under sensory_anatomy), and the TyD/ED row's conditions cite 'Table 2 gives the holotype TyD 0.8 mm'.
2. Method text is second-hand: 'Materials and methods' is printed 'Material and methods'; the TyD entry is printed 'TyD horizontal diameter of tympanum.' (last list entry, full stop), not the page reader's tooltip form with a semicolon; 'as returned by the page reader' paraphrase replaced by the printed Methods sentence. The note did not quote the Methods tympanum passage.
3. Heading path omits the printed subheading: Table 2 is cited under 'Colour of the holotype in preservative' by the bold run-in 'Morphological variation in the paratypes.' (paratypes) and under 'Description of the holotype' (holotype). No Class, Order or Family heading is printed.
4. Type series not as printed: field numbers (FN SJR 2070, 2286, 2325; 2323 & 2324) omitted, and the exception 'SAMA R70324 collected on 19-10-2001' dropped.
5. Inference in note: 'probably 84337' for the column printed 'ZMB 8433'; removed (column still not filed).
