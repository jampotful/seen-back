## Line 1: Prionailurus javanensis, 'strictly nocturnal' (Silmi et al. 2021): PASS WITH CORRECTIONS

Confirmed on the Frontiers PDF and full-text page: the Abstract sentence word for word ("The cats are strictly nocturnal ... at night."), whole sentence, no cut, value is the paper's own category; licence sentence "This is an open-access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY)." verbatim on the PDF; printed citation line Silmi M, Putra K, Amran A, Huda M, Fanani AF, Galdikas BM, Anggara S P and Traeholt C (2021), Front. Environ. Sci. 9:651939; DOI; title verbatim; Discussion sentence on no daytime activity verbatim. MDD 1006013: Prionailurus javanensis (A. G. Desmarest, 1816), living, wild, "split from P. bengalensis", countries Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei, Philippines. The paper uses P. bengalensis throughout, a different species name than MDD.

Fileability: yes. The cats are free-living animals captured, collared and released in place; a plantation population is a wild population in a human-modified habitat, and the conditions must say so, with the capture design (rat-baited box traps, sedation, collaring) and the pooled one-estate sample. No diurnal activity is reported, so there is no omitted null.

Faults (3):
1. Note misreads the authority: it says MDD "lists the Sabah, Borneo population under it". The MDD text names no Borneo or Kalimantan population; Sabah appears only in a photo caption, and the study site is Central Kalimantan, not Sabah. Fixed: the note now rests the filing on the split and the Indonesian range and states the name difference.
2. Conditions omit the capture design: Tomahawk steel-mesh box traps baited with live rats, sedation, release after 3-4 h, trapping July 2014 to June 2017. Fixed.
3. Conditions "2031 GPS locations" reads as GPS collars; the cats carried 45 g VHF collars and locations were recorded with a handheld Garmin GPS. Fixed; method also gains the random daytime locations (06:00 to 18:00 h) that ground the "none active in daytime" claim. The estate names (Abstract: United Plantations/PT SSS; Study Site: Lada Estate) are added.

## Line 2: Bos javanicus, 'cathemeral all year round' (Rahman et al. 2019): PASS WITH CORRECTIONS (2 counted errors)

Confirmed: Abstract sentence word for word on the article page and the PDF, whole sentence, no cut; category "cathemeral" is the paper's wording; byline on the PDF header (six authors, printed order) matches; header "Vol. 26 No. 2, April 2019 68-80", DOI:10.4308/hjb.26.2.68; title verbatim; 108 cameras, 134 sites, 46,116 trap days, 817 and 644 records; Rayleigh results (z=3.13, p=0.101; z=5.43, p=0.455) and "suggesting that this species may be cathemeral". The Rayleigh null (no deviation from uniform) supports the cathemeral claim and does not contradict it. MDD 1006263: Bos javanicus d'Alton, 1823, living, wild (domestic form included under the species; these are wild park animals).

Licence: the article page prints "HAYATI J Biosci is an open access journal and the article's license is CC-BY-NC." as a whole sentence, phrased for the article; the PDF prints only "Copyright ©2019 Institut Pertanian Bogor". CC BY-NC is on the rules' accepted list (held out: CC BY-NC-ND, CC BY-NC-SA, third-party and accepted-manuscript copies), the record's url is that article page, and the quoted Abstract sentence is on it. Acceptable; not held out.

Errors (2, both fixed):
1. Omitted significant result: Results 3.4 prints that banteng "tended to be more active at dusk than dawn (p=0.012)" with mean activity time 16:42±18:48 circular stdev, and Results 3.3 that it was most active in early morning and late evening. Omitted significant results are errors under the rules. Added to conditions with the non-significant day/night encounter tests (χ2=2.43, p=0.119; χ2=3.38, p=0.227).
2. Conditions say "Baiting is not mentioned in the text read"; Methods print "We did not bait the camera". Fixed.
