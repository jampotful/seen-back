Animals: adult male A. sagrei, wild-caught in Florida; 10 in the first experiment, 10 in the second of which two died and were discarded, so 10 and 8 as the note says. The two experiments "occurred at different times with different lizards and were analyzed separately": high light 30.7 and low light 0.41 umol m-2 s-1, giving 1.22 and 1.18 cycles/degree (0.82 and 0.85 degrees per cycle); the abstract's 1.21 has no derivation on the page. Threshold: the visual angle at which positive responses reached half the maximum positive response rate, by linear interpolation; the value is "based on average responses across time". Stimulus centre 21.0 cm from the eye, 30 degrees from the straight-out monocular gaze. Limitations: patterns carried both chromatic and luminance contrast and "this probably determined the threshold" (luminance); in the Appendix the response fell with days in captivity in low light only and "had a small impact" on the threshold; the authors say lower responsiveness in low light could be light level or cohort. The record's eye, conditions and note carry all of this correctly except two small points listed below.
