## Records
- Simon et al. 2019, PeerJ 7: e8148, DOI 10.7717/peerj.8148 (Crossref CC BY 4.0): Astyanax mexicanus Pachon cave morph, pinealectomized and control fish both more active in light (Fig. 6B sentence, three fetches of peerj.com). P = 0.005 and 0.008 sit in the next sentence, so they are in conditions with N and t values, not in the quote. Confound kept: juvenile cavefish eyes are mostly degenerated, not absent; the paper's pineal null is stated.
- Wang et al. 2019, J Comp Physiol A 205(6): 847-854, DOI 10.1007/s00359-019-01372-y (Crossref and page CC BY 4.0; Springer and PMC copies): Trachemys scripta elegans (subspecies ACCEPTED in gbif-names.tsv, key 6157026). Two records: female ABR thresholds lower than male from 0.2 to 1.1 kHz except 0.9 kHz (p = 0.053), about 3 dB difference, no tympanum-size difference; and the Discussion hearing range 0.2-0.9 kHz with best frequency 0.6 kHz (40 dB SPL females, 45 dB SPL males). The 0.9 kHz upper bound against significance to 1.1 kHz is flagged in conditions. Anaesthesia and body masses came from a single tool read and are marked so.
- Ruser et al. 2025, Biology Open 14(8): bio062059, DOI 10.1242/bio.062059 (Crossref CC BY 4.0; PMC12403521): Halichoerus grypus underwater audiogram, best sensitivity 4 kHz (43-44 dB re 1 uPa), and low-frequency thresholds below the standard phocid audiogram. n = 2 captive males, concrete pool, noise close to the critical ratio at some frequencies, authors ask for confirmation, inter-seal difference 6.0-13.3 dB below 4 kHz. Name accepted in MDD (taxon 1005913) per audit-249; mammaldiversity.org was not reachable now. Not a repeat: main holds only the in-air evoked-potential audiogram (PLoS ONE 2014) for the species.
- Caorsi et al. 2019, Sci Rep 9: 19456, DOI 10.1038/s41598-019-55639-0 (Crossref CC BY 4.0): Alytes obstetricans (ACCEPTED in gbif-names.tsv, key 2426612). Call-rate reduction under traffic and wind-turbine substrate vibration (15 and 17.5 calls/min), call duration and dominant frequency unchanged (null in the value). 8 of 26 males avoided and were excluded; no vibration amplitude and no airborne-sound control found in the passages read, so stated as a limit.
