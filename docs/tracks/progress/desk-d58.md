# desk-d58 progress (lead reading, fresh set)

Started 29 October 2026. Branch research/desk-d58. WebSearch used: about 93 of 100. Leads came from the plant gap queue (world species with all three open cells, skipping species named in the d27 to d57 notes) and from wanted-merged.csv rows whose title names a world species.
