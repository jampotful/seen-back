Heading path: Results > Taxonomy > Pristimantis ruidus (Lynch, 1979) > Variation. ("Morphometric variation is presented in Table 1."). No Class, Order or Family heading is printed; only the keyword list "Taxon classification" Animalia, Anura, Strabomantidae, without authorities. The desk did not say so (fault, fixed in the note).

Specimens as printed: "Newly-collected material." "Two females: DHMECN 19106 (field series: JCS-2081) and MUTPL 1613 (field series: JCS-2084)." Neither is a type. Remarks: "According to Lynch (1979), AMNH 17597 (SVL = 29.0 mm) is not a mature female, having moderate convolutions and small eggs. However, our specimen DHMECN 19106 (SVL = 30.0 mm) is an adult female because it has developed ovaries." The desk quoted the second sentence from "our specimen", dropping "However," (starts mid-sentence; fault, fixed).

Line 14: MUTPL 1613 (SVL 20.1 mm) is "adult" only in the Table 1 State cell; the text gives no maturity basis for it, and the desk's line 14 cites the DHMECN 19106 ovaries sentence as if it supported MUTPL 1613, while the paper's own Remarks treat a 29.0 mm female as immature. Sample composition misstated: ERROR, fixable; the replacement says adulthood rests on the State cell alone.
