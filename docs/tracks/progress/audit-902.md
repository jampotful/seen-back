# Audit 902: desk-d453 lines 33 to 36 (Pholcus yuncheng)

## Checked and correct
- Quotes verbatim on the publisher page. Male: 'Eye interdistances and diameters: PME–PME 0.25, PME 0.18, PME–ALE 0.07, AME–AME 0.06, AME 0.11.' Female: '... PME–PME 0.22, PME 0.16, PME–ALE 0.06, AME–AME 0.06, AME 0.10.' Each value is the number after its own eye label (PME 0.18 / AME 0.11 male; PME 0.16 / AME 0.10 female). Only PME and AME diameters are printed (no ALE/PLE diameter).
- Label is 'Eye interdistances and diameters' as the unit says, so eye_diameter_mm stays under the precedent.
- Methods (heading 'Materials and methods', read whole): no sentence defines an eye measurement; the abbreviation list defines ALE, AME, PME; 'Specimens were examined and measured with a Leica M205 C stereomicroscope.'; 'All measurements are given in millimetres (mm).' Unit mm confirmed.
- Licence sentence verbatim (CC BY 4.0). Authors, title, 100(1): 199-221, year 2024, DOI as printed in the citation line. No Class or Order heading printed.
- Type line as printed; female run-in label 'Female (paratype)' prints no code.
- WSC 62839: Pholcus yuncheng Yang & Yao, 2024, accepted; family from reference (Pholcidae).
- Not on main: git grep origin/main docs/ for 'yuncheng' and '116759' finds nothing.

## Faults (shared by all four lines)
1. where: top heading printed is 'Taxonomic accounts', not 'Taxonomy'.
2. where/conditions: the species-group heading IS printed: 'Pholcus yichengicus species group' heads P. luonan, luoyang, lushan, shangluo, weinan and yuncheng (P. jiaozuo alone is under 'Pholcus taishan species group'). The unit said it was not returned and omitted it.
3. where/conditions: species heading printed 'Pholcus yuncheng Yang & Yao, sp. nov.'; the unit dropped the authority.
4. method/conditions: Methods heading is 'Materials and methods', not 'Material and methods'.
5. Lines 33-34: run-in label is 'Male (holotype).', not 'Male holotype'.
6. Lines 35-36: the 'sentence before' quoted in conditions ends at a semicolon; the printed sentence runs on to '… tibia I L/d: 54.' (unmarked cut).
7. Type line quoted without marking that it continues after the trailing comma.

## Verdicts
- Line 33: error (heading path, label, Methods heading). Fixable.
- Line 34: error (same). Fixable.
- Line 35: error (heading path, Methods heading, unmarked cut in conditions). Fixable.
- Line 36: error (same). Fixable.
