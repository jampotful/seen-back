## The fork on line 9

Two questions decide whether line 9 enters the count, and the desk has answered the first.

The first is how a light-trap catch is filed. Main files a choice test of ultraviolet against white light in Lycorma delicatula under spectral_range_nm at grade m and marked partial (sweep-13#1), and it files a field result in which light traps beat coloured sticky traps with no wavelength compared under behaviour_vision (Metcalfa pruinosa, sweep-38#3). Row 201 is the weaker kind of that case, because the two sources caught equal numbers at the 14 paired traps of the national network and at Nagytétény. Point 9 of desk-screen.md, which the desk added at 09:08 UTC on 19 October (05:08 as git prints it, UTC-4) while this audit was under way, settles it. ## Verdict

**Harvest-37 passes the desk's 5% rule, with little room.** The 35 drawn records hold 1 error, 2.9% (0.07% to 14.9%). The sample of 20 holds the same error and sits at 5.0%, which is not under 5%, so the extension decided the outcome, as the protocol intends. A second error among the 35 would have made 5.7%. The interval is wide because 35 records cannot separate a true rate of 1% from one of 10%: a sample of 20 shows no error 36% of the time at a true rate of 5% and 12% of the time at a true rate of 10%. If the desk files only preferences under spectral_range_nm and books line 9 as a lead, the unit holds 0 errors in 35 (0.0% to 10.0%), and the verdict is still a pass. Pooled with the 19 animal audits on main, the rate is 5 in 512, 0.98% (0.32% to 2.3%), and with the six audits of my brief it is 2 in 177, 1.1% (0.14% to 4.0%). The one error is a row of a table that neither the unit's searches nor my three Euclidia searches returned, in a compilation that the desk added to the canon file after the unit was written. The unit is clean on form: the queries of all 51 records match the harvest, the `chased` reason of all 35 drawn records is supported, and none of the 232 `screened` entries of the drawn records reports the measurement.
