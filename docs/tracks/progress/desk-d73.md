## Advice
CSIRO publish pages print whole abstracts and the ExportCitation page prints authors, but the summariser often omits authors on the main page; ask for the ExportCitation or CrossrefCites page. Most Australian germination and flowering abstracts pool species, so only a species-specific sentence (Trachyandra) or a one-species title (Pimelea ciliata) gave a record.
