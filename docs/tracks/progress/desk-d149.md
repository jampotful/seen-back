- Ophiomastix wendtii behaviour_vision x2 (Sumner-Rooney, Kirwan, Luter and Ullrich-Luter 2021, J Exp Biol 224(11):jeb236653, 10.1242/jeb.236653): orientation to vertical bars of 50 deg and above (20 to 40 deg bars gave disorientation; 50% CI of the threshold 41 to 63 deg; the authors say the threshold may reflect detection or motivation); disorientation at Michelson contrast 0.1 and 0.05 with a 60 deg bar (orientation at 0.95). A ray-tracing calculation on a 3D reconstruction from the head of a single E. rowelli, not a behavioural test; the paper lists histological shrinkage and geometrical-optics-only as limits. GBIF ACCEPTED (Peripatopsidae); the match gave no order, so order is empty. First Onychophora record in the vision cells.

## Desk disposition after audit 234
Audit 234 found 0 of 3 records in error. All three were merged, and the auditor's uncounted additions were applied: animal counts and the 10:00 to 15:15 test window for the two Ophiomastix wendtii records (the species is nocturnal); the 41 to 63 degree interval is called a credible interval of a Bayesian psychometric model in the Discussion; for Euperipatoides rowelli the where field now points to Results, Ray tracing (Fig 6) and the Abstract, the conditions say eyes from 10 adults were sectioned and the 3D model came from the single eye with the fewest artefacts, and the note defines resolution as the full-width-at-half-maximum cut-off in Fourier space and flags the lens refractive index of 1.44 in the Methods against 1.485 in the Results. The velvet worm 0.11 cycles/degree is a computed optical limit, graded m as main does for author-computed anatomical acuities. The brittle star bar-width results are filed under behaviour_vision because a single-bar width threshold cannot be converted to cycles per degree and the authors raise motivation as a possible limit. Held-out leads: Ephoron virgo spectral sensitivity (Egri 2022; only an abstract page read), leech sensilla (PMC8568864, reCAPTCHA), right whale pigments (Fasick 2011, no open licence), the Ophiomastix acceptance angle of 31 degrees (a restated value from the 2020 paper).
