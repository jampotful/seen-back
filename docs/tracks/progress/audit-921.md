# Audit 921: desk-d459, lines 19 to 24

Unit desk-d459 (24 records, non-spider, trait eye_diameter_mm). This audit judges only lines 19 to 24 in file order. The line map was checked against the jsonl: line 19 is Leptobrachella jinzhongshanensis holotype NNU 001478, and lines 20 to 24 are Achalinus mirabilis QHU R2025051, QHU R2025054, SYS r003125, QHU R2025053 and SMNH 20250126.

Sources were opened only from URLs in this audit's own WebSearch results: the zse.pensoft.net article_preview pages for 195575 and 183453, their /download/xml/ files, the Amphibian Species of the World species page and the Reptile Database species page. 2 WebSearches were used. No Crossref call was made, and the notes make no Crossref claim beyond "Crossref not checked".

## Checks common to all six lines
- Repeats on main: a grep of docs/ (outside the unit files) for the species names and the DOIs 10.3897/zse.102.195575 and 10.3897/zse.102.183453 returned no hit.
- Licence: both preview pages print the whole sentence 'This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0), which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the original author and source are credited.' All six notes quoted only its opening clause, which counts as a fault. The replacements give the sentence whole.
- Headings: no Class, Order or Family headings are printed on either preview page. The Achalinus XML carries a classification (Animalia, Squamata, Xenodermidae) for A. jinggangensis, but the page reader returned no such heading lines, so the unit's "none printed" is accepted.

## Line 19, Leptobrachella jinzhongshanensis, holotype NNU 001478: ERROR (fixable)
- Confirmed: the Methods sentence 'Following Rowley et al. (2016), we measured specimens with digital callipers to the nearest 0.1 mm.' and the EYE entry 'EYE eye diameter, horizontal diameter of exposed eyeball;'. The heading 'Measurements of the holotype (in mm).' prints the unit.
- Confirmed: the quote is the whole list as printed (28 words), and EYE 3.9 is printed there as this one specimen's value. Table 3 gives only ranges and means by sex (male n = 2, female n = 8), so it holds no per-specimen value.
- Confirmed: the heading path is Results > Taxonomic account > species heading > 'Measurements of the holotype (in mm).'
- Confirmed: authors 'Chen W-C, Su T-P, Yang H-J, Pan M-Y' as on the byline, the title, 102(4): 1089-1105, the DOI, and ASW validity ('Leptobrachella jinzhongshanensis Chen, Su, Yang, and Pan, 2026', Zoosyst. Evol., 102: 1095; no synonymy). Family Megophryidae follows the ASW URL path.
- ERROR: the conditions field quotes only the first paratype sentence (NNU 001323–24, two adult females). It omits the printed second sentence: 'NNU 001479 (one adult male) and NNU 001480–85 (six adult females), collected from the same locality as the holotype on 17 May 2025 by Wei-Cai Chen.' The type list is therefore not stated as printed. The holotype locality was also a paraphrase. Both are replaced with the printed text.
- Fault: the licence sentence was cut.

## Lines 20 to 24, Achalinus mirabilis: ERROR in each (fixable)
- Confirmed: the Methods sentence 'Measurements of head dimensions were taken using Mitutoyo digital calipers (CD-15AX) to the nearest 0.01 mm.' and the entry 'ED (eye diameter)'.
- Confirmed: the Table 3 header, Sex row and Type row. The ED row is '1.03 | 0.99 | 1.05 | – | 0.99–1.05 | 0.94 | 0.82 | 0.82–0.94' under QHU R2025051 ♀ Holotype, QHU R2025054 ♀ Paratype, SYS r003125 ♀ Paratype, QHU R2025052 ♀ –, Range, QHU R2025053 ♂ Paratype, SMNH 20250126 S♂ Paratype, Range. Each filed value matches its column digit for digit.
- Confirmed: the holotype sentence 'Eye small, ED 1.03 mm; pupil vertically subelliptic.' and the type paragraphs (the unit marked its cuts with '...'). The ranges and the dash cell are not filed.
- Confirmed: the 12 authors in printed order, the title, 102(2): 387-409 and the DOI. The Reptile Database lists the name as a species (Higher Taxa begin 'Xenodermidae'), and its Synonym field holds only the name itself.
- ERROR: every method field says "the Table 3 caption prints no unit". This is false. The caption reads 'Main measurements and meristic characters of Achalinus mirabilis sp. nov. All measurements are in mm, the abbreviations of morphological characters are defined in the Materials and Methods section.' The desk flag repeated the same misreading. The replacement cites the caption.
- Fault: the licence sentence was cut.
- Observation, not counted: the paper's species heading prints 'Achalinus mirabilis Xu, Hu, Qi, Zhang, Zhou, Xu, Wang & Peng, sp. nov.' (8 names), while the Reptile Database gives 12 authority names. No field of these records carries the authority.
