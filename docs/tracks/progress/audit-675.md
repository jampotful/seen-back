## Eye-size definition (Materials and methods, read whole)

The section prints nine sentences and an abbreviation list. In order: "All measurements are given in millimeters." "Leg measurements are shown as: total length (femur, patella, tibia, metatarsus, tarsus)." "Eye diameters as AME, ALE, PME, PLE and interdistances as AME–AME, AME–ALE, PME–PME, PME–PLE." then epigyne clearing, microscopes, Helicon Focus, Photoshop, deposition in MHBU, "The following abbreviations are used:". The eye abbreviations are names only ("AME anterior median eyes", "AME–AME distance between AMEs", etc.).

RULING: the sentence "Eye diameters as AME, ALE, PME, PLE and interdistances as AME–AME, AME–ALE, PME–PME, PME–PLE." is a reporting-format statement, parallel to the leg sentence before it. It names the dimension the description's eye numbers give (diameters) and, with the unit sentence, the unit (mm). It does not say how or where the diameter was taken, so it is weaker than "Eye diameters were taken at the widest point." (EJT 968) or "maximum dorsal diameter" (ZooKeys 1210, EJT 917). It differs from the papers moved to sensory_anatomy (ZooKeys 1123, EJT 947, ZooKeys 1240), which never call the numbers diameters. The trait key records a diameter in mm and the paper says these numbers are diameters in mm, so eye_diameter_mm STANDS. The notes now say plainly that no measuring method is printed. Printed unit: millimeters, confirmed.
