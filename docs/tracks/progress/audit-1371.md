### Line 1, Scarus psittacus, hourly mean detections peaked during crepuscular periods

Abstract sentence, read as two pieces joined at a space: "Hourly mean detections peaked during crepuscular periods for palenose parrotfish and to a lesser extent" / "for ember parrotfish, which was linked to movements between shallower daytime (foraging) and nighttime (resting) areas." **Quote MATCH**, whole sentence, starts at the sentence start, 31 words. Preceding sentence ends "palenose parrotfish (3,725 ± 1,254 m2)." as the note says.

Pooling: the sentence names two species, but it attributes the full peak to palenose and only "to a lesser extent" to ember; the value is filed for palenose alone and the conditions say the ember part is not filed. Not pooled; accepted.

Captive question: Methods "Each fish was brought back to shore and placed in holding tanks." ... "Each fish was released the following morning in the general location of the capture site." One night in holding tanks, then tracked free-ranging for weeks: wild fish, acceptable (as for the trapped-collared-released Prionailurus javanensis diel row on main).

Fault: the conditions say "The text read did not state how long fish were held after capture." The Methods do state it (overnight, released the following morning). Tagging dates also misgiven as "13 and 17 June": Table 1 prints SP1 to SP4 "13/06/2012" and SP5 to SP8 "17/06/2012" (that one is a wording slip, folded into the same fix). Also worth stating: "crepuscular" appears only in the Abstract; Results and Discussion print no peak sentence (tool summary; Figure 3 caption gives hourly detection percentages without naming a peak). One record error (design detail misstated), fixable.

### Line 2, Lethrinus miniatus, larger core areas and extent at crepuscular and night periods

Abstract Results sentence: "Initial observations demonstrated significantly larger horizontal core areas and vertical areas of extent during" / "crepuscular and night periods compared to during the day." **Quote MATCH**, 23 words.

Trait fit: the value is activity-space size by period. `diel` records an activity category (BRIEF: diurnal, nocturnal, crepuscular, cathemeral; main's rows are activity timing from detections, cameras or accelerometers). Space-use size does not say when the fish is active, and the record omits the contrary Results sentence "Detections varied among the four time periods of dawn, day, dusk and night, with more detections" / "of individuals during daylight hours." (followed by sentinel-tag standardisation showing crepuscular and night absence was not an artefact). The authors do print a category, in the Discussion: "Fine-scale data revealed diel activity space use of L. miniatus which reflects a nocturnal lifestyle." It is their inference from space use, and the abstract Conclusions hedge it ("While further research is required to confirm foraging during the hours of twilight and darkness, this fine-scale approach identified patterns in nocturnal activity ..."). Fish numbers: "Of the eleven L. miniatus fitted with acoustic transmitters, seven were detected over 93 days of monitoring"; five "detected consistently ... and provided sufficient positions for inclusion in analyses"; the excluded count came back as "four" only as a tool summary and does not close against 7 and 5, so the conditions' arithmetic is unchecked (not counted).

As filed the value does not support a diel record and omits a contrary result: one record error. Per the desk's instruction this line is **held out**. A corrected line refiled on the Discussion sentence, with the daytime-detection result and the hedge in the conditions, is given in the Replacement for the record; it remains named for dropping because the category rests on space use alone with more daytime detections.

**Line 2 ERROR, HELD OUT (drop).**
