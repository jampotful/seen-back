# desk-d160 progress

Vein: animal hearing in teleost fishes (ABR/AEP audiograms), cells with few records on main.

Filed 12 records in docs/views/evidence/desk-d160.jsonl from 4 papers, each with an open licence statement read on the publisher page:
- Zhu et al. 2023, Front Mar Sci 10: 1135310 (Epinephelus awoara ABR, 3 records incl. the 2000 Hz top-of-set note). CC BY.
- Vetter, Brey and Mensinger 2018, PLOS ONE 13(3): e0192561 (Hypophthalmichthys molitrix, H. nobilis, Cyprinus carpio, genus-rank upper-limit record). CC0.
- Maruska, Ung and Fernald 2012, PLOS ONE 7(5): e37612 (Astatotilapia burtoni, 3 records incl. the null for male social status). CC BY.
- Maiditsch and Ladich 2022, PeerJ 10: e14230 (Trichopsis vittata quiet audiogram and white-noise shift). CC BY.
