# Audit 994: desk-d499 lines 7 to 11 (Boulenophrys dalaolingensis, eye_diameter_mm)

Auditor: Opus 994. Scope: lines 7-11 of docs/views/evidence/desk-d499.jsonl only (lines 1-6 judged elsewhere). 2 WebSearches, 6 WebFetches, all at URLs from my own results (zookeys.pensoft.net/article_preview.php?id=154757; amphibiansoftheworld.amnh.org Boulenophrys-dalaolingensis page). Title and DOI checked on every read: right paper each time. No Crossref read; no Crossref claim in the notes.

## Checks
- Paper: "A new horned toad of Boulenophrys (Anura, Megophryidae) was discovered in Hubei Province, China", DOI 10.3897/zookeys.1253.154757. The article page prints ZooKeys volume 1253, pages 305-320, 2025; ASW prints "ZooKeys, 1235: 310". The page prints 1253, so the unit's journal string stands.
- Authors as printed: Ruiqi Wang, Lu Chen, Jinliang Chen, Haochen Huang, Gangzhi Peng, Honglin Peng, Xinzhang Gao, Bangqing Chen, Naiyi Liu. The unit's order is correct (ASW leaves out the two Pengs).
- Licence sentence matches the page, whole: CC BY 4.0.
- Methods (Material and methods > Morphology), exact: "Morphological measurements from preserved specimens were conducted as described by Lyu et al. (2021) using a digital caliper (to the nearest 0.1 mm)."; list item "eye diameter (ED): diameter of exposed portion of eyeball". The page prints a diameter definition, so the trait stays eye_diameter_mm. Table 2 caption and headers print no unit. mm comes from the caliper sentence and the running text ("SNT 3.6 mm"). Accepted as the unit read it.
- Table 2 layout: trait rows, one column per specimen, 10 specimen columns. Body rows in printed order: Sex, SVL, HDL, HDW, SNT, IND, IOD, ED, TD, ... The header row and the ED and Sex rows match the unit cell for cell. Each column's ED/TD cell equals its ED divided by its TD (240506 5.9/3.5=1.69; 240507 4.5/3.2=1.41; 240508 4.7/3.4=1.38; 240509 5.2/3.4=1.53; 240510* 5.0/2.7=1.85), which confirms the column mapping.
- Type material: holotype NNUYC240510, adult male. Paratypes: six adult males (240501, 240503, 240505, 240507, 240508, 240509) and three adult females (240502, 240504, 240506). Sexes and codes for lines 7-11 are correct.
- Headings: the page prints no Class, Order or Family headings. The species heading is printed "Boulenophrys dalaolingensis Wang, Chen & Liu, 2025, sp. nov.". Table 2 sits under "Diagnosis", not under "Type material". The Methods heading is printed "Material and methods".
- Validity: the ASW species page (from my search) lists the species with holotype NNUYC 240510 by original designation and places it in the B. omeimontis group. Family Megophryidae is correct.
- Main: git grep of origin/main docs/ (f16413371) for "dalaolingensis" and "154757" finds nothing. No repeat.
- Quote "ED 5.2 5.3 5.2 4.4 5.7 5.9 4.5 4.7 5.2 5.0" is the whole ED row, 11 words, and every line's value is in it.
