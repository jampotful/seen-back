- Line 1: `journal` "Botanical Studies 58: 50" omits issue 1. Crossref gives 58(1), and the page carries issue 1 in its metadata, but the visible header prints volume and article number only, so under audits 178 and 181 this is not counted; desk-d99 wanted line 1 on main already prints "58(1): 50". The Fig. 2 caption gives the short day as 6 h/18 h L/D where the text says 8 h/16 h; the record copies the text without flagging the conflict. The paper's statistics are a one-tailed t-test at p < 0.1, which "decreased significantly" in the quote rests on and the record does not state.
