Abstract sentence read in two adjoining pieces, matching the filed quote verbatim. Value and measurement say no more than the P. dolloi clause. Conditions already give the yellow-pigment cone ("There is one example of an additional cone type that contained a yellow pigment within the oil droplet, but there are only 36 cells of this type counted in the retina, so it is unknown if this represents a population of a different, rare cone type or oxidation of the red pigment contained in the red cone oil droplets after enucleation.") and the eleven small red-droplet cones ("In this individual, eleven smaller cones with a small red oil droplet were counted along the nasal edge of the retina."), so the "2 cone types" value does not mislead. ## Line 3, Protopterus dolloi, no double cones observed

Section "Morphology of Retinal Photoreceptors in P. dolloi": preceding sentence "Like all other lungfish species described, most of the cell types within the retina of a juvenile P. dolloi are large (147.2 ± 17.9 µm thick in a 20.6 cm TL individual, n = 6, Figure 2)."; then "No double cones were observed."; then "The rods are large (16.0 ± 2.1 µm outer segment length; n = 32, ...". Whole sentence, 5 words; value "no double cones observed" says no more.

Fault (scientific meaning, omitted contrary result): the paper prints an earlier contrary finding for this species. Introduction: "P. dolloi appears to possess both single and double cones, while L. paradoxa and N. forsteri possess only single cones (Ali and Anctil, 1973; Bailes et al., 2006)." Discussion: "However, our investigation did not reveal double cones. Pfeiffer (1968) described the retina of P. dolloi in paraffin wax sections, and reported single cones with oil droplets, and double cones with only one member containing an oil droplet." and "Without a reference image of P. dolloi double cones from Pfeiffer (1968), this is difficult to confirm." The record quotes "did not reveal double cones" but not the report it contradicts, so a reader takes the absence as uncontested. Fault: order also. Verdict **correct** (conditions, order).

## Line 4, Lepidosiren paradoxa, no double cones observed

Earlier reports agree (Introduction: L. paradoxa possesses only single cones; Discussion: N. forsteri and L. paradoxa "do not possess double cones"); nothing contrary. Fault: order. Verdict **correct** (order).
