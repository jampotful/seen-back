- **Measurement design.** "Measurements of eye diameter and body length (mm, ±0.1 mm resolution) were collected using" [calipers]; "eye diameter was measured at the maximum distance found perpendicular to the dorsoventral eye axis". Adults: "Our data represent shrimp in the adult stage of their ontogenetic development as estimated by CL" and mature reproductive structures. Sex not mentioned. Specimens: first expedition (Florida Straits, R/V Weatherbird II, 4-8 May 2019, n = 49, paraformaldehyde-fixed), second (northern Gulf of Mexico, 9-22 June 2019, n = 193, fresh), FICC (n = 213, about 70% EtOH). Table means, no SD or range printed. Ns 23 to 43 for these five lines; no N of 1 or 2. Records' `eye`, `method`, `units` and conditions on these points are right. Met.
- **Preservation result.** Paper: preservation type improved model fit; "Comparison of model intercepts indicated that preservation type had a significant, but small effect on eye-body allometry."; "specimens preserved in EtOH had a slightly lower intercept (α = −1.37) than fresh specimens (α = −1.35)", paraformaldehyde-fixed −1.31; "we could neither determine the role of preservation type separate from species effects"; "the preservation methods did not appear to affect our study conclusions". The records give the effect, the EtOH and fresh intercepts and the authors' conclusion, and already say each mean mixes preservation types in unknown proportions. The omitted non-separability from species effects bears on cross-species allometry, and the per-species mixture caveat is already conveyed; uncounted, but added in the replacements because d320 carries it.
- **Outliers.** "Plotting the data revealed three large outliers among the 454 measurements" "which were removed from the dataset prior to subsequent analyses"; species not named, and whether Table 1 means include them is not stated (Table 1 n sums to 459 per the tool, sources sum to 455). Nothing ties an outlier to these five species; uncounted, unverifiable.
- **Omitted results.** Abstract: "light organ type was the only significant correlate of both relative and absolute eye size"; absolute eye size also correlated with mean daytime depth and maximum reported depth (to about 1,000 m); species with organs of Pesta (the -sergestes group) had smaller absolute and relative eyes. These are cross-species findings; none qualifies a per-species mean diameter, and no record claims an ecological effect. The reduced-eye (Pesta) species are other lines' taxa (Deosergestes, Allosergestes, Sergestes, Parasergestes), outside this audit. Neosergestes edwardsii (n = 7) excluded from regressions; not filed. Uncounted.
