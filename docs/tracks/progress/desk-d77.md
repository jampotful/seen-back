## Advice
Authority pages are the bottleneck: WebFetch only opens POWO pages whose URL appeared in a search result in the same session, so search "<Species> Plants of the World Online" and look for the species' own powo URL before screening a candidate. JTFS landing pages and Reading eprints print authors, volume and issue; the summariser paraphrases unless asked to quote sentence by sentence with character counts.
