### ERG values: measured peaks or template fits; sex and preparation

Springer text (tool summary): 12 female and 4 male eye preparations; 14 quasi-monochromatic LEDs, 346 to 744 nm; photon flux 2.4·10^11 to 2.4·10^15 photons cm⁻² s⁻¹; 40 min dark adaptation plus 10 min after electrode insertion under 660 nm light; sexes compared with Mann-Whitney U tests and "did not differ significantly at all wavelengths" (no statistics given), then pooled. The peaks come from the sum of two A1-based visual-pigment templates (Govardovskii et al. 2000) fitted in GNU Octave: "λmax,UV = 370.1 nm and λmax,G = 526.3 nm were obtained." No raw curve maxima are reported, and 370 nm lies between LED peaks (346 and 376 nm). So the values are template-fit estimates from whole-eye ERG, not measured maxima. The records say so in `method` and `note`, which is right. Uncounted wording: the note's "not pigment lambda max" is slightly off, because the templates are pigment templates. The values are pigment-template λmax estimated from whole-eye ERG, not microspectrophotometric pigment λmax. Uncounted additions: the authors' own limits, "Only from dark-adapted ERG recordings one cannot unambiguously identify different photoreceptor types", and ERG amplitudes much smaller than usual (tool summary).
