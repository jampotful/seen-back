Faults:
- a (counted, all 8): provenance misstated. `method` lists free-flying recordings in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama only. Methods print 'Recordings of molossid echolocation calls were obtained in Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Bolivia, and Brazil over a period of 10 years', reference recordings of high-flying individuals 'exclusively in Venezuela' that 'were collected with a shotgun', and never assign a species to a site or recording mode. The method omits two countries and the shot reference bats and does not say per-species origin is unprinted; it also drops the analyses' 'mean values per call type within recordings' correction for pseudo-replication.
- b (counted, all 8): `conditions` say flatly the paper states no number of individuals; the Figure 3 caption prints 'Symbols represent sequences (individuals) per species.' Omitted, and it bears on sample size (M. neglectus NS = 3).
- c (counted, lines 9 and 16): `conditions` say 'other call types of the species are printed in neighbouring rows and not all are filed here'. False for both: Table 1 prints three call types for M. temminckii (Low 77/11, High 74/11, High II 18/11) and three for M. molossus (Low 136/17, Middle 94/17, High 56/17), and the unit files all of them.
- d (counted, line 9): name scope. MDD notes that M. temminckii 'previously included M. griseiventer', revalidated by Ramírez-Chaves et al. (2023); the 2014 paper used the broader concept and prints no recording site per species, so the record may pool two current species. Not stated. Fixable by stating it; the owner may prefer to drop M. temminckii (lines 1-9) on the pooled-records rule.
- e (format, all 8): licence sentence cut after 'in any medium' with no cut mark; given whole.
- f (format/quote, all 8): quote keeps the text-layer '6' for ±; normalised to ± and checked cell by cell.
- g (format, all 8): `conditions` say the Table 3 footnote defines the Low/Middle/High labels; it defines only 'Call type ( = call frequency alternation) refers to changes in frequency.' Reworded.
- h (format, lines 10-15): 'not all are filed here' is true for M. neglectus (High II, 3/3, unfiled for thinness) but does not name the unfiled row; now named.
- Not faults, added as context: Intro and Discussion say M. temminckii forages 'very close to vegetation edges [31]' (an exception to open-space foraging) and 'the same may be the case of M. neglectus'; the Discussion places M. molossus among 12 molossids calling 'within a narrow frequency range of 20–35 kHz (Figure 3)', while Table 1 prints start frequencies of 35.6 to 42.8 kHz for it (unreconciled in the paper). `source.url` named the PMC article page that neither the unit nor this audit opened; it now names the PDF copy read (as audit 1046).
