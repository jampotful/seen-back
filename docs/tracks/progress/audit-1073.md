# audit-1073: desk-d527 lines 11 to 13

Range judged: docs/views/evidence/desk-d527.jsonl lines 11 to 13 (file order). Opus auditor, 3 Nov 2026. 2 WebSearches, 4 WebFetches.

## Brief mismatch (read first)
The launch brief described desk-d527 as 9 Rhinolophus capensis resting-frequency rows from Odendaal, Jacobs & Bishop 2014 (BMC Evol Biol 14: 60), with eye-diameter instructions. The unit file holds 13 records; lines 11 to 13 are Neophocaena phocaenoides click parameters from Caruso et al. 2022, Frontiers in Remote Sensing 3: 987105 (10.3389/frsen.2022.987105), which the brief's last note names (pooled clicks, 46-word sentence cut with marks). I judged the lines in the file. The Odendaal and eye-diameter instructions do not apply (as audit 1070 found for desk-d526).

## Checks (all three lines share one source)
- Page reached: my unfiltered WebSearch of the exact title returned frontiersin.org/journals/remote-sensing/articles/10.3389/frsen.2022.987105/full and /pdf (publisher copies); both opened. The unit reached only the /xml copy through a domain-filtered search; the replacement url is the /pdf.
- Byline as printed: Francesco Caruso, Giacomo Giorli, Lijun Dong, Likun Zhao, Mingming Liu, Mingli Lin, Songhai Li; "Caruso F, Giorli G, Dong L, Zhao L, Liu M, Lin M, Li S" is right. Title verbatim, volume 3, article 987105, 2022, DOI as filed. Article type printed: Brief Research Report (peer-reviewed journal section).
- Quote: the Results 3.2 sentence re-read on the PDF in two reader pieces: "The signals classified as echolocation clicks of IPFPs showed a mean duration of 90 ± 23 µs(mean ± standard deviation, SD), a mean peak frequency of 123.6 ± 4.4 kHz (mean ± SD) and a mean centroid frequency of 130.7 ± 3.8 kHz (mean ± SD)." The unit's three cut quotes match the characters, start at the sentence start, mark cuts, drop no qualifier and are 22, 34 and 23 words. Values match digit for digit and say no more than the quotes. Heading path "Results > 3.2 Signal features" is as printed.
- Methods 2.2 parameter list and "All parameters were measured as described in Caruso et al. (2020a)." confirmed; peak frequency is listed in Hz in Methods and printed in kHz in Results (unit noted this). No definition of the parameters in this paper (unit noted this).
- ERROR (design confound, all three lines): Methods 2.2 prints "Information on the position of the source was not considered, and therefore on the direction of origin and the distance of the recorded animals." The unit wrote only "The paper does not say whether the clicks are on-axis", which understates a printed statement that no source-position or on-axis selection was made; the means are over clicks of unknown aspect and range. On-axis clicks (Gong et al. 2019) were used only for the detection-range model. Added in method and conditions.
- Fault: the abstract describes the sample as "2,654 short-narrowband pulses detected within 47 days across the 10 sites", while the Results say 51 files across eight sites (stations #9 and #10 had no detections). Unit did not mention it; added.
- Fault: the licence sentence "This is an open-access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY)." is whole; the unit's "the printed sentence may continue" is not borne out (the next sentence begins "The use, distribution or reproduction in other forums is permitted"). CC BY accepted. Crossref not read (unit made no Crossref claim beyond that).
- Fault: the unit's "grep of species and DOI over docs/ found no record for N. phocaenoides" is false: docs/views/audit/corrected/mammal-groups.jsonl line 51 (and ALL.jsonl line 2290) carries N. phocaenoides under sense_echolocation from Akamatsu et al. 2005, 10.1098/rspb.2004.3024 (inspection range). Different paper and measure, so no repeat. No main hit for 987105. Main also holds N. asiaeorientalis on-axis click rows (desk-d516) and a genus-level oa-7 row. Corrected in notes.
- Fault: conditions said the "Same sentence also prints the other two click parameters" and then listed three; rewritten. Unit also omitted the remaining printed Results values (QRMS, zero crossings, ICI, SPLpk 143 ± 4 dB re 1 µPa, a received level at unknown range); listed as not filed.
- Species: Mammal Diversity Database taxon/1006447 (reached from my own search) prints Neophocaena phocaenoides, (G. Cuvier, 1829), Living, found in the wild, Artiodactyla (Cetacea as suborder), Phocoenidae, China in its country list. Taxon, family, order as filed are right.
- n = 2,654 clicks pooled over 51 files, number of animals not estimated: stated by the unit; kept. Grade m, access open, partial false, evidence_kind physiological_response, unit desk-d527: correct.
