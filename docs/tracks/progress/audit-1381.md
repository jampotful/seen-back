### Line 3, Parahyaena brunnea, diel: ERROR, held out as filed; replacement offered (1 fault)

(a) The quoted sentence is about the brown hyena alone, but only through the preceding sentence. (b) It is a whole sentence starting at its real start, but its subject ("the species") is not identifiable from the quote itself; it needs the previous sentence. Not self-standing: **fails**. (c) "Cathemeral" is the paper's own per-species category, not a pooling artefact: Methods classify each species by Diel.Niche ("to classify the species as cathemeral, diurnal, crepuscular or nocturnal, depending on the proportion of observations recorded during daytime, nighttime and twilight periods"), and the Results sentence gives separate percentages and calls each of brown hyena (64.5% nocturnal, 22.6% diurnal) and leopard (31%, 55.2%) cathemeral. That sentence names two species, so it is not used either.
Replacement: Abstract "Brown hyenas were most active during the central hours of the night, with a single broad activity peak suggesting increased foraging effort, independent of leopard activity patterns."
