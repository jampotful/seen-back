The significant seasonal result was also omitted: "There were significant differences in offsets across seasons in fox activity patterns (F33,641 = 23.36, p < 0.0001)", with winter differing through decreased daylight activity (the same omission audit-1106 corrected in d562). Byline verified: the UCC CORA record (cora.ucc.ie/handle/10468/8104, from my search) lists the same ten authors in the same order as the line.
- Line 6, Oryctolagus cuniculus (same paper): ERROR, fixable. Same "1.59 mean detection rate" misreading (body mass column). - Line 7, Apodemus peninsulae (Li et al. 2020, ZooKeys 992:139-155): PASS. Whole Abstract sentence confirmed verbatim from the Pensoft XML, along with the 7-author byline, title, DOI, CC BY 4.0 licence, 5618 of 6383 records and the seed stations 30-80 cm before the cameras. Free-ranging ruling: unmarked wild mice visiting seed stations in a natural forest qualify as wild free-ranging. The baiting is a design factor and is disclosed in conditions, as is the Abstract's crepuscular-preference sentence. It does not disqualify the line.
- Line 8, Leopardus pardalis (Macas-Pogo et al. 2023, Neotrop Biol Conserv 18(1):1-11): ERROR, fixable. Citation, licence, 22 independent records (74 photos, 21 videos), 60-min independence and 567 days/trap confirmed on the Pensoft XML. The whole Results sentence is 41 words. The desk cut its closing clause ", with mild activity during the morning hours (13.6% of records)", which qualifies "mainly during the night and early morning", so a qualifier was dropped from the quote even though conditions carry it. The fix moves the cut to the peak-hours clause (marked). Mineral-lick siting and small n are disclosed and acceptable.
- Line 9, Canis aureus (Debata 2021, JoTT 13(9):19363-19366): ERROR, fixable. The DOI is printed "10.11609/jott.5747.13.9.19363-19366" on the article page and PDF (also on sciencegate). The article mentions no bait.
