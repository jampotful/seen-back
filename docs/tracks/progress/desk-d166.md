# desk-d166 progress (sense cells with open leads; bird and mammal hearing and magnetic)

Records filed: 8, in docs/views/evidence/desk-d166.jsonl (check.py: 0 problems). Three open papers.

- Moffitt TB, Atcherson S, Padberg J 2023, PeerJ 11: e16602 (CC BY 4.0 on the PeerJ page and in Crossref): Dasypus novemcinctus ABR audiogram (eight wild-caught adults, isoflurane, insert earphone), 3 sense_hearing records: best band 8 to 12 kHz, lowest mean thresholds at 8 and 12 kHz, and the 48 kHz row (2 of 5 responded, maximum level 52.8 dB peSPL). Armadillo hearing was not on main.
- Keary N et al. 2009, Frontiers in Zoology 6: 25 (CC BY 2.0 stated on the Springer page): Taeniopygia guttata magnetic orientation, 3 sense_magnetic records: oriented in the 90-degree-shifted field (0.74), random with the 1.156 MHz oscillating field (0.49), and the visual-control birds unaffected. n = 7 of 10 trained birds.
- Ruscher B, Sills JM, Richter BP, Reichmuth C 2021, J Comp Physiol A 207(4): 561-573 (CC BY 4.0 on the Springer page and in Crossref): Neomonachus schauinslandi in-air behavioural audiogram, 2 sense_hearing records (functional range 0.1 to 33 kHz at the 60 dB criterion, tested-range edges noted; best threshold 40 dB at 0.8 and 3.2 kHz). One animal.

Names: Dasypus novemcinctus and Neomonachus schauinslandi checked on Mammal Diversity Database pages; Taeniopygia guttata accepted on worldbirdnames.com (IOC), with castanotis a subspecies there.
