The Scorpion Files Scorpiopidae page ("[Updated 05.08.26]") lists the species under Euscorpiops Vachon, 1980 as "S. krachan (Nawanetiwong, Kosulic, Warrit, Lourenço & Ythier, 2024)". The parenthesised authority marks a combination moved out of the original genus, and the "S." prefix is a typo on that page. Main files the genus at the same rank (desk-d305 "Euscorpiops lii"). Under the named authority the current name is Euscorpiops krachan, and "Scorpiops krachan" is the original combination it replaced. The desk chose the other genus on purpose because placement is "unsettled", but the rule is to follow the registry. Taxon fault, fixable.

Value. The record files 3 under `eyes_count` with units "pairs of lateral eyes (plus median eyes, number not stated)". main's eyes_count values are numbers of eyes (desk-d306 Pinelema "6 eyes", Tarsonops "2 eyes"; desk-d283 Leptonetela "2 eyes"). The paper gives no median count, so no eye total can be logged without going beyond the quote. Main's handling of the same kind of sentence, desk-d305 Euscorpiops lii, is `eye_type` with the sentence's content as the value. Fix: move the record to eye_type, keeping the sex difference and the uncounted median eyes. Counted once as one record's error. The conditions and eye fields are otherwise right.
