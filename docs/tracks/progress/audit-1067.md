# audit-1067: desk-d525 lines 1 to 2

Range judged: docs/views/evidence/desk-d525.jsonl lines 1 and 2 (file order). Opus auditor, 3 Nov 2026. 5 WebSearches, 5 WebFetches.

## Brief mismatch (read first)
The launch brief described desk-d525 as 9 Rhinolophus capensis resting-frequency rows from Odendaal, Jacobs & Bishop 2014 (BMC Evol Biol 14: 60), with eye-diameter instructions. The unit file holds no such rows: lines 1 and 2 are Rhinolophus mehelyi and R. euryale from Salsamendi et al. 2012, Frontiers in Zoology 9: 30 (10.1186/1742-9994-9-30). The Odendaal rows are desk-d522, already merged on main (commit 5074e721e). I judged the lines actually in the file, following the brief's last note ("for Salsamendi 2012 check the licence version and the Table value printing"). The eye-diameter instructions do not apply.

## Checks (both lines share one source)
- Page reached: frontiersinzoology.biomedcentral.com/track/pdf/10.1186/1742-9994-9-30 (publisher PDF), returned by my unfiltered WebSearch of the exact title plus "Salsamendi". The HTML article page (the unit's url) did not appear in my results, so I did not open it; the PDF is the publisher's own copy.
- Authors as printed: Egoitz Salsamendi, Inazio Garin, Inmaculada Arostegui, Urtzi Goiti and Joxerra Aihartza, so "Salsamendi E, Garin I, Arostegui I, Goiti U, Aihartza J" is right. Journal line "Frontiers in Zoology 2012, 9:30"; title verbatim; DOI as filed.
- Licence: the PDF prints "This is an Open Access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0), which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the original work is properly cited." CC BY 2.0, accepted. The unit's "licence URL is empty, no version stated" is corrected.
- Quote (Results and discussion > Echolocation and wing parameters): "R. mehelyi emitted significantly higher RF than R. euryale, 106.8 ± 1.5 kHz (mean ± SD) versus 105.1 ± 1.2 kHz," + "respectively (Table 1)." Whole sentence, matches the unit's quote character for character; check.py counts it within 40 words. Values in both lines match digit for digit and say no more than the quote and the Table 1 row.
- Table 1 ("Echolocation and wing parameters in R. mehelyi and R. euryale in sympatry"), header "R. mehelyi (16) R. euryale (24) F p", RF row "RF (kHz) 106.8 ± 1.5 105.1 ± 1.2 37. 2 ***". The space in "37. 2" is printed, as the unit said. FAULT: the unit wrote that the RF row shows no asterisk; the PDF prints "***" (footnote: *** = p < 0.001). Fixed.
- RF definition: printed in the Abbreviations ("Resting frequency: echolocation call frequency of maximum energy emitted by horseshoe bats when resting."), not in a Methods sentence as the unit said. Methods print the Doppler sentence ("Because bats were held motionless, calls were not affected by Doppler shift;"). FAULT (location) fixed.
- FAULT (sample composition): the method omits that each bat's value is the mean of ten calls ("We chose a random sample of ten echolocation signals from each individual"), starting signals removed. Added.
- FAULT (null result): sex was a main factor; "The interaction between species and sex was not significant (multivariate ANOVA: F = 2.0; d.f. = 7, 30; p = 0.110)." This test covers the echolocation and wing variables together, not RF alone, so I count it as a fault, not an error. Added. Sex split of the 16 and 24 bats is not printed in the main text (Additional file 1 not read).
- Heading path: printed as "Results and discussion > Echolocation and wing parameters"; the unit wrote "Results > ...". FAULT fixed.
- Study design facts in conditions (three adjacent disused train tunnels, harp traps, retention never over 90 minutes, adults only, noseleaf lancet and connecting process, June–July 2007, Sierra de Las Villuercas) all confirmed on the PDF.
- Species: MDD taxon/1004713 prints Rhinolophus mehelyi, Matschie, 1901, Chiroptera, Rhinolophidae, Living; taxon/1004682 prints Rhinolophus euryale, J. H. Blasius, 1853, Chiroptera, Rhinolophidae, Living. Both opened from my own searches.
- Repeats of main: grep over the whole docs/ tree outside desk-d525 finds no hit for 1742-9994-9-30. "mehelyi"/"euryale" hits are only progress notes (d505: pone.0103452 not reached; d521: Gyorossy 2020 not filed; d312: Ascodipteron euryale, a fly). No overlap with desk-d330 (pone.0199703) or d521/d522.
- Unit field reads desk-d525; grade m, access open, partial false: correct. The note makes no Crossref claim.
