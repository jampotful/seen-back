# sweep-90 (Round 3 genus sweep, batch sweep-1)

Date: 15 October 2026. All 40 genera searched at genus level, two or more separate searches each (scientific name with trait words; common name or family with trait words), first titles read, and every candidate page opened that looked as if it named the genus or a congener.

Result: two cells closed by a measurement (Phodopus sungorus receptor_classes and lambda_max_nm, from Calderone and Jacobs 1999, abstract only, so `partial: true`). Every other cell of the 40 genera has a search record in `docs/views/searched/sweep-90.jsonl`. No wanted lines: the only papers worth asking for (Peichl, Nemec and Burda 2004 on Fukomys anselli and F. mechowii cones; Kott, Sumbera and Nemec 2010 on F. mechowii light perception) could not have their DOI confirmed from an opened page, so they were not requested.

Access limits met: PMC and PubMed pages return a browser check to the fetch tool, and several publisher pages are blocked or rate-limited, so the full text of the Fukomys cone papers and of Van der Merwe et al. 2018 (Micaelamys namaquensis, a separate genus) could not be read. Banks et al. 2015 and Odeen and Hastad 2013 were opened in full but their species tables sit in supplements.

Near misses, not logged: Lukats et al. 2005 pools a cone share for four nocturnal species including Mastomys natalensis; Lukats et al. 2002 (IOVS 43:2468) shows all Siberian hamster cones coexpress S and M/L opsins (no DOI on the repository page).
