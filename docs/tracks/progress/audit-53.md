## Observations on the unit (outside the count)

1. **The canon file holds no plant work for eye_type.** The 15 eye_type records name the two canon compilations of the trait, which are animal books and reviews (the second edition of *Animal eyes* and "Eye evolution and its functional basis"). The six plant diel records name seven canon works, of which four are compilations of animal vision (nocturnal bees and wasps, the nocturnal bottleneck of mammals, frogs and toads, and the Dryad dataset of avian eye morphology) and three concern plants (lead 14). The records follow point 8 of desk-screen.md, and the animal works can hold neither a lens cell of a plant nor an hour of flower opening. The plant eye literature that my searches kept returning (Haberlandt, Bone et al., Brodersen, Yamashita and Baluška, leads 1 to 4) is not in the canon file, so point 8 gives the plant eye_type cell no compilation to log.
2. **Blank taxonomy for three genera, as for ten genera of harvest-46.** The queue gives Chelone, Comarum and Phalaris no family or order, so the harvest's citation step never ran for records 21 to 24 (finding 7). docs/views/gaps/taxonomy-gbif.tsv holds the three genera (Plantaginaceae, Rosaceae and Poaceae). The desk's 09:33 UTC note in docs/tracks/QUEUE.md reports the same blank taxonomy for ten genera of harvest-46 and proposes a queue built with that file. Appending the rows to taxonomy-extra.tsv would give the three genera a citation step on the next run.
3. **A second fork for eye_type: does a description of papillae or convex outer walls count without a claim about light?** The desk's web pair asks for "lens-like epidermal cells", which I read as a lens or focusing claim, and no work I read makes one for these genera. Several describe the surface only. Harb, El-Kobisy and Desoukey 2015 (Asparagus officinalis) report slightly elongated reticulate papillae and wax crystals on the cladode and tie them to a water-repellent surface. Tsarenko et al. 2025 describe papillae and slightly convex walls on the fruit exocarp of Valeriana stolonifera, and Tölke et al. 2015 report micropapillae on the trichomes of Heliotropiaceae. Only the first concerns a photosynthetic organ. If the desk counted a papilla description in a photosynthetic organ, record 13 would be an error: 1 of 24 on sense A (4.2%, 0.1% to 21.1%) and 2 of 24 on sense B (8.3%, 1.0% to 27.0%), which is over the 5% line, and the unit pooled with the six would stand at 2 in 166 (1.2%) on sense A and 3 in 166 (1.8%, 0.4% to 5.2%) on sense B. I did not count it, because the cladode papillae are tied to wetting and no work links them to light. The desk decides, and the answer applies to the 13 flagged records of finding 1 as well.
4. **Slips in the unit's notes and records.** The note of record 9 says that four papers on the wanted list may measure the diel and sense_chemical cells of Raphanus, and the wanted file holds ten lines for them (one for diel and nine for sense_chemical). The notes of records 13 and 21 mention a search that is not among their `queries` (herbivore-induced volatiles of Asparagus officinalis; the essential-oil composition of Comarum palustre). The note of record 16 repeats the note of record 14 about the sense_light_nonvisual cell of Botrypus and says nothing about its own trait. I counted none of these against the unit.
5. **A point in the unit's favour.** The `where` line of record 24 reports that one search result came from a library proxy mirror and was skipped unopened, as desk-screen.md requires.
