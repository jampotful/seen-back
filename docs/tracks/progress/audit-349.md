## Trait keys and the two rulings asked for

sense_chemical is on check.py's TRAITS list and main. Grade `m` right for all four (a measured field or behavioural outcome with a test).

Ruling 1, camera-trap detection-rate nulls for a scent lure (lines 6 to 8). A detection rate at a lured station is a whole-animal outcome that folds together attraction from a distance, lingering (limited here by a 30-minute independence threshold), changed movement and lure persistence; the authors themselves warn that more detections need not mean more animals. A null therefore says the lure did not change how often the species was photographed; it does not show the animal failed to smell it, and an ungulate or ground squirrel may smell a skunk-musk lure and ignore it. Two things keep the nulls usable rather than uninformative: the same tablespoon of lure at the same stations raised fisher and carnivore detections strongly (fisher beta = 2.23, z = 6.18), so the lure was active at those stations for at least some species over the survey; and the study is a large paired design (844 stations, 247 sites). Fileable as nulls under sense_chemical on the same footing as audit 346's predator-odour rats and audit 347's deterrent nulls, provided the record says in conditions that a null of detection rate does not show non-detection, that one tablespoon was applied with no refresh reported and persistence over 120 days was an assumption, and that lure status followed fixed corners rather than random assignment. For line 8 the estimate is uninformative in size (beta = 0.00 +/- 1.14 SE) and rests on a model with a simplified habitat term; that must be disclosed, but it is still the paper's reported test and the paper's own abstract states the null.

Ruling 2, one sentence for several species lines. Line 5 and line 6 share the abstract sentence "Scent lure significantly increased ... fisher specifically, but not of gray wolf."; lines 7 and 8 share "There was no effect of scent lure ... moose and Richardson's ground squirrel specifically." Under audits 334 and 336 this is allowed when each value is confined to its own clause and the quote is whole. Fisher and wolf sit in separate clauses ("and fisher specifically" / "but not of gray wolf"). Moose and ground squirrel share one predicate ("no effect ... moose and Richardson's ground squirrel specifically"), which gives the same reading for each and attributes nothing of one to the other; that is the same situation as audit 336's "respectively" clause. Allowed; these are one-binomial records, not pooled-species records. The line 6 value must however keep the quote's qualifier (below).

Line 9 (wolf urine) is a behavioural null to a predator odour in free-ranging deer with randomised water and soap controls; fileable under sense_chemical on the audit 346 footing with the same non-detection disclosure. The scored behaviour set includes sniffing, whose nulls are directly relevant and are not carried.
