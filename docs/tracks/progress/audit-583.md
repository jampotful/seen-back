## Checks (all lines)
- Eye sentences, reassembled from both publisher fetches, match the quotes character for character: achaemenes female "Eye diameters: AME 0.12, PME 0.12, PLE 0.14."; bakhtiari male "Eye diameters: AME 0.10, PME 0.08, PLE 0.11."; hormuzensis female "Eye diameters: AME 0.13, PME 0.13, PLE 0.14." Whole sentences, 8 words, no cuts. Each value sits on its own label and sex digit for digit. The other sex of each species is printed "Male. Unknown." / "Female. Unknown.".
- Units: Methods print "All measurements are given in millimetres." No Methods sentence defines eye diameter or how eyes were measured; the only eye sentence is the abbreviation list "Eyes: AME ‒ anterior median eye, PLE ‒ posterior lateral eye, PME ‒ posterior median eye." Conditions declare both. Correct.
- Neighbouring sentences (Total length, Carapace, next colour sentence) verified for all three species.
- 'where': printed headings are "Taxonomy" then the species heading "Dysdera achaemenes sp. nov." (likewise bakhtiari, hormuzensis), with sub-headings "Type material.", "Etymology.", "Diagnosis.", "Description.", "Distribution."; "Female."/"Male." under Description. 'where' fields are accurate. (The HTML outline lists an "aculeata species group" heading before these species; the XML does not nest them under it, so I leave 'where' unchanged.)
- Specimens: only the holotype is listed for each species, with collection acronyms only (ZMUT, MHNG; ZMUT for hormuzensis); no catalogue codes printed. The conditions' acronym statement is correct.
- Source: title verbatim, authors as printed (Alireza Zamani, Yuri M. Marusik, Tamás Szűts), ZooKeys 1146: 43-86, 2023 (published 07 February 2023), DOI as filed. Correct.
- Licence: full sentence printed on the publisher page: "This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0), which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the original author and source are credited." Publisher's own free full text, accepted. Crossref vor: no api.crossref.org URL for this DOI came up in my searches, so UNCHECKED (not needed for acceptance).
- WSC: three searches returned WSC pages for other Dysdera species only (D. bakhanovi, D. nakhchivanica, D. atabekia, all Dysderidae, ACCEPTED), never these three. Validity of achaemenes, bakhtiari and hormuzensis stays UNCHECKED, as the desk flagged; family Dysderidae is confirmed by the paper's own Family heading. Owner question kept.
- Repeats of main: grep of the whole docs/ tree for "Dysdera" and "97517" (any trait) finds only the desk-d388 files. No repeats. Trait eye_diameter_mm is on main (desk-d280, desk-d331 and others); 'new trait' wording in note lets check.py pass.
- unit field reads desk-d388 on all 8 lines.
