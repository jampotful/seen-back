### Lines 6 to 8, Giraffa giraffa, Table 4 rows

Table 4 as printed (reader): "LW vs. MRR | Reserve | within Dove | 0.007 | 0.196 | 0.035 | 0.972"; "LW vs. MRR | Reserve | within Lion | 1.585 | 0.196 | 8.088 | <0.0001"; "Dove vs. Lion | Stimulus | within LW | –1.745 | 0.201 | –8.698 | <0.0001"; "Dove vs. Lion | Stimulus | within MRR | –0.166 | 0.19 | –0.877 | 0.384". The three quoted rows match cell for cell (en-dash minus as printed). Values say no more than the rows. N 24 (LW, 12 F, 12 M) and 27 (MRR, 13 F, 14 M), adults, coat-pattern identification, three roars plus three grunts from fifteen lions, six dove coos, lions introduced 2019 to 2020, speaker 20 to 133 m: all confirmed.

Stimulus: "the sound pressure level (SPL) of each playback sequence was standardized to 85 dB (Z-weighted) using an NTI AL1 SPL meter with an NTI MiniSPL microphone"; no measurement distance; no received level at the giraffes; propagation tests only checked spectral integrity to 150 m (tool summary). **HOLD OUT** under the precedent of audits 317, 344, 345, 350: the stimulus at the animal is undefined.

Further faults, fixed in the Replacement:
- Conditions say "at 1 m from the source or at an unstated point"; for this paper the point is simply unstated (precision fault, uncounted).
- Conditions state lion and dove "on the same day in random order". The paper says "within the same day" and that "The calls per sequence, as well as the order of sequences within each package, were randomly assigned to control for order effects", with playback order a model term. Whether a "package" is the lion/dove pair is not printed, so the replacement quotes the wording instead of asserting randomisation (design statement, uncounted given the hold-out).
- The paper's headline contrast (lion responses between reserves, 1.585, SE 0.196, t 8.088, p < 0.0001) is described only in words; line 8's note leans on it. Added with values to all three (omitted effect size; counted once, line 8).
- Note on Table 3: the record calls +1.745 a conflict. Table 3 prints "Stimulus type (Dove vs. Lion) | 1.745 | 0.195 | 8.965" (a model coefficient with its own SE), so the replacement describes it as a sign difference against Table 4 rather than an error in the paper (uncounted).
- Line 6 note: the abstract durations are now read (513.34 +/- 421.34 s, N = 24 against 49.06 +/- 46.26 s, N = 27) and moved into the note.

The value says more than the quote. Fixed: the whole sentence (26 words) with the bracket; the 7 of 9 result (from the next sentence, "In response to calls from hatchlings, ... seven individuals out of the nine tested approached ..., with five coming to within one meter, and only two females ...") moved to conditions.

- **HOLD OUT** under the stimulus-level precedent (level only at 1 m from the loudspeaker, animals 10 to 30 m away), and no wild control stimulus.
