## Eye-size passage (Methods, whole section read)

Materials and methods prints, on eyes, only the abbreviation sentence "Eyes: ALE—anterior lateral eye, AME—anterior median eye, PLE—posterior lateral eye, PME—posterior median eye." Its measurement sentences are "Body measurements exclude the chelicerae and spinnerets.", "Leg segments were measured on the dorsal side.", "Measurements of legs are listed as: total (femur, patella, tibia, metatarsus, tarsus)." and "All measurements are given in millimeters." No eye dimension is defined, so sensory_anatomy stands; the printed unit is mm. The desk's `method` says the Methods state "only" the millimetre sentence, which omits the eye-abbreviation sentence (fault).
