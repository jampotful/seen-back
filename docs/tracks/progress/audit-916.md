## What checks out

- Every value matches its eye and sex digit for digit. Male: 'Eye diameters: AME 0.41, ALE 0.22, PME 0.08, PLE 0.21.' Female: 'Eye diameters: AME 0.46, ALE 0.23, PME 0.04, PLE 0.23.' The quotes are whole sentences, 9 words each.
- Neighbouring sentences ('Measurements: …', 'Ocular area …', 'Eye interdistances: …', 'Clypeus height …') are verbatim for both sexes.
- Type material is verbatim: 'Holotype ♂. … (NZC-ZSI-8313/18).' and 'Paratype: 1♀, same data as holotype (NZC-ZSI-8371/18).'
- Methods: the heading is 'Material and methods'. The unit sentence is 'All measurements are given in millimetres (mm).' The only eye text is the abbreviation list ('ALE – anterior lateral eye, AME – anterior median eye, … PLE – posterior lateral eye, PME – posterior median eye'). The publisher page confirms 'diameter' does not occur in the Methods. No diameter definition is printed.
- Trait: the label printed here is 'Eye diameters', the same as this paper's P. dentis, P. luna and P. rajbharathi records on main (desk-d456). The label does not differ, so eye_diameter_mm stays, giving one trait reading per paper. The owner question about the missing Methods definition stays open for this paper.
- Unit is mm, from the Methods.
- Authors, title, journal string, volume, issue, pages and DOI are as printed. Year 2024 is as printed in the copyright line.
- Species validity and family were checked on the WSC (accepted, Salticidae).
- Repeat check: I grepped the whole docs/ tree on origin/main for 'handersoni' and '113049'. There is no P. handersoni evidence record under any trait, so these lines are not repeats.
- The unit field reads desk-d458. No Crossref claim is made.

## Faults

- F1 (lines 1-8): **Licence sentence incomplete.** The unit gives only the opening clause. The whole sentence as printed is: 'This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0), which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the original author and source are credited.' The copyright line '© 2024 Puthoor Pattammal Sudhin, John T. D. Caleb, Souvik Sen.' was also missing.
- F2 (lines 1-8): **The note's main check is false.** It says the grep for the species and the DOI 'found no evidence record' and that the name 'appears only as a lead in docs/tracks/progress/desk-d452.md'. In fact the DOI is on main in desk-d456.jsonl for three other species. The name is not in desk-d452.md; it appears as a lead in desk-d456.md (lines 18 and 22).
- F3 (lines 1-8): **The heading path is not as printed.**
  - The species heading is given as 'Phintella handersoni sp. nov.' with the authority split off. It is printed 'Phintella handersoni Sen, Sudhin & Caleb, sp. nov.'.
  - The genus 'Type species' heading is omitted.
  - The subheadings are given with full stops that the publisher page does not render.
  - `where` cuts the run-in label: the male label is printed 'Male (Holotype, NZC-ZSI-8313/18) (Figs 2E–F, 3A–C, G, 4E, F):' and the female label 'Female (Paratype) (Figs 2G, H, 3D–F, H, 4G, H):'.
  - No Class or Order heading is printed. The unit correctly added none.
- F4 (lines 5-8): **The female's catalogue number is inferred.** `measurement` names the measured female 'female paratype NZC-ZSI-8371/18', but the female label prints no catalogue number. The female is now named as the label prints it, and the type-list number is given separately in conditions as the single paratype '1♀'.
