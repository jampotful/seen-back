## Rulings on the desk flags

- Sex conflict (lines 1-2): allowed with disclosure. The Holotype paragraph prints '• Adult male, field number XZ2024037 (Figs 4, 5, Table 2)' and Table 2's second header row prints 'Holotype (Female)'. The value is the holotype's ear/tragus under either sex, the measurement line states no sex and the record files none. d345's withholding was a desk choice, not a ruling.
- Quote form (lines 1-2): the reader shows 'HB (head-body length): 43.25, EL (ear length): 16.18, …'. On main, the desk-d391 audits found these expansions are xlink:title tooltips in Pensoft XML, so the unexpanded 'HB: 43.25, EL: 16.18, …' form, as in d345 and d347, is the printed text. Kept.
- Inferred specimen (line 3): the value is acceptable as the ear length of the single printed female of a named species (grade m). The holotype identity is removed from `measurement`; conditions give the Holotype paragraph and the FA/GTL match as printed facts without asserting identity.
- Two-row quote (line 3): replaced by the ♀ row with the merged species cell label.
- Eye passage: these are sensory_anatomy lines. No eye diameter definition is printed for them (Methods for 144375 define EL 'from lower edge of external auditory meatus to tip of pinna' and TRL 'from base to tip of tragus'; 147349 prints 'E, ear length' only). Unit mm, as printed in the headings and captions.
- Heading path: 144375 prints Results then 'Taxonomic account' then 'Murina beibengensis Luo, Mao & Zhou, sp. nov.'; 147349 prints Results > 'Taxonomic treatment' > 'Murina chayuensis Luo & Zhou, sp. nov.' > Materials, Etymology, Diagnosis, Description, Comparisons with other taxa. Neither prints Class, Order or Family headings.
- Bibliography: authors, titles, 101(3): 1023-1055 and 101(3): 983-997, year 2025 and DOIs match the article pages. Licence sentence on both pages: 'This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0), which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the original author and source are credited.'
- Names: MDD 1006943 Murina beibengensis (Vespertilionidae, Chiroptera, holotype XZ2024037, 'recently described', no species status field) and MDD 1006942 Murina chayuensis (species status 'Living', validity status species, holotype XZ2023107 with no sex) are both accepted.
- Main: neither species nor DOI 10.3897/zse.101.147349 appears as an evidence record on main under any trait. 10.3897/zse.101.144375 is on main only for M. medogensis (d345) and M. milinensis (d347); desk-d525 lists 'ZSE 147349 and 144375' as 'no values or already on main'. That is a note, not a record, and does not count as a repeat.
- Crossref: no Crossref claim is left in the lines ('Crossref not queried' removed from line 1).
