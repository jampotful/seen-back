Reference recordings were made in large clearings "(20–30 m width)" (en dash). Free-flying recordings come from Bolivia, Brazil and Panama. No site is printed for any species.

- Species validity: MDD 1005185 prints Eumops nanus (G. S. Miller, 1900), Molossidae, Chiroptera, status Living. It prints no Class heading (Subclass Theria is the highest) and no 'valid' word. The species is accepted.
- Main: no Eumops of any species is on main under any trait. The DOI is on main only as Tadarida brasiliensis (docs/views/audit/corrected/mammal-groups.jsonl). These lines do not repeat main.

2. The licence sentence stops at "in any medium" with no cut mark, and drops "provided the original author and source are credited."
3. Sample composition is misstated. `conditions` says the paper gives no individuals. The Figure 3 legend prints "Symbols represent sequences (individuals) per species.", so the authors treat NS = 4 as individuals.

Faults (listed, not counted):

- The method implies Bolivia, Brazil and Panama sites for this species, but no site is printed per species. MDD's range for E. nanus excludes Bolivia and Brazil.
- The note says "valid/living", but MDD prints only "Living".
- `source.url` is the PMC HTML page, although the note says the PMC PDF was read.
