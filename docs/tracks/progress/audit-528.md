## Unit-wide rulings

- **TRAIT KEY CHANGED: sensory_anatomy -> eye_diameter_mm on all eight lines (desk flag).** The Materials and methods, read whole, contain no eye-measuring sentence: the only measurement sentences are 'All measurements are provided in millimeters.' and 'Leg measurements are listed in the following order: total length (femur, patella, tibia, metatarsus, tarsus).', and the abbreviation list gives AME, ALE, PME, PLE and 'MOA median ocular area'. The desk's reading of the Methods is right. But each list is printed under its own label, 'Eye diameter:' (both males) and 'Eye diameters:' (both females), which names the numbers as diameters. That is the case already ruled by audit 433 (Pholcus, 'Eye interdistances and diameters' heading, Methods only 'All measurements are given in millimeters (mm).': sensory_anatomy was a fault, eye_diameter_mm the key) and merged on main in desk-d362 and desk-d292 ('Eye diameter: AME 0.03, ...' with no Methods definition). A reader of eye_diameter_mm would miss these records under sensory_anatomy. One trait-key fault per line. Replacement lines carry trait eye_diameter_mm, 'new trait' in the note (check.py line 36 needs it; the key is not new on main), and the method and note say the label, not the Methods, makes these diameters.
