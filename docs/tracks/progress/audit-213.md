Trait definition: BRIEF's trait table gives `sense_vibration` as "substrate or web vibration: organs, frequency range, threshold" and `sense_hearing` as "hearing range and best frequency". Plant sense records on main are living-plant responses to a stimulus (audits 209 and 210). So a plant sense_vibration line must show the plant responding (physiology, expression, behaviour) to vibration carried through a substrate. Body et al. qualifies: the Methods state "Playbacks were conducted using 2-inch audio speakers (8 ohms, 0.5 W), modified as in Michael et al. (2019) to minimize production of airborne sound, and with a dowel attached to the coil to allow coupling of vibrations to a leaf", with the leaf waxed to the dowel. Veits et al. does not: the Methods state "Acoustic playbacks were performed using a Vifa speaker" about 10 cm from the flowers, and the Results say the flowers "vibrated mechanically in response to the airborne sounds of a bee or a moth". Ghosh et al. also used airborne sound from a speaker in a chamber ("The plant growth chamber was equipped with a speaker (Sammi, Korea)"). Brito et al. measures no plant response at all (line 6).
