## Eye-diameter decision (LOUD)
MOVED TO sensory_anatomy. The unit's own reading of Material and methods ('Somatic measurements were made with a scaled eyepiece mounted into a stereo microscope and are expressed in mm.') prints no sentence defining an eye diameter, and audit 810 could not open the Methods to find one. Under the strict rule the word 'diameter' inside the description sentence does not supply a Methods definition, so all three lines go to sensory_anatomy. The printed unit, per the unit's Methods quotation, is mm (unverified by audit).

ALSO: the eye label. The sentence prints 'anterior eyes diameter', not 'AME'. The unit mapped 'anterior eyes' to AME by its own inference; in six-eyed dysderids the anterior pair is commonly called ALE, and the rule requires the value printed after that eye's label. The replacement files eye 'anterior eyes' with no AME/ALE mapping.
