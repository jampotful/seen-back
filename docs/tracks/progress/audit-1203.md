## Faults (same on every line)

1. Quote misprints the row (error, counted). The table's row label is printed "ED" only. In the XML it is `<abbrev xlink:title="eye diameter: from anterior to posterior corner of orbital opening">ED</abbrev>`; the article_preview reader expanded the tooltip into "ED (eye diameter: ...)", and the desk filed that expansion as printed text. The quote therefore contains words the table does not print. Corrected quote: "ED 2.2 2.4 1.9 2.1 2.3 2.1 2.0 2.2" (9 words by check.py count; whole row, nothing cut, last cell empty).
2. Conditions misquote the caption and row (fault). The caption is quoted with "MZB (Museum Zoologicum Bogoriense) 12094", another tooltip expansion; the XML prints "MZB 12094 is the holotype". The row is quoted with the expanded label. The note's "(same values in both)" claim for preview and XML hid the label disagreement.
3. Heading path not stated (fault). The notes say nothing of the printed heading path; the progress note says no Class/Order/Family headings are printed. Right that none are printed as headings, but the title ends "(Amphibia, Anura, Microhylidae)" and the XML treatment carries Taxon classification keywords Animalia, Anura, Microhylidae (the preview page did not show the box). Now stated.
