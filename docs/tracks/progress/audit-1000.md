## Line 3, Cyrtorhinus lividipennis, 16 antennal sensillum types

- Quote: Abstract "Sixteen types of antennal sensilla were recorded altogether." MATCH, 8 words. Results: nymphs "could be classified into twelve types"; "There were fifteen sensilla types identified on the female antennae."; no male total printed (the reader notes female types also occur in males plus CHA III in males only, which would make 16 for males, but the paper never says so and I do not infer it).
- **POOLED**: "altogether" is a union over five nymphal instars and both adult sexes; the record itself says so. Desk rule: pooled records are errors and dropped; the value is also Abstract-only (weak). **DROP.**
- Authors fault: page prints "Yang H-Y, Zheng L-X, Zhang Z-F, Zhang Y, Wu W-J"; record drops the hyphens (main keeps them, e.g. "Lassance J-M"). Class "Insecta" not printed (title "(Hemiptera: Miridae: Orthotylinae)"); blanked. Title, PLoS ONE 13(11): e0207551, 2018, DOI right. Licence sentence as line 1; accepted.
- Authority: not reached. AMNH "Names proposed in 'Cyrtorhinus'" index prints only "lividipennis" (no author, status or validity word); the names listing failed. Held out on authority too.
