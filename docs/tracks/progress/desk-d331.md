## Desk disposition after audit 445

Audit 445 found 2 fixable errors in 2 records. Line 1 (Teuthowenia pellucida eye diameter range) wrongly said the measured specimens were formalin-fixed and ethanol-stored (the paper states that only for the seven histology specimens, and never mentions shrinkage) and called the range the "whole size series" (the series runs to 210 mm ML, the measured eyes to 165 mm); name confirmed as Teuthowenia pellucida (Chun, 1910) on NZOR and SeaLifeBase. Line 2 (Photoblepharon steinitzi) carried a Methods clause and a "519 nm" detail in the value that the quote does not support; both removed, and the conditions now say the paper describes no unlit dummy control and that stimulus order is unstated. All "tool summary" details were verified verbatim. Fixed from the auditor's replacement text; both merged. Optional: line 1 class/order/family not added; line 2 order Trachichthyiformes (FishBase) differs from main's Anomalopidae rows (Beryciformes); owner may reconcile.
