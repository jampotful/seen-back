| 1 | Trema tomentosum / sense_light_nonvisual | ERROR (value contradicted by the light test) | Quote verbatim (Results 3.2), but it is the temperature-test sentence. The value says the species "germinated only under the simulated gap photo-thermal regime (gap light with the gap temperature cycle)" and "needs both light and a gap temperature cycle, not light alone". In the light test the paper says T. maxima, M. sanguineum and T. tomentosa "did not germinate under dark conditions" and that "they all germinated in the simulated light environment of the understory and forest gap"; for Trema, "The GS of T. tomentosa was significantly later in the simulated understory light environment" than under gap light (Table 4: GS 25 against 15 d, MTG 29 against 21.8 d), and FG, MTG and GS "were significantly different among the three light conditions". So Trema germinated under understory light (6 umol m-2 s-1) as well as gap light; the gap-only result belongs to temperature (20-30 C cycle, FG 93%; none at 30 C or 20/25 C). The understory-light germination and its delay, the study's main light result for this species, are omitted and the value states the opposite |
| 2 | Dyera costulata / sense_light_nonvisual | ERROR (quoted claim contradicted by the paper's own table; significant R:FR results omitted) | Quote verbatim (Abstract sentence 6). The record read the body (it carries the half-of-PFD estimate and treatment counts) but carries the abstract's "influenced exclusively by PFD" as the result. Table 6 (coefficients of determination, two-way ANOVA of the four low and medium PFD treatments) marks R:FR significant for stomatal conductance (0.079 *, PFD 0.008 not significant), palisade cell length (0.089 *, larger than PFD's 0.076 *) and stomatal density (0.062 *), so leaf physiology and anatomy did respond to R:FR. The Results also say "Internode length was promoted by greater PFD and reduced R:FR, but the effects were not large" (R:FR 0.057 *), the one elongation response to low R:FR, and "Stem robustness, as mass/length, was increased by higher PFD, and slightly decreased by low R:FR"; neither is carried. Treatments, duration (429-441 d), seedling counts and the leaf-area and mass-per-day R:FR effects are right |
| 3 | Cunninghamia lanceolata / sense_light_nonvisual | ERROR (abstract result omitted) | Quote verbatim (Results 3.6), 125 characters. GA3 and ABA are endogenous leaf contents quantified by HPLC on 3 seedlings per treatment, so this is a measured response to light quality, and sense_light_nonvisual is the right trait (not sense_chemical). Hormone, height (BL and 1:2L p < 0.05; RL, FrL, 1:1L not significant, against the abstract's FrL claim) and DEG figures (BL 323, RL 1,719, FrL 910) are carried correctly. The second half, the only growth gain the abstract gives for red light and the 1:1 mix (Results 3.1: RL gave the greatest root-collar diameter, BL the smallest), is not carried, and the value's "red light and the 1:1 mix had no significant effect on height" leaves red light looking inert |

Faults noted, not counted:
- Line 1: the conditions say Table 4 labels the gap column 20/25 C; as read here Table 4 gives Trema's MTG (20 d) and GS (15 d) under 20/30 C and nothing under 30 C or 20/25 C, so the claimed labelling conflict was not confirmed (table read through WebFetch). The Methods do not say which five light-temperature combinations were run, and the record does not say so.
- Line 2: the six treatment values are the abstract's; the Methods and Table 1 print other figures (for example 14% PFD and R:FR 1.30 or 0.23, Table 1 PFD 2.6% and 2.8%), and the record does not flag it. The text sentence "R:FR contributed little to leaf anatomy characteristics" could sit beside the Table 6 marks in a refile.
- Line 3: "blue light gave the fewest differentially expressed genes against white (323)" is not checkable: totals for 1:1L and 1:2L are not given (1:2L had 200 up-regulated). The Sturdiness index (highest under BL and FrL) and Dickson quality index (lowest under BL and FrL) are not carried. The page as fetched prints no licence statement.
- Line 4: the full-text light test ran "under continuous light" by the Methods while including a dark treatment; a refile should quote the Methods and note it. A 12 h pre-soak was chosen before the trials; seeds from trees on the UFRPE campus, 2006.
- Line 5: specific leaf area higher under 50% shade (202.41 dm2 g-1), shoot dry mass not significant, leaf weight ratio null and longest root length greater in full sun are in the Results and not carried.
- Line 6: "(and dry mass)" should be leaf and root dry mass; shoot dry mass did not vary. No GA3 effect on trichome number or root length is not carried. Growth data were tested by Tukey at 1%.
