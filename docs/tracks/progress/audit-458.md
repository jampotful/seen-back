## Unit-wide rulings

- **CSV rows as quotes (desk flag).** The CSV files are not supplementary data: they are Pensoft's own per-table download (the article page offers "CSV and XLSX download links" under each table) of a table that is printed in the HTML article itself. I read every quoted row on the publisher's article_preview page, which carries the licence sentence and is the published article (version of record): Johnius javaensis Table 2 (HTML table), C. phnomchiensis Table 1, C. peninsularis Table 7, H. lungcuensis Table 8, R. aquamarina Table 2, P. pluvialis Table 1, P. pulchridormientes Table 1. Each filed quote is the whole printed row with its cells joined by single spaces, the form audits 431/432/457 accepted for table rows. Ruling: a CSV export may be used to read cells, but the quote stands only because the same row is printed on the licensed page; where a row cannot be found on the page the line would be held out. Here every row matched, so no line is requoted on this ground. The `where` fields describe the CSV as the copy read; harmless (uncounted), and line 1's replacement says the row is on the page.
- **Licence.** CC BY 4.0 sentence printed on the article_preview page for 160425, 149552, 146533, 8295, 8507. CC0: the sentence "This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the CC0 Public Domain Dedication." is printed on the article_preview page for 48671 and 103713 (the record's copy), and the 48671 JATS licence element gives the same text and the CC0 URL. A printed CC0 dedication on the exact copy is accepted. Met for all eight.
- **ED means eye diameter in every paper.** Johnius javaensis: row label "Eye diameter" (no abbreviation). C. phnomchiensis: Methods define EyeD as "Eye diameter, measured as the horizontal distance from the anterior to the posterior margins of the eyeball"; ear measures are EarDH/EarDV. C. peninsularis: ED "the greatest horizontal diameter of the eye-ball". H. lungcuensis: EyeD "eye diameter (the greatest horizontal diameter of the eyeball)". R. aquamarina: Methods list "eye diameter (ED)" after Brown et al. (2011). P. pluvialis: Methods "eye diameter (ED)" and "tympanum diameter (TY)". P. pulchridormientes: "eye diameter (ED)". None is ear diameter. Met.
- **Holotype wording.** Each record that says holotype names the column the page labels Holotype: J. javaensis (MZB 28379, female), C. phnomchiensis (CBC 03012, type-status row "Holotype"), C. peninsularis (LSUHC 10202, "holotype"), H. lungcuensis (VNUF R.2021.01; the HTML table prints the type-status row with Holotype first, before Min-Max, so the desk's inference from the text is confirmed), P. pluvialis (CORBIDI 16510). R. aquamarina and P. pulchridormientes are filed as means and say so. Met.
- **Anomalous holotypes (desk flag).** R. aquamarina: Table 2 prints holotype ED 1.0 against males 2.0 ± 0.11 (1.8–2.2); the holotype description says "Eye-nostril distance 1.5 mm, 74.0% of horizontal eye diameter", which implies about 2.0 mm, and the other holotype cells (SVL 17.1, HL 5.1, HW 5.7, END 1.5, IOD 2.3, TD 0.9) sit inside or at the male ranges. So the 1.0 is the paper's own inconsistency, not a desk unit or column error. The record files the male mean, not the holotype, and states the anomaly; the omitted text ratio would strengthen the typo reading but does not change the filed value; uncounted. P. pluvialis: counted error, see line 7. The printed Table 1 column cannot be read as labelled from "Eye diameter" down: holotype "Eye diameter 0.8", "Tympanum diameter 3.0", "Eye to tympanum distance 1.3", "Forearm length 1.0", "Hand length 5.1", "Finger I length 6.6" (finger longer than hand, forearm 1 mm, tympanum nearly four times the eye, upper eyelid width 2.2 wider than the eye), the pattern of values offset one row against their labels (same reading in the article_preview page and the JATS XML). The holotype description gives "eye large, 33.9% of head length" (about 3.0 mm at HL 8.9). The desk filed 0.8 mm as eye diameter after flagging only its magnitude; the value is not an eye diameter. Not fixable as eye_diameter_mm (any mm value would be my inference from the offset, saying more than a quote); fixable as the printed ratio.
- **Johnius javaensis pair.** Line 1 is the absolute holotype diameter in mm (Table 2 morphometrics row); line 2 is the holotype ratio to head length from the Description sentence (Table 2 also prints 18.9 under % HL). Different trait keys, different quantities (the ratio needs head length, not in line 1), one specimen each; per audit 449's ruling the ratio goes under sensory_anatomy. Not double counting. The pair's only fault is line 1's conditions (see below).
- **Names (current status).** Johnius javaensis: Eschmeyer reference 42529 (Lin, B.-A. et al. 2025, ZooKeys No. 1259: 169-183) lists only Johnius javaensis as its new name; no status line shown; a 2025 name with no later act found; accepted provisionally, no error. H. lungcuensis: Reptile Database header "Hemiphyllodactylus lungcuensis LUU, NGUYEN, DO, PHAM, HOANG, NGUYEN, LE, ZIEGLER, GRISMER & GRISMER, 2023", holotype VNUF R.2021.01, cited as valid by Wang et al. 2025; valid.
