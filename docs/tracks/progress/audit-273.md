Scientific context: counted on lines 1, 2 and 3 (one fault per record, fixable). The records describe the stimuli as "LEDs of 380, 440, 520, 580 and 650 nm" and the red series as "625, 635, 645-700, 710, 720 and 730 nm", that is, as narrow-band light at those wavelengths. The Methods (2.2, tool summary) say the stimuli came from "10-watt LED panels (Anhui Aihaidi Lighting Technology Company, Anhui, China)" driven by "a mobile application called LED Magic Light 2.3.0" that "can separately adjust RGB values to generate different light colors on each panel", and "The conversion function of Bruton (…) was used to convert the selected wavelengths to RGB values." No emission spectrum was measured; the only measurement is "light illumination was measured using a Sigma AS803 digital illuminometer." Table 1 (verbatim cells): 380 nm = R 97, G 0, B 97, 1400 lx; 440 = 0, 0, 255, 2360; 520 = 54, 255, 0, 2100; 580 = 255, 255, 0, 2430; 650 = 255, 0, 0, 600; CK (white) = 255, 255, 255, 4500. Table 2: 625 = 255, 78, 0, 1230; 635 = 255, 39, 0, 900; 645–700 = 255, 0, 0, 600; 710 = 233, 0, 0, 530; 720 = 210, 0, 0, 500; 730 = 188, 0, 0, 460.

So the wavelength labels are nominal names for display-style colour mixes. The "380 nm" stimulus is the panel's red and blue channels at equal level, with no UV emission shown; "650 nm" is the red primary at full level; and in the red test the four longest labels (645–700, 710, 720, 730 nm) are the same red channel at R 255, 233, 210 and 188, with "645–700" identical in setting and illuminance to Table 1's "650 nm". The red-series result (females prefer "730 nm"; males "730 nm" and "635 nm") is therefore a choice among levels of one primary, plus two settings with a little green added, and the most-chosen "730 nm" stimulus is the red channel at its lowest setting. The desk's lux confound is correct, but the records still present the results as wavelength choices, which changes their causal interpretation and their use against any spectral-sensitivity row (main holds review lambda_max rows of 360-380 and 510-530 nm for this species). The records' sentence that the test "does not separate colour discrimination from phototaxis to brightness in a spectral band" does not convey that the bands were never measured and that the red series varied intensity of one channel.

The paper never states that intensities were matched, and the Discussion does not raise brightness as an explanation (tool summary), as the records say. Design as recorded: "In each experiment, 100 male and 100 female adults were separately tested with 3 replicates", "10 adults were placed simultaneously" per test, a stay of more than 1 min in an arena room scored as a choice, lights "randomly placed" and "repositioned after adults chose their preferred color" (verbatim fragments). How often each beetle was reused is not stated (uncounted; could be added). Grade m fits; behaviour_vision fits (colour choice).
