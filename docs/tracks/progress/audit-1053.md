## Faults
- a (counted, lines 25-32): provenance misstated. `method` says 'free-flying recordings in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama' with released bats in clearings. Methods print 'Recordings of molossid echolocation calls were obtained in Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Bolivia, and Brazil over a period of 10 years.', plus shotgun-collected reference individuals 'up to 60–80 m above ground'; no per-species site or method is printed. Two countries and a recording mode dropped and the gap unstated; en dashes ('20–30 m') restored.
- b (counted, lines 25-32): `conditions` say 'other call types of the species are printed in neighbouring rows and not all are filed here'. False: Table 1 prints three call types and all three are filed (lines 25-33).
- c (counted, lines 25-32): `conditions` say flatly the paper gives no number of individuals and omit the Figure 3 caption 'Symbols represent sequences (individuals) per species.' (NS = 6 here), and omit the Methods' 'mean values per call type within recordings' pseudo-replication correction.
- d (counted, lines 25-32): licence sentence cut after 'in any medium' with no cut mark; now whole.
- e (counted, lines 25-32): quote keeps the text-layer '6' for ± ('31.260.7'); re-quoted from the page read with ± restored and checked digit for digit.
- f (format, not counted, 25-32): `conditions` say the Low/Middle/High labels are 'defined in that footnote'; the footnote defines only 'Call type ( = call frequency alternation)'. `url` names the PMC article page that neither the unit nor I opened; replaced by the PDF copy read.
