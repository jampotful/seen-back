# Audit 907: desk-d455, lines 7 to 10 (Pristimantis paganus, Calamaria synergis, C. incredibilis, C. similis)

Auditor: Opus 907. Scope: lines 7-10 of docs/views/evidence/desk-d455.jsonl in file order only (checked against the jsonl: 7 P. paganus holotype ED 3.92; 8 C. synergis paratype EyeD 0.8; 9 C. incredibilis holotype 0.8; 10 C. similis holotype 0.8). WebSearches used: 8. All pages opened were URLs in my own search results; no proxy, ResearchGate or academia.edu result was opened.

- peerj.com/articles/20512 and peerj.com/articles/20512.pdf; Amphibian Species of the World page for Pristimantis paganus.
- zookeys.pensoft.net/article_preview.php?id=161412 and its download/xml (front matter and Methods only); Reptile Database Calamaria/synergis.

## Methods (read whole) and the eye-size definition
- Line 7 (PeerJ e20512): the morphology paragraph prints 'Morphometric measurements were taken with an electronic caliper (accuracy ± 0.01 mm).' and 'The following morphological measurements were taken (Duellman & Lehr, 2009).', followed directly by 'Codification of subarticular tubercles follow the classification of Ron et al. (2020).' No abbreviation list and no ED definition in the Methods. ED is defined in the paper by the Table 3 row label, printed 'Horizontal eye diameter (ED)'; caption 'Morphometric measurements (millimeters) of the type series of Pristimantis paganus sp. nov.' / 'Range of measurements (maximum, minimum, mean and ± standard deviation).' Table 3 has columns Males (n = 13), Females (n = 7), Juveniles (n = 3), ranges and mean ± SD only, with no holotype column. The holotype value 3.92 comes from 'Measurements (in mm) of holotype.' A printed diameter label exists, so eye_diameter_mm stands (no move to sensory_anatomy), as a table-label definition.
- Line 8 (ZooKeys 1253): 'The following measurements (all in mm) and counts were taken: head length (HL, from snout tip to jaw angles)' ... 'eye-nostril distance (EN, from anterior edge of orbit to posterior edge of nostril) ; eye diameter (EyeD, horizontal)'. Defined, mm. Stands.
- Line 9 (ZSE 102(2)): 'The following measurements (all in mm) and counts were taken: snout-vent length (SVL); tail length (TaL);' ...; the Methods do NOT define eye diameter. Line 10 (ZooKeys 1277): the Methods do NOT define eye diameter. In both, the quoted value sentence itself prints the label 'eye diameter 0.8 mm', so the diameter is printed literally with its unit; I keep eye_diameter_mm and say so here loudly. If the owner reads the rule as needing a Methods definition, lines 9 and 10 move to sensory_anatomy.

- Authors, titles, DOIs, journal strings: P. paganus authors printed 'Keyko D. Loza-Carvajal, Mario H. Yánez-Muñoz, Walter Quilumbaquin and H. The unit filed family Calamariidae against its own authority for reptiles: an error on lines 8, 9 and 10, corrected to Colubridae. C. incredibilis: no Reptile Database page reachable, so line 9 is held out per the unit brief.
- Crossref: the lines carried only 'Crossref ... not checked (UNCHECKED)' remarks, no claims; removed from the notes as unread.
