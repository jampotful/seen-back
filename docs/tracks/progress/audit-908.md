# Audit 908: desk-d456, lines 1 to 8 (Stenaelurillus megamalai, male holotype and female paratype)

Auditor: Opus 908. Scope: lines 1-8 of docs/views/evidence/desk-d456.jsonl in file order only, checked against the jsonl and the launch line map (1-4 S. megamalai male holotype NZC-ZSI-7941/18 AME/ALE/PME/PLE; 5-8 S. megamalai female paratype AME/ALE/PME/PLE). WebSearches used: 3. Every page opened was a URL in my own search results; no proxy, ResearchGate or academia.edu result was opened.

## Sources opened
- zse.pensoft.net/article_preview.php?id=97985 (publisher HTML) and zse.pensoft.net/article/97985/download/xml/ (publisher XML), both from my own search results.
- wsc.nmbe.ch/spec-data/67611 (World Spider Catalog species page) from my own search results.

## Methods (read whole) and the eye-size definition
- Methods unit sentence as printed (HTML and XML agree): 'All measurements are in millimeters (mm).' The unit quoted it as 'All measurements are given in millimetres (mm).' and then said in parentheses that "paper 1" prints the correct wording; this paper IS paper 1, so the unit's Methods quotation is wrong on all 8 lines. Counted as an error (misquotation of the source sentence the unit relies on for the unit of the value).
- Other Methods text: 'Morphological examination and measurements were made with a Leica M205A stereomicroscope.'; eye abbreviations 'ALE = anterior lateral eye', 'AME = anterior median eye', 'PLE = posterior lateral eye', 'PME = posterior median eye'. 136 specimens (80 males, 56 females) in 70% ethanol; terminology after Logunov and Azarkina (2018).
- LOUD: the Methods print NO definition of eye diameter. The only diameter wording is the label 'Eye diameters' printed in both quoted Description sentences (male and female, verified). I keep eye_diameter_mm on the desk label precedent (desk-d452 and its audits), with one trait reading for the paper. If the owner rules label-only evidence to sensory_anatomy, all 8 lines move.

- Quotes verified exactly, digit for digit: male 'Eye diameters: AME 0.37, ALE 0.24, PME 0.11, PLE 0.28.'; female 'Eye diameters: AME 0.50, ALE 0.30, PME 0.12, PLE 0.27.' Values per eye and sex match (0.37, 0.24, 0.11, 0.28; 0.5, 0.3, 0.12, 0.27). Label 'Eye diameters' printed in both sentences.
