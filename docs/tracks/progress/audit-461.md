## Unit-wide rulings

- **Monochamus totals, measured or computed (desk flag).** The Table 6 footnote, read in five adjoining pieces: "Data are presented as mean±SE." "The number of each sensillum type/subtype was calculated by multiplying the area of each segment" " by the corresponding sensillum density and summing the results." " Within each row, different lowercase letters indicate significant differences between the sexes (t-test, P<0.05)." " n=6 per sex." Methods 2.4: "The surface area of each segment was calculated by multiplying the length by the width." Methods 2.2: "six antennae (three dorsal surfaces and three ventral surfaces)" per sex. So the totals are the authors' estimates from measured density and a rectangular segment area, on one surface per antenna. The twelve row means sum to 21848.96 (female) and 24978.42 (male), matching the Total row within rounding. Grade: m is right. BRIEF grades by taxon ("m measured in this creature"); the desk's `i` precedents (audits 290, 299; desk-d208) are values the desk itself computed from printed numbers. Here the authors print the estimate from their own measurements in this species, as with the Dollocaris "∼18,000" ommatidia estimate kept at m after audit 402 and the model-derived threshold at m in audit 357, provided the record says it is an estimate. Both records say so in `measurement`, `method` and `conditions`. The value (one table cell) says no more than the row. Ruling: m stands; not counted.
- **Monochamus "per antenna" and surfaces (uncounted).** Neither the caption ("Number of antennal sensillum types/subtypes in male and female Mo. saltuarius.") nor the footnote prints a unit; per antenna is the natural reading with n=6 antennae, so it is not a misstatement, but the replacement marks it as the reading. The omitted one-surface sampling and length-by-width area bear on how literally the number can be taken; the record already says the total "is an estimate from density, not a direct count", so the omission does not change the reading of the value; uncounted, added in the replacement. The conditions also date collection "January to August 2022"; Methods 2.1 gives no collection date and 2.2 dates the removal of antennae to that period. Harmless; fixed.
