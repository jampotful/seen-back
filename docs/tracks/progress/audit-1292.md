## Checks that pass on all eight lines
- Values: each TD cell, catalogue cell, field number and Sex cell matches Table 1 digit for digit; rows 2 to 9 of the table in printed order (421, 420, 418, UADBA 60294, 60298, 60296, 60297, 60295) equal file lines 31 to 38. Quotes (row through ED, cut marked) pass check.py.
- Trait: TD is the printed 'horizontal tympanum diameter (TD)'; sensory_anatomy is right. ED is a printed diameter but is a different quantity (on main, desk-d609). Unit mm from 'Morphological measurements were taken to the nearest 0.1 mm using a digital calliper.'; caption and header cells print no unit.
- Source: DOI, title, 9 authors in printed order, journal string 94(2): 247-261, year 2018, licence. Unit field desk-d626.
- Repeat: grep of 'lomorina' and 'zse.94.21037' over docs/ finds only desk-d575 (holotype TD 2.1 and 53% ratio) and desk-d609 (ED of all 16 rows). The d609 row quotes are the same strings as these quotes and contain the TD cells only inside the row, the same situation d609 was ruled on against d575. No paratype TD is on main. Not repeats.

## Hold-out questions in the brief
- UADBA 60294 TD 2.7 with ED 3.4 (line 34): the Variation section prints 'Tympanum diameter ranges from 47–79 % of eye, without strong sexual dimorphism in tympanum size.' 2.7/3.4 = 79% (my arithmetic), inside the printed range, and ZSM 1548/2012 (2.2/2.8) is similar. Keep, with the Variation sentence in the note. For the owner (line 45, outside my scope): ZSM 318/2016 2.1/2.6 = 81% and no row gives 47% (lowest 2.0/4.1 = 49%), so the printed range does not exactly match the table.
- Sex conflict (lines 34 to 38): Table 1 sexes UADBA 60294 to 60299 m, f, sa, f, f, f; the Paratypes paragraph gives 'one male, three females, a subadult and an unsexed adult' and assigns no sex to any number. The TD value does not depend on sex, and each record states the Table sex cell, which is as printed. This does not need hold-out, but the conflict has to be printed in each UADBA record's conditions, which the desk did only as a general remark in the note. Lines 31 to 33 agree with the paragraph (418 female; 420 to 421 two males).

- d. The Table 1 caption is quoted to 'The holotype is bolded.' without a cut mark. The caption goes on: 'Additive measurements (FARL, FORL, and HIL) are not explicitly shown [...]'.
- e. Line 34 only: the note calls the 2.7 cell high but leaves out the paper's own Variation range, which brackets it.
