## Line 2 detail

Verified on the publisher PDF: byline "Tamara van Molken, Hannie de Caluwe, Cornelis A. Hordijk, Antonio Leon-Reyes, Tjeerd A. L. Snoeren, Nicole M. van Dam, Josef F. Stuefer" (matches the unit), title verbatim, Oecologia (2012) 170:433-444 (no issue printed), DOI 10.1007/s00442-012-2322-z; licence sentence verbatim as in the unit's note (CC BY). Trifolium repens L. accepted on POWO ("This species is accepted.") and WFO (June 2025 snapshot, accepted). The unit's abstract quote is confirmed as one whole sentence (34 words).

Decision on fileability: the emission result alone (infected against mock-inoculated blends, kappa 0.9231, p < 0.0001; beta-caryophyllene only in infected headspace) is induced emission after a pathogen; CHEMICAL-EVIDENCE.md says such outputs are not chemical-response endpoints. It is not fileable as a plant sense_chemical physiological_response. The paper does supply a fileable result: Methods place three pots per treatment "in a 5-l glass cuvette at the upwind end of one of the two arms of a closed-system Y-tube olfactometer", so adult female Bradysia sp. chose on odour only, and "These adults clearly preferred non-infected plants (67 %) over virus-infected plants (33 %) [...]" (chi-square 4.84, p = 0.0278). That is an odour-only causal test of a living emitter's volatiles on a defined receiver, the 10(a) signalling allowance, evidence_kind behavioural_response. Design limits: single genotype A120; olfactometer plants inoculated 18 and 3 days before testing against 4 days for volatile sampling; separately prepared plants, no volatiles taken from choice-test plants; no isolated or synthetic compound tested ("further experiments testing the repelling function of isolated compounds are needed"); receiver printed only as Bradysia sp.; 8 to 10 females per set, seven sets, six non-choosers excluded.

Faults: (a) trait/kind misclassification as described (error); (b) value and conditions misstate the gnat link as untested and omit the odour-only olfactometer design; (c) value carries p, kappa and beta-caryophyllene not in the quote; (d) "gnat larval feeding by 52 percent" misreads the Results (gnat larvae cut total biomass by 52 percent; virus by 27 percent); the abstract's "only marginal effects of WClMV infection on the interaction between fungus gnat larvae and white clover" was omitted. Fix: quote the Results preference sentence (29 words), value limited to it, emitter and receiver named, design limits in conditions, kind behavioural_response.
