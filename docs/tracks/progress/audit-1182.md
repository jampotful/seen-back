## Why d510 and d512 held the paper out

Both held it out because ED is ear length, not eye diameter. That reason does not touch the OrbD row, but the unit ruling does: orbital diameter is not eye diameter and not the sensory_anatomy structure unless the Methods define it so. Here the Methods define it only as "greatest diameter of orbit". The one precedent on main for an orbit measure (d481, Cyrtodactylus jayadityai) had a Methods definition "taken horizontally between the external eyeball borders"; this paper prints nothing comparable. d519 left Eublepharis jhuma's "greatest diameter of orbit" OD unfiled for the same reason.
