Faults:
- a (counted, all 8): provenance misstated. The method lists only released bats in clearings and free-flying recordings in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama. The paper records in 'Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Bolivia, and Brazil over a period of 10 years', with released bats in Costa Rica and Panama and shot high-flying reference individuals in Venezuela, and prints no country, site or method per species (no supplementary locality table either). The pseudo-replication sentence ('mean values per call type within recordings') was omitted.
- b (counted, all 8): conditions say 'other call types of the species are printed in neighbouring rows and not all are filed here'. False: Table 3 prints exactly two call types for each species and the unit files all of them (lines 49-60).
- f (counted, lines 49-54): taxon concept omitted. MDD's E. glaucinus page notes 'previously included E. ferox and E. floridanus' and lists only Colombia, Venezuela, Guyana, Brazil, Bolivia, Paraguay and Argentina; MDD's Eumops ferox ('split from E. glaucinus') covers Costa Rica and Panama, two of the paper's five recording countries. The 2014 paper's E. glaucinus may therefore include bats MDD calls E. ferox, and the paper does not say. The replacement states this in conditions. The lines are kept, since three recording countries lie in MDD's E. glaucinus range and no Central American origin is printed; the desk may prefer to hold them out as possibly pooled.
- c (format, all 8): where omits the period of the printed Table 3 title 'Search flight call parameters for the genus Eumops.' (the correct table; no Table 2 caption on these lines).
- d (format, all 8): licence sentence cut after 'in any medium' with no cut mark and without 'Copyright: 2014 Jung et al.'; url names the PMC article page while the PDF copy was read; note says only Tadarida brasiliensis is on main, stale since desk-d518 merged.
- e (format, all 8): quote keeps the text-layer '6' for ±; replacement decodes it, checked digit by digit.
- g (format, all 8): Figure 3 caption 'Symbols represent sequences (individuals) per species.' omitted from the statement that the paper gives no number of individuals.

Counts: 8 lines judged, 8 errors (all fixable), 0 dropped, 0 held out. Counted faults 22 (a 8, b 8, f 6); format faults 32 (c 8, d 8, e 8, g 8).
