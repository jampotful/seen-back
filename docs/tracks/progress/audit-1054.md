## Faults
- a (counted, 33-40): provenance misstated. `method` gives 'free-flying recordings in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama'; Methods print 'Recordings of molossid echolocation calls were obtained in Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Bolivia, and Brazil over a period of 10 years.' and shotgun-collected reference bats 'up to 60–80 m above ground'. Two countries and a recording mode dropped, the absence of per-species sites unstated, and the printed en dash ('20–30 m') changed to a hyphen.
- b (counted, 33-40): `conditions` claim 'other call types of the species are printed in neighbouring rows and not all are filed here'. False for all three species (see call-type counts).
- c (counted, 33-40): `conditions` omit the Figure 3 caption 'Symbols represent sequences (individuals) per species.' and the Methods' 'mean values per call type within recordings ... while correcting for possible pseudo-replication.'
- d (counted, 33-40): licence sentence cut after 'in any medium' with no cut mark; the sentence ends 'provided the original author and source are credited.'
- e (counted, 33-40): quote keeps the text-layer '6' for ± ('26.360.4'); re-quoted with ± decoded, checked digit for digit.
- f (counted, 34-39 only): taxon scope unstated. MDD 1005205 notes M. rufus 'Previously included M. nigricans and M. fluminensis' (Loureiro, Engstrom and Lim 2020) and its country list leaves out Costa Rica and Panama, two of the paper's five recording countries; the 2014 paper does not say where its M. rufus were recorded, so the values may cover bats outside the current M. rufus. Stated in `conditions`.
- g (format, not counted, 33-40): `conditions` say the Low/High labels are 'defined in that footnote' (the footnote defines only 'Call type ( = call frequency alternation)'); `url` names the PMC article page the unit did not open (replaced by the PDF copy read); note says only Tadarida is on main (d518 now merged); line 40's note says the MDD page has 'no explicit valid or living line', but the page I opened prints 'Living', 'Found in the wild' and 'Listed in MSW3 2005'.
