Ruling on the unit's question: an odour-conditioning result on a living animal belongs under sense_chemical as evidence that the odour was detected and discriminated, because a conditioned response to an odour cannot occur without detecting it. The owner's "living responses and volatile signalling only" reading was made for plants and excludes extracts and processed tissue as plant-side evidence; it does not bar an animal responding to an odour from processed tissue. Two limits: such a record must say it measures learning (not innate preference or a detection threshold), and a failure to condition (here, harnessed bees, PER = 0) is not evidence that the odour was not sensed. The record meets the first and does not file the null.
