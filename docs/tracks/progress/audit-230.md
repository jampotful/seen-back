## The unit error in lines 1 and 2

The JEB PDF's text layer prints the micro sign as "m". The desk normalised "cm21" to "cm-1" but kept "mV" and "mS", so both Eigenmannia values are 1000 times too large. The evidence, all from opened pages:
- Figures 1 and 3 print their axis reference as "[re: 0.6 µVp–p cm−1]", while the body text gives the same reference as "0.6 mVp–p cm21".
- Results: the fish's own EOD field is "3 mVp–p cm21" and a threshold stimulus is "approximately 1 mVp–p cm21"; the Discussion gives "The ratio between the stimulus intensity and that of the EOD was 1/3000 at threshold levels." That ratio holds only if the threshold is 1 µV/cm against a 3 mV/cm EOD.
- The Regensburg repository abstract (an independent text) reads "0.6 mu V cm(-1)" and "100 mu S cm(-1)".
So the lowest threshold is 0.6 µV/cm and the conductivity 100 µS/cm (a freshwater value; 100 mS/cm would be about twice seawater). The DF means are 25.07, 2.46 and 1.27 µV/cm. This is a magnitude error under oa-audit.md, and it sits in the quote as well as the value.
