## What the four checks found

Zero-hit queries are kept, as desk-screen.md point 7 asks. Each record then lists the prescribed "web: " pair, shared by traits that share their words, and for eight genera a third search under an older name. Queries differ in 0 of 35 (and in 0 of 72).

**(b) `chased`.** Among the 35 drawn records, four name a DOI that the output prints as a citation step and that is the source of the queue's closest record (lines 24, 25, 47 and 63), one gives a reason that the queue and the output support (line 22), and thirty say "none applies", with closest none in the queue for every species of the genus and trait.
