### Lines 1 and 2, Pelophylax ridibundus and Rhinella marina, absolute_sensitivity, green/red rod ratio 1.9 and 1.4

Values say no more ("on average" kept as "averaged").

Next sentences, verbatim in three pieces: "It should be noted that, due to a large scatter of I0.5 for toad red and green rods (SD/mean ratio > 40%)," "it turned out that their sensitivity did not significantly differ statistically, although the "best" of" "the recorded GRs had a sensitivity up to four-times higher than the average RRs." Line 1 says this correctly.

Method, verbatim: "were registered by suction pipette recording from single cells (see [61])." "All records were performed on single isolated rods with the outer or inner segment drawn into the pipette."; LEDs "λmax = 460 and 525 nm (blue and green)" with "short flashes of light (10 or 2 ms length)"; "Before the experiment, animals were dark-adapted overnight." Figure 2 caption: "Pooled data for 12 red rods and 12 green rods of frogs." and "Pooled data for 8 red rods and 9 green rods of toads." Animals: "Adult marsh frogs (P. ridibundus) were captured in wild in southern Russia and cane toads (R. marina)" "were purchased from local breeder."; "Frogs were kept for up to 8 month with free access to water at 10–15 °C"; "Toads were kept for up to 6 months with free access to water at 26–30 °C". No recording temperature (tool summary), as the records say. Animal numbers not given (tool summary).

Stimulus, verbatim: "The “effective light intensity” Ie(λ) expressed as number of photoisomerizations in a unite cell volume" "was calculated by following formula (according to [34]):" "Ie(λ) = 2.303 * I(λ)*q*f*amax*S(λ)," "where I(λ) is the light intensity expressed as the number of photons falling on the unit square (photons/µm2),"; amax is the pigment's peak density from microspectrophotometry and S(λ) its relative spectral sensitivity; q = 0.67 (tool summary). "The mean value of half-saturating effective intensities serves further as a sensitivity parameter."

**Error (calculated stimulus and units omitted), both lines.** The records name I0.5 but not its units (photoisomerizations per µm3 per flash) and do not say that the intensity is calculated from photon flux, pigment density, quantum yield, a polarisation factor and spectral sensitivity. That calculation is what the 1.9 and 1.4 mean: sensitivity at equal photoisomerization density in each rod, with each pigment's light catch already divided out, not sensitivity to equal incident photons. The rules list calculated against measured stimulus as a design point, and the task asked for units; a reader of the record could take the ratio as a light threshold. Smaller: captive holding of up to 8 months (frogs) and 6 months (toads) before recording is omitted. Everything else asked for (single-cell suction pipette, flash sensitivity, n cells, dark adaptation, both rod types, no recording temperature, wild frogs against bred toads, the toad null, the unread frog significance) is in the records. Fixable.
