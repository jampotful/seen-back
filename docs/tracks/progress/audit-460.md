## Unit-wide rulings

- **Bibliography.** Both article pages print Zoosystematics and Evolution 101(3), year 2025. 145776: authors "Yu-Yang Zeng, Xin-Rui Pu, Hao-Tian Lei, Thaung Naing Oo, Xiao-Yong Chen", pages 1123-1136, title as filed (Eschmeyer ref. 42212 agrees: v. 101 (no. 3): 1123-1136, 24 June 2025). 144375: ten authors in the filed order, pages 1023-1055, title as filed (MDD citation agrees). Metadata faults 0.
- **Licence.** The CC BY 4.0 sentence is printed on both article_preview pages (the records' copies) and in the 145776 JATS licence element. Publisher's own free full text. Met.
- **Bagarius table columns (audit 458 offset check).** Table 2 header on the page and in the JATS: row label, then B. protos (n = 13) Holotype, Range, Mean ± SD, then B. dolichonema (n = 21) Holotype, Range, Mean ± SD. The Eye diameter row's holotype cells (7, 9) lie inside their ranges (5–8, 6–11), and the neighbouring % HL rows (snout 53.5/48, interorbital 30/25, nasal barbel 5/3) are plausible in order, so no row or column offset. The ratio is eye diameter as % of head length (block heading "% of head length"; head length itself is in the % SL block). Holotype sex is not stated for either holotype (KIZ2024000178, 319 mm SL; KIZ2024010545, 216.6 mm SL); the records say so. Trait key sensory_anatomy for fish eye ratio per audit 449. Met.
- **Bagarius quote (counted).** The filed quote "% of head length Eye diameter 7 5–8 6 ± 0.7 9 6–11 8 ± 1.2" splices the block heading, a spanning row of its own, onto the Eye diameter row, which sits two rows below it (Snout length and Interorbital distance intervene, JATS and page agree). That is not one printed row and the gap is unmarked. Quote-form fault, value right: requote to the whole row "Eye diameter 7 5–8 6 ± 0.7 9 6–11 8 ± 1.2" (12 words) and name the block in `where`.
- **Names.** Eschmeyer's Catalog: "Valid as Bagarius protos Zeng & Chen 2025" (holotype KIZ 2024000178) and "Valid as Bagarius dolichonema Zeng & Chen 2025" (holotype KIZ 2024010545), both Sisoridae: Sisorinae. MDD taxon 1006944: Murina medogensis, Vespertilionidae, holotype XZ2024038, status field blank, note "recently described"; a 2025 name with no synonymy, accepted. Taxon errors 0.
- **Murina quote cut.** The measurement line under the heading "Measurements (in mm) and body weight (in g) of the holotype." runs to 24 abbreviation-value pairs (48 words, over the limit), so a cut is needed. The quote starts at the line start, keeps EL with its value, and marks the cut with […]; what is dropped are other measurements (skull values, BW), not a qualifier of EL. Units and holotype status come from the heading, which `where` names. Acceptable. EL is defined in Methods as "ear length (from lower edge of external auditory meatus to tip of pinna)", neither notch nor crown; the record's measurement field carries that definition. Holotype wording "Adult male, field number XZ2024038" is on the page, and Table 3 heads the column "XZ2024038 (Male)" with EL 11.89. Trait fit: main has no pinna record, but sensory_anatomy/anatomical_measurement with mm units is the main form for ear structures (Spermophilus citellus stapes footplate, Scalopus aquaticus cochlear nerve); fit accepted.
- **M. beibengensis skip.** Page: holotype line "Adult male, field number XZ2024037" vs Table 2 header "Holotype (Female)", unexplained. The desk was right not to file it.
- **Murina method/conditions (uncounted).** The record says "Instrument and fixation state not read"; Methods give digital calipers to the nearest 0.1 mm and fixation in 95% ethanol (so preserved specimens). The record did not misstate anything and the value's scale is unaffected; harmless, but the replacement adds it.
