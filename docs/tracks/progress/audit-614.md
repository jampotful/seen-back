## Checks

- Trait (owner ruling). The Material and methods, read whole sentence by sentence, has no sentence or heading defining eye size; the only eye or diameter words are in the abbreviation list ("ALE = anterior lateral eye", "L/d = length/diameter", "PME = posterior median eye"). Decision: KEEP eye_diameter_mm. "Eye interdistances and diameters" is fairly read as "Eye interdistances and [eye] diameters": the sentence prints two figures each labelled as a distance between two named eyes (PME–PME, PME–ALE) and one figure under the bare eye label PME, which can only be one of the "diameters".
