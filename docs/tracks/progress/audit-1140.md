## Rule questions put to this audit

- (a) Pupil shape (line 10): **MUST MOVE to pupil_shape.** SENSORY-ANATOMY.md says to use sensory_anatomy "when no existing anatomical trait fits", and main carries 1716 pupil_shape rows (canon-7, desk-d573 and others), none with evidence_kind. The two older sensory_anatomy pupil rows on main (desk-d353 Eublepharis jhuma, desk-d354 Phrynobatrachus bibita) are precedent the desk should not follow. The replacement moves line 10 to trait pupil_shape, value "horizontal", drops evidence_kind/structure/measurement and carries the "new trait: pupil_shape (same key as main ...)" note declaration that desk-d573 uses so check.py passes.
- (b) Iris colour in life (line 13): **DROP.** No iris trait exists, the BRIEF trait table and EYES fields have no slot for it, and iris pigmentation in a photographed living frog is a coloration character, not the anatomy of a sensory structure; sensory_anatomy would make every colour-in-life sentence fileable. No precedent on main files iris colour.
- (c) Long semicolon sentences (lines 10 to 13): each quote was re-read on the publisher page in consecutive pieces. Each starts at its sentence's start, marks every cut with "…", stays within 40 words by check.py's count (16, 28, 34, 15), and drops no qualifier of the clause carrying the value. Quotes MATCH. oa-read.md permits marked cuts; a long source sentence is not by itself a fault.
