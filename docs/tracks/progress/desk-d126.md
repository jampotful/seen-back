# desk-d126 (30 October 2026): plant diel (flower opening and scent timing) and floral sense_thermal

Count on main before filing: plant diel lines exist for about 80 taxa. Among the vein's species: Ipomoea nil, I. purpurea, I. quamoclit, I. batata, Gentiana leucomelaena, Cucurbita pepo, Hemerocallis fulva, Selenicereus undatus, Silene latifolia and S. colorata, Cestrum nocturnum, Portulaca spp. No plant diel line on main for Nicotiana, Petunia, Oenothera, Mirabilis, Nymphaea, Antirrhinum, Rosa, Jasminum, Lilium, Gossypium, Hibiscus, Tulipa, Crocus. Plant sense_thermal on main has no floral thermogenesis taxon (no Nelumbo).

Filed: 2 evidence records in docs/views/evidence/desk-d126.jsonl (no search records, no wanted lines). check.py: 0 problems.
- Mirabilis jalapa, diel: flowers open once at 17h00 and close 4h30 to 6h00 next morning (Leal, Terada, Machado 2001, Acta Scientiarum Biol Sci 23(2): 587-591, CC BY 4.0 on the journal page). No DOI filed: Crossref returned no record for the DOI printed on the page. Conflicts carried in conditions (dusk temperature 34.4 vs 30.4 C; Portuguese abstract says early afternoon).
- Rosa rugosa, diel (scent): total volatile release V-shaped over 9:00 to 21:00 (Yang et al. 2020, Open Chemistry 18(1): 1030-1040, CC BY 4.0, Crossref checked). Weak points in conditions: no absolute amounts, flowers on or off the plant not stated, cultivar not named.

Names: Mirabilis jalapa POWO 162591-2 "This species is accepted", WFO wfo-0000448428 (list.worldfloraonline.org page, December 2023). Rosa rugosa POWO 927373-1 accepted, WFO wfo-0001017421 (2025-06) accepted.

## Held out
- Nelumbo thermal refile: no source found whose own text says heat production changes with ambient temperature. Grant et al. 2008 (J Exp Bot 59(3): 705-714, CC BY-NC, core.ac.uk copy) measures heating against a non-thermogenic bud, not ambient; its ambient 8 to 45 C sentence is Introduction background. Seymour and Schultze-Motel (nature.com 383305a0) and Adelaide repository copies carry no open licence; PMC pages return reCAPTCHA.
- Nymphaea colorata (Ke et al. 2018, BMC Plant Biol 18(1): 143, CC BY 4.0, Crossref checked; natural flowers closed 7:30, open 8:00, maximum 9:00, closing after 16:30): WFO (2023-06 page) lists Nymphaea colorata as a synonym; held out. The sentence is also 41 words. N. nouchali there was cut flowers of a cultivar.
- Clerodendrum inerme (Raju and Kumar 2016, JoTT 8(5), CC BY 4.0; buds open 15:00 to 18:00): POWO lists it as a homotypic synonym of Volkameria inermis; held out. A refile under Volkameria inermis needs the paper's own name handled (WFO not checked).
- Schiedea kaalae (Powers et al. 2020, Front Plant Sci 11: 1116; repository PDF states CC BY 4.0; moth-attracting volatiles peaked within 2 h of the moth's peak visitation): WFO June 2025 lists S. kaalae as Unplaced; held out. No clock time in text.
- Passiflora actinia (Varassin et al. 2018, Acta Bot Bras 32(3): 426-433; anthesis began 5h00 to 6h00): accepted on POWO (324146-2) and WFO (wfo-0001091265), but no licence statement on the PDF or SciELO pages reached; held out.
- Victoria amazonica (Prance and Arias 1975, Acta Amazonica 5(2): 109-139; opens 17:30 to 18:30, flower up to 11 C above ambient): core.ac.uk copy has no licence; SciELO page refused (client error) and old.scielo.br denied; a candidate for both diel and sense_thermal if the SciELO licence line can be read.
- Epiphyllum oxypetalum (Zhang et al. 2025, Hortic Res 12(5): uhaf039; emission rises 200-fold from 8 p.m.): licence not on the pages reached (hep.com.cn), PMC reCAPTCHA; no clock time for opening seen.
- Nicotiana attenuata (Bing et al. 2021, Front Ecol Evol 9: 680463, CC BY on core.ac.uk copy): four accessions, opening described relative between accessions, no clock time per accession; pooled provenance; held out. Yon et al. 2016 New Phytol (LHY/ZTL) Wiley page permission unanswered.
- Petunia axillaris (Hoballah et al. 2005, Planta 222(1): 141-150): doc.rero.ch record shows no licence; Petunia x hybrida papers are hybrids.
- Jasminum sambac (Wang et al. 2022, Plants 11(1): 13): total volatiles peaked around midnight but from methanol or hexane extracts of ground petals (and the two sections disagree), not emission; Yu et al. 2017 Molecules used cultivar 'Bifoliatum'.
- Osmanthus fragrans (Zheng et al. 2017, Front Plant Sci 8: 1869): cultivar 'Liuye Jingui'.
- Nymphaea 'Blue Bird' (Li et al. 2021, Front Plant Sci 12: 701633): cultivar, cut flowers.
- Oenothera (Anton and Denisow 2018, Acta Agrobot 71(2), CC BY 4.0): five species pooled under one opening window (8 to 9 p.m.); pooled.
- Ipomoea cairica, I. grandifolia (Maimoni-Rodella and Yanagizawa 2007, Planta Daninha 25(1)): species-specific times only in Table 2, which the page shows as an image; the text sentence pools all three.
- Hibiscus rosa-sinensis (Trivellini et al. 2016, J Exp Bot 67(20), CC BY): cultivar 'La France', detached flowers, no clock hours. Hibiscus hirtus (ispm.co.in 2025): no licence line.
- Rosa x damascena diurnal volatiles (J Appl Bot Food Qual): hybrid name. Tulipa gesneriana in the Yang 2020 paper: no sentence on total emission, only compound counts.
- Tulip petal opening vs temperature (Azad et al., Ann Appl Biol 2007 and Plant Cell Physiol 2008) and Crocus thermonasty: paywalled or no open licence found.
- Gossypium: searches returned only gene-silencing and ovule papers with no flower-opening hour and no licence.
- Reviews (Fenske and Imaizumi 2016 Front Plant Sci 7: 462; typeset flower-opening review): reviews, not filed.
