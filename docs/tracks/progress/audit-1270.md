### Line 57 faults (4)
1. Heading path omits the printed 'Results' heading: the page nests 'Taxonomic account' under 'Results' (where and note corrected; desk-d512 carries the same omission).
2. Method quote starts mid-sentence with no cut mark (the sentence opens 'Following Fei et al. (2009) and Rowley et al. (2013), measurements were taken ...'), and the TMP entry is quoted without its printed '(TMP)'.
3. ASW holotype line silently corrected: ASW prints 'by original designaiton'; restored with [sic].
4. Eye-size passage not quoted in the note as the brief requires; added: 'eye diameter (EYE) from the anterior corner of the eye to posterior corner of the eye'.

### Lines 58 to 62 faults (5 each, same in every line)
1. Note's 'Printed heading path: Taxonomic account > Odorrana nagao sp. nov. > Type material.' is wrong and contradicts `where`. The XML nests sec 'Taxonomic account' inside sec 'Results', and Table 4 is cited under 'Variation.' (printed with a period). Both fields now read 'Results > Taxonomic account > Odorrana nagao sp. nov. > Variation.'.
2. Table 4 rows misdescribed: '(N = 4)' was put in the header cells, but it is printed in the Sex row ('Sex | M | F | F | F | F | (N = 4) | (N = 4)'), and the Sex and Type status rows were quoted without their labels. Caption now quoted with the reader's SD expansion removed.
3. Type material given as 'reader's paraphrase' rather than as printed. Replaced with the printed lines (institution expansions removed, cuts marked).
4. Note gives the licence reading twice and contradicts itself ('three pieces' and then 'two pieces'). The duplicate is removed.
5. Eye-size passage not quoted in the note; added: 'ED eye diameter (from the anterior corner to posterior corner of the eye);'.
