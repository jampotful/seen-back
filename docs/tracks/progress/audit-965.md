# Audit 965: desk-d480, lines 16 to 17

Unit desk-d480 (17 records, trait eye_diameter_mm). This audit judges only lines 16 and 17 (file order), Vietnamophryne abscondita, Poyarkov NA et al. 2026, Herpetozoa 39: 145-160, DOI 10.3897/herpetozoa.39.e191333. Two WebSearch searches were run. Every page was opened from a URL in those results: the Pensoft article_preview id 191333 (title and DOI checked on each of four reads, and each read was the right paper) and the Amphibian Species of the World species page.

## Checks
- Title (verbatim), all nine authors in printed order, Herpetozoa 39: 145-160 (2026) and the DOI all agree with the page. No issue number is printed.
- Licence: the whole sentence is printed as the unit quotes it (CC BY 4.0).
- Methods (Materials and methods > Morphological analysis): '(4) eye length (EL; distance between anterior and posterior corners of eye)'. **No eye-diameter definition is printed in Methods.** The only 'diameter' entries there are for finger and toe disks and the outer palmar tubercle. The holotype description uses 'longer than eye diameter (SL/EL 1.33)' loosely, but that is not a Methods definition. Units in mm: Methods says 'All measurements were taken to the nearest 0.02 mm and subsequently rounded to 0.1 mm precision from preserved specimens using a digital caliper under a light dissecting microscope.', and the Measurements text begins '(in mm):'.
- **Trait wrong on both lines: they are moved from eye_diameter_mm to sensory_anatomy** (desk precedent: Sphenomorphus tamchucensis EL was filed as sensory_anatomy).
- Type material as printed: 'Holotype.' VRTC NAP-12097 (field tag NAP-12097), adult male; 'Paratype (n = 1).' ZMMU A-8446 (field tag NAP-12098), adult male. Codes, sexes and the count agree. Values EL 1.65 (holotype) and EL 1.87 (paratype) agree digit for digit.
- Heading path as printed: Results > Taxonomic conclusions > Vietnamophryne abscondita sp. nov. > Measurements. No Class, Order or Family headings are printed. The unit's `where` leaves out 'Taxonomic conclusions'.
- The holotype measurement sentence begins '(in mm): Holotype (...'. The unit's quote starts at 'Holotype', which is mid-sentence. The paratype entry is its own sentence, so its quote is fine.
- Table 3 prints only the SL/EL ratio. The unit's claim that it gives 'SVL ranges' was not confirmed, so the claim is reworded. No table prints EL in mm.
- ASW: heading 'Vietnamophryne abscondita Poyarkov, Gorin, Le, Trofimets, Duong, Nguyen, Pawangkhanant, Suwannapoom, and Nguyen, 2026', Herpetozoa, Wien, 39: 152, 'Holotype: VRTC NAP-12097, by original designation.' The page prints no validity statement, no synonymy and no classification path. The URL path is Anura/Microhylidae/Asterophryinae/Vietnamophryne, so family Microhylidae is right.
- Not on main: git grep of the species and the DOI over docs/ on origin/main returns no hit. Neither note makes a Crossref claim. The unit field reads desk-d480.

## Verdicts
- Line 16 (holotype VRTC NAP-12097, EL 1.65): ERROR, fixable. Faults (3): wrong trait (eye_diameter_mm should be sensory_anatomy); `where` omits 'Taxonomic conclusions'; quote starts mid-sentence (it omits '(in mm): '). There is also a minor unverified Table 3 'SVL ranges' claim in `conditions`.
- Line 17 (paratype ZMMU A-8446, EL 1.87): ERROR, fixable. Faults (2): wrong trait; `where` omits 'Taxonomic conclusions'. The same `conditions` rewording applies.

Errors: 2 of 2 lines (16, 17); 5 counted faults. Nothing is dropped; both lines are replaced below. check.py on a scratch copy reports 2 records and 0 problems, both plain and with --require-evidence-kind.
