## Desk disposition after audit 256

Audit 256 judged all 8 records: 5 counted errors (line 1, Kogia sima highest tested frequency is 180 kHz in the text but 152 kHz in Table 1; line 2, the 149 kHz limit sat outside the quote; line 6, Spodoptera moths were reared from field larvae and the visual-cue groups were a laboratory colony; line 7, the Periplaneta light-filter null and the 1.1 s bound were missing; line 8, the termite comparison sat outside the quote). Because the audit covered the whole unit, the error records are corrected with the auditor's replacement text and all 8 merge. Rulings confirmed by the audit: the Frontiers PDFs print the CC BY statement, so the licence rule is met; the eLife record cites the Version of Record with its eLife Assessment, so it is not a held-out preprint; an abstract-only record flagged partial with access "abstract" is allowed. Kogia breviceps and Delphinus capensis from Houser 2022 remain unlogged.
