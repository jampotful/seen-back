## Checks

- Citation: the PDF prints "Driving Factors for the Evolution of Species-Specific Echolocation Call Design in New World Free-Tailed Bats (Molossidae)", the authors Kirsten Jung, Jesús Molinari and Elisabeth K. V. Kalko, and "Citation: Jung K, Molinari J, Kalko EKV (2014) ... PLoS ONE 9(1): e85279. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0085279". The unit's title, authors, journal string, year and DOI are all correct.
- Licence: the full printed sentence is "This is an open-access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License, which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the original author and source are credited." The unit cut it after "in any medium" without a cut mark. Fault on all 8 lines, fixed.
- Values: each was checked digit for digit against the Table 3 rows. E. dabbenei Low: 21.3±1.2 / 13.7±0.5 / 7.6±1.1, CD 28.3±2.8, 22/6. E. dabbenei High: 24.6±2.3 / 15.8±0.8 / 8.9±2.9, CD 25.6±1.8, 23/6. E. nanus Low: 27.9±0.1 / 25.2±0.2 / 2.8±0.3, CD 15.6±2.4, 8/4. All match. EF is below SF in every row and SF − EF = BW to rounding, so no row is physically inconsistent. The mirror shows ± as '6' just as the unit said, so the decoding holds.
- Heading/where (fault, fixed): the Table 3 caption is printed as "Search flight call parameters for the genus Eumops.". The unit wrote "for the genera ...", which is Table 1/2 wording. The column headers are now given as printed.
- Search flight: printed as "we focus our attention only on calls produced during the search phase [4]". Approach and terminal phase calls were excluded. This matches.
- Recording conditions (ERROR, all 8): the method field gives "free-flying recordings in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama", all "in open space". The paper instead prints "Recordings of molossid echolocation calls were obtained in Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Bolivia, and Brazil over a period of 10 years". It describes three kinds of recording: captured bats released while being recorded (Costa Rica and Panama, in 20–30 m clearings toward open space), high-flying individuals in Venezuela that were then collected with a shotgun, and free-flying bats in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama. Open space is printed only for the Panama high-flying recordings. The paper does not say where or how E. dabbenei or E. nanus were recorded, and its prose never mentions either species. The unit dropped Costa Rica and Venezuela and over-assigned "open space". Fixed.
- Individuals (ERROR, all 8): the conditions field says "The paper states neither the number of individuals ...". However, the Figure 3 caption prints "Symbols represent sequences (individuals) per species.", which means the paper equates each sequence (NS) with an individual. The unit omitted this sample-composition statement. Fixed.
- "Second call type not filed here" (fault, all 8): this is false. Both call types of E. dabbenei (lines 16-21) and of E. nanus (lines 22-27) are filed. Fixed.
- Low/High labels: the Table 3 footnote prints "Call type ( = call frequency alternation) refers to changes in frequency.", and the Figure 2 caption prints "Call frequency alternation (2 or 3 frequencies)". Low and High themselves are not defined. The unit's statement is broadly right but did not quote the footnote, and the fix adds it. Call types were assigned "Based on differences in peak frequency (>2 kHz) and structure" (the mirror extracts this as '(.2 kHz)').
- Taxonomy (MDD): 1005178 gives Eumops dabbenei O. Thomas, 1914, Living, Molossidae (Molossinae). 1005185 gives Eumops nanus (G. S. Miller, 1900), Living, Molossidae, with the taxonomy note "split from bonariensis" (synonyms include Eumops bonariensis nanus Sanborn, 1932). The table prints Eumops nanus and Eumops dabbenei, both accepted MDD names. The unit omitted the "split from bonariensis" note on lines 22-24 (fault, added). The MDD pages print Subclass and no Class heading, as the unit says.
- Repeats: I grepped origin/main (7b43aedc6) docs/ for "Eumops" and "dabbenei"/"nanus". The only hits are this unit's untracked files. The DOI appears only in docs/views/audit/corrected/mammal-groups.jsonl, as Tadarida brasiliensis. Not a repeat.
- The unit field reads desk-d518, and there is no Crossref claim. The eye/diameter clauses of the launch prompt do not apply because this is an echolocation unit.
