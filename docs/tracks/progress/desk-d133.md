## Filed: 3 evidence records, desk-d133.jsonl (check.py 0 problems)
Lygodium japonicum, Ohishi et al. 2021, Plants 10(12): 2709, doi 10.3390/plants10122709 (Crossref checked; CC BY 4.0; MDPI page states open access licence). sense_chemical (hormone responses of cultured protonema), grade m, full text read.
- ABA added to GA4-methyl ester medium: protonema length 78.02 um (N = 76), about 50% of GA4-Me alone (Results sentence, 36 words).
- Same treatment, Figure 2 legend: 0.23 +/- 0.31 antheridia per spore against 2.05 +/- 0.49 with GA alone.
- IAA added: no apparent antheridium, abnormal swelling (Results sentence with Figure S1).
Carried in conditions: every treatment contained GA4-Me, so there is no GA-free control in Figure 2; the text gives 10 uM ABA and IAA but the Methods sentence says 100 uM (and GA4-Me 10 or 100 nM); Steel-Dwass p < 0.05, N = 60/76/44; at least three independent samples; antheridia still formed on some ABA protonema and the authors say ABA does not seem to suppress antheridia directly; IAA protonema were 1.3 times longer (200.52 um, N = 44); non-significant results elsewhere in the paper (PPBo 1 and 10 uM, TIBA 1 uM, yucasin and kynurenine alone) are not in these records.
Names: POWO 17143040-1 Lygodium japonicum (Thunb.) Sw. "This species is accepted"; WFO wfo-0000748052, wfoplantlist.org/taxon/wfo-0000748052-2024-12 accepted (December 2024 snapshot).
Not filed from this paper: the IAA protonema-length sentence (its quote names no hormone), and the abstract sentences (partial reads of the same findings).

## Desk disposition after audit 220
Audit 220 found 2 errors in 3. Line 1 (ABA, protonema length 78.02 um) merged with one wording fix (the antheridia statement comes two sentences later, not in the following sentences). Line 2 (ABA, antheridia 0.23 +/- 0.31) was dropped: the quote was the Results sentence, not the Figure 2 legend, so "per spore" and the 2.05 +/- 0.49 comparison were outside it, and the drop was stated as one fifth where the means fall to about one ninth (the paper's "one fifth" refers to protonema carrying antheridia). Refile with the whole 28-word legend sentence. Line 3 (IAA) was dropped: the paper's Results 2.2, after the Figure S2 transfer experiment, says the abnormal protonema morphology is not directly linked to disrupted antheridium formation, and that auxin inhibits GA-induced antheridium formation; the record's conditions stated the reverse causal reading. Correction to this unit's note: main does hold a Lygodium japonicum sense_chemical line (oa-9 line 227, Hornych, conspecific antheridiogen). Trait scope: the auditor judged agar hormone assays on living gametophytes in scope; if the owner reads the culture-medium clause as covering every agar assay, line 1 and some lines already on main would fall. Held-out leads: Ceratopteris richardii auxin (Woudenberg 2024, no licence line on the readable copy), Azolla filiculoides far-red (Dijkhuizen 2021).
