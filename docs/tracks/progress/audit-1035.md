Desk note said the notes cite this paper "for the split"; reworded to the printed text (fault). Accepted as a species page under MDD; blank status flagged for the owner.
- Pooling: one bat, one site, one species; four parameters of the same calls are separate records (desk rule). Means with n of calls unstated kept on the d505 / audit 1001 stand (single-species means kept; if the owner reads every mean as pooled, all four drop together).

- No Crossref claim in any note.
