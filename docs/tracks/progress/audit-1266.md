## Faults (all fixable; values and mappings correct)

2. Lines 25-32, where and note heading path: the unit gives Results > Taxonomic proposal > Nidirana guangxiensis ... sp. nov. On the page the species heading is a sibling of 'Taxonomic proposal' under Results, and Table 4 sits under the 'Variations' subsection ('Measurements of type series are given in Table 4.'). Fixed to Results > Nidirana guangxiensis Mo, Lyu, Huang, Liao & Wang, sp. nov. > Variations.
3. Lines 25-32, conditions: 'block 'second block'' quotes a label the table does not print, and 'Paratypes as printed (18)' does not print the paratype line as printed ('Eighteen specimens.'). Reworded.
4. Lines 30-32, note: the Methods eye passage is not quoted in the note (brief requires it). Added, with cut marks.
5. Lines 30-32, eye field: paraphrase 'to the posterior corner' inserts 'the' not printed. Set to the printed wording.
