Faults:
- a (counted, all 4): provenance misstated. The method gives 'free-flying recordings in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama'; the paper records 'in Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Bolivia, and Brazil over a period of 10 years', with Venezuelan reference recordings of high-flying individuals 'collected with a shotgun', and prints no per-species site or recording mode. The method drops two countries and the shot reference recordings and does not say per-species sites are not printed; the 'mean values per call type within recordings' pseudo-replication correction is also omitted.
- b (counted, all 4): conditions claim 'other call types of the species are printed in neighbouring rows and not all are filed here'. False: Table 3 prints only Low and High for E. auripendulus and both are filed (lines 55-60).
- c (counted, all 4): omission of the Figure 3 caption 'Symbols represent sequences (individuals) per species.', which bears on the unit's statement that the paper gives no number of individuals.
- d (format, all 4): licence sentence cut after 'in any medium' with no cut mark and without 'Copyright: 2014 Jung et al.'; overlap note stale (says only Tadarida brasiliensis carries the DOI on main; the five desk-d518 species now do too); url names the PMC article page although the PMC PDF storage copy was read (replaced with the copy this audit read).
- e (format, all 4): quote keeps the text-layer '6' for ± ('35.864.1'); replaced with the decoded row as main's merged Eumops dabbenei lines do, checked digit by digit.

Counts: 4 lines judged, 4 errors (all fixable), 0 dropped, 0 held out. Counted faults 12 (a 4, b 4, c 4); format faults 8 (d 4, e 4).
