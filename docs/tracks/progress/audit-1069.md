# Audit 1069, 3 November 2026: desk-d525 lines 7 to 10

Unit: docs/views/evidence/desk-d525.jsonl (10 records, unit desk-d525, copied untracked into the worktree). Only lines 7 to 10 in file order were judged. Opus auditor. 3 WebSearches, 6 WebFetches.

## Brief mismatch (read first)

The launch brief's desk flags describe nine Rhinolophus capensis resting-frequency rows from Odendaal, Jacobs and Bishop 2014 (BMC Evol Biol 14: 60) and ask for eye-diameter checks. The unit file has neither. Lines 7 to 10 are Vu et al. 2025 (Biodiversity Data Journal 13: e165516): Rhinolophus perniger peak frequency and duration, and Tylonycteris tonkinensis peak energy and duration. The Odendaal flags match desk-d522, already merged on main (commit 5074e721e); a grep of 10.1186/1471-2148-14-60 over docs/ finds only desk-d522 files. The Odendaal decisions (sex-count sums, 248 vs 210, RF definition, d330/d521 overlap) and the eye_diameter_mm checks have no line in my range. All four lines are sense_echolocation with an empty eye field. I audited the lines as they stand, with the same checks audits 1065 and 1066 applied to this paper's other species.

No repeat. The unit field reads desk-d525 on all four lines.

## Text checks

- Quotes, re-read on the XML: line 7 'Echolocation calls recorded from the captured female were broadband FM–CF–FM (Fig. 10d), with peak frequency 31.9 ± 0.7 kHz, starting at 32.4 kHz and ending at 22.7 kHz.' (one sentence; en dashes U+2013 as printed here, where the R. pearsonii account prints hyphens); line 8 'Calls averaged 33.4 ± 0.7 ms in duration, with the longest call lasting 35.3 ms.'; line 9 'Acoustic recordings revealed FM calls with peak energy at 58.2 ± 1.3 kHz, starting at 88.5 kHz and ending at 38.6 kHz (Fig. 14, right).'; line 10 'The mean call duration was 2.1 ± 0.3 ms, with the longest call lasting 10.2 ms.' All exact, whole sentences of at most 40 words, values digit for digit.
- Species headings as printed: 'Rhinolophus perniger Hodgson, 1843' and 'Tylonycteris tonkinensis Tu, Csorba, Ruedi & Hassanin, 2017', under 'Family Rhinolophidae' and 'Family Vespertilionidae' (no family authority printed). The unit's `where` omits both authorities.
- Individuals: the Results print 'In contrast, eleven species were represented by a single individual, including ... R. perniger, H. griffini, M. alticraniatus, M. muricola, T. tonkinensis and T. tenuis.' The R. perniger account prints 'a single female was captured' and 'The individual was non-reproductive.' The T. tonkinensis account refers to 'the specimen' and gives no sex, recording mode, locality or date. The unit's T. tonkinensis lines say no individual is named and file 'individual ... not stated', which the Results contradict.
- Methods (Material and methods > Echolocation recordings and analyses) read whole: detector sentence ends '(Wildlife Acoustics, Inc., Maynard, Massachusetts, USA) connected to the smartphone device.'; two recording conditions; 'All recordings were analysed visually using spectrograms to characterise call structure and frequency parameters.'; comparison with earlier Vietnamese references. No definition of peak frequency, peak energy or duration, no '± SD' definition, no call count. The unit's method quotes cut the detector sentence without a cut mark and paraphrase '(smartphone)'.
- Line 10: as printed, the longest call (10.2 ms) is almost five times the mean (2.1 ± 0.3 ms), and 10.2 ms is also the longest call printed in the H. armiger account. The unit filed it without flagging this; the replacement files it as printed and flags a possible typesetting error.
