# desk-d289: ocelli and eye statements in millipedes and woodlice (small soil/cave arthropods), 6 records

Branch research/desk-d289 from origin/main. About 40 WebSearch and WebFetch calls. `python3 -I tools/senses/check.py | grep 'd289\.'`: 6 records, 0 problems. Every page URL was returned by a WebSearch first; Crossref read for all five DOIs (author order matches the pages). Whole-docs grep and desk-* branch evidence checked by genus and DOI: no repeats.

Filed (docs/views/evidence/desk-d289.jsonl):
1. Glyphiulus longus (Cambalopsidae, Spirostreptida), 2-5 ocelli per eye patch, type series, ZooKeys 1056.
2. Paracortina kyrang (Paracortinidae, Callipodida), 19 ommatidia (9+3+7), holotype male only; Diagnosis says 19 or 20, ZooKeys 1149.
3. Hyleoglomeris alba, "Ocelli totally absent.", holotype male, ZooKeys 1108.
4. Hyleoglomeris halang, "Ocelli 6+1", printed notation, side not stated, ZooKeys 1108.
5. Hyleoglomeris translucida, "Ocelli ca 6+1", single holotype male, Biodiversity Data Journal 1: e1000 (CC BY 3.0).
6. Trichoniscoides castanheirensis, one large black ocellus, EJT 1034 (colour sentence, both sexes, holotype male); Crossref licence "unspecified" plus page-printed CC BY 4.0.

Dropped:
- Glyphiulus promdami (same paper): eye sentence 137 characters, only a clause reachable.
- Diploexochus cacique ("20-21 ommatidia") and D. troglobius ("four ommatidia"), ZooKeys 1223: the eye statements are clauses after semicolons in a character list, not whole sentences.
- Porcellio aguerensis (ZooKeys 1225): "eyes with 24-28 ommatidia" is a clause in a long cephalon sentence.
- Trichoniscoides oloensis, T. cordovensis: no explicit count word.
- Hyleoglomeris subreducta, H. insularis (BDJ 1): sentences over the readable length, only the opening reachable.
- Chthonius kirghisicus (Subterranean Biology 40): "one pair of eyes" sits inside a ~400-character carapace sentence and a 2,000-character diagnosis.
- Spelaeochthonius yinae, Allochthonius lini/xuae (EJT 2065), Allochthonius spp. (ZooKeys 1139): clauses; already held out by d287.
- Tonkinosoma tiani, Haplocookia enghoffi, Desmoxytes (ZooKeys 185), Trichopolydesmidae (ZooKeys 891): no eye sentence on the pages.
- Plazi pages return a redirect placeholder; PMC refused; Zenodo PDFs unreadable. Nothing from them used.
- Harvestmen, schizomids, palpigrades, symphylans, pauropods, proturans, diplurans, mites, amphipod ommatidia: searches returned no readable OA short eye sentences.
