## Desk disposition after audit 285

Audit 285: 1 of 1 record in error (100%), fixable. Pooled audits 172 to 285: 263 of 613 (42.9%). The record merged with the auditor's replacement text: the 1 mg null is stated as a ceiling (unheated 1 mg gave 0.34 ± 0.05 mV, which the authors read as the maximal receptor response), with the sample sizes, the unpaired design, the absence of a heated blank and of a degradation check at 135 °C. The progress note did not carry the authors' ceiling explanation.

Owner questions: whether a null at a dose the authors say already saturates the response should be filed under sense_chemical at all, or only the positive 0.1 mg heating result; confirm that heating an odour source with the antenna airstream measured nearly unchanged (about 1 °C) takes sense_chemical with no sense_thermal row implied.
