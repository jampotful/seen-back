# Light-response classification after audit 224

30 October 2026. Inspected main: `e8a17e020c9aa7cf6e4a6f5064ca4f0d4cc79dc2`. This is a prospective classification assessment and targeted, informed replay, not a new statistical audit or a migration of accepted evidence. [Audit 224](audit-224.md), [desk-d138](desk-d138.md) and all historical decisions remain unchanged.

## Recommendation

Adopt [LIGHT-RESPONSE.md](../../views/research/LIGHT-RESPONSE.md). Require a documented light exposure–endpoint comparison for response evidence. Admit controlled interventions and explicitly qualified natural associations. Distinguish measured results from supported interpretations and untested explanations. Keep provenance, taxonomic grade and causal support separate.

This is stronger than either a laboratory-only rule or a rule accepting any author attribution. A natural-light experiment can manipulate exposure; an observation can measure an association without identifying its cause. Conversely, a carefully replicated common garden can establish population differentiation without identifying light as its driver. Prior biological knowledge can make an explanation plausible without changing what the present comparison measured.

The measurement/interpretation distinction is our methodological assessment of the designs below, not a newly discovered quotation from a consensus standard. The project-specific scope still includes growth, germination and epidermal focusing under the owner's 25 October decisions. Requiring molecular proof of sensory transduction would unnecessarily exclude those authorized endpoints.

## Audit 224 ruling

The 2015 Soolanayakanahally paper reports **2007 measurements**, not a paper published in 2007. In the inspected Methods, five balsam-poplar populations shared the Indian Head garden. The initial greenhouse 19-hour regime was common propagation, not a contrasted treatment for the 2007 bud-set result. Results give two illustrative population means, not the observed extremes of all five populations. Discussion attributes fall bud set to day length and cites prior work.

Therefore the White Horse and Fredericton bud-set means are evidence of a provenance-associated phenological difference under a common environment. They do not estimate a response to different light exposures or establish a critical day length. Two dates along a shared seasonal trajectory do not isolate light from the other seasonal cues. The photoperiod interpretation is biologically motivated, but it cannot convert this contrast into the measurement it lacks. Audit 224's rejection stands on trait fit and the independent unsupported-range wording.

This does not mean Populus balsamifera is insensitive to light. The inspected Charrette thesis abstract itself describes controlled photoperiod work on balsam poplar as well as tamarack. That is a distinct candidate source requiring its own ordinary reading/audit before any new evidence is filed. No rejected record was reinstated and no new biological evidence was submitted here.

## Source trail and practical limits

Sources opened directly through public publisher/repository URLs after the web-search service returned empty errors. No login, proxy library or unauthorized copy was used. Full source texts remained outside the repository. PDF extraction was used for prose; rotated/graphical elements were not treated as verified numerical data.

- [Soolanayakanahally et al. 2015](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2015.00528/full): Balsam poplar common garden Methods; phenology Results; Discussion distinguishes earlier extended-day work.

- [Ruttanaprasert et al. 2013](https://journals.gau.ac.ir/article_1111.html), publisher-linked PDF: planting-date design; calculated photoperiod; GDD co-variation. Other restrictions on elite lines remain independently applicable.
- [Jezdinský and Řezníček 2011](https://acta.mendelu.cz/pdfs/acu/2011/03/11.pdf): Methods p. 82 specifies artificial shading; this changes the abstract-only design impression.
- [Doroszewski and Berbeć 2019](https://journals.iung.pl/wydane/PJA38_2.pdf): pp. 16–17 distinguish exposure intervention, natural measurements and regression. The old record URL returned 503; DOI resolved to this publisher PDF. Natural conditions do not imply absence of manipulation.

- [Vujičić et al. 2011](https://botanicaserbica.bio.bg.ac.rs/arhiva/pdf/2011_35_1_534_full.pdf): controlled-condition combinations and germination-null statement.

## Implementation and coordination with PR #45

The new rule is linked from BRIEF, desk-screen, oa-read, desk-audit and oa-audit. It uses existing `value`, `method`, `conditions` and `note` fields; no new trait, grade, JSON schema, validator or runtime behavior is introduced. Observational qualifications must survive in `value` so a consumer cannot silently present the causal interpretation by hiding notes. Existing binary coverage/grade summaries are not proof of causal sufficiency; a future consumer must preserve the distinction before claiming demonstrated light regulation.
