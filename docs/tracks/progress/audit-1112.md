The unit's note says the DOI "has no ligature loss"; that is false. Corrected to 10.11609/jott.4662.11.4.13432-13447 and the note rewritten. (Side observation, not counted here: main's desk-d562 row with 10.11609/jot.4466.11.4.13478-13491 likely has the same ligature loss; the desk should check it on the article page.)
- Quote: Abstract sentence 8, verbatim from the HTML pieces: "The Asiatic Golden Cat and Marbled Cat were strongly diurnal, Clouded Leopard largely crepuscular and nocturnal, and Leopard Cat largely nocturnal." 22 words, whole sentence, one section. Correct.

Results say 90% of captures from protected areas. No contrary result omitted: Results/Discussion support each category (Clouded Leopard "most active during dawn and dusk"; Golden Cat and Marbled Cat "almost entirely diurnal"; Leopard Cat "predominantly nocturnal", unchanged with Marbled Cat present n=85/absent n=503). Marbled Cat's slight altitude variation (decline after a pre-noon peak above 1,500 m) does not contradict "strongly diurnal"; not counted.
