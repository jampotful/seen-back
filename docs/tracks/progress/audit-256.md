### Ma et al. 2026 (line 6), Spodoptera frugiperda, sense_magnetic

Quote MATCH (abstract, whole sentence). Value restates it and says no more. Method confirmed: flight simulator in a 3D Helmholtz coil; changed field "reversed the horizontal direction of the local magnetic field by 180°" with intensity, horizontal component and inclination "statistically unchanged"; control with "the coil was switched off"; no zero-field condition; phases I to V with the cue conflict in phases II and III; the conflict response "was not instantaneous but delayed until the second 5 min experimental phase with this condition" (verbatim). - **Counted (scientific meaning; conditions misstate the animals).** The record says "wild moths collected from maize farmland in Yuanjiang ...". The Methods say field moths were collected "as mature larvae at the 5th to 6th instar stages" and reared to adults (fall generations September to October 2023 and 2024, spring generation April to May 2024), and that the Fall Exp. Lab, Fall Control, Fall Exp. Dark and Fall Exp. BMVC groups were a laboratory colony (3rd to 5th laboratory generations under simulated fall photoperiods) founded from Yuanjiang maize-field moths (tool summary, two passes). The two experiments that remove visual cues, darkness and reduced visual cues (BMVC: no significant group orientation in any of the four phases, tool summary), are the basis of the value's "visual cues are indispensable for magnetic orientation", and both used laboratory-reared moths, not wild-caught adults. The record also leaves out the seasonal design (spring and fall generations tested for their seasonal headings). Misstating the source population of the key result changes its applicability. Fix below.

### Slaby et al. 2018 (line 7), Periplaneta americana, sense_magnetic

Quote MATCH (abstract, whole sentence). Method and n confirmed: 1010 animals, 66 excluded, "regardless of sex", single Petri dishes; Merritt coil, "The total vector of local GMF intensity was 46.8 uT (homogeneity ± 0.2 µT) and inclination 68◦ on the testing table", "feeding the coil with current 1.05 A rotated the North position by 60◦" (verbatim, PDF). Inter-stimulus interval results: "under simultaneous onsets of GMF rotation and hot air in ISI = 0 s no association is created" (n = 78, P = 0.647) and "if GMF rotation is advanced by only 1 s prior to hot air onset" (n = 79, P = 0.011), verbatim. The note's 0 s null is right.

- **Counted (scientific meaning; value and conditions omit two limits).** (a) Light dependence: "After filtering off the blue and green part of the light spectrum (<550 nm)" "no significant MIF was found" (n = 90, P = 0.068), verbatim; the experiments ran under a 75 W bulb at 0.22 W/m² (tool summary). The conditioned magnetic response is therefore shown only under light that includes wavelengths below 550 nm and fails without them; the record mentions cryptochrome only as the title's attribution and gives no light condition. This is a null result that limits the claimed effect (oa-audit.md). (b) Magnitude: the tested intervals near the limit were 0 and 1 s (no 0.5 s), and the Discussion bounds the delay at "a maximum of 1.1 s, likely much shorter" (verbatim); the value states "sub-second" as the measured span without the authors' own bound. The unpowered-coil and double-wrapped (sham) coil controls were null (tool summary, P = 0.425 and 0.809), which supports the magnetic reading and belongs in conditions. Counted once. Fix below.
