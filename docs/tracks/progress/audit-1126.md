## Key question (sentence record versus table record)

For Litoria hastula the sentence's TYM/EYE = 0.35 is computed from the same holotype measurements printed in Table 1 (TYM 1.4, EYE 4). The two records are one measurement of one specimen stated twice, as a ratio and as a raw cell; filing both double-counts it. Ruling: keep the sentence record (ratio, author-stated, unitless, whole sentence) and drop the table record. A table-row quote should include its printed row label (here 'TYM', as main d476 does with 'EL'); for Vences Table 1 the row's species is printed only in a group row above it, now named in `where`. The same reasoning applies to lines 1 to 6 (another auditor's lines).
