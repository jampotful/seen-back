Scope: lines 41-47 of docs/views/evidence/desk-d517.jsonl only (Pham et al. 2021, Diversity 13(1): 18, DOI 10.3390/d13010018). File order verified: 41-43 Scotophilus kuhlii (FMAXE, sf, tf), 44-47 Myotis hasseltii (d, FMAXE, sf, tf). 3 WebSearches (exact paper title, unfiltered; MDD S. kuhlii; MDD M. hasseltii). ## Checks

- Citation: page gives Long Kim Pham, Bang Van Tran, Quy Tan Le, Trung Thanh Nguyen, Christian C. Voigt; Diversity vol. 13, issue 1, article 18, 2021; DOI 10.3390/d13010018. Authors "Pham LK, Tran BV, Le QT, Nguyen TT, Voigt CC" in printed order, journal string "Diversity 13(1): 18", title and DOI correct. No en-dash page range is involved.
- Licence: printed as "© 2021 by the authors. Licensee MDPI, Basel, Switzerland." then "This article is an open access article distributed under the terms and conditions of the Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) license (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)." The publisher's own copy is CC BY 4.0, so it is accepted. The unit's quoted sentence ends at "license." with the URL parenthesis dropped and no cut mark (fault, fixed on all 7).
- Methods read whole (2.1 Bat Captured, 2.2 Species Identification, 2.3 Acoustics Recording and Data Analysis) through the reader in pieces. Netting, recorder, 256 kHz/16 bit and "at least ten files for each animal" match. The unit omits that "we recorded bat echolocation calls at each mist netting sites ... to obtain echolocation calls of free-flying animals", and it presents the Table 2 rows as tent recordings. Neither Table 2 nor 3.1 says which recordings the "This study" rows hold (design/sample-composition omission). sf/tf are printed as "the peak frequency at the onset [...] and end of each call, respectively"; the unit wrote a looser definition (fault, fixed). The unit gave no tent dimensions (2 m x 4 m x 2 m; fault, added). The eye-size clause of the brief does not apply: no eye_diameter_mm or sensory_anatomy lines are in range, the paper prints no eye passage, and no Class/Order/Family headings are printed. Table 2 sits under "3. Results" / "3.1 The Species Diversity and Their Acoustic Parameters", which has been added to `where`.
- Omitted significant/null results (ERROR, all 7 lines): Section 3.1 and Table 3 test tent against free-flying calls of the same species. The relevant sentences are "Scotophilus kuhlii demonstrated large differences between echolocation call properties emitted by animals flying in the tent and free-flying conspecifics", "significant differences in six out of seven acoustic parameters to this species, except for the lowest frequency", "In all studied species, we observed the effect of the flight environment on the duration of the call" and, for M. hasseltii, no FMAXE difference (t = −0.4311, p = 0.67). Table 3 as read gives S. kuhlii FMAXE 3.6796 (0.001)*, sf 3.2563 (0.002)*, tf 3.7237 (0.001)*, and M. hasseltii d −9.8947 (0)*, sf 2.2077 (0)* (the reader's p looks implausible, flagged), tf 0.7845 (0.43). The Discussion also prints "the tent's confined space affects FMAXE, of the other two species" and "using a flying tent was perhaps not an adequate method to record and describe some bat species' [...] echolocation calls." The unit carries none of these.
- Regional contrast (ERROR, line 46): the Discussion prints "The start frequency was lower in our study than in Malaysia, with a mean frequency of 67.6 kHz (±3.7 kHz) [...] compared to a range of 82 to 104 kHz for conspecifics from Malaysia [36]." This was omitted and has been fixed. Context added as faults: line 44 duration "close to the lowest value observed before [...] 2.5 and 5.5 ms"; line 41 S. heathii 30 kHz against 45.6 kHz; S. kuhlii DFA 67.3% with misidentification as M. hasseltii/P. javanicus; M. hasseltii DFA 80.8%.
- M. hasseltii dispersion discrepancy: Discussion "about 3.86 ms (sd = 0.92 ms) [...] about 50.0 ± 2.8 kHz" against Table 2's 0.50 and 3.09. The means agree, and the Discussion's sf "67.6 kHz (±3.7 kHz)" matches Table 2's 67.55 ± 3.75. Ruling: a note, not a drop. The filed values are the means, which both places print alike. The dispersion is given "as printed" with the conflict stated, and the paper never says which subset (tent, free-flying, pooled) either figure describes.
- "mean" in value: the unit wrote "mean ± x as printed" while its own measurement field says the table does not say it gives means, so the value claims more than the quote (fault, reworded on all 7). The note's "search-phase calls" is unsupported because the article never uses search phase, approach or buzz (fault, fixed on all 7).
- Physical consistency of rows: S. kuhlii sf 60.41 ≤ hf 76.27, tf 42.66 ≥ lf 38.94, fr 37.32 = hf − lf; M. hasseltii sf 67.55 ≤ hf 84.63, tf 47.80 ≥ lf 38.82, fr 45.81 = hf − lf. Both are consistent, so there is nothing to drop on that ground. The quoted rows and every filed value match digit for digit. The rows have no presence columns, so no cuts were made. The S. kuhlii duration cell 5.48 ± 23.8 was correctly left unfiled.
- Authority (MDD): S. kuhlii, Leach, 1821, Vespertilionidae (Vespertilioninae, Scotophilini), "Living", "Listed in MSW3 2005", Vietnam in range. Its taxonomy note "may included S. collinus as a synonym" and says the species "is tentatively retained here until molecular data is used to determine its species status". The unit omitted this (fault, added). M. hasseltii (Temminck, 1840), Vespertilionidae (Myotinae), "Living", MSW3, Vietnam in range, notes "NA". Both names are accepted and the families are correct.
- Repeats: "Scotophilus kuhlii", "Myotis hasseltii" and "d13010018" over the whole docs/ tree on origin/main (b293ab149). The only hit is docs/tracks/progress/desk-d195.md, which names the DOI as a refused fetch and files no record. Not a repeat.
- No Crossref claim in any note. The unit field reads desk-d517 on all 7.
