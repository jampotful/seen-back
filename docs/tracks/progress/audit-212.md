Trait framing (plant sense_thermal and thermogenesis): plant sense_thermal on main is a living plant's response to temperature (phyB thermal reversion, cold acclimation, vernalization, germination at set temperatures, Oxalis leaf closing and flower opening as temperature rose). Floral heat production on its own is a metabolic output, not a sense; it fits sense_thermal only when the record states the response, i.e. that heat output changes with ambient temperature. Line 1's value ("held at 30 to 35 °C while ambient temperature fluctuated between about 8 and 45 °C") implies that response only through regulation against ambient, and its note gives the filing reason as "temperature regulation of a living flower", which is the thermogenesis reading, not the response reading. I judge the framing weak but not by itself wrong: a refiled line should state the response directly (heat production rises as ambient temperature falls), from a source that measures it. One WebSearch surfaced a title of that kind ("Heat-production by sacred lotus flowers depends on ambient-temperature, not light cycle", indexer page, not opened) and a nature.com PDF 383305a0 (not opened); the desk can screen these for licence and a quotable sentence.
