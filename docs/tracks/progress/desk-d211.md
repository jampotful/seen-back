# desk-d211 (animal sense-trait screen, 2024 to 2026 tactic)

About 36 WebSearches and 30 WebFetches. Crossref queries (api.crossref.org) were used to list 2025 to 2026 papers by title words; almost every animal paper that turned up was already on main (for example iScience 2026 jumping-spider flicker paper, Scientific Reports 2025 Electrogena, PeerJ 2023 armadillo, PeerJ 2024 Betta, JEB 2023 dolphin electroreception). The new items came from open JEB papers on harbour seal vibrissal sensing, found through a seal-whisker news item.

## Queries (web)
auditory brainstem response / electroretinogram / flicker fusion / magnetoreception / infrared pit / electroreception / substrate vibration / thermosensation / airflow / tactile / eyes-count phrasings with 2025 and publisher names; sciencedaily and phys.org 2025 to 2026 sensory-discovery phrasings; title searches for each candidate paper. Crossref title queries for: flicker fusion, electroretinogram, visual acuity, vibration, magnetic orientation, thermal preference, electroreception, hearing (2024 to 2026).

## Records (6, docs/views/evidence/desk-d211.jsonl, all Phoca vitulina, sense_touch, grade m)
- Krüger et al. 2018 (10.1242/jeb.170753): vortex rings detected on vibrissal stimulation (abstract sentence).
- Niesterok et al. 2017 (10.1242/jeb.158055): hydrodynamic thresholds 4.2 and 3.7 cm/s for two seals.
- Schulte-Pelkum et al. 2007 (10.1242/jeb.02708): 90% successful trail-following (444 of 495); separate record for the vibrissae-covered control (no success, trial number not reported).
- Niesterok et al. 2017 (10.1242/jeb.148676): all three seals better than chance finding the active nozzle.
- Wieskotten et al. 2010 (10.1242/jeb.041699): 93.7% correct direction at a 5 s delay (above chance to 35 s).
All five papers were read on cob.silverchair.com (publisher's free text, no licence line, Crossref lists the biologists.com user licence). The quotes come from WebFetch output, not from a machine-readable text, so quotecheck was not run; the owner may wish to confirm them against the PDFs. Records 2, 5 and 6 sit in sentences of 118 to 140 characters that the fetch tool flagged but returned whole.

Not on main. Good leads if the owner can open them.
