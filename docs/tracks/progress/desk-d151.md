## Desk disposition after audit 236
Audit 236 judged all 6 Ephoron virgo lambda_max_nm records (Egri, Mészáros and Kriska 2022) errors because no open licence could be found, so none was merged; this note and the yield line are all that landed. The Crossref record carries only the Royal Society text-and-data-mining policy entry, not a Creative Commons licence. The CORE PDF is a copy the authors deposited (also held by REAL, the Hungarian Academy of Sciences Library repository, real.mtak.hu/141896) with no licence, rights statement or version. It is a lawful-looking author deposit, but the rule requires a repository copy to state an open licence (audits 178, 194, 223). Everything else in the records checked out (citation, accepted name, quotes of 15 to 25 words, groups of 14 female larvae, 5 male larvae and 13 female adults, nothing pooled). For any refile the content needs fixing: each note said adults and larvae did not differ in the UV, but the paper's Mann-Whitney test on per-preparation fits gave a UV peak shift of p = 0.047, which the authors call practically similar, and a significant green peak shift (p < 0.01); on the male larvae lines the sentence applied to a comparison the paper made only between female larvae and female adults; female adults are subimagos, and the conditions should say how and when they were collected. A refile needs a stated open licence on the publisher or repository version, so it stays held out unless one appears. Lesson: check the licence of the exact copy read before reading it. Held out: Ephoron larval negative phototaxis paper (not opened); thin groups with no hit on main (Hermissenda, Haliotis, Pyrrhocoris, Placopecten, Idiosepia); groups with one file each (Aurelia, Centruroides, Chiton, Ciona, Dolomedes, Doryteuthis, Dytiscus, Eptatretus, Euprymna, Lithobius, Loligo, Lymnaea, Tegenaria).
