## Methods (whole section read)

A collecting sentence (Mount Kaz Dağı, Balıkesir Province, pitfall traps, 70% ethanol). "All samples were examined and measured with Leica S8 APO Stereomicroscope." A photography sentence (Leica MC170 HD). "Measurements were taken with a micrometric ocular from the dorsal side of the palps and legs and are given in millimetres (mm)." "The epigyne was cleared in 10% potassium hydroxide (KOH) aqueous solution before photography." "The left male palp was used for description and illustration." "The colouration pattern is described, based on specimens preserved in 70% ethanol." Terminology (Bolzern et al. 2013, 2009, 2008; Bosmans 2023). "The taxonomy and distribution follow WSC (2025)." Five SEM sentences. "The following abbreviations are used in the text and figures:" "Eyes: AER ‒ anterior eye row, ALE ‒ anterior lateral eye, AME ‒ anterior median eye, PER ‒ posterior eye row, PLE ‒ posterior lateral eye, PME ‒ posterior median eye" (then spination, palp and epigyne abbreviations). Comparative material (H. paraschiae holotype female). Depositories ZMUT, ZMUU.
