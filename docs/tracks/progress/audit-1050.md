Counted faults:
- a (all 8): provenance misstated. `method` gives 'free-flying recordings in Bolivia, Brazil and Panama' with released bats 'in open space'. The paper prints 'Recordings of molossid echolocation calls were obtained in Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Bolivia, and Brazil over a period of 10 years', includes Venezuelan high-flying individuals collected with a shotgun, and assigns no species to a site or recording mode. MDD lists M. temminckii from neither Panama nor Costa Rica. The Discussion also prints that M. temminckii 'forages for small insects very close to vegetation edges [31]' and reads its calls as reflecting 'the need to discriminate prey echoes against cluttered background', which the unit's blanket 'open space' hides. Fixed.
- b (all 8): conditions say 'other call types of the species are printed in neighbouring rows and not all are filed here'. False: Table 1 prints three call types and all three are filed. Fixed.
- c (all 8): conditions say 'The paper states neither the number of individuals ...' and omit the Figure 3 caption 'Symbols represent sequences (individuals) per species.' (NS = 11 here) and the Methods statement that analyses 'were based on mean values per call type within recordings'. Fixed.

Format faults (listed, not counted): d (8) licence sentence cut after 'in any medium' with no cut mark and without 'provided the original author and source are credited.'; e (8) quote keeps the text-layer '6' for ±, now decoded and said so; f (8) '20-30 m' for the printed '20–30 m'; g (6, lines 1-6) 'start minus end frequency reproduces the printed bandwidth' is wrong in sign for upward sweeps (the note's 'EF minus SF' qualifier was not carried into conditions); h (8) MDD taxonomy note 'previously included M. griseiventer' not given (MDD 1006816 lists M. griseiventer for Colombia only, where the paper did not record, so the name stands); i (8) source.url names the PMC article page while the note says the PMC PDF was read; the replacement names the copy actually read.
