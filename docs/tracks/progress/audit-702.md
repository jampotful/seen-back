## Methods checks (whole Materials and methods read for both papers)

- BDJ 13: e143433 (Falcileptoneta): 14 sentences. Eye passage, exact: 'Eye sizes are measured as the maximum diameter from either the dorsal or frontal view.' Unit: 'All measurements are in millimetres and obtained with an Olympus SZX16 stereomicroscope with a Zongyuan CCD industrial camera.' A diameter definition IS printed, so eye_diameter_mm stands for lines 17-22. The desk omitted 'Measurements method follows Seo (2015).' and 'Specimens were examined under a LEICA M205C stereomicroscope.' (added to method). No AME value is printed.
- BDJ 13: e157512 (Hellamalthonica): 24 sentences. No sentence defines an eye dimension; eyes occur only in the abbreviation sentence 'Eyes: AER ‒ anterior eye row, ALE ‒ anterior lateral eye, AME ‒ anterior median eye, PER ‒ posterior eye row, PLE ‒ posterior lateral eye, PME ‒ posterior median eye.' Unit: '... and are given in millimetres (mm).' sensory_anatomy is correct for lines 23-24. Male count: Methods print 'The present study is based upon the examination of one female and 11 male specimens belonging to the new species.'; the listed material is G5 Holotype male ZMUU, G6 Paratype female ZMUU, G7-G14 Paratype male ZMUU (each individualCount 1), G15 Paratype male ZMUT = 10 males, 1 female. WSC prints holotype ZMUU m, paratype ZMUU 8m 1f, paratype ZMUT 1m (agrees with the list, not the Methods).
