Encounters

      Counts are iNaturalist research-grade records gathered by GBIF for Gainesville, October and November, all years. It is a first list, for one town and one season.

      About

      Regards lets you view the world, for one week, through the eyes of a species you photograph. How many species can you encounter?

      This app is made by Conrad Cheung, an artist, writer, and Assistant Professor of 3D + Extended Media at the University of Florida.
