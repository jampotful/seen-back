MATERIALS AND METHODS:
Study area
Taranga Hills forest is located (23.540 N and 72.380 E) at the start of the Aravalli range in the
Mahesana district of Gujarat. The study area falls in the unclassified reserve (under section-IV)
forest (Champion et.al., 1968) with 18.12 sq. km. The forest has tropical thorn-scrub-like
vegetation. The climate of the study area is semi-arid. Rainfall is an annual average of 663.60mm
and irregular. Diurnal temperature ranges are also irregular with the season, averaging 39°C in
summer and 21°C in winter. The main rivers are Rupen Pushpavti, Saraswati, and the Sabarmati.

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION:
The present study was done from 2017 to 2019 with a collection of a total of 2708 specimens. In the
results of collected specimens, a total of 105 spiders belonging to 68 genera spread over 18 families
were identified (Table:2). The family Araniedae (24 Species) was numerically dominant and also
found in all sites with an orb-weaver guild. The family Salticidae (17 Species) was recorded during
the daytime most diverse in Tirthankar Van. The family Thomisidae (10 species) was dominant in
Tirthankar Van. The family Tetragnathidae and Lycosidae consist of eight species near a pond of
Ajitnath Jain temple. Other Families were recorded as less than eight species. Based on the group of
spiders total eight guilds (Table:3) were recorded which were Orb web builder(33 Species), Foliage
runner (21 Species), Ground runner (19 species), Ambusher(10 Species), Scattered line weaver
(7 Species), Foliage hunter(4 Species), Foliage weaver (2 Species) and Snare/sheet web builder
(1 Species). Seven different types of webs were documented from the study area namely, orb webs,
funnel web, irregular web, sheet web, tangle web, tube web, and tent web.

CONCLUSION:
From these studies, it can be concluded that the spider Fauna of the Taranga Hills Forest is very rich
in quality and quantity and can be used as an effective naturally occurring biological control agent to
fight with the insect pests.
Also, it can be concluded that most diversity of the spider was documented in those parts where
human Activities like Pilgrims, ecotourism, local agro practice in forest area, firewood collection,
Overgrazing, transport were found less. If the essential spider fauna protection steps are not
employed to save their population, it will consequence in the threatening of the spider population.
